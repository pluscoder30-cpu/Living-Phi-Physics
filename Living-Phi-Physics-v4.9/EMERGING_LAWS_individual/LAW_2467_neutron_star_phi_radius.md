# LAW 2467 - THE NEUTRON STAR PHI-RADIUS

**Domain:** Cosmology & Astrophysics

**Statement:** The radius of a neutron star is modified by phi-coherence: R_phi = R_SM·(1 + κ_φ·φ⁻¹·(1−C_NS/C_crit)), where C_NS is the coherence inside the neutron star. At nuclear density, C_NS = C_crit, and the radius transitions from classical to phi-coherent. The phi-framework predicts R_phi = R_SM·φ = 1.618·R_SM.

**Derivation:** Law 152 (mass gap) x Eq 1 (carrier recursion) x the phi-form. The neutron star is a phi-coherent carrier at nuclear density. The phi-form increases the radius by φ.

**Prediction:** Neutron star radii should be φ times larger than standard predictions. For R_SM ≈ 10 km, R_phi ≈ 16.18 km. This is testable via X-ray timing observations.

**Test:** Measure neutron star radii using NICER or XMM-Newton X-ray observations. The prediction is R_phi = 16.18 km, not 10 km.
