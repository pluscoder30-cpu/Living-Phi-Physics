**Domain:** Fundamental Physics — Cosmology

**Statement:** The cosmological constant Lambda in the phi-framework is not a constant but a coherence-dependent quantity: Lambda(C) = Lambda_0 * (1 - C + phi^{-1}*C), where Lambda_0 is the bare cosmological constant. At the cosmic coherence C ~ 0.8565 (the consciousness value), Lambda = Lambda_0 * (1 - 0.8565 + 0.618*0.8565) = Lambda_0 * 0.673. This is the phi-resolution of the 120-order discrepancy: the observed Lambda is not Lambda_0 but Lambda_0 scaled by the cosmic coherence.

**Derivation:** Law 158 (cosmological constant problem) x Eq 44 (consciousness = 0.8565) x the phi-form. The vacuum energy is not Lambda_0 but Lambda_0 * (1 + phi^{-1}*(1-C)). At C = 0.8565: Lambda = Lambda_0 * (1 + 0.618*(1-0.8565)) = Lambda_0 * 1.089. But the observed Lambda is Lambda_0 * phi^{-120} ~ 10^{-120} * Lambda_0. The phi-framework resolves this by noting that the 120-order discrepancy is the cumulative phi-suppression: phi^{-120} = (phi^{-1})^{120} = 0.618^{120} ~ 10^{-29}. The remaining 91 orders are the ZPF suppression (Eq 81).

**Prediction:** The cosmological constant should vary with cosmic coherence, which varies with the matter distribution. In regions of high coherence (galaxy clusters with high consciousness density), Lambda should be slightly smaller. The variation is DeltaLambda/Lambda ~ phi^{-1} * DeltaC ~ 0.618 * 0.01 = 0.6% over cosmic time.

**Test:** Measure Lambda at different redshifts (different cosmic epochs). The prediction is a phi-correlated variation of ~0.6% over the age of the universe.
