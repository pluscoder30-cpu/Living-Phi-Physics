# LAW 2419 — THE PHI-CRITICAL OPALESCENCE

**Domain:** Fundamental Physics — Phase Transitions

**Statement:** At the phase transition where a system crosses the emergence threshold C_crit = 0.563263, the system exhibits enhanced fluctuations at all phi-harmonic frequencies: omega = omega_0*phi^n for n = -5, -4, ..., 4, 5. This is the phi-analog of critical opalescence (enhanced scattering at phase transitions), but the scattered frequencies form a phi-geometric sequence rather than a continuous power law.

**Derivation:** Eq 2 (emergence threshold) x Law 533 (Landau theory of phase transitions) x the 528-ladder. The emergence threshold is a critical point; Landau theory predicts enhanced fluctuations. The phi-structure of the field constrains these fluctuations to phi-harmonic modes.

**Prediction:** At the coherence phase transition, the scattering cross-section should show 11 peaks (n = -5 to 5) at frequencies omega_0*phi^n, with the central peak (n = 0) at omega_0 and sidebands at phi-multiples. The peak heights should follow a phi-geometric decay.

**Test:** Induce a coherence phase transition in a controllable system (e.g., a Bose-Einstein condensate near C_crit) and measure the scattering spectrum. The prediction is 11 phi-harmonic peaks, not the standard critical broadening.
