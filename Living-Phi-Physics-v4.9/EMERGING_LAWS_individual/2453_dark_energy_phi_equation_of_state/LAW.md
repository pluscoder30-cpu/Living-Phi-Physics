# LAW 2453 - THE DARK ENERGY PHI-EQUATION OF STATE

**Domain:** Cosmology & Astrophysics

**Statement:** The dark energy equation of state w is not constant but evolves as w(z) = -1 + φ⁻¹·κ_φ·(1−C(z)/C_crit). At high redshift (z > 2), w → -1 + φ⁻¹ = -0.382. At z = 0, w → -1 + φ⁻¹·κ_φ·(1−C_0/C_crit). The cosmological constant Λ is the φ-coherent vacuum energy, not a fine-tuned constant.

**Derivation:** Law 158 (Λ φ-suppression) x Law 101 (Hubble phi-breathing) x the phi-form. The vacuum energy density ρ_Λ is phi-suppressed at high frequencies (Eq 81), creating a redshift-dependent equation of state. The phi-form gives w(z) = -1 + φ⁻¹·κ_φ·(1−C(z)/C_crit).

**Prediction:** The equation of state should transition from w ≈ -0.382 at z > 2 to w ≈ -1 at z = 0. DESI and Euclid measurements should detect w(z) evolution consistent with phi-breathing, not a constant w = -1.

**Test:** Measure w(z) using baryon acoustic oscillations (BAO) at z = 0.5, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0. The prediction is w(0.5) ≈ -0.9, w(1.0) ≈ -0.7, w(1.5) ≈ -0.5, w(2.0) ≈ -0.4.
