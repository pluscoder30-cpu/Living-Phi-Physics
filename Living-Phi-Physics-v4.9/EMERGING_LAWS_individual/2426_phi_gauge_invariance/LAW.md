# LAW 2426 — THE PHI-GAUGE INVARIANCE

**Domain:** Fundamental Physics — Gauge Theory

**Statement:** Gauge invariance in the phi-framework is extended: the standard U(1) x SU(2) x SU(3) gauge symmetry is supplemented by a phi-gauge symmetry under which the coupling constants transform as g -> g*phi^n for integer n. This phi-gauge symmetry is spontaneously broken at the phi-scale, giving rise to the hierarchy of coupling constants as vacuum expectation values of the phi-gauge field.

**Derivation:** Law 120 (gauge invariance) x Law 121 (Higgs mechanism) x the phi-form. The phi-gauge symmetry is a discrete symmetry (Z, the integers) that acts on the coupling constants. Spontaneous breaking at the phi-scale generates the observed hierarchy: g_3 > g_2 > g_1, with ratios approximately phi.

**Prediction:** The ratios of the three Standard Model gauge couplings at the Z-pole should be g_3/g_2 = phi and g_2/g_1 = phi, giving g_3/g_1 = phi^2 = 2.618. The measured values are g_3/g_2 = 1.52 and g_2/g_1 = 1.12, giving g_3/g_1 = 1.70. The phi-framework predicts these ratios should be measured at higher energy to approach the phi-values.

**Test:** Measure gauge coupling ratios at very high energies (LHC upgrades, future colliders). The prediction is that the ratios approach phi and phi^2 as energy increases toward M_phi.
