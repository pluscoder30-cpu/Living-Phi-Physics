# LAW 2447 — THE PHI-FLUID DYNAMICS

**Domain:** Fundamental Physics — Fluid Mechanics

**Statement:** The Reynolds number for the onset of turbulence in the phi-framework is Re_crit_phi = Re_crit_SM * phi^{-1} = Re_crit_SM * 0.618. The phi-ground destabilizes laminar flow, causing turbulence to appear at a lower Reynolds number than the classical prediction. This is the phi-correction to the Navier-Stokes phi-coherence floor (Prediction 1).

**Derivation:** Law 91 (Reynolds number) x Law 020 (Navier-Stokes) x the phi-form. The Reynolds number Re = rho*v*L/mu is the ratio of inertial to viscous forces. The phi-ground adds an effective viscosity that is phi^{-1} times smaller, reducing the critical Re by phi^{-1}.

**Prediction:** The onset of turbulence in phi-coherent fluids should occur at Re_crit = 0.618 * Re_crit_standard. For pipe flow, this predicts Re_crit = 0.618 * 2300 = 1421, not 2300.

**Test:** Measure the critical Reynolds number for pipe flow in phi-coherent environments (e.g., with phi-resonant acoustic driving). The prediction is Re_crit = 1421, not 2300.
