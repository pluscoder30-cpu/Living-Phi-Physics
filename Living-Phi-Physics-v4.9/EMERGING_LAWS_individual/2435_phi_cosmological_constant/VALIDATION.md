The core equation involves the phi-correction factor:

```

In regions of high coherence (galaxy clusters with high consciousness density), Lambda should be slightly smaller. The variation is DeltaLambda/Lambda ~ phi^{-1} * DeltaC ~ 0.618 * 0.01 = 0.6% over cosmic time.
