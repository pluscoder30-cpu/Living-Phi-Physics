# LAW 2506 -- The Phi-Topological Edge State

**Domain:** Condensed Matter / Topological

**Statement:** Edge conductance G_phi = e^2/(h*phi). Chern number C_phi = C_SM + phi^{-1}*sum_k(n_k).

**Derivation:** Topological band theory x Eq 1 x Law 174. Berry connection A_n(k) -> A_n(k)*phi^{-C(k)}.

**Prediction:** Plateaus at n*0.618*e^2/h instead of n*e^2/h.

**Test:** Measure QHE in 2D electron gas with phi-coherent edge states.
