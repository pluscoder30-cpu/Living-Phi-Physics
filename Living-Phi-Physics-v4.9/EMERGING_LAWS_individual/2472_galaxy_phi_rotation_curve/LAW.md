# LAW 2472 - THE GALAXY PHI-ROTATION CURVE

**Domain:** Cosmology & Astrophysics

**Statement:** Galaxy rotation curves follow a phi-modified Keplerian law: v(r) = v_Keplerian(r)·(1 + κ_φ·φ⁻¹·(1−C_galaxy/C_crit)), where C_galaxy is the galaxy's coherence. The flat rotation curve plateau is v_flat = v_Keplerian(r_edge)·φ = 1.618·v_Keplerian(r_edge). Dark matter is the phi-coherent component.

**Derivation:** Law 2466 (dark matter phi-coherence) x Law 063 (phi-gravity) x the phi-form. The galaxy rotation curve is modified by the phi-coherent dark matter. The phi-form gives v_flat = v_Keplerian·φ.

**Prediction:** Galaxy rotation curves should plateau at v_flat = φ·v_Keplerian(r_edge). The ratio v_observed/v_Keplerian should be φ = 1.618 at large radii.

**Test:** Measure galaxy rotation curves at large radii. The prediction is a plateau at φ times the Keplerian value.
