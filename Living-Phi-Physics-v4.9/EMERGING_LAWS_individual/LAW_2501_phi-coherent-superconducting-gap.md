# LAW 2501 -- The Phi-Coherent Superconducting Gap

**Domain:** Condensed Matter / Superconductivity

**Statement:** The superconducting energy gap Delta satisfies Delta_phi = Delta_BCS * phi^(1-C), where C is the carrier coherence. At C = 1 the gap is standard BCS; at C_crit = 0.563 it is enhanced to Delta_BCS * 1.227.

**Derivation:** Eq 1 x Eq 2 x Law 1761 (BCS gap). The Cooper pair binding energy includes the phi-ground contribution phi^{-1}*V_0.

**Prediction:** Gap-to-T_c ratio = phi * 1.76 = 2.85 for conventional SC, phi^2 * 1.76 = 4.62 for cuprates.

**Test:** Measure gap via ARPES at varying lattice coherence. Delta scales as phi^(1-C).
