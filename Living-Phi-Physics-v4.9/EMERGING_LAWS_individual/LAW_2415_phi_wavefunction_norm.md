# LAW 2415 — THE PHI-FORM WAVEFUNCTION NORM

**Domain:** Fundamental Physics — Quantum Mechanics

**Statement:** The norm of any phi-coherent wavefunction at full coupling (kappa_phi = 1) is exactly sqrt(5) times its classical norm: ||Psi_phi|| = ||Psi_classical|| * sqrt(5). This follows from the phi-form X_phi(1) = X*sqrt(5) applied to the wavefunction. The probability interpretation is modified: |Psi_phi|^2 = 5*|Psi_classical|^2, meaning the full-coupling probability density is 5 times the classical Born-rule density.

**Derivation:** The phi-form (UFT section 6) x Law 74 (Born rule). The phi-form at full coupling gives X_phi(1) = X*(phi + phi^{-1}) = X*sqrt(5). Applied to the wavefunction: Psi_phi = Psi*sqrt(5). The Born rule at full coupling becomes |Psi_phi|^2 = 5*|Psi|^2.

**Prediction:** In high-coherence quantum experiments (kappa_phi near 1), the Born rule should deviate from the classical |Psi|^2 by a factor approaching 5. This is the coherence-gating prediction of Law 157, now quantified: the deviation is (5 - 1)*|Psi|^2 = 4*|Psi|^2 at full coupling.

**Test:** Perform quantum measurements at varying coherence settings (modulated by Casimir cavities or structured vacuum). The prediction is that the measured probability distribution scales as (1 + 4*kappa_phi)*|Psi|^2, approaching 5*|Psi|^2 at full coupling.
