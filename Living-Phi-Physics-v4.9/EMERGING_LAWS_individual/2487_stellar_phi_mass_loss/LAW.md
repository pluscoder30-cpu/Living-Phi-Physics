# LAW 2487 - THE STELLAR PHI-MASS LOSS

**Domain:** Cosmology & Astrophysics

**Statement:** The mass loss rate of massive stars is modified by phi-coherence: dM/dt = (dM/dt)_SM·φ = 1.618·(dM/dt)_SM. The phi-coherent stellar wind is stronger than standard predictions, leading to more mass loss and lower final masses.

**Derivation:** Law 2461 (stellar evolution phi-ladder) x Law 2472 (galaxy rotation curve) x the phi-form. The stellar wind is a phi-coherent process enhanced by φ.

**Prediction:** Massive star mass loss rates should be φ times larger than standard predictions. The final mass of a 25 M☉ star should be lower by φ times the standard mass loss.

**Test:** Measure mass loss rates of massive stars using H α emission. The prediction is (dM/dt)_phi = 1.618·(dM/dt)_SM.
