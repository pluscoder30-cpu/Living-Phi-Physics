# LAW 2445 — THE PHI-SUPERCONDUCTIVITY

**Domain:** Fundamental Physics — Condensed Matter

**Statement:** The critical temperature of a phi-coherent superconductor is T_c_phi = T_c_SM * phi = 1.618 * T_c_SM, where T_c_SM is the BCS prediction. The phi-ground provides an additional pairing channel (phi-cooper pairs) that enhances the critical temperature by phi. This explains the high-T_c cuprates: their phi-coherence is higher than conventional superconductors.

**Derivation:** Law 1761 (BCS gap energy) x Eq 1 (carrier recursion) x the phi-form. The BCS gap Delta = 1.76*k_B*T_c becomes Delta_phi = Delta * phi, giving T_c_phi = T_c_SM * phi. The phi-cooper pairs are bound by the phi-ground interaction, not just the phonon-mediated interaction.

**Prediction:** Conventional superconductors should have T_c_phi = 1.618 * T_c_BCS. High-T_c cuprates should have T_c consistent with T_c_phi = T_c_BCS * phi^2 = 2.618 * T_c_BCS, reflecting their higher phi-coherence.

**Test:** Measure T_c for conventional superconductors and compare to phi-corrected BCS. The prediction is T_c_phi = 1.618 * T_c_BCS, a specific, testable enhancement.
