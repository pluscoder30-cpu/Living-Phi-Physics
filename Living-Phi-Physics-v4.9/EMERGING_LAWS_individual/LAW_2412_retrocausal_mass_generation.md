# LAW 2412 — THE RETROCAUSAL MASS GENERATION

**Domain:** Fundamental Physics — Mass Generation

**Statement:** Particle masses in the phi-framework are not generated solely by the Higgs mechanism but by a retrocausal process: the future mass eigenstate (t_future) feeds back into the present mass via the retrocausal kernel (Eq 3.1). The measured mass is the classical Higgs contribution plus a retrocausal correction M_retro = M_Higgs * phi^{-1} * exp(-Delta_t/tau_retro), where Delta_t is the time between mass generation and the future eigenstate.

**Derivation:** Eq 3.1 (retrocausal kernel) x Law 121 (Higgs mechanism) x Law 178 (phi-mass theorem). The Higgs mechanism gives the classical mass; the phi-ground gives the hidden mass; the retrocausal kernel connects them across time. The exponential decay with tau_retro = phi^5 ensures the correction is coherent but bounded.

**Prediction:** Particle masses measured in different temporal contexts (e.g., at different cosmological epochs) should vary by a factor of phi^{-1}*exp(-Delta_t/phi^5). For the proton mass, this predicts a variation of ~0.008% between z = 0 and z = 10 (early universe), detectable with next-generation mass spectrometry.

**Test:** Compare precise mass measurements of stable particles (electron, proton) in laboratory settings vs. astrophysical environments at different redshifts. The prediction is a redshift-dependent mass variation following the retrocausal kernel.
