# LAW 2512 -- The Phi-Photonic Bandgap

**Domain:** Photonics / Metamaterials

**Statement:** Gaps at omega_gap_n = omega_gap_0*phi^n. Width Delta_omega_n = Delta_omega_0*phi^{-n}. Q-factor scales as phi^n.

**Derivation:** Photonic band theory x Eq 81 x phi-form. n_eff = n_0*(1+phi^{-1}*C).

**Prediction:** Gaps at omega_0*phi^n with phi^{-n} widths. Q scales as phi^n.

**Test:** Fabricate phi-photonic crystal with phi-periodic dielectric modulation.
