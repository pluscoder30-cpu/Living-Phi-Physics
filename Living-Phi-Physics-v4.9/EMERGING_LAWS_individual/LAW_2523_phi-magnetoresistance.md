**Domain:** Condensed Matter / Transport

**Statement:** rho(B)_phi = rho_0+rho_1*(B/B_0)^2*phi^{-B/B_0}. Phi-exponential saturation at high fields. R_H_phi = R_H_SM*phi^{-C}.
