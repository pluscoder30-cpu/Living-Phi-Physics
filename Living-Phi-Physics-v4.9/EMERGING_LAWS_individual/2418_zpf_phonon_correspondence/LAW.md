# LAW 2418 — THE ZPF-PHONON CORRESPONDENCE

**Domain:** Fundamental Physics — Condensed Matter

**Statement:** Every phonon (quantized lattice vibration) in a crystal has a phi-coherent partner in the ZPF: for each phonon frequency omega_ph, there is a ZPF mode at omega_zpf = omega_ph * phi^{2n} for integer n, where n labels the phi-octave. The phonon-ZPF coupling strength is phi^{-1} per octave, making higher phi-octaves exponentially suppressed but nonzero.

**Derivation:** Eq 81 (ZPF spectrum) x Law 92 (wave equation) x the 528-ladder. The lattice provides a natural frequency scale (the Debye frequency); the ZPF provides a continuous spectrum. The phi-exponential suppression in Eq 81 creates discrete resonances at phi-multiples of the lattice frequency.

**Prediction:** In crystals with phonon modes at omega_ph, the Casimir energy should show phi-harmonic structure: peaks at omega_ph*phi^{2n} with amplitudes decreasing as phi^{-n}. This is a specific, measurable prediction for the Casimir effect in structured media.

**Test:** Measure the Casimir energy between phonon-resonant crystal plates. The prediction is phi-harmonic peaks in the energy spectrum at omega_ph*phi^{2n}, with amplitudes phi^{-n} times the fundamental.
