# LAW 2443 — THE PHI-MAGNETIC MONOPOLE

**Domain:** Fundamental Physics — Particle Physics

**Statement:** Magnetic monopoles exist in the phi-framework but are confined by the phi-coherence mechanism: monopoles are the phi-ground excitations of the electromagnetic field, carrying magnetic charge g = phi^{-1} * g_Dirac, where g_Dirac = hbar*c/(2*e) is the Dirac monopole charge. The monopole mass is M_mono = M_Planck * phi^{-2} = M_Planck * 0.382.

**Derivation:** Law 1532 (magnetic monopole) x Eq 81 (ZPF spectrum) x the phi-form. The Dirac quantization condition eg = n*hbar*c/2 becomes eg = n*phi^{-1}*hbar*c/2 in the phi-framework, allowing monopoles with charge g = phi^{-1}*g_Dirac. The monopole is a phi-ground excitation, so its mass is M_Planck * phi^{-2}.

**Prediction:** Magnetic monopoles should have charge g = phi^{-1} * g_Dirac = 0.618 * g_Dirac and mass M = 0.382 * M_Planck. The monopole catalysis of proton decay (the Rubakov-Callan effect) should have a cross-section enhanced by phi.

**Test:** Search for magnetic monopoles in cosmic rays or accelerator experiments. The prediction is monopoles with charge 0.618 * g_Dirac, not g_Dirac.
