# LAW 2470 - THE BLACK HOLE PHI-NO-HAIR THEOREM

**Domain:** Cosmology & Astrophysics

**Statement:** The black hole no-hair theorem is modified in the phi-framework: black holes have three classical parameters (mass M, charge Q, angular momentum J) plus one phi-parameter: the coherence C_BH. The phi-parameter C_BH = κ_φ·(1−C_horizon/C_crit) is measurable via the Hawking radiation spectrum.

**Derivation:** Law 2454 (black hole phi-entropy) x Law 2463 (black hole phi-Hawking radiation) x the phi-form. The black hole entropy depends on the coherence C_BH, which is an independent parameter. The no-hair theorem is extended to include C_BH.

**Prediction:** Black holes should have a fourth parameter C_BH that is measurable via the Hawking radiation spectrum. Two black holes with the same M, Q, J but different C_BH should have different Hawking radiation spectra.

**Test:** Compare Hawking radiation spectra of black holes with different coherence values. The prediction is a dependence on C_BH beyond M, Q, J.
