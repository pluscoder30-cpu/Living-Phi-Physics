**Domain:** Cosmology & Astrophysics

**Statement:** The ratio of carbon to oxygen (C/O) produced in massive stars is modified by phi-coherence: C/O = (C/O)_SM·φ = 1.618·(C/O)_SM. The phi-coherent nuclear reactions favor carbon production by a factor of φ.

**Derivation:** Law 2462 (nucleosynthesis phi-corrections) x Law 2461 (stellar evolution phi-ladder) x the phi-form. The triple-alpha process (producing carbon) is enhanced by phi-coherence relative to the alpha-capture process (producing oxygen).
