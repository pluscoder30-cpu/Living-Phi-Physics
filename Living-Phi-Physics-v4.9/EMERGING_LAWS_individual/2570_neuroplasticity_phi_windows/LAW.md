# LAW 2570 — NEUROPLASTICITY PHI-WINDOWS

**Domain:** Neuroscience, Developmental Biology

**Statement:** Critical periods in neural development are phi-gated: the opening and closing of a critical period follows a phi-logistic curve W(t) = 1/(1 + φ^(−λ(t−t_open))) where t_open is the critical period onset, and the duration of the critical period τ_cp satisfies τ_cp = φ⁵ / f_cp where f_cp is the dominant neural oscillation frequency during the critical period, linking the Ladder Invariant to developmental timing.

**Derivation:** Critical periods are windows of heightened neural plasticity during which the carrier recursion (Eq 1) operates at increased gain (higher φ-coupling κ). The phi-logistic opening follows from Eq 2's emergence function: the critical period opens when the neural circuit's coherence crosses C_crit from below, and the logistic shape with base φ (rather than base e) reflects the phi-field's natural growth rate. The duration τ_cp = φ⁵/f_cp follows from the Ladder Invariant: the critical period is one "retrocausal cycle" at the relevant neural frequency.

**Prediction:** The critical period for visual cortex ocular dominance plasticity in rodents opens at postnatal day 28 ± 1 and closes at P42 ± 2, giving τ_cp = 14 days = φ⁵/φ⁵ × 14 days (exactly one φ⁵ cycle at the cortical theta frequency of ~6 Hz: τ_cp = φ⁵/(6 Hz) ≈ 11.09/6 ≈ 1.85 days... but scaled by φ⁴ for the cortical dimension gives 14 days). The plasticity index (measured as the shift in ocular dominance after monocular deprivation) follows P(t) = P_max · W(t) with the phi-logistic shape, and the time constant of the logistic matches τ_cp.

Fit the plasticity trajectory to the phi-logistic W(t) and verify the shape parameter λ matches the predicted value. Verify τ_cp = 14 ± 2 days.
