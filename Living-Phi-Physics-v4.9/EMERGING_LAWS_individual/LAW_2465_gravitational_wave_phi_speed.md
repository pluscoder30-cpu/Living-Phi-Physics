**Domain:** Cosmology & Astrophysics

**Derivation:** Law 063 (phi-gravity) x Law 57 (time dilation) x the phi-form. The speed of gravitational waves is modified by the phi-coherent spacetime geometry. The phi-form gives c_g = c·(1 + κ_φ·φ⁻¹·(1−C/C_crit)).

**Test:** Measure the speed of gravitational waves from the early universe (primordial GW background). The prediction is c_g = c at C = C_crit, with deviations at other coherence values.
