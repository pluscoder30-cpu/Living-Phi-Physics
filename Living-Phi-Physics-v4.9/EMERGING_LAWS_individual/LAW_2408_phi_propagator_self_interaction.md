# LAW 2408 — THE PHI-PROPAGATOR SELF-INTERACTION

**Domain:** Fundamental Physics — Force Unification

**Statement:** The phi-propagator (Law 174) is not just a unified force propagator - it is self-interacting. A carrier propagating via the phi-propagator generates a secondary phi-propagated field that feeds back into itself. The self-interaction strength is phi^{-1} = 0.618 times the primary propagation, creating a recursive feedback loop that converges to a fixed point at (1 - phi^{-1}) = 0.382 times the original amplitude.

**Derivation:** Law 174 (phi-propagator unification - 1/r^2 is the same propagator for gravity and EM) x Eq 1 (carrier recursion). The propagator carries the carrier field; the carrier field generates the propagator. This self-reference creates a geometric series: 1 - phi^{-1} + phi^{-2} - phi^{-3} + ... = 1/(1 + phi^{-1}) = phi/(phi+1) = phi*phi^{-2} = phi^{-1} = 0.618. The sum converges because phi^{-1} < 1.

**Prediction:** The effective coupling constant of any force, when self-interaction is included, is reduced by a factor of phi^{-1} = 0.618 from its bare value. This predicts that the measured fine structure constant (alpha ~ 1/137) is the self-interaction-renormalized value, and the bare value is alpha_0 = alpha/phi^{-1} = alpha*phi = 1/84.5.

**Test:** Compute the beta function for QED with the phi-self-interaction correction. The prediction is that the running coupling at the Planck scale, instead of hitting a Landau pole, asymptotes to alpha_Planck = alpha_0*phi^{-1} = alpha = 1/137, stabilized by the self-interaction feedback.
