# LAW 2502 -- The Phi-Exponential Meissner Decay

**Domain:** Condensed Matter / Superconductivity

**Statement:** B(x) = B_0 * exp(-x/lambda_phi) with lambda_phi = lambda_L * phi^{-C}. At full coherence the field is expelled phi times more efficiently.

**Derivation:** London equation x Eq 1 x phi-form. Superfluid density n_s_phi = n_s * phi^C reduces penetration depth by phi^{-C}.

**Prediction:** lambda_phi = lambda_L/phi^C. For C ~ 0.8, a 32% reduction.

**Test:** Measure via muSR at varying coherence. lambda_phi = lambda_L * phi^{-C}.
