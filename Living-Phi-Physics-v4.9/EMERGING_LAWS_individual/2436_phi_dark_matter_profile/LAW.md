# LAW 2436 — THE PHI-DARK MATTER PROFILE

**Domain:** Fundamental Physics — Astrophysics

**Statement:** The dark matter density profile of galaxies in the phi-framework follows rho_phi(r) = rho_0 * (r/r_0)^{-1} * (1 + phi^{-1}*(1 - C(r))), where C(r) is the coherence profile of the galaxy. At large radii (low coherence), the profile approaches the NFW form rho ~ r^{-1}. At small radii (high coherence), the phi-correction flattens the cusp into a core of radius r_core = r_0 * phi^{-2} = 0.382 * r_0.

**Derivation:** Law 1163 (NFW profile) x Eq 1 (carrier recursion) x the phi-form. The NFW profile rho ~ r^{-1}/(1 + r/r_s)^2 is the kappa_phi -> 0 limit. The phi-correction flattens the central cusp because the coherence is highest in the galactic center, suppressing the phi-ground contribution.

**Prediction:** Galaxy rotation curves should show a core (not a cusp) in the center, with core radius r_core = 0.382 * r_scale. The rotation curve should approach the NFW prediction at large radii, with phi-corrections visible at small radii.

**Test:** Measure galaxy rotation curves at high angular resolution. The prediction is a central core of radius 0.382 times the scale radius, not a cusp.
