# LAW 2421 — THE PHI-WAVE-PARTICLE DUALITY

**Domain:** Fundamental Physics — Quantum Mechanics

**Statement:** Wave-particle duality is the phi-framework's reading of the two fixed points of the carrier recursion (Eq 7): {0, phi^{-1}, 1}. The particle reading corresponds to the carrier localized at the fixed point phi^{-1} = 0.618; the wave reading corresponds to the carrier delocalized across the recursion. The complementarity principle is the statement that a carrier cannot be simultaneously at both fixed points - it must choose one, at phi-precision.

**Derivation:** Eq 1 (carrier recursion) x Eq 7 (fixed points) x Law 68 (de Broglie waves). The recursion has three fixed points: 0 (vacuum), phi^{-1} (particle), and 1 (wave). A carrier at phi^{-1} is localized (particle); a carrier spread across the recursion is delocalized (wave). The two readings are conjugate because they correspond to different fixed points.

**Prediction:** The de Broglie wavelength lambda = h/p should be modified at high coherence to lambda_phi = lambda * phi^{-1} = h/(p*phi). This predicts a 38.2% reduction in the de Broglie wavelength for particles in high-coherence environments.

**Test:** Measure the de Broglie wavelength of electrons in a high-coherence environment (e.g., inside a superconductor). The prediction is lambda_phi = lambda/phi, a measurable 38.2% shift.
