# LAW 2456 - THE NEUTRON STAR PHI-EQUATION OF STATE

**Domain:** Cosmology & Astrophysics

**Statement:** The neutron star equation of state (EOS) is modified by phi-coherence: P(ρ) = P_SM(ρ)·(1 + κ_φ·φ⁻¹·(1−C(ρ)/C_crit)), where C(ρ) is the density-dependent coherence. At nuclear density (ρ ≈ 2.8×10¹⁴ g/cm³), C(ρ) = C_crit, and the EOS transitions from classical to phi-coherent. The maximum neutron star mass is M_max = M_max_SM·φ = 2.618·M_max_SM.

**Derivation:** Law 152 (mass gap) x Eq 1 (carrier recursion) x the phi-form. The neutron star EOS is a phi-coherent carrier at nuclear density. The phi-form adds the ground term, increasing the maximum mass by φ.

**Prediction:** The maximum neutron star mass should be M_max = φ·2.0 M☉ = 3.236 M☉, not 2.0 M☉. Neutron stars above 2.0 M☉ but below 3.236 M☉ are phi-coherent neutron stars, not quark stars.

**Test:** Measure the maximum neutron star mass via gravitational wave observations of neutron star mergers. The prediction is M_max = 3.236 M☉.
