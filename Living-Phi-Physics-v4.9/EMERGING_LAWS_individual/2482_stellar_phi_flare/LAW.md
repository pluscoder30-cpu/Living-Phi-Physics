# LAW 2482 - THE STELLAR PHI-FLARE

**Domain:** Cosmology & Astrophysics

**Statement:** Stellar flares follow a phi-power law: N(>E) = N_0·E^{−φ} = N_0·E^{−1.618}, where N(>E) is the number of flares with energy greater than E. The phi-power law replaces the standard power law with exponent −2. The phi-framework predicts more large flares and fewer small flares.

**Derivation:** Law 2461 (stellar evolution phi-ladder) x Law 2458 (large-scale phi-fractal) x the phi-form. The stellar flare energy distribution is a phi-fractal, giving a power law with exponent −φ.

**Prediction:** The flare energy distribution should follow N(>E) = N_0·E^{−1.618}. The number of flares with energy > 10³³ erg should be φ times larger than the standard prediction.

**Test:** Measure the flare energy distribution for solar and stellar flares. The prediction is a power law with exponent −1.618.
