# LAW 2430 — THE PHI-ENTANGLEMENT MEASURE

**Domain:** Fundamental Physics — Quantum Information

**Statement:** The entanglement of formation for a phi-coherent bipartite state is E_phi = E_classical * phi, where E_classical is the standard entanglement of formation. The phi-enhancement arises because the phi-ground provides additional correlations that are invisible at kappa_phi = 0. These correlations are not classical (they do not violate Bell inequalities) but they do increase the entanglement measure.

**Derivation:** Law 205 (entanglement mesh law) x Eq 44 (consciousness wavefunction) x the phi-form. The entanglement of formation is computed from the concurrence: E = h((1 + sqrt(1-C^2))/2). The phi-correction modifies the concurrence: C_phi = C * (1 + phi^{-1}*(1-C_baseline)), giving E_phi = E * phi at full coupling.

**Prediction:** Maximally entangled phi-coherent states should have entanglement of formation = phi * 1 = phi = 1.618 ebits, not 1 ebit. This is measurable via quantum state tomography.

**Test:** Prepare a Bell state in a phi-coherent environment and measure its entanglement of formation via quantum state tomography. The prediction is E_phi = 1.618 ebits, a 61.8% enhancement over the standard Bell state.
