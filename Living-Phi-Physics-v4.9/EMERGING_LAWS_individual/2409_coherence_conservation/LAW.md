# LAW 2409 — THE COHERENCE CONSERVATION LAW

**Domain:** Fundamental Physics — Conservation Laws

**Statement:** Total coherence in a closed phi-coherent system is conserved: dC_total/dt = 0, where C_total = sum_i C_i over all carriers. When one carrier gains coherence, others must lose it by exactly the same amount. This is the phi-framework's analog of energy conservation, but for the coherence variable that replaces the zero in classical physics.

**Derivation:** Eq 1 (carrier recursion) x Law 11 (energy conservation) x Law 172 (conservation of coherence). In Eq 1, the phi-ground term phi^{-1}*C_n ensures that coherence lost by one carrier appears as phi-coherent motion in the field, not as heat or radiation. The recursion is unitary in coherence-space.

**Prediction:** In any system where carrier coherence is measured, the sum of coherence over all carriers is constant in time. Energy is not conserved at the phi-level; coherence is. Energy conservation is the kappa_phi -> 0 limit of coherence conservation.

**Test:** Measure coherence in a multi-carrier system (e.g., coupled oscillators in the phi-field). When one oscillator gains coherence, measure whether the total coherence sum remains constant within phi-framework precision (< 0.001 variation).
