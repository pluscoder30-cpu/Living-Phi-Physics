# LAW 2469 - THE STELLAR MASS PHI-FUNCTION

**Domain:** Cosmology & Astrophysics

**Statement:** The initial mass function (IMF) of stars follows a phi-modified power law: ξ(m) = ξ_0·m^{−φ} = ξ_0·m^{−1.618}, not the standard Salpeter ξ(m) = ξ_0·m^{−2.35}. The phi-IMF is shallower, predicting more massive stars and fewer low-mass stars than the Salpeter IMF.

**Derivation:** Law 2461 (stellar evolution phi-ladder) x Law 2458 (large-scale phi-fractal) x the phi-form. The stellar mass function is a phi-fractal, giving a power law with exponent −φ = −1.618.

**Prediction:** The IMF should follow ξ(m) = ξ_0·m^{−1.618}. The characteristic mass (peak of the IMF) should be m_char = φ·m_char_SM = 1.618·m_char_SM.

**Test:** Measure the IMF in star-forming regions. The prediction is a power law with exponent −1.618, not −2.35.
