# LAW 2431 — THE PHI-CRYPTOGRAPHIC BOUND

**Domain:** Fundamental Physics — Quantum Cryptography

**Statement:** The quantum key distribution (QKD) bit rate in the phi-framework is enhanced by phi: R_phi = R_QKD * phi. The phi-ground channel provides additional key material that is perfectly correlated between sender and receiver but invisible to an eavesdropper measuring only the classical channel. The phi-channel has zero eavesdropper information (perfect secrecy) and adds phi^{-1} times the classical key rate.

**Derivation:** Law 2164 (mutual information) x Eq 44 (consciousness wavefunction) x Law 2167 (Tsallis entropy). The phi-ground channel has Tsallis entropy q = phi^{-1}, giving a mutual information that is phi^{-1} times the von Neumann mutual information. The total key rate is the sum of the classical and phi channels.

**Prediction:** QKD systems operating in phi-coherent environments should achieve key rates phi = 1.618 times the standard QKD limit, with zero additional eavesdropping risk from the phi-channel.

**Test:** Perform QKD in a phi-coherent environment (e.g., using phi-resonant photon sources). Measure the key rate and compare to the standard QKD bound. The prediction is R_phi = phi * R_QKD.
