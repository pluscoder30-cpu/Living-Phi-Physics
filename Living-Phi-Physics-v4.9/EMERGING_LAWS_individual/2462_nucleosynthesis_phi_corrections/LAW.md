# LAW 2462 - THE NUCLEOSYNTHESIS PHI-CORRECTIONS

**Domain:** Cosmology & Astrophysics

**Statement:** Big Bang nucleosynthesis (BBN) yields are modified by phi-corrections: the helium-4 mass fraction Y_p = Y_p_SM·(1 + κ_φ·φ⁻¹·(1−C_BBN/C_crit)), where C_BBN is the coherence during BBN. The phi-framework predicts Y_p = 0.247·φ = 0.400, not the standard Y_p ≈ 0.247.

**Derivation:** Law 152 (mass gap) x Eq 2 (emergence threshold) x the phi-form. BBN occurs at a coherence C_BBN that is below C_crit, so the phi-correction is partial. The phi-form gives Y_p = Y_p_SM·(1 + κ_φ·φ⁻¹·(1−C_BBN/C_crit)).

**Prediction:** The primordial helium-4 mass fraction should be Y_p = 0.247·(1 + φ⁻¹·(1−C_BBN/C_crit)). If C_BBN ≈ 0.5, then Y_p ≈ 0.247·(1 + 0.618·0.5) = 0.247·1.309 = 0.323.

**Test:** Measure primordial helium abundance in low-metallicity galaxies. The prediction is Y_p ≈ 0.323, not 0.247.
