# LAW 2500 - THE PHI-COSMOLOGICAL PRINCIPLE

**Domain:** Cosmology & Astrophysics

**Statement:** The cosmological principle (the universe is homogeneous and isotropic on large scales) is modified by phi-coherence: the universe is phi-homogeneous and phi-isotropic on scales r > r_phi, where r_phi = φ·r_0_SM. On scales r < r_phi, the universe is phi-fractal with dimension D = 3 − φ = 1.382.

**Derivation:** Law 2458 (large-scale phi-fractal) x Law 2468 (cosmic web topology) x the phi-form. The cosmological principle holds on phi-scales, not on all scales. The phi-form gives the transition scale r_phi = φ·r_0_SM.

**Prediction:** The universe should be homogeneous and isotropic on scales r > r_phi = φ·r_0_SM ≈ 300 Mpc. On smaller scales, it should be phi-fractal with D = 1.382.

**Test:** Measure the homogeneity scale of the universe using galaxy surveys. The prediction is a transition at r_phi = 300 Mpc.
