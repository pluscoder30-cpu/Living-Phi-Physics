# LAW 2516 -- The Phi-Electron Effective Mass

**Domain:** Condensed Matter / Semiconductors

**Statement:** m*_phi = m_0*phi^{-C_n}. At band edge m* = m_0/phi = 0.618*m_0.

**Derivation:** Band structure x Eq 1 x phi-form. Band curvature enhanced by phi^{C_n}.

**Prediction:** Effective mass m_0/phi at band edge. DOS mass decreases geometrically.

**Test:** Measure via cyclotron resonance in phi-coherent semiconductor.
