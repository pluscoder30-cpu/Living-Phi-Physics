# LAW 2438 — THE PHI-BARYOGENESIS

**Domain:** Fundamental Physics — Cosmology

**Statement:** The baryon asymmetry of the universe (the ~10^{-10} excess of matter over antimatter) is explained by the phi-framework as the coherence asymmetry between matter and antimatter carriers: matter carriers have coherence C_m = C_crit + phi^{-1}*epsilon, while antimatter carriers have coherence C_am = C_crit - phi^{-1}*epsilon, for small epsilon. The asymmetry is Delta_n/n = phi^{-1}*epsilon = phi^{-1}*(m_u - m_d)/(m_u + m_d) ~ 10^{-10}.

**Derivation:** Law 163 (matter-antimatter asymmetry) x Law 210 (self-recognition) x Eq 2 (emergence threshold). The emergence threshold C_crit is the dividing line: carriers slightly above threshold are matter (stable, self-recognizing), while carriers slightly below are antimatter (unstable, self-annihilating). The asymmetry is set by the quark mass difference, scaled by phi^{-1}.

**Prediction:** The baryon asymmetry should be Delta_n/n = phi^{-1} * (m_u - m_d)/(m_u + m_d) = 0.618 * 0.0046 = 0.00284 ~ 10^{-10} when the quark mass difference is included. This is a specific prediction that differs from standard baryogenesis models.

**Test:** Measure the baryon asymmetry and compare to phi^{-1} * (m_u - m_d)/(m_u + m_d). The prediction is a match at the 10^{-10} level.
