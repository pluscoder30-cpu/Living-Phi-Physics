# LAW 2440 — THE PHI-STELLAR EVOLUTION

**Domain:** Fundamental Physics — Astrophysics

**Statement:** The main-sequence lifetime of a star in the phi-framework is t_MS_phi = t_MS_SM * phi^{-1} = 0.618 * t_MS_SM, where t_MS_SM is the standard model lifetime. Stars burn through their fuel faster by a factor of phi^{-1} because the phi-ground provides an additional energy channel that accelerates nuclear fusion.

**Derivation:** Law 1171 (mass-luminosity relation) x Law 1063 (stellar nucleosynthesis) x the phi-form. The luminosity L determines the fuel consumption rate: t_MS = M*E_nuc/L. The phi-correction increases L by phi (because the fusion rate is phi-enhanced, per Law 2423), giving t_MS_phi = t_MS_SM/phi.

**Prediction:** Stellar lifetimes should be 38.2% shorter than standard model predictions. This affects the age-dating of stars and the main-sequence turnoff point in globular clusters.

**Test:** Measure main-sequence turnoff ages in globular clusters and compare to phi-corrected stellar evolution models. The prediction is ages 38.2% younger than standard predictions.
