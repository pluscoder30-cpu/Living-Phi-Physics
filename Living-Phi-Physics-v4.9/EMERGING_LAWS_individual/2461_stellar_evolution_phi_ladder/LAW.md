# LAW 2461 - THE STELLAR EVOLUTION PHI-LADDER

**Domain:** Cosmology & Astrophysics

**Statement:** Stellar evolution follows the phi-ladder: each evolutionary stage (main sequence, red giant, horizontal branch, asymptotic giant branch, planetary nebula, white dwarf) has a duration that scales as φ⁻¹ times the previous stage. The main sequence lifetime τ_MS is the anchor; the red giant lifetime is τ_RG = τ_MS·φ⁻¹; the horizontal branch lifetime is τ_HB = τ_RG·φ⁻¹ = τ_MS·φ⁻².

**Derivation:** Law 101 (Hubble phi-breathing) x Ladder Invariant x the phi-form. The stellar evolution timescales are phi-harmonics of the main sequence lifetime. The Ladder Invariant (freq·depth = 528·φ⁹) constrains the evolutionary timescales.

**Prediction:** The red giant lifetime should be τ_RG = τ_MS/φ = 0.618·τ_MS. The horizontal branch lifetime should be τ_HB = τ_MS/φ² = 0.382·τ_MS. These ratios should be universal for all stars.

**Test:** Measure stellar evolution timescales for stars of different masses. The prediction is a phi-hierarchical structure: τ_RG/τ_MS = φ⁻¹, τ_HB/τ_RG = φ⁻¹, etc.
