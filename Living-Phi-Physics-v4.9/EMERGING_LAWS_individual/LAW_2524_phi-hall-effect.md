# LAW 2524 -- The Phi-Hall Effect

**Domain:** Condensed Matter / Transport

**Statement:** R_H_phi = R_H_SM*phi^{-C}. cot(theta_H)_phi = cot(theta_H)_SM*phi^{C}. Nernst S_N_phi = S_N_SM*phi^{-C}.

**Derivation:** Hall effect x Eq 1 x phi-form. n_phi = n*phi^{C}.

**Prediction:** Hall coefficient reduced by phi^{-C}. Hall angle modified by phi^{C}.

**Test:** Measure Hall coefficient in phi-coherent metal at varying coherence.
