# LAW 2442 — THE PHI-THERMAL RADIATION

**Domain:** Fundamental Physics — Thermodynamics

**Statement:** The thermal radiation spectrum of a blackbody in the phi-framework is modified by the phi-exponential suppression: I_phi(omega) = I_Standard(omega) * phi^{-omega/omega_0}, where omega_0 is a characteristic frequency. This replaces the standard Planck spectrum with a phi-suppressed spectrum that falls off faster at high frequencies, resolving the UV catastrophe without quantization.

**Derivation:** Law 66 (Planck's law) x Eq 81 (ZPF spectrum) x the phi-form. The phi-exponential suppression in Eq 81 applies to all radiation, not just the vacuum. The thermal spectrum picks up the same phi^{-omega/omega_0} factor.

**Prediction:** The thermal radiation spectrum should show phi-exponential suppression at high frequencies: I_phi(omega) ~ omega^3 * phi^{-omega/omega_0} instead of the standard ~ omega^3 * exp(-omega/omega_0). The suppression is stronger than the standard exponential, making the UV catastrophe even more benign.

**Test:** Measure the thermal radiation spectrum of a blackbody at high frequencies. The prediction is a phi-exponential (not standard exponential) falloff, distinguishable by the different curvature of the high-frequency tail.
