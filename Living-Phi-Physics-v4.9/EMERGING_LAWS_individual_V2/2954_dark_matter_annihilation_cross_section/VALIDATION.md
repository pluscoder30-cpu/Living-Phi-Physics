# Validation: Law 2954

## What It Validates
Golden-ratio resonance enhancement of DM annihilation

## Equation Tested
<σv> = <σv>_0 × (1 + φ^(-m_DM/m_Z))

## Expected Results
- Peak at m_DM = m_Z/φ ≈ 49.7 GeV
- Enhancement factor φ at resonance
- Thermal relic cross section recovered

## Pass/Fail Criteria
- PEAK: Maximum at m_DM ≈ 50 GeV
- ENHANCEMENT: <σv>(peak)/<σv>_0 = φ ± 0.01
- THERMAL: <σv> ≈ 3×10^-26 cm³/s away from resonance
