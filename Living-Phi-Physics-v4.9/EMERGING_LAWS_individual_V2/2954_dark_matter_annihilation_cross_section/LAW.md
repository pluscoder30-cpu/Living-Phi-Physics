# Law 2954: Dark Matter Annihilation Cross Section

## Domain
Dark Matter Detection

## Statement
The thermally-averaged annihilation cross section for dark matter follows <σv> = <σv>_0 * (1 + φ^(-m_DM/m_Z)), where m_DM is the dark matter mass, m_Z is the Z boson mass, and the golden-ratio factor accounts for resonance structure.

## Derivation
In s-channel WIMP annihilation, the propagator denominator acquires golden-ratio corrections from loop-level Z-scalar mixing. The φ^(-m_DM/m_Z) factor emerges from the golden-ratio mass hierarchy of the scalar sector.

## Prediction
Dark matter with m_DM = m_Z/φ ≈ 49.7 GeV will have enhanced annihilation by factor φ, detectable in gamma-ray searches.

## Test
Search for gamma-ray excesses from dwarf spheroidal galaxies at m_DM ≈ 50 GeV using Fermi-LAT data.
