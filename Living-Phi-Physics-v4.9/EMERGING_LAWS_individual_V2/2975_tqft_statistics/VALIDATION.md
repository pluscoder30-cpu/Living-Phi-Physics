# Validation: Law 2975

## What It Validates
Golden-ratio genus-dependent topological entanglement entropy

## Equation Tested
γ = ln(D)(1 - φ^(-g))

## Expected Results
- γ approaches ln(D) for large g
- genus-0 gives γ = 0
- Consistent with Verlinde formula

## Pass/Fail Criteria
- GENUS: γ increases with g
- LIMIT: γ → ln(D) for g → ∞
- FIBONACCI: D = φ for Fibonacci anyons
