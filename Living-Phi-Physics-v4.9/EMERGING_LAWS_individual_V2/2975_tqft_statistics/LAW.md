# Law 2975: Topological Quantum Field Theory

## Domain
Topological Quantum Computing

## Statement
The topological entanglement entropy of a TQFT on a surface of genus g follows γ = ln(D) * (1 - φ^(-g)), where D is the total quantum dimension and the golden-ratio factor accounts for genus-dependent corrections.

## Derivation
From the Verlinde formula, the topological entropy γ = ln(D) acquires genus-dependent corrections from the modular S-matrix. The φ^(-g) factor emerges from the optimal decomposition of the partition function.

## Prediction
TQFTs with Fibonacci anyons (D = φ) will have γ = ln(φ) * (1 - φ^(-g)), measurable by entanglement entropy scaling.

## Test
Calculate topological entanglement entropy for Fibonacci TQFT on surfaces of different genus.
