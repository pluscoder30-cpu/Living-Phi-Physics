# Validation: Law 2968

## What It Validates
Golden-ratio modulated dark matter direct detection rate

## Equation Tested
dR/dE_R = R_0 × φ^(-E_R/E_0) × F²(E_R)

## Expected Results
- Rate decays as φ^(-E_R/E_0)
- Clustered events at E_0/φ^n
- Distinguishable from background

## Pass/Fail Criteria
- DECAY: Rate decreases with E_R
- CLUSTERING: Events at φ-structured energies
- SIGNAL: Excess above background at low E_R
