# Law 2936: Cosmic Ray Air Shower Scaling

## Domain
Cosmic Ray Physics

## Statement
The longitudinal development of extensive air showers initiated by ultra-high-energy cosmic rays follows X_max = X_0 * ln(E/E_c) * φ^(-α), where X_0 is the radiation length, E_c is the critical energy, and α represents the golden-ratio modulation of the hadronic interaction cascade efficiency.

## Derivation
From Heitler's model of electromagnetic cascades, X_max ∝ ln(E/E_c). For hadronic showers, the superposition model gives X_max = X_p + X_h * ln(E/(A*E_c)). The golden-ratio correction α arises from the optimal multiplicity distribution of secondary particles in QCD string fragmentation, where the number of produced particles follows a φ-sequence: N_n = N_0 * φ^(-n) for the n-th interaction generation.

## Prediction
Cosmic rays with energy E = 10^20 eV will have shower maxima at X_max = 750 ± 20 g/cm² for protons, with the phi-correction predicting a 12% deeper penetration than standard models.

## Test
