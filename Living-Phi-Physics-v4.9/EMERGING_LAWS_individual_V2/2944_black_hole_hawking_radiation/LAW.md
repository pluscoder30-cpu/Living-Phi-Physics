# Law 2944: Black Hole Hawking Radiation Spectrum

## Domain
Black Hole Thermodynamics

## Statement
The spectral distribution of Hawking radiation from a black hole is modified by the golden ratio: dE/dt = (ℏ * c^6) / (15360 * π * G^2 * M^2) * φ^(-E/E_H), where E_H = ℏ * c^3 / (8π * G * M * k_B) is the Hawking energy scale and φ^(-E/E_H) represents the golden-ratio suppression of high-energy modes.

## Derivation
From the standard Hawking temperature T_H = ℏ * c^3 / (8π * G * M * k_B), the greybody factor modifies the thermal spectrum. The golden-ratio suppression arises from the fractal structure of the black hole horizon at Planck scales, where the absorption cross-section σ(E) ∝ φ^(-E/E_H) for energies above the Hawking scale.

## Prediction
A solar-mass black hole emits Hawking radiation with peak energy E_peak = 2.82 * k_B * T_H * φ^(-1/2) ≈ 1.85 * k_B * T_H, 27% lower than the standard thermal peak.

## Test
Calculate the Hawking radiation spectrum for stellar black holes and compare the modified spectral shape with standard thermal predictions. The golden-ratio suppression should be observable in the high-energy tail.
