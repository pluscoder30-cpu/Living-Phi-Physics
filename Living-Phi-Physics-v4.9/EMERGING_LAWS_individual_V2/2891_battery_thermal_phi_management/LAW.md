# PHI Battery Thermal Management

**Domain**: Automotive Engineering

**Statement**:
Battery pack thermal uniformity maximized when cooling channel spacing follows phi-geometric progression sn = s0*phi^n, reducing temperature gradient by factor phi.

**Derivation**:
Heat equation: Var(T)_phi = Var(T)_uniform/phi. Temperature gradient: dT_phi = dT_uniform/phi.

**Prediction**:
Phi-spaced cooling channels reduce max cell-to-cell temp difference by 38.2% vs uniform spacing.

**Test**:
Build phi-spaced vs uniform thermal systems. Operate at 2C discharge. Measure temp distribution. Expect phi-system dT_max ≈ 0.618× uniform.
