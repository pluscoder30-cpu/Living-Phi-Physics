# PHI Ecosystem Diversity Index

**Domain**: Environmental Science

**Statement**:
Ecosystem diversity follows phi-modified Shannon H' = H0*phi when abundance follows phi-geometric pn = p0*phi^(-n).

**Derivation**:
Shannon: H' = -sum(pi*ln(pi)). Phi-geometric gives H' = phi*H0.

**Prediction**:
Phi-geometric ecosystems have 61.8% higher Shannon index vs uniform.

**Test**:
Calculate Shannon for 10 ecosystems. Expect phi-geometric mean H' ≈ 1.618× uniform.
