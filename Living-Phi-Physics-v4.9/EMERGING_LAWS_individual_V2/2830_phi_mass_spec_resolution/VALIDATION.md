# Validation: Law 2830 — PHI-Harmonic Mass Spectrometry Resolution

**What it validates:** PHI multi-stage MS multiplies resolution by φ per stage with better transmission.

**Equation tested:** R(n) = R₀·φⁿ, T(n) = 1/φⁿ

**Expected results:**
- Resolution multiplied by φ per stage
- Transmission 1/φ per stage (better than standard 1/2)
- 3-stage: R ≈ 4.24R₀ with T ≈ 0.237

**Test methodology:** Compare PHI and standard multi-stage models for 1-5 stages. Compare resolution and transmission.

**Pass criteria:** PHI transmission > standard transmission at 3 stages
