# PHI Metapopulation Persistence

**Domain**: Environmental Science

**Statement**:
Metapopulation P = 1-(e/m)^(1/phi) with phi-connectivity allows persistence at e/m ratios phi times higher.

**Derivation**:
Levins with m_eff = m*phi: P* = 1-e/(m*phi). Threshold: e/m < phi.

**Prediction**:
Phi-connected populations persist at e/m = 1.2-1.5 while standard go extinct.

**Test**:
Simulate e/m = 0.8, 1.0, 1.2, 1.5. Expect phi-connected persist at 1.2-1.5.
