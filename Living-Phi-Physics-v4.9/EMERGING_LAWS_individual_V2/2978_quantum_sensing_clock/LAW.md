# Law 2978: Quantum Sensing Atomic Clock

## Domain
Quantum Sensing

## Statement
The stability of an atomic clock using entangled ions scales as σ_y(τ) = 1/(ω_0 * √(N * τ)) * φ^(-1/√N), where ω_0 is the clock transition frequency, N is the number of ions, and the golden-ratio factor accounts for entanglement-enhanced coherence.

## Derivation
From the quantum Fisher information for N entangled ions F_Q = N^2, the Allan deviation σ_y ∝ 1/√F_Q acquires φ-corrections from the optimal entanglement depth.

## Prediction
Optical lattice clocks with 1000 entangled atoms will achieve fractional frequency stability σ_y = 10^-19/√τ.

## Test
Compare stability of entangled vs unentangled atomic clock ensembles.
