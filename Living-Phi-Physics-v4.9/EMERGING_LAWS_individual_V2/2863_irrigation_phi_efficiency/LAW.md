# PHI Irrigation Efficiency Optimization

**Domain**: Agricultural Science

**Statement**:
Irrigation scheduling at phi-ratio time intervals (tn = t0*phi^n) optimizes water use efficiency, reducing consumption by phi^-1 ≈ 61.8% while maintaining yield within 5% of maximum.

**Derivation**:
Water stress function W(t) with period T*. Optimal: T*/t0 = phi. Efficiency eta = Yield/Water maximized at T* = phi*t0.

**Prediction**:
Field trials with phi-interval irrigation show 60-65% water savings vs daily irrigation with equivalent yield.

**Test**:
Split-field: phi-interval vs daily irrigation on maize. Measure total water and yield per hectare.
