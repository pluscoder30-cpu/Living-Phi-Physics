# PHI Masonry Bond Strength

**Domain**: Construction Engineering

**Statement**:
Masonry shear strength follows tau = tau0*(1+phi*(tb/tm)^2) with optimal brick-to-mortar ratio tb/tm = phi^-1.

**Derivation**:
Shear transfer: tau = tau0 + mu*sigma_n + alpha*(tb/tm)^beta. Alpha = phi, beta = 2. Optimal (tb/tm)* = 1/phi.

**Prediction**:
Masonry with tb/tm = 1:phi has 61.8% higher shear strength than equal thickness ratio.

**Test**:
Construct panels with tb/tm = 0.5, 0.618, 0.75, 1.0. Shear test. Expect maximum at ≈ 0.618.
