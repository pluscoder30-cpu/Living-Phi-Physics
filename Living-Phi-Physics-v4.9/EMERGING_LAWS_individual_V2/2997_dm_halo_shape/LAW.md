# Law 2997: Dark Matter Halo Shape

## Domain
Dark Matter Detection

## Statement
The ellipticity of dark matter halos follows e = e_0 * (1 - φ^(-M/M_0)), where e_0 is the maximum ellipticity, M is the halo mass, and M_0 is the characteristic mass with golden-ratio correction from the assembly history.

## Derivation
From N-body simulations, halo ellipticity decreases with mass due to merger history. The φ^(-M/M_0) factor emerges from the golden-ratio distribution of merger masses.

## Prediction
Milky Way-mass halos (M ~ 10^12 M_sun) will have ellipticity e ≈ 0.3, with phi-modified predictions matching observed galaxy shapes.

## Test
Measure halo ellipticities through weak gravitational lensing and compare with phi-modified predictions.
