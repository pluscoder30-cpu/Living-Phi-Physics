# Validation: Law 2997

## What It Validates
Golden-ratio modified dark matter halo ellipticity

## Equation Tested
e = e₀(1 - φ^(-M/M₀))

## Expected Results
- Ellipticity increases with mass
- Approaches e_0 for large M
- Consistent with lensing observations

## Pass/Fail Criteria
- SCALE: 0 < e < 0.5
- MASS: Ellipticity increases with M
- OBSERVATION: Matches weak lensing data
