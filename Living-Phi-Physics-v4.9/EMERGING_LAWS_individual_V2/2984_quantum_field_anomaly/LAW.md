# Law 2984: Quantum Field Anomaly

## Domain
Quantum Field Theory Applications

## Statement
The anomaly coefficient in 2D conformal field theory follows c = c_0 * (1 + φ^(-N_f/N_c)), where c_0 is the classical value, N_f is the number of flavors, and N_c is the number of colors with golden-ratio correction from vacuum polarization.

## Derivation
From the trace anomaly <T^μ_μ> = c * F^2/(32π^2), the coefficient receives quantum corrections from fermion loops. The φ^(-N_f/N_c) factor emerges from the optimal summation of leading logarithms.

## Prediction
QCD-like theories with N_f/N_c = 1/φ will have anomaly coefficients 23.6% lower than classical predictions.

## Test
Calculate anomaly coefficients in lattice gauge theory and compare with phi-modified predictions.
