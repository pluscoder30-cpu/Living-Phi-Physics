# PHI Carbon Sequestration Rate

**Domain**: Environmental Science

**Statement**:
Soil carbon follows phi-RothC: C(t) = Ceq*(1-exp(-t/(tau*phi))) achieving 61.8% faster equilibration.

**Derivation**:
RothC with tau_eff = tau/phi. Equilibration time: tau/phi vs tau.

**Prediction**:
Phi-managed soils reach carbon equilibrium 61.8% faster than conventional.

**Test**:
Measure carbon 5 years phi-managed vs conventional. Expect phi tau ≈ 0.618× conventional.
