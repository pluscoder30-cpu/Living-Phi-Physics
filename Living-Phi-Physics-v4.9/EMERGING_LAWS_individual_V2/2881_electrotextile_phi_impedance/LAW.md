# PHI Electrotextile Impedance Matching

**Domain**: Textile Engineering

**Statement**:
Conductive textile transmission lines achieve minimum reflection when conductor width ratios follow phi-progression with reflection coefficient Gamma = (phi-1)/(phi+1) ≈ 0.226.

**Derivation**:
Stepped impedance: Zn = Z0*phi^(-n/N). Reflection: Gamma = ((phi-1)/(phi+1))^N.

**Prediction**:
3-section phi-stepped textile line achieves |Gamma| < 0.012 for wearable RF applications.

**Test**:
Fabricate 3-section phi-stepped coplanar waveguide on fabric. Measure S11 1-10 GHz. Expect |S11| < -38 dB at design frequency.
