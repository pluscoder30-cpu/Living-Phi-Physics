# Validation: Law 2959

## What It Validates
Golden-ratio fractional quantum Hall quantization

## Equation Tested
σ_xy = e²/(φh) at ν = 1/φ

## Expected Results
- Quantized plateau at ν = 1/φ
- Quasiparticle charge e* = e/φ
- ρ_xx = 0 at plateau

## Pass/Fail Criteria
- QUANTIZATION: σ_xy = e²/(φh) ± 10^-6
- CHARGE: e*/e = 1/φ ± 0.01
- PLATEAU: ρ_xx vanishes at ν = 1/φ
