# Law 2959: Quantum Hall Effect Quantization

## Domain
Quantum Field Theory Applications

## Statement
The Hall conductance in the fractional quantum Hall effect at filling fraction ν = 1/φ is quantized as σ_xy = e^2/h * (1/φ), where the golden-ratio filling produces quasiparticles with fractional charge e* = e/φ.

## Derivation
From Laughlin's wavefunction at ν = 1/m, golden-ratio filling ν = 1/φ emerges from composite fermion theory where flux attachment number p = φ - 1 = 1/φ.

## Prediction
Transport measurements at ν = 1/φ ≈ 0.618 will show quantized Hall plateau with σ_xy = e^2/(φh).

## Test
