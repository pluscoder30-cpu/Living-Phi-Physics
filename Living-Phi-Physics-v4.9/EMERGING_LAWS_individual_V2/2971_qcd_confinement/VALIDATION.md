# Validation: Law 2971

## What It Validates
Golden-ratio corrected QCD string tension

## Equation Tested
σ = σ₀(1 + φ^(-r/r₀))

## Expected Results
- String tension increases with r
- Approaches σ₀ at large r
- Consistent with lattice QCD

## Pass/Fail Criteria
- SCALE: σ ≈ (440 MeV)² at r → ∞
- CORRECTION: φ^(-r/r₀) → 0 for r >> r₀
- POTENTIAL: Linear at large r
