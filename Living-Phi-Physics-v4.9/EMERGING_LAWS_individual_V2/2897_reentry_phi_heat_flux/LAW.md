# PHI Re-entry Heat Flux Distribution

**Domain**: Aerospace Engineering

**Statement**:
Re-entry heat flux follows phi-modified Sutton-Graves q = q0*(rho/rho0)^(1/phi)*(v/v0)^(2+1/phi) from phi-scale boundary layer turbulence.

**Derivation**:
Sutton-Graves: q = k*sqrt(rho/Rn)*v^3. With phi-turbulent BL: density exponent 1/phi, velocity exponent 2+1/phi ≈ 2.618.

**Prediction**:
Phi-modified model predicts re-entry heat flux with 61.8% lower prediction error vs Sutton-Graves for turbulent BL.

**Test**:
Compare predictions against wind tunnel data Mach 20-25. Expect phi-modified RMSE 61.8% lower.
