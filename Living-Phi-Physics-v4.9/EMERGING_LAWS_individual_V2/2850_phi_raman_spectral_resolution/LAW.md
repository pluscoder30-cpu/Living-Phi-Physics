# Law 2850: PHI-Harmonic Raman Spectral Resolution

**Domain:** Spectroscopy — Raman Resolution

**Statement:**
Raman spectral resolution improves by factor φ when using PHI-spaced grating grooves: R = R₀·φ^(N_grooves/N_φ) where N_φ = 2π/φ·d₀ is the PHI groove constant. The PHI grating creates constructive interference at φ-spaced diffraction orders, concentrating more light into the desired order.

**Derivation:**
For a diffraction grating with PHI-modulated groove spacing (d_n = d₀·φ^(n/N)), the grating equation sinθ = mλ/d gives diffraction orders at angles θ_m = arcsin(mλφ^(n/N)/d₀). The resolving power R = mN increases by factor φ because the PHI spacing creates additional constructive interference conditions.

**Prediction:**
A 1200 groove/mm grating with PHI modulation achieves resolution of R = 1200·φ ≈ 1942, compared to R = 1200 for standard. The PHI advantage grows with groove count.

**Test:**
Compute resolving power for 600-6000 grooves using PHI and standard models. Verify φ improvement.
