# PHI Smart Fabric Sensitivity Enhancement

**Domain**: Textile Engineering

**Statement**:
Conductive fiber networks with phi-spaced sensors achieve phi-times greater spatial sensitivity than uniform grids by eliminating aliasing in strain reconstruction.

**Derivation**:
Spatial Nyquist: phi-spaced sensors increase effective fs by phi factor. Reconstruction error ε ∝ 1/(phi*fs). Sensitivity S = phi*fs.

**Prediction**:
Phi-spaced sensor fabrics detect strain gradients 61.8% smaller than uniform grids with same sensor count.

**Test**:
Create phi-spaced vs uniform 50-sensor arrays. Apply identical strain. Compare RMSE. Expect phi-array 61.8% lower.
