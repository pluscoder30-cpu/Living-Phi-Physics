# Validation: Law 2837 — PHI-Harmonic Water Purification Flux

**What it validates:** PHI tortuosity reduces membrane resistance by factor φ.

**Equation tested:** J = ΔP/(μ·R₀/φ^(1/3)), τ = φ^(1/3)

**Expected results:**
- PHI flux > standard at all pressures
- Improvement factor = φ^(1/3) ≈ 1.174
- Reduced tortuosity from PHI pore structure

**Test methodology:** Compute flux for 1-20 bar. Compare PHI and standard Darcy models.

**Pass criteria:** PHI flux > standard flux at all pressures
