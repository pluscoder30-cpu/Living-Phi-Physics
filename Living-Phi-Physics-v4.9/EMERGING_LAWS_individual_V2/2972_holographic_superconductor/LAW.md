# Law 2972: Holographic Superconductor

## Domain
Holographic Principle Applications

## Statement
The critical temperature of a holographic superconductor follows T_c = T_c0 * φ^(-λ/g^2), where λ is the scalar coupling, g is the gauge coupling, and the golden-ratio factor accounts for the backreaction of the condensed scalar on the AdS black hole geometry.

## Derivation
From the AdS/CFT correspondence, the superconducting condensate forms when the scalar field in AdS exceeds the Breitenlohner-Freedman bound. The φ^(-λ/g^2) correction arises from the backreaction on the black hole horizon.

## Prediction
Holographic superconductors with λ/g² = 1 will have T_c ≈ 0.2T_H/φ ≈ 0.124T_H, where T_H is the Hawking temperature.

## Test
Calculate T_c from holographic superconductor model and compare with high-T_c cuprate observations.
