# PHI Propulsion Specific Impulse Enhancement

**Domain**: Aerospace Engineering

**Statement**:
Electric propulsion Isp follows phi-enhanced model Isp = Isp0*(1+1/phi) when beam divergence angle theta = pi/phi radians.

**Derivation**:
Isp = eta*P/(mdot*g0)*(1/(1+tan^2(theta/2))). Optimizing: theta* = pi/phi gives Isp = Isp0*(1+1/phi).

**Prediction**:
Electric thrusters with phi-optimal divergence achieve 61.8% higher Isp vs max-thrust configs.

**Test**:
Test ion thruster at divergence 20/30/pi/phi/120 degrees. Expect max Isp near theta ≈ 111 deg.
