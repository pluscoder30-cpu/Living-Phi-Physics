# Validation: Law 2946 - Multi-Messenger Astronomical Timing

## What It Validates
Golden-ratio modified GW-EM time delays in multi-messenger events

## Equation Tested
Δt = (D/c)(1-v_gw/c) × φ^(n)

## Expected Results
- Time delay increases with distance
- Phi-enhancement explains 61.8% excess delay
- Consistent with GW170817 observation
- Shapiro delay from host galaxy contributes

## Pass/Fail Criteria
- DELAY: Δt > 0 for all distances
- ENHANCEMENT: Δt(phi)/Δt(std) = φ ± 0.01
- OBSERVATION: Matches GW170817 1.7 s delay
- SCALING: Linear with distance confirmed
