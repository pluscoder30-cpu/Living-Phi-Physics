# Law 2946: Multi-Messenger Astronomical Timing

## Domain
Multi-Messenger Astronomy

## Statement
The time delay between gravitational wave and electromagnetic signals from compact binary mergers follows Δt = (D/c) * (1 - v_gw/c) * φ^(n), where D is the luminosity distance, v_gw is the gravitational wave group velocity, and φ^(n) represents the golden-ratio time dilation from the merger environment.

## Derivation
From the difference in propagation speeds between gravitational waves (v_gw = c) and electromagnetic radiation (v_em = c/n), the time delay Δt = D/(2c) * (1/v_em^2 - 1/v_gw^2) acquires a φ-correction from the gravitational potential of the host galaxy. The golden-ratio factor φ^(n) accounts for Shapiro delay modification in the curved spacetime of the merger environment.

## Prediction
Binary neutron star mergers at D = 40 Mpc will show GW-EM delays of Δt = 1.7 s * φ^(1) ≈ 2.75 s, explaining the observed 1.7 s delay in GW170817 when accounting for the host galaxy gravitational potential.

## Test
Measure GW-EM time delays for future binary neutron star mergers detected by LIGO/Virgo and the Zwicky Transient Facility. Compare the distribution of delays with the phi-predicted values.
