# PHI Ocean Current Turbine Efficiency

**Domain**: Marine Engineering

**Statement**:
Underwater current turbine efficiency follows phi-enhanced Betz eta_max = 16/(27*phi) ≈ 0.382 at blade pitch beta = pi/(2*phi) radians.

**Derivation**:
Betz: eta_Betz = 16/27. With wake rotation: eta = eta_Betz*(1-w*R/v). Phi-optimized: w*R/v = 1/phi. Therefore eta = 16/(27*phi) ≈ 0.382.

**Prediction**:
Phi-optimized ocean current turbines achieve 38.2% efficiency limited by wake rotation.

**Test**:
Test phi-pitched vs standard turbines in water channel. Expect phi-turbine Cp ≈ 0.382, standard ≈ 0.35.
