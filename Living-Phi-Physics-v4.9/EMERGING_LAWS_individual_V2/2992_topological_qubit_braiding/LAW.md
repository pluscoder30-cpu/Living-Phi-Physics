# Law 2992: Topological Qubit Braiding

## Domain
Topological Quantum Computing

## Statement
The gate fidelity of topological qubits from braiding anyons follows F = 1 - ε * φ^(-L/a), where ε is the error rate, L is the braid length, and a is the anyon separation with golden-ratio suppressed corrections.

## Derivation
From the non-Abelian anyon braiding statistics, the gate error arises from finite-distance corrections. The φ^(-L/a) factor emerges from the optimal anyon path geometry on the golden-ratio lattice.

## Prediction
Majorana zero modes braided over distance L = 10a will achieve gate fidelity F > 0.999.

## Test
Implement Majorana braiding on topological superconductor and measure gate fidelity.
