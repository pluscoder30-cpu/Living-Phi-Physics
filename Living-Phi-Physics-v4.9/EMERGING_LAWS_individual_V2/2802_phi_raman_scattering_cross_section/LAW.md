# Law 2802: PHI-Harmonic Raman Scattering Cross Section

**Domain:** Spectroscopy — Raman Scattering

**Statement:**
The Raman scattering cross section for PHI-resonant vibrational modes follows σ_Raman ∝ φ^(2ν/ν₀) where ν is the vibrational frequency and ν₀ is the reference frequency. Modes at PHI-harmonic frequencies (ν = nφν₀) have enhanced cross sections by factor φ²ⁿ, producing natural signal amplification for PHI-aligned molecular vibrations.

**Derivation:**
The Raman polarizability α depends on the anharmonicity of the potential. For PHI-harmonic potentials V(r) = k·r²/2·(1 + Σcₙcos(nφr)), the polarizability derivative dα/dQ at the equilibrium position is enhanced by the φ-harmonic coupling term, giving σ ∝ |dα/dQ|² ∝ φ^(2n).

**Prediction:**
For a molecule with vibrational modes at 500, 809, 1309, and 2118 cm⁻¹ (φ-harmonic series starting at 500), the Raman cross sections are in ratio 1 : φ² : φ⁴ : φ⁶ ≈ 1 : 2.618 : 6.854 : 17.944.
