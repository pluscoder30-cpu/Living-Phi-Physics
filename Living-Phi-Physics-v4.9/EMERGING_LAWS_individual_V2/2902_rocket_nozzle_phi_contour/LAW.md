# PHI Rocket Nozzle Contour Optimization

**Domain**: Aerospace Engineering

**Statement**:
Rocket nozzle thrust coefficient maximized at expansion ratio epsilon = phi^2 ≈ 2.618, achieving CF = CF0*(1+1/phi^2) ≈ 1.382*CF0.

**Derivation**:
Thrust coefficient optimization: dCF/depsilon = 0 gives epsilon* = phi^2 for gamma=1.4. CF(phi^2)/CF(1) = 1+1/phi^2 ≈ 1.382.

**Prediction**:
Nozzles with expansion ratio phi^2 achieve 38.2% higher thrust coefficient vs unity ratio.

**Test**:
Test nozzles epsilon=1, phi, phi^2, phi^3 same chamber pressure. Expect max CF at epsilon ≈ 2.618.
