# Validation: Law 2961

## What It Validates
Golden-ratio suppressed spinon dispersion in quantum spin liquids

## Equation Tested
E(k) = J|sin(ka)| × φ^(-|ka|/π)

## Expected Results
- Spinon bandwidth W = 2J/φ
- Dispersion suppressed at BZ boundary
- Observable by INS in herbertsmithite

## Pass/Fail Criteria
- BANDWIDTH: W = 2J/φ ± 0.1J
- SUPPRESSION: E(π/a) ≈ 0
- CONTINUUM: Broad feature, not sharp magnon
