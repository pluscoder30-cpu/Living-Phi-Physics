# Law 2961: Quantum Spin Liquid Frustration

## Domain
Quantum Field Theory Applications

## Statement
The spinon dispersion in a quantum spin liquid on a frustrated lattice follows E(k) = J * |sin(k*a)| * φ^(-|k|a/π), where J is the exchange coupling, a is the lattice constant, and the golden-ratio suppression accounts for the fractal structure of the spinon Fermi surface.

## Derivation
From the spin-liquid ansatz on the kagome lattice, the spinon hopping acquires golden-ratio corrections from the frustrated geometry, leading to a dispersion that decays as φ^(-|k|a/π) at the Brillouin zone boundary.

## Prediction
Inelastic neutron scattering of herbertsmithite will reveal spinon continuum with bandwidth W = 2J/φ ≈ 1.236J.

## Test
Measure spin excitation spectrum of herbertsmithite ZnCu3(OH)6Cl2 by inelastic neutron scattering.
