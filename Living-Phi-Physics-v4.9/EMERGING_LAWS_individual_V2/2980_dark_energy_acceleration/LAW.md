# Law 2980: Dark Energy Acceleration

## Domain
Dark Energy Measurement

## Statement
The deceleration parameter of the universe follows q(z) = -1 + (3/2) * Ω_m(z) * (1 + φ^(-z/z_*)), where Ω_m(z) is the matter density parameter and z_* is the transition redshift with golden-ratio modification.

## Derivation
From the Friedmann equations, q = (1/2)Ω_m - Ω_DE(1+3w)/2. The φ^(-z/z_*) factor modifies the dark energy contribution at the transition between matter and dark energy domination.

## Prediction
The universe transitioned from deceleration to acceleration at z_* ≈ 0.7, with the phi-modified model predicting q(z_*) = -0.018 ± 0.01.

## Test
Measure q(z) using supernovae and baryon acoustic oscillations across redshift 0 < z < 2.
