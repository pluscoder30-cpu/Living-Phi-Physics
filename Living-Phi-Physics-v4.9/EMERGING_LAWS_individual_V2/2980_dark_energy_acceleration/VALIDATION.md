# Validation: Law 2980

## What It Validates
Golden-ratio modified deceleration parameter evolution

## Equation Tested
q(z) = -1 + (3/2)Ω_m(z)(1 + φ^(-z/z_*))

## Expected Results
- q < 0 at z = 0 (acceleration)
- Transition at z ≈ 0.7
- Consistent with supernova data

## Pass/Fail Criteria
- PRESENT: q(0) ≈ -0.55
- TRANSITION: q(z_*) ≈ 0 at z_* ≈ 0.7
- CONSISTENT: Matches ΛCDM observations
