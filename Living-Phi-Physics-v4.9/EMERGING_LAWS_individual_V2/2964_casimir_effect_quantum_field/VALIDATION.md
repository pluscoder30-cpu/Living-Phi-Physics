# Validation: Law 2964

## What It Validates
Golden-ratio enhanced Casimir force for fractal geometries

## Equation Tested
F = F_0(1 + φ^(-d/a))

## Expected Results
- Force enhanced for d ~ a
- Enhancement decays at large separation
- 15% boost at d = a

## Pass/Fail Criteria
- ENHANCEMENT: F/F_0 > 1 for d < 5a
- DECAY: F → F_0 for d >> a
- GEOMETRY: Specific to golden-ratio tiling
