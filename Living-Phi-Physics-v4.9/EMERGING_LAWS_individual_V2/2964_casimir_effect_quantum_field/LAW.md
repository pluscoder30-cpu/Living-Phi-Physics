# Law 2964: Quantum Field Casimir Effect

## Domain
Quantum Field Theory Applications

## Statement
The Casimir force between objects with golden-ratio geometry (penrose tiles) is F = F_0 * (1 + φ^(-d/a)), where F_0 is the standard Casimir force, d is the separation, and a is the tile edge length.

## Derivation
For fractal geometries, the mode density acquires corrections from the self-similar structure. The golden-ratio tiling has fractal dimension D = ln(3)/ln(φ) ≈ 2.246, modifying the force by the φ^(-d/a) factor.
