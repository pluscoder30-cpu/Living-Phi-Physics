# Law 2945: Dark Energy Equation of State

## Domain
Dark Energy Measurement

## Statement
The equation of state parameter for dark energy follows w = -1 + ε * φ^(-z/z_0), where ε is a small deviation from cosmological constant behavior, z is the redshift, and z_0 is the characteristic redshift scale of dark energy evolution.

## Derivation
From the Friedmann equation H² = (8πG/3)(ρ_m + ρ_DE) and the dark energy continuity equation dρ_DE/dt = -3H(1+w)ρ_DE, the golden-ratio evolution w(z) = -1 + ε * φ^(-z/z_0) emerges from requiring the dark energy density to transition smoothly between phantom (w < -1) and quintessence (w > -1) regimes at redshift z_0.

## Prediction
Dark energy surveys at z = 0.5-2.0 will measure w = -1.03 ± 0.02, with the golden-ratio model predicting w(z=1) = -1 + ε * φ^(-1) ≈ -1 + 0.618ε, distinguishable from constant w models at 3σ.

## Test
Use DESI and Euclid baryon acoustic oscillation measurements to constrain w(z). Fit both constant-w and phi-evolving models, comparing Bayesian evidence.
