# Validation: Law 2945 - Dark Energy Equation of State

## What It Validates
Golden-ratio evolving dark energy equation of state parameter

## Equation Tested
w(z) = -1 + ε × φ^(-z/z₀)

## Expected Results
- w ≈ -1.03 at z = 0 for ε = 0.03
- Smooth transition from phantom to quintessence
- Distinguishable from constant w at 3σ
- Consistent with DESI/Euclid observations

## Pass/Fail Criteria
- EQUATION: -1.1 < w(z) < -0.9 for z < 2
- EVOLUTION: w approaches -1 at high redshift
- CONSTRAIN: ε < 0.1 from BAO measurements
- DISTINGUISH: Bayesian evidence favors phi-model over ΛCDM
