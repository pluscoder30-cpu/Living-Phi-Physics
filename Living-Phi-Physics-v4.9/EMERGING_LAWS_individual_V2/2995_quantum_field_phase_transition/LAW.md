# Law 2995: Quantum Field Phase Transition

## Domain
Quantum Field Theory Applications

## Statement
The critical temperature of a quantum phase transition follows T_c = T_c0 * φ^(-g^2/g_0^2), where g is the coupling constant, g_0 is the critical coupling, and the golden-ratio factor accounts for the quantum fluctuations near the critical point.

## Derivation
From the renormalization group flow near a quantum critical point, the correlation length diverges as ξ ∝ |g-g_c|^(-ν). The φ^(-g^2/g_0^2) factor emerges from the golden-ratio scaling of the RG eigenvalues.

## Prediction
Quantum phase transitions in cold atom systems will show critical temperatures suppressed by factor φ^(-g^2/g_0^2).

## Test
Measure T_c in ultracold atom experiments at varying interaction strengths.
