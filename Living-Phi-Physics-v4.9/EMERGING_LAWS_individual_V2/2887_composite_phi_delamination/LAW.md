# PHI Composite Delamination Resistance

**Domain**: Construction Engineering

**Statement**:
Laminated composite delamination toughness follows phi-hierarchical stacking: GIC = GIC0*phi^L where L is hierarchical levels.

**Derivation**:
Delamination energy: crack path length increases by phi per level. Energy: GIC = GIC0*phi^L. For L=3: GIC ≈ 4.236*GIC0.

**Prediction**:
3-level phi-hierarchical composites have 4.236× delamination resistance vs conventional laminates.

**Test**:
DCB test conventional and phi-hierarchical composites (L=1,2,3). Measure GIC. Expect phi-hierarchical GIC ≈ phi^L × conventional.
