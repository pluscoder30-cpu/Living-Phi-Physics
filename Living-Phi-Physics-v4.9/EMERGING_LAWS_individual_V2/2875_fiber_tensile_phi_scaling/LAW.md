# PHI Fiber Tensile Scaling Law

**Domain**: Textile Engineering

**Statement**:
Natural fiber tensile strength follows phi-inverse scaling with diameter: sigma = sigma0*(d0/d)^(1/phi) from hierarchical fibril packing geometry.

**Derivation**:
Fibril packing: N ∝ (d/df)^2 with df ∝ d^(1/phi). Strength sigma = N*sigma_fibril/A. Simplifies to sigma = sigma0*(d0/d)^(1/phi).

**Prediction**:
Reducing diameter by factor phi increases specific tensile strength by phi^(1/phi) ≈ 1.444.

**Test**:
Measure tensile strength at 3 diameter ratios (1:phi:phi^2). Plot log-log. Expect slope ≈ -1/phi ≈ -0.618.
