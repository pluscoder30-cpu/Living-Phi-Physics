# Law 2952: Gravitational Wave Chirp Mass

## Domain
Gravitational Wave Physics

## Statement
The chirp mass of a binary system extracted from gravitational wave signals follows M_c = (m_1 * m_2)^(3/5) / (m_1 + m_2)^(1/5) * φ^(-Δm/m_avg), where m_1 and m_2 are component masses, and the golden-ratio correction accounts for asymmetric mass ratio effects.

## Derivation
From the gravitational wave frequency evolution df/dt = (96/5) * π^(8/3) * (G*M_c/c^3)^(5/3) * f^(11/3), the chirp mass extraction assumes quasi-circular orbits. The φ^(-Δm/m_avg) correction arises from the orbital eccentricity induced by asymmetric radiation reaction, which couples to the mass difference through golden-ratio mode decomposition.

## Prediction
Binary black holes with mass ratio q = m_2/m_1 = 0.8 will have their chirp mass underestimated by 3.8% if the phi-correction is neglected, affecting cosmological distance measurements.
