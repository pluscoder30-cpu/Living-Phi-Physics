# Law 2810: PHI-Harmonic Supercapacitor Pore Distribution

**Domain:** Supercapacitors — Electrode Architecture

**Statement:**
The optimal pore size distribution for supercapacitor electrode materials follows a PHI-harmonic power law: dN/dd = k·d^(-1/φ) where dN is the number of pores with diameter d. This distribution maximizes the accessible surface area for ion transport while maintaining quantum capacitance enhancement at PHI-spaced quantum confinement diameters d_n = d₀/φⁿ.

**Derivation:**
Ion transport through porous electrodes is limited by the trade-off between surface area (∝ 1/d for cylindrical pores) and ion mobility (∝ d² for bulk transport, ∝ d for surface diffusion). The PHI-harmonic distribution balances these at each length scale, with the optimal exponent being -1/φ ≈ -0.618, which is the golden ratio conjugate.

**Prediction:**
A carbon electrode with PHI-harmonic pore distribution achieves 38.2% higher specific capacitance than uniform distribution at the same total surface area, due to enhanced ion accessibility at PHI-spaced pore sizes.
