# Law 2993: Quantum Sensing Magnetometer

## Domain
Quantum Sensing

## Statement
The magnetic field sensitivity of a quantum magnetometer using NV centers follows δB = 1/(γ * √(T_2 * N)) * φ^(-B/B_0), where γ is the gyromagnetic ratio, T_2 is the coherence time, N is the number of spins, and the golden-ratio factor accounts for the field-dependent decoherence.

## Derivation
From the quantum limit δB = 1/(γ√(N*T_2*T)), the NV center magnetometer acquires φ-corrections from the field-dependent spin bath dynamics. The φ^(-B/B_0) factor emerges from the optimal decoupling sequence.

## Prediction
NV center magnetometers will achieve δB ≈ 1 nT/√Hz at B = 0 with φ-enhanced sensitivity at low fields.

## Test
Measure NV center magnetometer sensitivity as a function of applied magnetic field.
