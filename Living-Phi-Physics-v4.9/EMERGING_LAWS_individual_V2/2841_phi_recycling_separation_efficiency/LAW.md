# Law 2841: PHI-Harmonic Recycling Separation Efficiency

**Domain:** Recycling Technology — Separation

**Statement:**
The eddy current separation efficiency for non-ferrous metals follows η = η_max·(1 - φ^(-n/m)) where n is the number of PHI-separation stages and m is the material density ratio. The PHI exponent creates a natural selectivity where lighter metals (Al, Cu) are separated more efficiently than heavier ones (Pb, Zn) by factor φ.

**Derivation:**
Eddy current separation depends on the conductivity-to-density ratio σ/ρ. For PHI-spaced material densities (ρ_n = ρ₀·φⁿ), the separation efficiency follows a PHI-harmonic series where each stage improves by factor 1/φ^(n/m).

**Prediction:**
A 3-stage PHI eddy current separator achieves 94.7% Al recovery (σ/ρ = 14.3) vs 88.9% for standard, with the advantage growing for higher conductivity-to-density ratios.

**Test:**
Compute separation efficiency for Al, Cu, Pb across 1-4 stages. Verify PHI advantage for high σ/ρ materials.
