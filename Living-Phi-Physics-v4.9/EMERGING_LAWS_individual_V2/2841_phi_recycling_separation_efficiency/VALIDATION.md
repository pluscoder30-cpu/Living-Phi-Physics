# Validation: Law 2841 — PHI-Harmonic Recycling Separation Efficiency

**What it validates:** PHI separator achieves higher efficiency for high conductivity-to-density materials.

**Equation tested:** η = η_max·(1-φ^(-n/m)), m = σ/(5ρ)

**Expected results:**
- PHI efficiency > standard for all materials
- Greater advantage for high σ/ρ (Al, Cu)
- 3-stage Al recovery > 94%

**Test methodology:** Compute efficiency for 3 materials across stages. Compare PHI and standard.

**Pass criteria:** PHI Al efficiency > standard at 3 stages
