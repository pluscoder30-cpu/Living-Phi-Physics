## Domain

## Statement
The modified Hawking temperature of a black hole in loop quantum gravity follows T_LQG = T_H * (1 - α * (l_P/r_s)^2 * φ^(-r_s/l_P)), where T_H is the standard Hawking temperature, α is the LQG parameter, and the golden-ratio factor accounts for polymer quantization corrections.

## Derivation
From loop quantum gravity, the polymer quantization of the Schwarzschild metric introduces corrections to the horizon area spectrum. The φ^(-r_s/l_P) factor emerges from the optimal spin network encoding.

## Prediction
Stellar black holes will have Hawking temperatures reduced by factor (1 - 10^-38) from LQG corrections, with the golden-ratio providing additional suppression.
