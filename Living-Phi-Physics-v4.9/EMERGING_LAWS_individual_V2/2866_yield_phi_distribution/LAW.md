# PHI Yield Distribution Law

**Domain**: Agricultural Science

**Statement**:
Crop yield distributions follow phi-modified log-normal P(y) ∝ (1/y)*exp(-(ln(y/mu))^2/(2*sigma^2*phi)).

**Derivation**:
Standard log-normal with spatial coupling: sigma^2_eff = sigma^2*phi from correlated micro-environment variations.

**Prediction**:
Yield maps show phi-modified log-normal histograms with tail index sigma*sqrt(phi).

**Test**:
Collect 100+ harvest points. Fit standard and phi-modified distributions. Expect phi-model AIC improvement > 15.
