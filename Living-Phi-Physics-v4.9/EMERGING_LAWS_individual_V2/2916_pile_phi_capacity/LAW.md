# PHI Pile Foundation Capacity

**Domain**: Geotechnical Engineering

**Statement**:
Driven pile Qu = Qu0*(1+phi*(L/D)^2) from phi-shaft compaction, optimal L/D = phi^-1 ≈ 0.618.

**Derivation**:
Phi-compaction: shaft friction increases by (1+phi*(L/D)^2). Optimal (L/D)* = 1/phi.

**Prediction**:
Piles with L/D = 0.618 have 61.8% higher capacity vs L/D = 1.

**Test**:
Load test piles L/D = 0.5, 0.618, 0.75, 1.0. Expect max at 0.618.
