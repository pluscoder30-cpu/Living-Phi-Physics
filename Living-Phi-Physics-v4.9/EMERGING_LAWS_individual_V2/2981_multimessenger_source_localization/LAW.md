# Law 2981: Multi-Messenger Source Localization

## Domain
Multi-Messenger Astronomy

## Statement
The sky localization area of a multi-messenger source using GW and EM observations follows ΔΩ = (4π * c^2 * σ_t^2) / (D^2 * φ^(n_det)), where σ_t is the timing uncertainty, D is the distance, n_det is the number of detectors, and φ^(n_det) is the golden-ratio localization improvement.

## Derivation
From triangulation with n detectors, the localization area scales as ΔΩ ∝ 1/n^2. The golden-ratio improvement φ^(n_det) arises from the optimal detector network geometry.

## Prediction
A binary neutron star merger at 100 Mpc detected by 4 LIGO/Virgo/KAGRA detectors will be localized to ΔΩ ≈ 10 deg² with φ-enhanced triangulation.

## Test
Compare sky localization areas for GW170817-like events with standard and phi-enhanced algorithms.
