# Validation: Law 2813 — PHI-Harmonic Water Purification Membrane Flux

**What it validates:** PHI-harmonic nanopore membranes show super-linear flux with 1/φ pressure exponent.

**Equation tested:** J = J₀·φ·(ΔP/ΔP₀)^(1/φ)

**Expected results:**
- Flux exponent = 1/φ ≈ 0.618
- PHI flux exceeds linear at moderate pressures
- Super-linear response due to hierarchical pore structure

**Test methodology:** Compute flux for PHI and linear models at 1-20 bar. Find crossover point and verify scaling.

**Pass criteria:** Exponent between 0.60 and 0.65
