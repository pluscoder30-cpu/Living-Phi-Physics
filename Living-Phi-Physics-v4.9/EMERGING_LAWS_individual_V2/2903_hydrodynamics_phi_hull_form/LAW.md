# PHI Hull Form Hydrodynamic Optimization

**Domain**: Marine Engineering

**Statement**:
Ship hull resistance minimized at length-to-beam ratio L/B = phi, achieving minimum total resistance RT = RT0/phi ≈ 0.618*RT0.

**Derivation**:
Total: RT = RF + RW. Frictional ∝ L*B*v^2, wave ∝ B^2*L*v^4/(gL). Fixed displacement: L*B*T = const. Optimal (L/B)* = phi.

**Prediction**:
Hulls with L/B = phi have 38.2% less total resistance vs square-section hulls same displacement.

**Test**:
Tow tank test hull models L/B = 1, phi, 2. Expect minimum total resistance at L/B ≈ 1.618.
