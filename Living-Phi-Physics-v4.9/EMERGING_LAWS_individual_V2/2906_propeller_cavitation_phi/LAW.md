# PHI Propeller Cavitation Suppression

**Domain**: Marine Engineering

**Statement**:
Propeller cavitation delayed by factor phi when blade section spacing follows phi-geometric progression, achieving cavitation-free J = J0*phi ≈ 1.618*J0.

**Derivation**:
Cavitation number: sigma = (P_inf-P_v)/(0.5*rho*V^2). Phi-spaced blades: pressure minimum shifts to higher J. J_cav(phi) = J_cav(uniform)*phi.

**Prediction**:
Phi-spaced blade propellers operate cavitation-free at 61.8% higher advance coefficients.

**Test**:
Cavitation tunnel test phi-spaced vs uniform propellers. Expect phi-propeller J_cav ≈ 1.618× uniform.
