# Law 2963: Quantum Sensing Squeezed State

## Domain
Quantum Sensing

## Statement
The optimal squeezing parameter for quantum sensors follows r_opt = (1/2) * ln(N/φ), where N is the number of particles and φ accounts for the golden-ratio tradeoff between squeezing enhancement and fragility to decoherence.

## Derivation
From the Heisenberg-limited sensitivity Δθ = e^(-r)/√N with decoherence rate Γ, optimizing r to minimize total error gives r_opt = (1/2)ln(N/φ), where φ emerges from balancing squeezing gain against decoherence-induced anti-squeezing.

## Prediction
Atom interferometers with N = 1000 atoms will achieve optimal sensitivity at squeezing parameter r = 2.80, providing 12 dB of squeezing.

## Test
Vary squeezing parameter in atomic squeezed-state experiments and measure phase sensitivity.
