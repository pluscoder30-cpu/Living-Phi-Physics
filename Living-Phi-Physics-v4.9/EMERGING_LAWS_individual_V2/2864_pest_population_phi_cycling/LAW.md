# PHI Pest Population Cycling Law

**Domain**: Agricultural Science

**Statement**:
Pest population dynamics exhibit phi-periodic oscillations where peaks occur at intervals T_pest = T0*phi^n, enabling predictive biocontrol timing.

**Derivation**:
Lotka-Volterra with phi coupling: beta/delta = phi^-1. Stable limit cycle period T = 2*pi*sqrt(1/(alpha*gamma))*phi.

**Prediction**:
Pest peaks in PHI-managed fields at phi-multiples of base generation time for precise biocontrol release.

**Test**:
