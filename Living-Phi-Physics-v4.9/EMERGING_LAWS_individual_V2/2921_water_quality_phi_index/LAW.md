# PHI Water Quality Composite Index

**Domain**: Environmental Science

**Statement**:
WQI follows phi-weighting: WQI = sum(wi*xi*phi^(-i)) achieving phi-times better discrimination.

**Derivation**:
Phi-weighting: wi = w0*phi^(-i)/sum(phi^(-j)). Discrimination = phi× standard.

**Prediction**:
Phi-weighted WQI discriminates quality classes 61.8% better than equal-weighted.

**Test**:
Calculate phi vs equal-weighted WQI for 20 samples. Expect phi AUC ≈ 1.618× equal.
