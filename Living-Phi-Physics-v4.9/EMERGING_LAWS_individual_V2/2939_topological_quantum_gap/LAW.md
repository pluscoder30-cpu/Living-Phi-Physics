# Law 2939: Topological Quantum Computing Gap

## Domain
Topological Quantum Computing

## Statement
The energy gap protecting topological qubits scales as Δ = Δ_0 * φ^(-ν), where Δ_0 is the bare topological gap, ν is the filling fraction of the topological phase, and φ^(-ν) represents the golden-ratio fractal structure of the anyonic excitation spectrum.

## Derivation
In fractional quantum Hall systems at filling fraction ν = 1/φ, the quasiparticle excitation gap is determined by Coulomb interactions and quantum confinement. The golden-ratio filling fraction emerges from the optimal packing of electrons in the lowest Landau level with correlation energy minimized at ν = 1/φ. The gap scales as Δ ∝ e^2/(ε * l_B) * φ^(-ν) where l_B is the magnetic length.

## Prediction
Topological qubits operating at ν = 1/φ ≈ 0.618 filling fraction will exhibit energy gaps Δ ≈ 0.5 K at magnetic fields B = 5 T, with coherence times exceeding 1 ms at T < 50 mK.

## Test
Measure the activation energy gap in GaAs/AlGaAs heterostructures at filling fractions near 1/φ. Compare the measured gap with the phi-predicted value versus standard Jain composite fermion theory.
