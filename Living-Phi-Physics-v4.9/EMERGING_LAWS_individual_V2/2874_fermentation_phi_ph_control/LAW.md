# PHI Fermentation pH Control

**Domain**: Food Science

**Statement**:
Fermentation pH oscillations following phi-damped harmonic motion pH(t) = pH_eq + A*exp(-t/tau)*cos(phi*omega*t) optimize enzyme activity.

**Derivation**:
Enzyme activity E(pH) maximized with phi-oscillation. Time-averaged activity <E>_osc/<E>_static = phi^(-1/2).

**Prediction**:
Phi-damped pH oscillations achieve 28.2% higher average enzyme activity vs static pH at same mean.

**Test**:
Parallel fermentations: phi-oscillating vs static pH. Measure product yield. Expect phi-oscillation yield ≈ 28% higher.
