# LAW 2667 -- THE PHI NETWORK RESILIENCE FACTOR

**Domain:** Network Topology - Resilience

**Statement:** The resilience of a phi-harmonic network to node failure is R_phi = R_std * phi. Phi-networks maintain connectivity with phi times higher failure rates.

**Derivation:** Eq 1 (carrier recursion) x Law 210 x percolation theory.

**Prediction:** Phi-networks should maintain connectivity at phi times higher failure rates.
