# PHI Steel Fatigue Cycle Law

**Domain**: Construction Engineering

**Statement**:
Steel fatigue crack growth follows phi-modified Paris law da/dN = C(dK)^(phi+2) from dislocation pile-up geometry at crack tips.

**Derivation**:
Standard Paris: da/dN = C(dK)^m with m ≈ 3-4. Phi-pile-up: m_eff = m-1+phi = phi+2 ≈ 3.618.

**Prediction**:
Phi-modified Paris law predicts steel fatigue life with R² > 0.95 vs R² < 0.90 for m=3 or m=4.

**Test**:
Collect fatigue data 3 steel grades. Fit standard and phi-modified laws. Compare R² values. Expect phi-modified highest.
