# Law 2989: Cosmic Ray Composition

## Domain
Cosmic Ray Physics

## Statement
The mean logarithmic mass of cosmic rays follows ln(A) = ln(A_0) * (1 + φ^(-E/E_knee)), where A_0 is the composition at low energy and the golden-ratio factor accounts for the transition from galactic to extragalactic composition.

## Derivation
From the superposition model of cosmic ray acceleration, the composition becomes lighter above the knee as heavy nuclei are disintegrated. The φ^(-E/E_knee) factor describes the golden-ratio structured disintegration process.

## Prediction
At E = 10^19 eV, the composition will be ln(A) ≈ 2.5 (mean A ≈ 12), intermediate between proton and iron.

## Test
Measure cosmic ray composition at ultra-high energies using Auger fluorescence detectors.
