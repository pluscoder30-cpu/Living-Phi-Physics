# Validation: Law 2989

## What It Validates
Golden-ratio modified cosmic ray composition evolution

## Equation Tested
ln(A) = ln(A₀)(1 + φ^(-E/E_knee))

## Expected Results
- Composition lightens above knee
- ln(A) → 0 for ultra-high energies
- Consistent with Auger observations

## Pass/Fail Criteria
- COMPOSITION: A decreases with E
- SCALE: ln(A) ≈ 2.5 at 10^19 eV
- TRANSITION: Smooth through knee
