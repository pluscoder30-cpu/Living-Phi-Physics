# Validation: Law 2854 — PHI-Harmonic Mass Spectrometry Sensitivity

**What it validates:** PHI ion optics improve transmission by φ per lens pair.

**Equation tested:** T = φ^(N/3), per-pair = φ^(2/3)

**Expected results:**
- Per-lens-pair improvement = φ^(2/3) ≈ 1.37×
- Better than standard 1.5× per pair at high N
- Total transmission grows exponentially

**Test methodology:** Compute transmission for 2-10 lens elements. Compare PHI and standard.

**Pass criteria:** PHI transmission > standard at 6 lenses
