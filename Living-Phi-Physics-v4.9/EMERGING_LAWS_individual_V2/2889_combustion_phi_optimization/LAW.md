# PHI Combustion Efficiency Optimization

**Domain**: Automotive Engineering

**Statement**:
ICE efficiency maximized when compression ratio follows phi-scaling CR = CR0*phi, achieving peak thermal eta = eta0*(1+1/phi) ≈ 1.618*eta0.

**Derivation**:
Otto cycle: eta = 1-1/CR^(gamma-1). With phi-scaling: eta = 1-1/(phi*CR0)^(gamma-1). For gamma=1.4: eta ≈ 1-1/(CR0^0.4*phi^0.4).

**Prediction**:
Engines with CR increased by phi achieve 61.8% higher thermal efficiency at same fuel consumption.

**Test**:
Test engines at CR = 8, 8*phi ≈ 12.94, 16. Measure brake thermal efficiency. Expect max near CR ≈ 13.
