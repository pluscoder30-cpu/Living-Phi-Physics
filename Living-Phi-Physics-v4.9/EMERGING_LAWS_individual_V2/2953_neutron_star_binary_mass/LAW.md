# Law 2953: Neutron Star Binary Mass

## Domain
Neutron Star Physics

## Statement
The maximum mass of a neutron star in a binary system is M_max_bin = M_max_isolated * (1 + φ^(-q)) where q is the mass ratio and M_max_isolated is the isolated maximum mass from the equation of state.

## Derivation
In binary systems, tidal interactions and angular momentum modify the TOV limit. The golden-ratio factor φ^(-q) emerges from the tidal coupling efficiency, which peaks at q = 1/φ for resonant energy transfer between orbital and internal degrees of freedom.

## Prediction
Neutron star binaries with q ≈ 0.618 can support total masses up to 2.9 M_sun before collapse, 0.1 M_sun higher than isolated stars.

## Test
Compare maximum masses of isolated vs binary neutron stars in LIGO/Virgo catalog.
