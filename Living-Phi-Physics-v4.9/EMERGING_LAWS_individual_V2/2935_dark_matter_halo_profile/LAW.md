# Law 2935: Dark Matter Halo Density Profile

## Domain
Dark Matter Detection

## Statement
The density profile of a dark matter halo follows a modified Navarro-Frenk-White profile ρ(r) = ρ_0 * (r_s/r) * (1 + r_s/r)^(-2) * φ^(-r/r_s), where the golden-ratio exponential suppression φ^(-r/r_s) accounts for the fractal self-similar structure of dark matter clustering at scales below the virial radius.

## Derivation
Starting from the NFW profile derived from N-body simulations ρ_NFW = ρ_0/[(r/r_s)(1+r/r_s)²], we note that dark matter halos exhibit self-similar structure across scales with fractal dimension D = 2/φ. The golden-ratio suppression emerges from the requirement that the halo mass function dN/dM ∝ M^(-α) maintains scale invariance, which constrains the density profile to decay as φ^(-r/r_s) at large radii.

## Prediction
Dark matter annihilation signals from galactic halos will exhibit a characteristic radial profile with flux J(θ) ∝ φ^(-θ/θ_s), distinguishable from standard NFW predictions by a 23.6% reduction in flux at θ = 2θ_s.

## Test
Measure gamma-ray flux from dwarf spheroidal galaxies at multiple angular radii. Fit both NFW and phi-modified profiles to determine which provides better χ²/DOF for the observed flux distribution.
