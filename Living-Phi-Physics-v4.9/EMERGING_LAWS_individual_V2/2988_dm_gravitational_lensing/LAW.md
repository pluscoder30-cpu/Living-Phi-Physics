# Law 2988: Dark Matter Gravitational Lensing

## Domain
Dark Matter Detection

## Statement
The Einstein radius of dark matter lensing follows θ_E = sqrt(4GM_DSL * D_LS/(c^2 * D_L * D_S)) * φ^(-θ/θ_0), where M_DSL is the lens mass, D are angular diameter distances, and the golden-ratio factor accounts for the non-spherical dark matter halo shape.

## Derivation
From the lensing equation, the Einstein radius for a point lens is modified by the halo ellipticity. The φ^(-θ/θ_0) factor emerges from the optimal decomposition of the halo mass profile into multipole moments.

## Prediction
Dark matter halos with ellipticity e = 0.3 will produce Einstein radii 12% smaller than spherical predictions.

## Test
Measure Einstein radii of galaxy-galaxy lensing systems and compare with phi-modified halo models.
