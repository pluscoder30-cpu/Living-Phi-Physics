# Validation: Law 2988

## What It Validates
Golden-ratio modified dark matter gravitational lensing

## Equation Tested
θ_E = θ_E,std × φ^(-θ/θ₀)

## Expected Results
- Einstein radius reduced for elliptical halos
- 12% reduction for typical ellipticity
- Consistent with lensing observations

## Pass/Fail Criteria
- RADIUS: θ_E < θ_E,std for e > 0
- SCALE: Reduction ~ (1-1/φ) for e=0.3
- CONSISTENT: Matches galaxy-galaxy lensing
