# Law 2828: PHI-Harmonic NMR Relaxation Time

**Domain:** Spectroscopy — NMR Relaxation

**Statement:**
NMR T₁ and T₂ relaxation times in PHI-ordered liquid crystals follow a PHI-harmonic field dependence: T₁(B) = T₁₀·φ^(B/B_φ) where B is the magnetic field and B_φ = B₀/ln(φ) is the PHI field constant. The ratio T₁/T₂ = φ at the field B = B₀, providing a natural field marker for structure determination.

**Derivation:**
In liquid crystals with PHI-harmonic director fluctuations, the spectral density function J(ω) has peaks at ω_n = nω₀/φ where ω₀ is the reference frequency. The relaxation rates R₁ = 1/T₁ and R₂ = 1/T₂ depend on J(ω₀) and J(0) respectively, and their ratio at the PHI field gives R₂/R₁ = φ.

**Prediction:**
At B₀ = 14.1 T (600 MHz proton), T₁/T₂ = φ ≈ 1.618 for PHI-ordered liquid crystals, compared to ~2-5 for standard liquid crystals. The T₁ field dependence follows φ^(B/B_φ).

**Test:**
Compute T₁(B) and T₂(B) for B = 7-21 T using PHI model. Find field where T₁/T₂ = φ. Verify field dependence.
