# Validation: Law 2947 - Quantum Gravity Planck Scale Correction

## What It Validates
Golden-ratio suppressed Planck-scale modifications to photon dispersion

## Equation Tested
E² = p²c²(1 + ξ(E/E_Plg)^n × φ^(-E/E_Plg))

## Expected Results
- Corrections vanishingly small at E << E_Plg
- Golden-ratio suppression at E ~ E_Plg
- Time delays detectable for E > 10 TeV
- Consistent with Lorentz invariance tests

## Pass/Fail Criteria
- CORRECTION: ξ × (E/E_Plg)^n < 10^-15 for E < 1 TeV
- SUPPRESSION: φ^(-E/E_Plg) ≈ 1 for E << E_Plg
- DELAY: Δt < 100 s for D < 1 Gpc, E < 100 TeV
- BOUND: ξ < 10^(-3) from current observations
