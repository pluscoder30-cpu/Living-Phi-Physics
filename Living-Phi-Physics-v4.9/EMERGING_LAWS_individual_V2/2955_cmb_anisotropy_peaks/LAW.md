# Law 2955: Cosmic Microwave Background Anisotropy

## Domain
Cosmic Microwave Background

## Statement
The angular power spectrum of CMB temperature anisotropies exhibits golden-ratio peaks at multipoles ℓ_n = ℓ_0 * φ^n, where ℓ_0 ≈ 220 is the first acoustic peak and n labels the harmonic series.

## Derivation
From acoustic oscillations in the photon-baryon fluid, peak positions acquire golden-ratio spacing from the baryon loading effect, which shifts odd and even peaks differently by factor φ.

## Prediction
The CMB power spectrum will show peaks at ℓ = 220, 540, 860 with ratios approaching φ as n increases.

## Test
Measure peak positions in Planck CMB data and compare with φ-harmonic prediction.
