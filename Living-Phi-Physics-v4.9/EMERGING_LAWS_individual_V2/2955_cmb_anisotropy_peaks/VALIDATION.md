# Validation: Law 2955

## What It Validates
Golden-ratio harmonic spacing of CMB acoustic peaks

## Equation Tested
ℓ_n = ℓ_0 × φ^n

## Expected Results
- First peak at ℓ ≈ 220
- Ratio ℓ_2/ℓ_1 ≈ φ
- Consistent with Planck measurements

## Pass/Fail Criteria
- FIRST: ℓ_1 = 220 ± 10
- RATIO: ℓ_{n+1}/ℓ_n → φ for large n
- PLANCK: Matches observed peak positions
