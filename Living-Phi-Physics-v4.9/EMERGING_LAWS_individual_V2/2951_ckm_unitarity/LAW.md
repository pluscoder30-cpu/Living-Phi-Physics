# Law 2951: Particle Physics CKM Unitarity

## Domain
Particle Physics Predictions

## Statement
The unitarity of the CKM matrix is modified by golden-ratio corrections: Σ_i |V_ij|^2 = 1 + ε * φ^(-m_i/m_W), where V_ij are CKM elements, ε is the deviation parameter, m_i are quark masses, and m_W is the W boson mass.

## Derivation
From the Standard Model, the CKM matrix must be unitary Σ_i |V_ij|^2 = 1. However, in extensions with golden-ratio mass hierarchies m_u:m_c:m_t = 1:φ:φ^2, the loop corrections to vertex functions introduce φ^(-m_i/m_W) deviations from exact unitarity.

## Prediction
Precision measurements of CKM elements will show |V_ub|^2 + |V_cb|^2 + |V_tb|^2 = 1 + 10^-6 * φ^(-3), a deviation of 2.36 × 10^-7 from exact unitarity.

## Test
Measure CKM elements at Belle II and LHCb with precision δ|V_ij| < 10^-4. Compare the unitarity sum with the phi-modified prediction.
