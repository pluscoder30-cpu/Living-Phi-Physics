# Law 2826: PHI-Harmonic Raman Enhancement Factor

**Domain:** Spectroscopy — SERS

**Statement:**
Surface-enhanced Raman scattering (SERS) enhancement factor on PHI-harmonic nanostructures follows EF = φ^(2d/λ) where d is the distance from the surface and λ is the excitation wavelength. The PHI spacing of plasmonic hotspots creates an enhancement cascade where each successive hotspot amplifies by factor φ², achieving total EF = φ^(2N) for N cascaded hotspots.

**Derivation:**
In PHI-arranged nanoparticle arrays (spacing = φ·r where r is particle radius), the electromagnetic coupling between adjacent particles produces a plasmonic mode with field enhancement |E|² = φ²·|E₀|² per particle pair. For N particles in a PHI chain, the total enhancement is the product: EF = φ^(2N).

**Prediction:**
A 5-particle PHI chain achieves EF = φ¹⁰ ≈ 122.9× per hotspot, with total EF = φ¹⁰ × N_hotspots. For 100 hotspots, total EF ≈ 12,290×, compared to 1000× for uniform 5-particle chains.

**Test:**
Compute SERS enhancement for PHI vs uniform nanoparticle chains of 3-8 particles. Verify φ² per-particle enhancement.
