# Validation: Law 2826 — PHI-Harmonic Raman Enhancement Factor

**What it validates:** PHI nanoparticle chains achieve φ² per-particle SERS enhancement.

**Equation tested:** EF = φ^(2N) for N-particle chain

**Expected results:**
- Per-particle enhancement = φ² ≈ 2.618×
- 5-particle chain EF ≈ 123×
- Better than uniform 2.5× per-particle

**Test methodology:** Compute EF for 3-8 particle chains. Compare PHI vs uniform enhancement.

**Pass criteria:** PHI EF > uniform EF for all chain lengths
