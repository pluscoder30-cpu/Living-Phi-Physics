# PHI Radiative Transfer Enhancement

**Domain**: Atmospheric Science

**Statement**:
Two-stream: F_up = F0*(1-exp(-tau/phi))/(1+g*phi) from phi-multiple scattering.

**Derivation**:
Phi-scattering: g_eff = g*phi. Albedo: alpha_eff = (1-g*phi)/(1+g*phi).

**Prediction**:
Phi model predicts 61.8% more accurate clear-sky fluxes for SZA > 60 degrees.

**Test**:
Compare vs line-by-line for 50 profiles. Expect phi RMSE 61.8% lower.
