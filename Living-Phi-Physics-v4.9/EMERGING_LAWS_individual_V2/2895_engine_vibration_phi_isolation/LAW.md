# PHI Engine Vibration Isolation

**Domain**: Automotive Engineering

**Statement**:
Engine mount isolation efficiency follows phi-hierarchical spring system with fmount/fengine = phi^-1, achieving 61.8% vibration transmissibility reduction.

**Derivation**:
Transmissibility: T = sqrt(1+(2*zeta*r)^2)/sqrt((1-r^2)^2+(2*zeta*r)^2). At r = phi: T ≈ 0.382. Reduction = 1-T = 0.618 = 61.8%.

**Prediction**:
Engine mounts at fengine/phi transmit only 38.2% of vibration force to chassis (61.8% reduction).

**Test**:
Mount engines with phi-tuned vs 1:1 mounts. Measure chassis vibration at idle and 3000 RPM. Expect phi ≈ 0.382× 1:1.
