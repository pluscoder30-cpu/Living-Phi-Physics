# Law 2949: Information Paradox Complementarity

## Domain
Entropy of Information

## Statement
The complementarity principle for black hole information states that the information content measured by inside and outside observers differs by I_inside - I_outside = (A * k_B * ln(φ)) / (4 * l_P^2), where the golden-ratio term represents the irreducible information contained in the quantum correlations between interior and exterior degrees of freedom.

## Derivation
From the black hole information paradox, the outside observer sees thermal radiation with entropy S_rad = A/(4l_P^2), while the inside observer sees pure state evolution with S_inside = 0. The complementarity resolution requires a third term: the quantum correlations S_corr = (A * ln(φ))/(4l_P^2) that ensure total entropy conservation S_total = S_rad + S_inside + S_corr = A/(4l_P^2) * (1 + ln(φ)).

## Prediction
The information encoded in black hole evaporation will show a golden-ratio modulation in the entanglement entropy between early and late radiation: S_ent(t) = S_0 * (1 - φ^(-t/t_H)), where t_H is the Hawking time.

## Test
Calculate the Page curve for black hole evaporation with the phi-modified complementarity. The Page time should be shifted by factor φ^(-1/2) compared to standard calculations.
