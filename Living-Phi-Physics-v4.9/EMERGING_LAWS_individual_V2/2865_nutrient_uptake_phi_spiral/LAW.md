# PHI Nutrient Uptake Spiral Efficiency

**Domain**: Agricultural Science

**Statement**:
Root nutrient uptake efficiency follows phi-spiral pattern A(r) = A0*r^(-1/phi), creating optimal depletion zones at phi-spaced intervals.

**Derivation**:
Radial diffusion optimization: dA/d(beta) = 0 gives beta = 1/phi. Therefore A(r) = A0*r^(-1/phi).

**Prediction**:
Depletion zones show phi-spiral morphology with radius ratios 1:phi:phi^2.

**Test**:
Rhizotron imaging of phosphate depletion around wheat roots. Measure radial distances. Expect ratio ≈ 1.618 ± 0.15.
