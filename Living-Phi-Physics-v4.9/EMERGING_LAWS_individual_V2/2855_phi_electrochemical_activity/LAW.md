# Law 2855: PHI-Harmonic Electrochemical Activity

**Domain:** Electrochemistry — Electrocatalysis

**Statement:**
The electrocatalytic activity of PHI-nanostructured electrodes follows j = j₀·φ^(η/b_φ) where η is the overpotential and b_φ = b₀/φ is the PHI Tafel slope. The PHI Tafel slope is φ× smaller than standard, meaning the current increases by factor φ per decade of overpotential instead of the standard 10×.

**Derivation:**
In PHI-harmonic catalyst surfaces (active sites at φ-spaced energies), the rate-determining step occurs at the PHI energy level E* = E₀/φ, giving a Tafel slope b = RT/(αF·φ) where α is the transfer coefficient. The PHI spacing reduces the effective transfer coefficient by factor φ.

**Prediction:**
A PHI-Pt catalyst achieves 38.2% higher current density at η = 100 mV compared to standard Pt, with Tafel slope of 38.2 mV/dec (vs 62 mV/dec standard). The PHI catalyst reaches 10 mA/cm² at 38% less overpotential.

**Test:**
Compute current density for η = 0-200 mV using PHI and standard Tafel models. Compare at 100 mV.
