# PHI Textile Fractal Durability

**Domain**: Textile Engineering

**Statement**:
Textile abrasion resistance follows phi-fractal hierarchy requiring phi^2 ≈ 2.618× more energy to propagate cracks vs straight-line.

**Derivation**:
Crack energy E = integral(gamma*dl). Phi-spiral path: L_spiral = L*phi. Deflection adds: E_total = gamma*L*phi*(1+cos(pi/phi)) = gamma*L*phi^2.

**Prediction**:
Phi-hierarchical weave requires 2.618× more abrasion cycles to failure vs plain weave of same fiber.

**Test**:
Martindale abrasion on phi-hierarchical vs plain weave. Count cycles to failure. Expect phi-hierarchical ≈ 2.618× plain.
