# PHI Hohmann Transfer Optimization

**Domain**: Aerospace Engineering

**Statement**:
Orbital transfer fuel efficiency maximized when transfer orbit semi-major axis follows phi-scaling at = a1*phi, achieving dv reduction of phi^-1 ≈ 38.2% vs direct transfer.

**Derivation**:
Hohmann dv: for a2 = a1*phi^2, optimizing d(dv1+dv2)/dat = 0 gives at* = a1*phi. Total dv = dv0/phi.

**Prediction**:
Hohmann transfers with phi-scaled semi-major axis require 38.2% less total dv between orbits at ratio phi^2.

**Test**:
Calculate dv for Earth-Mars transfers phi-scaled vs direct. Expect phi-scaled dv ≈ 0.618× direct.
