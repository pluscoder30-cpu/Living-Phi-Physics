# PHI Geogrid Reinforcement Efficiency

**Domain**: Geotechnical Engineering

**Statement**:
Soil-geogrid pullout P = P0*(1+phi*(Lg/Lf)^2) with optimal Lg/Lf = phi^-1.

**Derivation**:
Pullout with phi-enhanced interlocking: f_eff = f0*(1+phi*(Lg/Lf)^2). Optimal: (Lg/Lf)* = 1/phi.

**Prediction**:
Geogrids with Lg/Lf = 1/phi achieve 61.8% greater pullout resistance.

**Test**:
Pullout tests Lg/Lf = 0.5, 0.618, 0.75, 1.0. Expect max at 0.618.
