# PHI Aerosol Coagulation Rate

**Domain**: Atmospheric Science

**Statement**:
Coagulation K(d1,d2) = K0*(d1+d2)^2/(d1*d2)^(1/phi) from fractal aggregate cross-sections.

**Derivation**:
Fractal: d_eff = d^(Df/3) with Df = 3/phi. K_eff = K0*(d1+d2)^(2/phi)/(d1*d2)^(1/phi).

**Prediction**:
Fractal soot coagulates 1.618× faster than spherical particles.

**Test**:
Measure coagulation of soot vs latex. Expect fractal ≈ 1.618× spherical.
