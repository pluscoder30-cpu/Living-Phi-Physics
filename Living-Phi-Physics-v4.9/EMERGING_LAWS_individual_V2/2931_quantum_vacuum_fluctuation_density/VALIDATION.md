# Validation: Law 2931 - Quantum Vacuum Fluctuation Energy Density

## What It Validates
Golden-ratio modulation of quantum vacuum zero-point energy in confined geometries

## Equation Tested
ρ_eff = ρ_Casimir × φ^(-n) with holographic bound E_max = (c³ℏ ln(φ))/(4GL)

## Expected Results
- Casimir energy density scales as 1/d³ for separations 10-100 nm
- Holographic bound exceeds Casimir energy by factor >10³
- Phi-modulated modes show discrete exponential decay
- Energy ratios between adjacent modes equal PHI

## Pass/Fail Criteria
- CASIMIR: Energy density matches QED prediction (ε < 0.1%)
- HOLOGRAPHIC: Bound consistently > measured energy
- PHI-MODES: Ratio E_n/E_{n+1} ≈ PHI ± 0.01
- SCALE: Energy values within 10 orders of magnitude of theoretical
