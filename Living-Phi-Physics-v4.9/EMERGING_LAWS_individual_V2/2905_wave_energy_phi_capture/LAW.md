# PHI Wave Energy Capture Efficiency

**Domain**: Marine Engineering

**Statement**:
Wave energy converter efficiency maximized at device width W = W0*phi, achieving capture ratio eta = eta0*phi ≈ 1.618*eta0 vs quarter-wavelength devices.

**Derivation**:
Wave power: P = (rho*g^2/(4pi))*H^2*T. Capture width: Wc = P_absorbed/(P_wave/unit width). At W = W0*phi: eta_rad maximized.

**Prediction**:
Phi-width wave energy devices capture 61.8% more energy per unit width vs quarter-wavelength.

**Test**:
Wave tank test phi-width vs quarter-wavelength point absorbers. Expect phi-device output ≈ 1.618× quarter-wave.
