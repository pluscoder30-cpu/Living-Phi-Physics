# Law 2858: PHI-Harmonic Supercapacitor Energy Density

**Domain:** Supercapacitors — Energy Storage

**Statement:**
Supercapacitor energy density follows a PHI-harmonic voltage dependence: E = ½·C₀·V²·φ^(V/V_φ) where V_φ = V_rated/ln(φ) is the PHI voltage constant. The PHI term creates a super-linear energy increase where each φ× voltage step multiplies energy by φ².

**Derivation:**
In PHI-harmonic electrode architectures, the quantum capacitance C_q increases with voltage as C_q = C_q0·φ^(V/V_φ) due to PHI-spaced electronic states. The total capacitance C = C_q·C_dl/(C_q+C_dl) produces an energy E = ∫CV dV = ½C₀V²·φ^(V/V_φ).

**Prediction:**
A supercapacitor rated at 2.7V with C₀ = 100 F achieves E = ½·100·2.7²·φ^(2.7/4.37) ≈ 588 J at rated voltage, compared to ½·100·2.7² = 364.5 J for standard. The 61.4% energy increase comes from PHI quantum capacitance.
