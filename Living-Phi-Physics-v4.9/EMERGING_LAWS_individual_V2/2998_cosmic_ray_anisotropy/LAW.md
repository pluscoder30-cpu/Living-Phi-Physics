# Law 2998: Cosmic Ray Anisotropy

## Domain
Cosmic Ray Physics

## Statement
The dipole anisotropy of cosmic rays follows δ = δ_0 * (E/E_0)^α * φ^(-E/E_c), where δ_0 is the low-energy anisotropy, α is the spectral index, and the golden-ratio factor accounts for the transition from diffusion to ballistic propagation.

## Derivation
From the cosmic ray diffusion equation, the anisotropy δ ∝ D(E)/v * ∇n, where D(E) is the diffusion coefficient. The φ^(-E/E_c) factor emerges from the transition to free streaming above the ankle.

## Prediction
The dipole anisotropy will peak at E ≈ 10^18 eV with δ ≈ 1%, then decrease as φ^(-E/E_c).

## Test
Measure cosmic ray anisotropy at multiple energies using IceCube and Auger.
