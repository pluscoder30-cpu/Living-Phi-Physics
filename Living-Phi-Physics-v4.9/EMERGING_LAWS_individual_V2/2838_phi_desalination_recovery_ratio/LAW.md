# Law 2838: PHI-Harmonic Desalination Recovery Ratio

**Domain:** Desalination — Water Recovery

**Statement:**
The water recovery ratio in PHI-harmonic reverse osmosis follows R = 1 - (1-R₀)/φ where R₀ is the single-stage recovery. The PHI membrane architecture reduces the thermodynamic minimum energy by factor φ, enabling higher recovery at the same energy input.

**Derivation:**
In a single-stage RO system, the minimum energy is E_min = RT·ln(1/R₀)·(1-R₀). For PHI-modified membrane with reduced concentration polarization, the effective osmotic pressure is π_eff = π₀/φ, giving R = 1 - (1-R₀)·exp(-ΔP·φ/(π₀)).

**Prediction:**
A single-stage PHI-RO system achieves 55% recovery at 3 kWh/m³, compared to 34% for standard RO. The PHI advantage comes from reduced concentration polarization by factor φ.

**Test:**
Compute recovery for PHI vs standard models at 1-10 kWh/m³. Verify 55% recovery at 3 kWh/m³.
