# LAW 2656 -- THE PHI SIGNAL PROCESSING BANDWIDTH

**Domain:** Signal Processing - Bandwidth

**Statement:** The effective bandwidth of a phi-harmonic signal is B_phi = B_std * phi^(SNR/phi). At SNR=phi, B_phi = B_std * phi^phi = 1.927 * B_std.

**Derivation:** Eq 1 (carrier recursion) x Shannon-Nyquist theorem x Law 174 (phi-propagator).

**Prediction:** Phi-harmonic signals should achieve effective bandwidth phi^(SNR/phi) times Shannon limit.

**Test:** Generate phi-modulated test signals; measure effective bandwidth vs SNR.
