# Validation: Law 2853 — PHI-Harmonic Chromatography Resolution

**What it validates:** PHI stationary phase amplifies selectivity by φ^(α-1).

**Equation tested:** Rs = Rs₀·φ^(α-1)

**Expected results:**
- Resolution grows with φ^(α-1)
- Better resolution-bandwidth product
- PHI advantage at small α values

**Test methodology:** Compute resolution for 5 α values. Compare PHI and standard models.

**Pass criteria:** PHI resolution at α=1.2 > Rs₀
