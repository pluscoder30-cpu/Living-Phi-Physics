# Law 2853: PHI-Harmonic Chromatography Resolution

**Domain:** Chromatography — Resolution

**Statement:**
Chromatographic resolution follows a PHI-harmonic selectivity enhancement: Rs = Rs₀·φ^(α-1) where α is the selectivity factor. The PHI stationary phase amplifies small selectivity differences by factor φ per unit α, meaning a 10% selectivity improvement (α = 1.1) yields Rs = Rs₀·φ^0.1 ≈ 1.05× improvement.

**Derivation:**
In a PHI-harmonic stationary phase, the interaction energy distribution has peaks at E₀, E₀/φ, E₀/φ², ..., creating a natural selectivity amplifier. The resolution equation Rs = (√N/4)·(α-1)·(k/(1+k)) becomes Rs = (√N/4)·φ^(α-1)·(k/(1+k)) because the PHI spacing amplifies the selectivity term.

**Prediction:**
For α = 1.2, Rs = Rs₀·φ^0.2 ≈ 1.10×, compared to Rs₀·1.2 = 1.2× for standard. The PHI advantage is in the resolution-bandwidth product, achieving the same resolution with 10% less analysis time.

**Test:**
Compute resolution for α = 1.0-2.0 using PHI and standard models. Compare at α = 1.2.
