# Law 2804: PHI-Harmonic NMR Chemical Shift Ratio

**Domain:** Spectroscopy — NMR

**Statement:**
Chemical shift differences between PHI-related nuclear environments (e.g., α, β, γ carbons in PHI-spaced chains) follow the ratio Δδ_n/Δδ_0 = 1/φⁿ, where n is the separation index. The J-coupling constants between PHI-spaced nuclei follow J_n = J₀·φ⁻ⁿ, providing a natural frequency scale for structure determination.

**Derivation:**
The chemical shift δ depends on the electron density at the nucleus, which is modulated by through-bond and through-space effects. In PHI-harmonic molecular frameworks where atoms are spaced at φ multiples of a reference distance, the electron density perturbation decays as 1/φⁿ, giving δ_n = δ₀ + Δδ₀/φⁿ.

**Prediction:**
For a chain of 4 carbons with PHI spacing, the chemical shift differences from the reference are Δδ₀, Δδ₀/φ, Δδ₀/φ², Δδ₀/φ³. For Δδ₀ = 40 ppm, this gives 40, 24.7, 15.3, 9.4 ppm differences.

**Test:**
Compute chemical shifts for 5 PHI-spaced nuclear environments. Verify shift differences follow 1/φⁿ progression and J-couplings follow φ⁻ⁿ.
