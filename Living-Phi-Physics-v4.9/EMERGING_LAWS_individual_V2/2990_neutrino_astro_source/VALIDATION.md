# Validation: Law 2990

## What It Validates
Golden-ratio suppressed neutrino source spectrum

## Equation Tested
L_ν = L₀(E_ν/E₀)^α × φ^(-E_ν/E_cut)

## Expected Results
- Power-law with φ-cutoff
- Consistent with IceCube observations
- Galactic plane source detected

## Pass/Fail Criteria
- SPECTRUM: E^(-2) at low energies
- CUTOFF: φ^(-E/E_cut) at high E
- DETECTION: Consistent with IceCube
