# Law 2990: Neutrino Astrophysics Source

## Domain
Neutrino Astronomy

## Statement
The neutrino luminosity from astrophysical sources follows L_ν = L_0 * (E_ν/E_0)^α * φ^(-E_ν/E_cut), where L_0 is the normalization, α is the spectral index, and the golden-ratio factor accounts for the energy-dependent pion production efficiency.

## Derivation
From the π^0 → γγ and π^+ → μ^+ν_μ decay chains, the neutrino spectrum inherits the pion spectrum with φ-corrections from the energy-dependent cross section for pp and pγ interactions.

## Prediction
The IceCube neutrino flux from the Galactic plane will show φ-suppressed high-energy tail above 100 TeV.

## Test
Search for golden-ratio spectral features in IceCube through-going muon data.
