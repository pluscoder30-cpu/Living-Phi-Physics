# PHI Transmission Gear Ratio Optimization

**Domain**: Automotive Engineering

**Statement**:
Multi-speed transmission efficiency maximized when gear ratios follow phi-progression rn = r0*phi^n, minimizing shift-point power interruption by factor phi.

**Derivation**:
Gear efficiency: eta_gear = 1-mu*tan(alpha). Phi-ratio spacing: power interruption = (phi-1)*fraction. Average efficiency weighted by 1/phi^i.

**Prediction**:
Phi-ratio transmissions have 61.8% less power interruption during shifts vs linear-ratio.

**Test**:
Simulate 8-speed phi-ratio vs linear-ratio. Measure average efficiency over drive cycle. Expect phi-ratio ≈ 1.618× linear.
