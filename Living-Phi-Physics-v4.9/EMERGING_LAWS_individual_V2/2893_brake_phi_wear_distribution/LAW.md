# PHI Brake Wear Distribution

**Domain**: Automotive Engineering

**Statement**:
Brake pad wear uniformity optimized at caliper piston diameter ratio dpiston/dpad = phi^-1, achieving uniform wear W(x) = W0*(1+cos(pi*x/L)).

**Derivation**:
Wear: W(x) = mu*p(x)*v*t. Phi-spaced pistons: p(x) uniform when Fi/F_{i+1} = phi. Peak wear W_max = W0*(1+1/phi) ≈ 1.618*W0.

**Prediction**:
Phi-ratio piston brake systems have 38.2% more uniform wear, extending pad life by same factor.

**Test**:
Test assemblies with equal vs phi-ratio pistons. Measure wear profile 100 stops. Expect phi-ratio variance 38.2% lower.
