# Law 2958: Topological Insulator Surface State

## Domain
Topological Quantum Computing

## Statement
The Dirac cone dispersion of topological insulator surface states acquires golden-ratio anisotropy: E(k) = v_F * sqrt((k_x * φ)^2 + (k_y/φ)^2), where the anisotropy factor φ arises from hexagonal crystal symmetry.

## Derivation
From the surface Hamiltonian H = v_F(σ_x k_y - σ_y k_x), hexagonal warping introduces φ-anisotropy when projected onto principal axes of the Fermi surface.

## Prediction
ARPES measurements of Bi2Se3 will reveal Fermi velocity anisotropy v_Fx/v_Fy = φ ≈ 1.618 at binding energy > 0.5 eV.

## Test
Measure angle-resolved photoemission spectra of Bi2Se3 and fit Dirac cone dispersion.
