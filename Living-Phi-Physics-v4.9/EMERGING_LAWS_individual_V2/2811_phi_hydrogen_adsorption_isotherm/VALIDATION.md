# Validation: Law 2811 — PHI-Harmonic Hydrogen Adsorption Isotherm

**What it validates:** Multi-site Langmuir with PHI-spaced binding energies produces step-like isotherm.

**Equation tested:** θ(P) = Σ K₀φⁱP/(1+K₀φⁱP), step at P_n = P₀/φⁿ

**Expected results:**
- Step positions at PHI-spaced pressures
- Multiple binding sites with φ× energy spacing
- Higher uptake than single-site Langmuir

**Test methodology:** 4-site PHI Langmuir isotherm vs single-site. Compare isotherms and identify step positions.

**Pass criteria:** Correct number of steps detected
