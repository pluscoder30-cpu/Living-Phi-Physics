# PHI Fire Resistance Insulation

**Domain**: Construction Engineering

**Statement**:
Fire resistance rating increases phi-fold per insulation layer following phi-thickness progression tn = t0*phi^n, achieving R = R0*phi^L.

**Derivation**:
Heat transfer resistance: R_total = sum(tn/kn) = (t0/k)*sum(phi^n) = t0*phi^N/k*(phi/(phi-1)).

**Prediction**:
3-layer phi-insulation provides 4.236× fire resistance vs single equivalent-thickness layer.

**Test**:
Fire resistance test (ASTM E119) 1-layer vs 3-layer phi-insulated columns. Expect 3-layer time ≈ 4.236× single-layer.
