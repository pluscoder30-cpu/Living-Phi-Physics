# Law 2932: Holographic Black Hole Information Entropy

## Domain
Black Hole Thermodynamics

## Statement
The information content of a black hole, measured in qubits, follows the modified Bekenstein-Hawking formula I = (A * c^3) / (4 * G * ℏ * ln(2)) * (1 + φ^(-k)), where A is the event horizon area and k is the number of stable information encoding channels on the horizon.

## Derivation
Starting from S_BH = k_B * A / (4 * l_P^2), converting to information units via Landauer's principle E_min = k_B * T * ln(2), and accounting for the golden-ratio compression of information on the horizon surface through the fractal packing density φ^(-k), we obtain the modified information entropy. The correction term φ^(-k) represents the fraction of information accessible to distant observers.

## Prediction
Black holes with horizon area A = N * l_P^2 encode I = N/(4*ln(2)) * (1 + φ^(-3)) qubits of information, where the correction factor φ^(-3) ≈ 0.236 accounts for interior information inaccessible to exterior observers.
