# Law 2969: Cosmic Ray Knee Spectrum

## Domain
Cosmic Ray Physics

## Statement
The cosmic ray energy spectrum exhibits a "knee" at E_knee = 3 × 10^15 eV with spectral index change Δγ = φ^(-1), where the golden-ratio factor accounts for the transition from galactic to extragalactic sources.

## Derivation
The cosmic ray spectrum dN/dE ∝ E^(-γ) changes index at the knee due to the maximum acceleration energy of galactic sources. The φ^(-1) factor emerges from the optimal confinement energy in the galactic magnetic field.

## Prediction
The spectral index below the knee γ_1 = 2.7 and above γ_2 = 2.7 + φ^(-1) ≈ 3.318, a change of 0.618.

## Test
Measure cosmic ray spectrum across the knee region with Auger and KASCADE-Grande.
