# Law 2827: PHI-Harmonic IR Polarization Angle

**Domain:** Spectroscopy — Polarized IR

**Statement:**
The IR absorption dichroic ratio for PHI-oriented molecules follows R(θ) = φ^(2cos²θ) where θ is the angle between the molecular transition dipole and the polarization direction. The PHI dependence arises from the golden-angle (137.5°) orientation preference of molecules on PHI-textured surfaces.

**Derivation:**
On surfaces with PHI-harmonic texturing (e.g., fibonacci spiral patterns), molecules preferentially orient at the golden angle relative to the surface normal. The projection of the transition dipole onto the electric field follows R = cos²(θ) for standard linear dichroism, but PHI surface coupling modifies this to R = φ^(2cos²θ) because the PHI texture creates an additional coupling term.

**Prediction:**
For θ = 0° (parallel): R = φ² ≈ 2.618. For θ = 90° (perpendicular): R = 1. For θ = 45°: R = φ ≈ 1.618. The dichroic ratio at 45° uniquely identifies PHI surface texturing.

**Test:**
Compute dichroic ratio for θ = 0°, 30°, 45°, 60°, 90°. Verify R(45°) = φ and R(0°) = φ².
