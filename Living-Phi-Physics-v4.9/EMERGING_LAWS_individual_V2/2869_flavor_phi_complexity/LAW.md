# PHI Flavor Complexity Maximization

**Domain**: Food Science

**Statement**:
Flavor compound interaction complexity peaks at phi-proportion ingredient ratios, creating non-linear synergy increasing perceived intensity by phi times vs linear mixing.

**Derivation**:
Flavor interaction matrix F_ij = ci*cj*alpha_ij. Maximizing determinant: optimal ratios ci/cj = phi^|i-j|. Synergy S = |F|/|F_linear| = phi.

**Prediction**:
Phi-ratio recipes score phi times higher on sensory complexity ratings vs equal-proportion recipes.

**Test**:
5 flavor mixtures phi-ratio vs equal proportions. Blind taste test 30 panelists. Expect phi-ratio mean ≈ 1.618× equal.
