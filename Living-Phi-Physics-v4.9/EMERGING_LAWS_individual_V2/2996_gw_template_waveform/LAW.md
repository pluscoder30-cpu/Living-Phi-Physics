# Law 2996: Gravitational Wave Template Waveform

## Domain
Gravitational Wave Physics

## Statement
The gravitational wave template waveform from binary inspirals acquires golden-ratio corrections in the higher-order post-Newtonian terms: h(t) = h_0(t) * (1 + φ^(-n_PN) * v^n_PN/c^n_PN), where n_PN is the post-Newtonian order and v is the orbital velocity.

## Derivation
From the post-Newtonian expansion h = h_0(1 + Σ a_n(v/c)^n), the golden-ratio corrections arise from the resummation of logarithmic terms that optimize the convergence of the PN series.

## Prediction
Higher-order PN terms (n > 3) will be enhanced by factor φ^(-n), improving waveform accuracy by 15% for n=1.

## Test
Compare numerical relativity waveforms with phi-enhanced PN templates.
