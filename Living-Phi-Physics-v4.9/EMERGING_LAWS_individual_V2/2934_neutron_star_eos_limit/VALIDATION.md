# Validation: Law 2934 - Neutron Star Equation of State Limit

## What It Validates
Golden-ratio enhanced maximum neutron star mass from quark matter packing

## Equation Tested
M_max = (φ c³)/(4G^(3/2) √(π ρ_QCD))

## Expected Results
- Maximum mass ~2.8 M_sun (vs standard 2.17 M_sun)
- Phi enhancement factor φ ≈ 1.618
- Quark core fraction increases with total mass
- Stars above M_max collapse to black holes

## Pass/Fail Criteria
- MASS: 2.5 < M_max < 3.5 M_sun (reasonable QCD range)
- ENHANCEMENT: M_max(phi) / M_max(standard) ≈ PHI ± 0.1
- QUARK: Core fraction > 0.5 for M > 0.9 × M_max
- CONSISTENCY: Heavier stars have larger quark cores
