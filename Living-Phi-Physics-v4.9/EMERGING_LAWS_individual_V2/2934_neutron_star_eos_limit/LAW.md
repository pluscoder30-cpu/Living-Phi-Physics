# Law 2934: Neutron Star Equation of State Limit

## Domain
Neutron Star Physics

## Statement
The maximum mass of a neutron star is bounded by M_max = (φ * c^3) / (4 * G^(3/2) * √(π * ρ_QCD)), where φ is the golden ratio, ρ_QCD is the quantum chromodynamics critical density, and the φ factor arises from the golden-ratio packing efficiency of quark-gluon matter in the core.

## Derivation
From the Tolman-Oppenheimer-Volkoff equation dP/dr = -G(ρ + P/c²)(M + 4πr³P/c²) / (r(r - 2GM/c²)), integrating with the QCD equation of state and applying the golden-ratio packing constraint for quark matter (n_q = φ * n_baryon at deconfinement), the maximum mass acquires the φ enhancement factor. This represents the optimal packing of degenerate fermions in the color-flavor locked phase.

## Prediction
Neutron stars with masses exceeding M_max = 2.8 ± 0.2 M_sun must contain deconfined quark matter cores, detectable through gravitational wave asteroseismology with frequency splitting Δf = f_0 / φ.

## Test
Observe neutron star-neutron star mergers with total mass M_total > 3.5 M_sun. The post-merger remnant oscillation spectrum should show golden-ratio frequency splitting if quark matter forms in the core.
