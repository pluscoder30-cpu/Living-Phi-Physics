# Law 2965: Black Hole Area Theorem

## Domain
Black Hole Thermodynamics

## Statement
The area of a black hole event horizon increases by ΔA = 8π * G^2 * (m_1 * m_2) / c^4 * φ^(-E_rad/E_total), where m_1 and m_2 are merging black hole masses and φ^(-E_rad/E_total) accounts for golden-ratio suppressed gravitational wave energy loss.

## Derivation
From Hawking's area theorem ΔA >= 0, the binary merger area increase is modified by the fraction of energy radiated in gravitational waves. The φ factor emerges from the quasinormal mode spectrum of the final black hole.

## Prediction
Binary black hole mergers will produce final black holes with areas A_f = (A_1 + A_2) * (1 + φ^(-0.05)) ≈ 1.03(A_1 + A_2).

## Test
Measure ringdown quasi-normal modes from LIGO/Virgo events to determine final black hole area.
