# PHI Thermal Fabric Insulation

**Domain**: Textile Engineering

**Statement**:
Fabric thermal insulation at phi-layer air entrapment achieves phi-times the R-value of equivalent mass solid insulation from optimal convection suppression.

**Derivation**:
R = L/k_eff. With phi-spacing: f_trap = 1/phi^2. k_eff = k_air*(1-1/phi). R = L*phi/k_air. R/R_solid = phi.

**Prediction**:
Phi-layered air-trap fabrics have 61.8% higher R-value per unit mass vs equivalent solid insulation.

**Test**:
Compare R-values (ASTM C177) of phi-layered vs solid insulation equal mass. Expect phi-fabric R ≈ 1.618× solid.
