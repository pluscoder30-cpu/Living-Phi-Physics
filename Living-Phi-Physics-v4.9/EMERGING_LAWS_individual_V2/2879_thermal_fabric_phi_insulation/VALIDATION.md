# Validation: PHI Thermal Fabric Insulation

**Law ID**: 2879

**Validates**: Textile Engineering phi-harmonic relationship

**Equation**: R = L/k_eff. With phi-spacing: f_trap = 1/phi^2. k_eff = k_air*(1-1/phi). R = L*phi/k_air. R/R_solid = phi.

**Expected**: Phi-layered air-trap fabrics have 61.8% higher R-value per unit mass vs equivalent solid insulation.

**Test**: Compare R-values (ASTM C177) of phi-layered vs solid insulation equal mass. Expect phi-fabric R ≈ 1.618× solid.
