# PHI Aerodynamic Drag Reduction

**Domain**: Automotive Engineering

**Statement**:
Vehicle drag coefficient minimized at body proportions L/H = phi and W/H = phi^-1, achieving Cd = Cd0/phi ≈ 0.618*Cd0.

**Derivation**:
Drag: Cd = Cd_pressure + Cd_friction. Pressure ∝ W*H, friction ∝ 2(LW+LH+WH). Optimizing L/W: (L/W)* = phi.

**Prediction**:
Vehicles with L:H:W = phi:1:phi^-1 have 38.2% lower drag coefficient vs box-shaped reference.

**Test**:
Wind tunnel test phi-ratio vs box models same frontal area. Expect phi-ratio Cd ≈ 0.618× box Cd.
