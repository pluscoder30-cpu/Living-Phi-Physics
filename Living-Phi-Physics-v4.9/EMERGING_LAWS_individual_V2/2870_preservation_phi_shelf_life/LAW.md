# PHI Preservation Shelf Life Extension

**Domain**: Food Science

**Statement**:
Food preservation using phi-ratio combination hurdle factors (pH*aw*temperature) achieves shelf life extension phi^n where n is number of hurdles.

**Derivation**:
Hurdle: mu = mu0*prod(1-hi) with hi = 1/phi. Shelf life L ∝ 1/mu = phi^(2n).

**Prediction**:
Three-hurdle PHI preservation extends shelf life by phi^6 ≈ 17.9× vs single-factor.

**Test**:
Apply 1/2/3 phi-hurdles to food samples. Track microbial counts. Expect 3-hurdle ≈ 18× single-hurdle shelf life.
