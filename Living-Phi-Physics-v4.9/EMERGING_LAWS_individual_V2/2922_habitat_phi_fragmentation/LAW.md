# PHI Habitat Fragmentation Threshold

**Domain**: Environmental Science

**Statement**:
Fragmentation threshold at d_iso = d0*phi where metapopulation viability drops below 50%.

**Derivation**:
Occupancy P = 1/(1+(d_iso/(d0*phi))^2). P = 0.5 when d_iso = d0*phi.

**Prediction**:
Patches isolated beyond phi×dispersal distance have <50% occupancy.

**Test**:
Analyze patch occupancy for 5 species. Expect phi-model better predicts thresholds.
