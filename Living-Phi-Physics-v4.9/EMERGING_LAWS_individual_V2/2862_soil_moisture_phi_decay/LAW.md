# PHI Soil Moisture Decay Law

**Domain**: Agricultural Science

**Statement**:
Soil moisture retention follows phi-exponential decay M(t) = M0*exp(-t/(tau*phi)) where tau is the base drainage time constant, resulting in 61.8% longer effective moisture availability.

**Derivation**:
Darcy modified: K_eff = K0/phi. Therefore tau_eff = tau0*phi. Moisture half-life: t1/2 = tau0*phi*ln2.

**Prediction**:
In soils with PHI-optimized aggregate structure, moisture half-life increases by factor phi ≈ 1.618 vs conventional tillage.

**Test**:
Compare drainage in phi-structured vs conventionally tilled soil columns. Expect phi-structured to retain 61.8% more moisture at t=tau0.
