# Law 2960: Fractional Quantum Hall State

## Domain
Quantum Field Theory Applications

## Statement
The quasi-particle excitation spectrum at ν = 1/φ follows E_n = E_0 * φ^(-n) + n * ℏω_c/φ, where E_0 is the gap energy and ω_c is the cyclotron frequency scaled by golden ratio.

## Derivation
The composite fermion picture at ν = 1/φ maps interacting electrons to free fermions in reduced field B* = B/φ. The excitation spectrum inherits φ-scaling from composite fermion Landau level structure.

## Prediction
Resonant inelastic light scattering at ν = 1/φ will reveal collective modes at energies spaced by ℏω_c/φ.

## Test
Perform RIXS on GaAs 2DEG at ν = 1/φ to map collective mode spectrum.
