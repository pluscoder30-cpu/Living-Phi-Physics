# Validation: Law 2960

## What It Validates
Golden-ratio scaled excitation spectrum of FQH state

## Equation Tested
E_n = E_0 φ^(-n) + nℏω_c/φ

## Expected Results
- Gap energy decays as φ^(-n)
- Landau levels spaced by ℏω_c/φ
- Observable by RIXS

## Pass/Fail Criteria
- GAP: E_n/E_0 → φ^(-n) for large n
- SPACING: ΔE ≈ ℏω_c/φ
- MEASURABLE: Resolvable by RIXS
