# LAW 2663 -- THE PHI INFORMATION THEORETIC ENTROPY

**Domain:** Information Theory - Entropy

**Statement:** The phi-entropy is H_phi = -sum(p_i*log_phi(p_i)) = H_std/log(phi). For binary source, H_phi_max = 1/log(phi) = 1.4404.

**Derivation:** Eq 1 (carrier recursion) x Shannon entropy x Law 2413 (phi entropy principle).

**Prediction:** Phi-entropy should provide tighter bounds on compression for phi-structured data.

**Test:** Compute phi-entropy for various distributions; compare to Shannon entropy.
