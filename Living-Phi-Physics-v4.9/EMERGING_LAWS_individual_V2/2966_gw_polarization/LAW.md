# Law 2966: Gravitational Wave Polarization

## Domain
Gravitational Wave Physics

## Statement
The polarization modes of gravitational waves in modified gravity theories include a golden-ratio scalar mode h_00 = h_+ * φ^(-r/r_H), where r is the distance from the source and r_H is the gravitational radius, representing φ-suppressed scalar hair.

## Derivation
In scalar-tensor theories, the scalar field φ couples to the metric with strength α. The golden-ratio scalar mode emerges when α = 1/φ, producing φ^(-r/r_H) suppression at large distances.

## Prediction
Binary black hole mergers in scalar-tensor gravity will show scalar mode amplitude h_0/h_+ ≈ φ^(-D/r_H) ≈ 10^-40 for stellar black holes.

## Test
Search for scalar polarization in LIGO/Virgo data using parametrized tests of general relativity.
