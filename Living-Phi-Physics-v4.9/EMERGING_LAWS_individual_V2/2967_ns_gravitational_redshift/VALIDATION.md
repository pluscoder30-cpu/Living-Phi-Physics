# Validation: Law 2967

## What It Validates
Golden-ratio corrected neutron star gravitational redshift

## Equation Tested
z_phi = z_g × (1 + φ^(-M/M_max))

## Expected Results
- Redshift increases with mass
- Enhancement ~23.6% near M_max
- Detectable by X-ray spectroscopy

## Pass/Fail Criteria
- SCALE: z_g ≈ 0.2-0.4 for typical NS
- ENHANCEMENT: z_phi > z_g always
- MASS: Stronger correction near M_max
