# Law 2967: Neutron Star Gravitational Redshift

## Domain
Neutron Star Physics

## Statement
The gravitational redshift of photons emitted from a neutron star surface follows z_g = (1 - 2GM/(Rc^2))^(-1/2) - 1, with golden-ratio correction z_phi = z_g * (1 + φ^(-M/M_max)), where M_max is the maximum neutron star mass.

## Derivation
The standard GR redshift acquires corrections from the neutron star equation of state through the compactness parameter x = GM/(Rc^2). The φ^(-M/M_max) factor accounts for the changing density profile as the star approaches maximum mass.

## Prediction
Neutron stars near maximum mass (M > 2.0 M_sun) will show gravitational redshifts 23.6% higher than the standard GR prediction.

## Test
Measure redshifts of spectral lines from massive neutron stars and compare with standard and phi-modified predictions.
