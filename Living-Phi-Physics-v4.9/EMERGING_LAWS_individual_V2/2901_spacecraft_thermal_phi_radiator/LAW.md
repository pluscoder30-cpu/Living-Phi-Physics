# PHI Spacecraft Radiator Efficiency

**Domain**: Aerospace Engineering

**Statement**:
Spacecraft radiator efficiency maximized at fin spacing ratio s/h = phi^-1, achieving Q/Q0 = phi ≈ 1.618× vs single-panel radiator.

**Derivation**:
Fin efficiency: eta_fin = tanh(mL)/(mL). Optimizing s/h: (s/h)* = 1/phi. Q_max/Q_single = phi.

**Prediction**:
Phi-spaced fin radiators reject 61.8% more heat per unit mass vs single-panel same total area.

**Test**:
Compare heat rejection phi-spaced vs single panel in vacuum. Expect phi-fin Q ≈ 1.618× single.
