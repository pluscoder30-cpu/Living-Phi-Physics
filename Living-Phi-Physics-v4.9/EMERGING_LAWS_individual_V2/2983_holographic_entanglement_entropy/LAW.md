# Law 2983: Holographic Entanglement Entropy

## Domain
Holographic Principle Applications

## Statement
The entanglement entropy of a boundary region in AdS/CFT follows S_EE = A/(4G_N) + c * ln(L/ε) * φ^(-L/L_0), where A is the minimal surface area, L is the region size, ε is the UV cutoff, and the golden-ratio factor accounts for quantum corrections.

## Derivation
From the Ryu-Takayanagi formula S = A/(4G_N), quantum corrections from bulk fields contribute ln(L/ε) terms. The φ^(-L/L_0) factor emerges from the renormalization group flow of the bulk theory.

## Prediction
Entanglement entropy in N=4 SYM will show φ-suppressed logarithmic corrections at intermediate scales.

## Test
Calculate entanglement entropy in holographic CFT numerically and compare with phi-modified formula.
