# PHI Slope Stability Factor

**Domain**: Geotechnical Engineering

**Statement**:
Slope FOS = FOS0*phi when failure surface follows phi-spiral geometry, providing 61.8% greater resistance vs circular.

**Derivation**:
Bishop FOS with phi-spiral: arc length increases by phi. Normal force increases by phi. FOS_spiral = FOS_circle*phi.

**Prediction**:
Phi-spiral FOS is 1.618× circular FOS for same slope.

**Test**:
Analyze 5 slopes with circular vs phi-spiral surfaces. Expect phi-spiral FOS ≈ 1.618× circular.
