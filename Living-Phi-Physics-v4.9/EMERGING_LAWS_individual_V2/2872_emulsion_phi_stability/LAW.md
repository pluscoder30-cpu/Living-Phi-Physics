# PHI Emulsion Stability Law

**Domain**: Food Science

**Statement**:
Emulsion stability maximized when droplet size distribution has phi-polydispersity DPI = d90/d10 = phi, balancing Ostwald ripening and coalescence.

**Derivation**:
Ostwald ripening rate ∝ 1/r, coalescence ∝ r^3. Total degradation minimum at DPI = phi.

**Prediction**:
Emulsions with DPI ≈ 1.618 have 61.8% longer half-life vs monodisperse (DPI=1).

**Test**:
Prepare O/W emulsions DPI=1, 1.618, 2. Track droplet size 30 days. Expect phi-emulsion minimum coalescence.
