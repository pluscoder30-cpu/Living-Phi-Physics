# PHI Weave Packing Density

**Domain**: Textile Engineering

**Statement**:
Maximum woven fabric packing occurs at thread diameter ratio d2/d1 = phi^-1, achieving packing fraction eta ≈ 0.952.

**Derivation**:
Circle packing: optimal binary mixture at radius ratio r2/r1 = 1/phi. Area fraction: eta = pi/(2*sqrt(3))*(1+1/phi^2) ≈ 0.952.

**Prediction**:
Fabrics with thread diameter ratio 1:0.618 have 95.2% surface coverage, 18% denser than equal-diameter weaving.

**Test**:
Weave fabrics with diameter ratios 1:0.5, 1:0.618, 1:0.75. Measure coverage by image analysis. Expect maximum at ≈ 0.618.
