# PHI Corrosion Rate Law

**Domain**: Marine Engineering

**Statement**:
Marine corrosion follows phi-modified power law R(t) = R0*(t/tau)^(1/phi) from fractal oxide layer geometry, predicting 61.8% slower long-term corrosion.

**Derivation**:
Oxide growth: dx/dt = k/x. With fractal: x_eff = x^(1/phi). Therefore x = (kt)^(phi/2). Rate: R = (phi/2)*k^(phi/2)*t^((phi-2)/2).

**Prediction**:
Fractal oxide layers grow as t^(1/phi), predicting 61.8% less material loss at t=tau vs linear models.

**Test**:
Measure oxide thickness on steel in seawater at 10 time points. Fit linear vs phi-power. Expect phi-power R² > 0.95.
