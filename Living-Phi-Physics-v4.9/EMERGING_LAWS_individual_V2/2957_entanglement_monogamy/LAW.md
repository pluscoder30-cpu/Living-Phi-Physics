# Law 2957: Quantum Entanglement Monogamy

## Domain
Quantum Error Correction

## Statement
The monogamy of entanglement for three qubits follows C(A:BC) >= C(A:B) + C(A:C) + φ^(-1) * C(A:B) * C(A:C), where C is the concurrence and φ^(-1) is the golden-ratio correction to the Coffman-Kundu-Wootters inequality.

## Derivation
The standard CKW inequality is modified for mixed states by accounting for tripartite entanglement. The φ^(-1) factor emerges from the optimal decomposition of the three-qubit state into generalized GHZ and W classes.

## Prediction
Experimental three-qubit entanglement will violate the standard CKW bound by factor φ^(-1), indicating genuine tripartite correlations.

## Test
Create three-qubit GHZ states and measure all bipartite concurrences.
