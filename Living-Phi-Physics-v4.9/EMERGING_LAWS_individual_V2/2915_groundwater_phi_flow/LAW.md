# PHI Groundwater Flow Pattern

**Domain**: Geotechnical Engineering

**Statement**:
Groundwater in phi-heterogeneous aquifers: v = -K0*phi^(-n)*grad(h), 61.8% reduced seepage vs homogeneous.

**Derivation**:
Darcy with K_eff = K0/phi^n. For n=1: v = -K0/phi*grad(h). Reduction = 1/phi.

**Prediction**:
Phi-layered aquifers have 38.2% lower seepage velocity.

**Test**:
Tracer test phi-layered vs homogeneous sand. Expect phi peak arrival ≈ 1.618× homogeneous.
