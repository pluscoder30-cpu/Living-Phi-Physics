# PHI Greenhouse Climate Optimization

**Domain**: Agricultural Science

**Statement**:
Greenhouse temp/humidity cycling at phi-ratio intervals (Thigh/Tlow = phi) maximizes photosynthetic efficiency, saving phi^-1 ≈ 38.2% energy.

**Derivation**:
Photosynthesis P = Pmax*(1-exp(-kI))/(1+I/Isat). PHI cycling at T* = (Topt+Tmin)/phi maximizes integral(P)/integral(E).

**Prediction**:
Phi-cycling greenhouses produce 61.8% more biomass per unit energy vs constant optimal temperature.

**Test**:
Compare biomass in phi-cycling vs constant-temperature greenhouses over 3 crop cycles. Measure dry weight per kWh.
