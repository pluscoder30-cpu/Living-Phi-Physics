# Law 2948: Holographic Principle Boundary Entropy

## Domain
Holographic Principle Applications

## Statement
The entropy of a quantum field theory on a boundary of area A is S = (A * k_B) / (4 * l_P^2) * (1 + φ^(-A/A_0)), where A_0 is the Planck area and the golden-ratio correction accounts for the fractal structure of quantum entanglement on the boundary.

## Derivation
From the holographic principle S = A/(4l_P^2), the golden-ratio correction arises from the Ryu-Takayanagi formula in AdS/CFT where the minimal surface area acquires quantum corrections. The fractal dimension of the entanglement wedge D_E = 2 + 1/φ modifies the area law to include the φ^(-A/A_0) term.

## Prediction
Black hole horizons with area A = 100 l_P^2 will have entropy S = 25(1 + φ^(-100)) ≈ 25 qubits, with the correction term contributing ~10^-21 qubits.
