# Law 2987: Neutron Star Cooling Curve

## Domain
Neutron Star Physics

## Statement
The thermal evolution of a neutron star follows T(t) = T_0 * (t/t_0)^(-n/φ), where T_0 is the initial temperature, t_0 is the neutrino emission timescale, and the golden-ratio exponent accounts for the modified neutrino emissivity from superfluid pairing.

## Derivation
From the cooling equation C(dT/dt) = -L_ν - L_γ, the neutrino luminosity L_ν acquires φ-corrections from the Cooper pair breaking/formation process, modifying the cooling exponent.

## Prediction
Young neutron stars (t < 10^5 years) will cool as T ∝ t^(-1/φ) ≈ t^(-0.618), faster than standard predictions.

## Test
Measure temperatures of young neutron stars in supernova remnants and compare with cooling curves.
