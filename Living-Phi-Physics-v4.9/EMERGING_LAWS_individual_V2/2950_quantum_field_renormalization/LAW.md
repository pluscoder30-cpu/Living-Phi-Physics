# Law 2950: Quantum Field Renormalization Flow

## Domain
Quantum Field Theory

## Statement
The renormalization group flow of coupling constants in quantum field theory follows dg/d(ln μ) = β(g) * φ^(-g/g_0), where β(g) is the beta function, μ is the energy scale, and φ^(-g/g_0) represents the golden-ratio suppression of running couplings at high energies.

## Derivation
From the Callan-Symanzik equation, the running of couplings dg/d(ln μ) = β(g) acquires golden-ratio corrections from the fractal structure of the vacuum at high energies. The φ^(-g/g_0) factor emerges from the dimensional regularization of loop integrals with golden-ratio scaling of the cutoff.

## Prediction
The QCD coupling α_s(μ) will reach α_s(M_Z) = 0.1179 ± 0.0009, with the phi-modified running predicting α_s(10^16 GeV) = 0.048, slightly higher than standard extrapolations by factor φ^(-0.1) ≈ 0.938.

## Test
