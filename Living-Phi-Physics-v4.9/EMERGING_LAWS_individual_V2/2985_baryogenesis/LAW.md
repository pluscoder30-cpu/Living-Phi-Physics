# Law 2985: Particle Physics Baryogenesis

## Domain
Particle Physics Predictions

## Statement
The baryon asymmetry of the universe generated by electroweak baryogenesis follows η = (Γ_L - Γ_R)/s * φ^(-T/T_EW), where Γ_L and Γ_R are left and right sphaleron rates, s is the entropy density, and the golden-ratio factor accounts for CP violation enhancement.

## Derivation
From the Sakharov conditions, baryogenesis requires CP violation, which in the Standard Model arises from the CKM phase. The φ^(-T/T_EW) factor emerges from the temperature dependence of the CP-violating phase near the electroweak phase transition.

## Prediction
The observed baryon asymmetry η ≈ 6 × 10^-10 can be explained by CP violation enhanced by factor φ^(T_EW/T) near the phase transition.

## Test
Calculate baryon asymmetry from electroweak baryogenesis with phi-modified CP violation.
