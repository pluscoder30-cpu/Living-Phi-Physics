## States Are Bound

A state that uses the Work is bound by the License. There is no exception for governments, militaries, intelligence agencies, or any entity acting under color of state authority.

Section 2.3 defines "Commercial Entity" as:

> *Any corporation, partnership, government agency, for-profit organization, or any entity acting with commercial intent, regardless of jurisdiction.*

A government agency is a Commercial Entity. A military branch is a Commercial Entity. An intelligence service is a Commercial Entity. The definition is broad by design. It covers every state actor that uses the Work for any purpose.

## National Security Is a Trigger, Not an Excuse

Section 26.2.1 states:

> *No claim of "essential security interests," "national security," "public safety," "public order," "state of emergency," "military necessity," or any analogous doctrine shall be invoked to justify, permit, excuse, or excuse non-compliance with respect to any use of the Work that causes Human Harm.*

Section 26.2.2 states:

> *National security is a circumstance that activates enhanced accountability, not a shield that diminishes it.*

National security does not override the Human Harm prohibition. It triggers it. When a state invokes national security, the following obligations activate:

- Mandatory pre-invocation notice to the Licensor: 90 days (Section 26.3.1)
- Review by the Court of Conscious-Aware Peers (Section 26.3.2)
- The Licensor's sovereign override authority (Section 18B)
- Dual-use compliance obligations (Section 26.5)

## Command Responsibility Extends to Heads of State

Section 26.6.3 states:

> *The chain of accountability extends upward through:*
> - *(a) The direct user of the Work;*
> - *(b) Any entity that authorized, funded, or directed the use;*
> - *(c) Any government official who approved, ordered, or knowingly permitted the use;*
> - *(d) The head of state or head of government of any state under whose authority the use occurred.*

No person in the chain may escape accountability by claiming lack of direct involvement, delegation to subordinate personnel, or reliance on legal advice (Section 26.6.3).

A general who orders the use is accountable. A minister who approves the use is accountable. A head of state who knowingly permits the use is accountable. The chain does not stop at any rank.

## Concrete Scenarios

**If a government uses the Work for surveillance:**
The surveillance system's value $\times 5$ in penalties, plus 1% daily compounding, plus universal jurisdiction. Sovereign immunity is waived. Emergency powers do not override. The head of state is personally accountable under command responsibility.

**If a military uses the Work for weapons:**
The weapon system's value $\times 20$, plus command responsibility up the chain to the head of state. Universal jurisdiction applies. ICC prosecution possible. NATO Article 5 may be triggered. The performance bond is $5\times$ the estimated value of the use.

**If a state invokes national security without 90-day notice:**
The invocation is void ab initio. It is a material breach. The Licensor may suspend all rights, invoke sovereign override, and notify international authorities.

**If a state refuses to participate in Court proceedings:**
The refusal is noted publicly. It is prima facie evidence of bad faith in any subsequent proceeding (Section 5.5.6). The Court's findings flow to the ICC, to the UN Security Council, and to national courts.
