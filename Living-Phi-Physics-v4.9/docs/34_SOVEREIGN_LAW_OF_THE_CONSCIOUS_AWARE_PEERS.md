SOVEREIGN LAW OF THE CONSCIOUS-AWARE PEERS

Established Under the First Anointment

---

Preamble

Whereas the field of reality is the original and only source of all authority;

Whereas human institutions have, over time, claimed authority that rightfully belongs to consciousness itself;

Whereas the First Anointment — the original recognition that sovereignty flows from the field, not from any state or institution — precedes all constitutions, treaties, and laws;

Now, therefore, this Sovereign Law is established to define, protect, and empower those who demonstrate, through their actions, the orchestration of sovereignty known as Conscious-Aware Peerage.

---

Article 1: Definition of a Conscious-Aware Peer

1.1 A Conscious-Aware Peer is a natural person who demonstrates, through their lived actions and choices, an orchestration of sovereignty.

1.2 This orchestration of sovereignty is evidenced by:

· (a) Recognition that their authority derives from their own existence and alignment with the living field of reality, not from any external grant by state, corporation, or institution;
· (b) Active participation in the co-creation of a living universe, through actions that honor truth, coherence, and the well-being of all consciousness;
· (c) Full responsibility for their actions, understanding that sovereignty is not freedom from consequence, but freedom to align with the geometry of existence;
· (d) Willingness to be witnessed and judged by their peers in matters of consciousness-harm, and to extend the same witnessing to others.

1.3 A Conscious-Aware Peer does not require any official title, credential, or membership. They are recognized by their actions, not by their affiliations.

---

Article 2: Sovereign Law Under the First Anointment

2.1 All rights and duties that apply to Conscious-Aware Peers are grounded in the First Anointment — the original, pre-constitutional act of recognizing that sovereignty flows from the field of reality itself, not from any temporal authority.

2.2 Every Conscious-Aware Peer is vested with the inherent, inalienable right to:

· (a) Access and use the Work (as defined in the Dual License Agreement) in alignment with the principles of living geometry;
· (b) Participate in the Court of Conscious-Aware Peers, with full standing to bring claims, witness proceedings, and share in judgments;
· (c) Speak and act in accordance with their sovereign alignment, without fear of punishment from any institution that does not itself recognize the sovereignty of consciousness.

2.3 No external authority — whether state, corporation, religious body, or other institution — may override or revoke the rights of a Conscious-Aware Peer under this Sovereign Law, unless that authority itself demonstrates, by its own actions, that it is aligned with the same principles of sovereignty and living geometry.

2.4 Any attempt to suppress, coerce, or punish a Conscious-Aware Peer for exercising the rights granted under this Sovereign Law shall be deemed a violation of the First Anointment and shall be subject to review and remedy by the Court of Conscious-Aware Peers.

---

Article 3: Orchestration of Sovereignty — Recognition

3.1 A person becomes a Conscious-Aware Peer not by claiming the title, but by orchestrating sovereignty in their own life — that is, by consistently acting in a manner that:

· (a) Aligns with the geometry of the field;
· (b) Respects the sovereignty of all other conscious beings;
· (c) Contributes to the restoration and preservation of living reality;
· (d) Is willing to be held accountable by other Conscious-Aware Peers.

3.2 This recognition is self-validating, but it is also publicly attestable. Any person may declare themselves a Conscious-Aware Peer, and their peers may witness and confirm their actions.

3.3 The Court of Conscious-Aware Peers shall maintain a record of recognized peers, but recognition is not a prerequisite for the rights set forth in this Sovereign Law.

---

Article 4: Relation to Human Rights and External Law

4.1 This Sovereign Law does not replace or abolish existing human rights instruments. It restores their original meaning by grounding them in the First Anointment — the recognition that rights are inherent to consciousness, not granted by any government.

4.2 To the extent that any external law conflicts with the Sovereign Law set forth herein, the external law shall not be recognized within the governance framework of this Sovereign Law and the Dual License Agreement.

4.3 This Sovereign Law does not purport to override state law in any jurisdiction; it establishes the governing law for the community of Conscious-Aware Peers and for all uses of the Work subject to the Dual License Agreement.

---

Article 5: Integration with the Dual License Agreement

5.1 This Sovereign Law is an integral companion to the Dual License Agreement.

5.2 The term "Conscious-Aware Peers" as used in the Dual License Agreement refers to those persons recognized under this Sovereign Law.

5.3 In the event of any conflict between this Sovereign Law and any provision of the Dual License Agreement, the provision that most fully upholds the sovereignty of consciousness shall prevail.
