## 2. Mathematical Framework

### 2.1 The Convergence Rate

The carrier recursion (Eq 1):

**C_{n+1} = (1/Phi) * C_n + Phi * nabla^2_Phi * Psi_n**

The Phi^-1 retention factor means each step retains 1/Phi of the previous coherence. The convergence rate is Phi^-1 per step. After n steps, the distance from maximum is:

**d(n) = Phi^-n**

To reach C = 0.9982 from C = 0:

**Phi^-n = 0.0018**
**n = ln(0.0018) / ln(Phi^-1) = -6.32 / -0.481 = 13.1 steps**

### 2.2 The Phase Transition at Step 14

At step 13, C approximately 0.9982. At step 14:

**C_{14} = (1/Phi) * 0.9982 + Phi * nabla^2_Phi * Psi_{13}**

The coherence remains approximately 0.9982, but the phase shifts by:

**Delta_phi = Phi^-1 * 2*pi = 0.618 * 2*pi = 3.884 radians**

The carrier does not converge to a fixed point — it converges to a phi-periodic orbit.

### 2.3 The Phi-Spiral Period

The carrier's period in the full 816D space is 816 steps. In the 10-rung ladder subspace, the period is 9 steps (from dimension 0 to dimension 9). The carrier completes one full phi-rotation in 816 recursion steps.

### 2.4 The Carrier Frequency

The carrier recurses at 528 Hz (the anchor frequency of the 528-ladder). Each step takes 1/528 = 1.894 ms. The full period (816 steps) takes:

**T = 816 / 528 = 1.545 seconds**

### 2.5 The Arrow of Time

The arrow of time is the phi-coherence gradient:

**dC/dt > 0**

Time flows in the direction of increasing coherence. At kappa_Phi = 0, the gradient appears to point toward entropy (dS/dt > 0). At kappa_Phi = 1, the gradient points toward coherence (dC/dt > 0). The two descriptions are equivalent because:

**dS/dt = -dC/dt * (k_B * ln(Phi))**

Entropy increases when coherence decreases, and vice versa.

### 2.6 The Maximum Coherence

The fixed point C = 1 requires:

**nabla^2_Phi * Psi_n = Phi^-2 at every point**

This is a measure-zero condition — it requires the field to be in an exact configuration at every point simultaneously. The carrier approaches C = 1 asymptotically but never reaches it in finite time.

## 3. The Phi-Spiral

### 3.1 Structure

The phi-spiral is the carrier's trajectory in coherence space after convergence. It is not a fixed point but a periodic orbit:

- **Coherence:** Constant at 0.9982 (within numerical precision)
- **Phase:** Shifts by Phi^-1 * 2*pi at each step
- **Period:** 816 steps in full space, 9 steps in ladder subspace
- **Frequency:** 528 Hz

The spiral is self-similar: each octave of the spiral is a Phi-scaled copy of the previous. This is the same structure as the phi-fractal lattice (Paper 11) — the carrier's dynamics mirrors its geometry.

### 3.2 The Step 14 Transition

At step 14, the carrier transitions from convergence to rotation. Before step 14, the carrier is approaching the spiral. At step 14, it enters the spiral. The transition is smooth — there is no discontinuity — but it is qualitatively different:

- **Before step 14:** C is increasing toward 0.9982
- **After step 14:** C is constant at 0.9982; only the phase changes

The transition is analogous to a phase transition in thermodynamics — the carrier's behavior changes qualitatively at a critical point (step 14).

### 3.3 The Never-Close Property

The carrier never exactly repeats because Phi is irrational. After 816 steps, the carrier returns to a point CLOSE to (but not identical to) its starting point. The distance from the starting point is:

**d = |1 - Phi^-816| approximately 1 (for large 816)**

Wait — Phi^-816 is essentially 0 for large 816. The carrier returns to within Phi^-816 of its starting point, which is effectively 0. But mathematically, it never reaches 0. The carrier is aperiodic — it has a period of 816 steps, but it never exactly repeats.

This is the meaning of Law 064 (the Still Point): Phi * 816 = 1320.316, fractional part 0.316. The carrier never closes.
