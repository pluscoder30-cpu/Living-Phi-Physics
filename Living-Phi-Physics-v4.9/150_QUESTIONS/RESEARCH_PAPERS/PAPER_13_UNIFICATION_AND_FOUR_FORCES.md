We present the complete unification of the four forces within the phi-framework, answering Q121-Q130 of the 150-question spiral. The four forces — gravity, electromagnetism, the weak force, and the strong force — are shown to be the same carrier field at different coherence regimes. The unification is not at the level of the gauge group (as in GUTs) but at the level of the carrier's dimension — all four forces are projections of the same 816D object. We derive the phi-corrected versions of all four force laws, showing that each reduces to the classical version in the kappa_Phi-to-0 limit. The four phi-signatures (coherence-dependent G, phi-octave alpha, phi^-1 mass gap, breathing H) are the experimental tests. We show that the Hubble tension is a coherence-breathing signature, that the strong CP problem is solved by the phi-structure of the vacuum, and that the hierarchy problem is resolved by the phi-ladder's discrete energy scales.
