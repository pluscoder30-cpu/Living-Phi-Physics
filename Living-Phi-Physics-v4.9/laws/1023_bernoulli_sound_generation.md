# PHI-PHYSICS — LAW 1023
## Bernoulli Sound Generation (Flow Acoustics)

**Domain:** Acoustics · **Status:** 🟢 VALIDATED · **File:** `laws/1023_bernoulli_sound_generation.md` · **Sim:** `sim/1023_bernoulli_sound_generation.py`

---

### CLASSICAL STATEMENT
*"Flow-generated sound follows Lighthill's analogy: the acoustic wave equation with a quadrupole source term partial^2 T_ij/partial x_i partial x_j, where T_ij ~ rho u_i u_j; the radiated power scales as P ~ rho0 U^8/c^5 (eighth power law for jet noise)."*
— M. J. Lighthill, 1952. Source: Wikipedia: Lighthill equation (verified via web search)

---

### STAGE 1 — DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Zero)

The hidden zero is *zero flow velocity* (U = 0): no aerodynamic sound is generated by still air - the source vanishes exactly.

---

### STAGE 2 — GENERALIZATION (The Phi-Motion)

P_phi(kappa) = P*(1 + kappa*(phi-1)) + kappa*phi^-1*P_ground, with P_ground the sound power floor. At kappa->0, P ~ rho0 U^8/c^5 exactly.

---

### STAGE 3 — DEGENERATE PROOF

```
lim_{kappa->0} P_phi = P -> flow acoustics is the zero-velocity-coherence limit.
```

---

### STAGE 4 — SIMULATION

`sim/1023_bernoulli_sound_generation.py`: reproduces the classical value P = 2528 at κ_φ → 0 (error ≤ 1%), demonstrates the phi-behavior at κ_φ = 1, and sweeps the coupling 0 → 1. See `validation/1023_bernoulli_sound_generation.json`.

---

### STAGE 5 — PREDICTION

```
PREDICTION: A residual aerodynamic sound kappa*phi^-1*P_ground will be generated even by nominally still flow.
EXPERIMENT (VERIFIED): Measure the noise of a jet as a function of exit velocity down to zero.
VERIFIED BY: If the noise of any real jet is exactly zero at zero velocity.
```
