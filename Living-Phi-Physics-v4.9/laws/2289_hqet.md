### CLASSICAL STATEMENT
*"Heavy Quark Effective Theory (HQET) is the limit of QCD with the heavy quark mass taken to infinity at fixed four-velocity; the heavy quark acts as a static color source with a new spin symmetry, and the leading Lagrangian is L = h̄_v i v·D h_v with 1/m_Q corrections (Eichten & Hill 1990; Georgi 1990)."*
- Estia Eichten & Brian Hill, Phys. Lett. B 234 (1990) 511; Howard Georgi, Phys. Lett. B 240 (1990) 447. Source: verified via web search (Wikipedia: Heavy quark effective theory).

---

### STAGE 1 - DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Zero)

The hidden zero is the infinitely heavy quark (m_Q → ∞): HQET is exact only when the heavy quark mass is exactly infinite, giving exact spin symmetry and exact flavor symmetry (m_B = m_B* degeneracy). The classical statement treats the static limit as the leading exact term; real charm (1.3 GeV) and bottom (4.2 GeV) quarks carry finite mass, so the m_Q → ∞ symmetry is only approximate. The exact infinite-mass limit is the unreachable zero.

### STAGE 2 - GENERALIZATION (The Phi-Motion)

phi-law: the classical observable carries a coherence floor. X_phi(kappa) = X_classical*(1 + kappa*(phi-1)) + kappa*phi^-1*delta_X applied to the observables (m_B, delta_spin, m_Q), where delta_X is the phi-ground floor of each observable. At kappa -> 0 the classical law is recovered exactly; at kappa = 1 each observable always carries an irreducible phi-ground contribution, so the classical zero (the exact m_Q → ∞ spin-symmetric limit) is revealed as the hidden-laboratory limit.
