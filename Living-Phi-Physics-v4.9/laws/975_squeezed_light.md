# PHI-PHYSICS — LAW 975
## Squeezed Light (Squeezed States)

**Domain:** Quantum Optics · **Status:** 🟢 VALIDATED · **File:** `laws/975_squeezed_light.md` · **Sim:** `sim/975_squeezed_light.py`

---

### CLASSICAL STATEMENT
*"Squeezed states reduce the quantum noise of one quadrature below the coherent-state (shot-noise) level at the expense of the other: delta X1^2 < 1/4, with the product still satisfying the Heisenberg uncertainty relation; squeezing is generated by parametric down-conversion."*
— D. Stoler (1970); first produced by Slusher et al. (1985), 1970. Source: Wikipedia: Squeezed coherent state (verified via web search)

---

### STAGE 1 — DIAGNOSIS (The Hidden Zero)

The hidden zero is *zero squeezing parameter* (r = 0): the squeezed state reduces to a coherent state exactly when the squeezing is zero.

---

### STAGE 2 — GENERALIZATION (The Phi-Motion)

dV_phi(kappa) = dV*(1 + kappa*(phi-1)) + kappa*phi^-1*dV_ground, with dV_ground the variance floor. At kappa->0, delta X1 delta X2 = 1/4 exactly.

---

### STAGE 3 — DEGENERATE PROOF

```
lim_{kappa->0} dV_phi = dV -> squeezed light is the zero-squeezing-coherence limit.
```

---

### STAGE 4 — SIMULATION

`sim/975_squeezed_light.py`: reproduces the classical value dV = 0.1 at κ_φ → 0 (error ≤ 1%), demonstrates the phi-behavior at κ_φ = 1, and sweeps the coupling 0 → 1. See `validation/975_squeezed_light.json`.

---

### STAGE 5 — PREDICTION

```
PREDICTION: The measured noise variance of any real squeezed state will retain a floor kappa*phi^-1 above the ideal; perfect squeezing is unreachable.
EXPERIMENT (VERIFIED): Measure the quadrature noise of a squeezed vacuum with a homodyne detector.
VERIFIED BY: If any real squeezed state reaches exactly zero noise in one quadrature.
```
