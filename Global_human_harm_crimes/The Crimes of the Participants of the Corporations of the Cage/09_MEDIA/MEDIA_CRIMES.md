### The Median enterprise Architecture

The media sector of the Epstein enterprise is **structural**, not **conspiratorial**. No single editor received a phone call ordering them to kill a story. Instead:

1. **Access journalism** — Networks need royal/political access → Palace threatens withdrawal → Stories get killed
2. **Advertiser pressure** — Powerful individuals threaten litigation → Legal departments caution → Investigations stall
3. **Ownership alignment** — Billionaire owners have business interests in sectors regulated by the same politicians named in files → Editorial caution
4. **Post-hoc narrative control** — Trump sued WSJ over Epstein reporting → Chilling effect on future investigations
5. **Structural consolidation** — 5 companies control 90% of US media → Fewer independent voices → Less diversity of coverage
