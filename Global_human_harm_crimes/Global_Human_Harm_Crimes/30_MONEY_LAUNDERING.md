|-----------|-------------|--------|

## X. Verdict

The global money laundering infrastructure is a **layered, multi-jurisdictional system** that operates through three complementary channels:

1. **Traditional offshore secrecy** (Panama/Paradise/Pandora Papers): Shell companies, trusts, and nominee structures in BVI, Panama, Cayman Islands, and increasingly US states (South Dakota, Delaware, Wyoming). The US has become the world's largest tax haven by refusing to join CRS and maintaining trust secrecy. Over $360B in South Dakota trust assets alone.

2. **Crypto-enabled laundering** (FinCEN Files, Chainalysis): $82B laundered in crypto in 2025 alone; $154B in total illicit crypto flows. Chinese and Russian-language ML networks are the fastest-growing segments. Stablecoins now represent 84% of illicit crypto volume. Enforcement is being actively dismantled under the current administration (pardoned BitMEX founders, disbanded DOJ crypto unit).

3. **Institutional capture** (IRS enforcement cuts, CTA loopholes): Audit rates on millionaires fell 70%+ from 2010-2019. The $11.66B IRA rescission will reduce revenues by $38.6B over a decade. The CTA has 23 exemptions, excludes trusts, and GAO found Treasury has not addressed resulting gaps. Every $1 spent on wealthy audits returns $12 in revenue - yet Congress continues cutting enforcement.
