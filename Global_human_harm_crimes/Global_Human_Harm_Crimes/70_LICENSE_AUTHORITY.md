## APPLICABLE FEDERAL STATUTES

### RICO (18 U.S.C. §§ 1961-1962)

The Racketeer Influenced and Corrupt Organizations Act provides the primary framework for prosecuting the enterprise.

**Enterprise Definition (§ 1961(4)):**
> "enterprise" includes any individual, partnership, corporation, association, or other legal entity, and any union or group of individuals associated in fact although not a legal entity

**Pattern of Racketeering Activity (§ 1961(5)):**
> "racketeering activity" means (A) any act or threat involving... bribery (18 U.S.C. § 201), extortion (18 U.S.C. § 1951), money laundering (18 U.S.C. § 1956), fraud (18 U.S.C. §§ 1341, 1343), or obstruction of justice (18 U.S.C. §§ 1503, 1512)
