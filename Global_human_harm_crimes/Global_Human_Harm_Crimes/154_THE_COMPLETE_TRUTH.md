### 5.1 The "Born Classified" Doctrine

The Atomic Energy Act of 1954 creates **Restricted Data (RD)** — information that is "born secret." > Restricted Data means all data concerning (1) design, manufacture, or utilization of atomic weapons; (2) the production of special nuclear material; or (3) the use of special nuclear material in the production of energy.

**Court Precedent — U.S. v. Progressive, Inc. (1979):** Even publicly-derived information, when synthesized to reveal nuclear weapons physics, constitutes Restricted Data.
