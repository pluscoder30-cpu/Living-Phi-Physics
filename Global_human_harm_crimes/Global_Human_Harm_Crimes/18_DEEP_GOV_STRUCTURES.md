## Executive Summary

Physics funding decisions flow through: Presidential budget request, OMB review, congressional appropriations subcommittee markup, full committee, floor vote, conference, agency discretion. Key decision-makers: ~40 appropriators (House/Senate E&W subcommittees), ~150 advisory committee members across DOE/NSF/NIH, and OSTP/OMB directors who set R&D priorities. The National Science Board was disbanded April 2026.

## 5. NSF - NATIONAL SCIENCE BOARD DISBANDED

### Pre-Disbandment
- 25 members, presidential appointment, 6-year terms
- Established by Congress in 1950
- Approved major awards, testified to Congress

### Termination (April 24, 2026)
All 22 members terminated by email. Reason cited: United States v. Arthrex (2021) - constitutional questions about non-Senate confirmed appointees.

### Current State

| Factor | Status |
|--------|--------|
| Director | None confirmed (Panchanathan resigned April 2025) |
| Nominee | Jim O'Neill (finance, Thiel Foundation - not a scientist) |
| NSB | Disbanded, no replacements announced |
| Advisory Committees | 12 disestablished (incl. Mathematical and Physical Sciences) |
| Grants Terminated | 1,700+ since 2025 |
| New Awards | ~50% of 2024 pace |
| Workforce | ~10% fired Feb. 2025 |

Key quote - former NSB Chair Victor McCrary: "We were told that those plans were solely going to be with NSF leadership... And leadership was told not to share this with anybody else, including the board."

Source: California Tech, May 19, 2026

## 8. REVOLVING DOOR

### Federal Restrictions
- Senators: 2-year ban on lobbying Congress
- House members: 1-year ban on lobbying Congress
- Executive branch: Lifetime ban on lobbying on issues personally worked on
- Penalties: Up to 5 years prison, $50K fine per violation

### Key Revolving Door Patterns in Physics Funding

| From Government | To Industry | Pattern |
|----------------|-------------|---------|
| DOE program managers | National labs (UT-Battelle, etc.) | Lab management contracts |
| NSF program directors | University administration | Grant leverage |
| OSTP officials | Tech sector (AI, quantum) | Policy-to-profit pipeline |
| Congressional staff | Lobbying firms | Appropriations knowledge |

Sources: 18 U.S.C. 207; OpenSecrets.org; Revolving Door Project (therevolvingdoorproject.org)

## 12. VERDICT

[VERIFIED] The deep government structure controlling physics funding has been significantly consolidated in 2025-2026. Key changes:

1. DOE replaced 6 specialized advisory committees (150+ members) with single 21-member SCAC with doubled industry representation
2. NSF disbanded its National Science Board (25 members) and 12 advisory committees including Mathematical and Physical Sciences
3. NSF has no confirmed Director; nominee is finance professional, not scientist
4. OMB directed NSF to withhold budget details from independent oversight board
5. Total federal R&D proposed to decline 6% ($10.7B cut)
6. 1,700+ NSF grants terminated, new awards at 50% of prior pace

The mechanism is not conspiracy but structural: independent expert oversight bodies were consolidated, disbanded, or bypassed, leaving physics funding decisions to fewer people with greater industry ties and less independent scrutiny.
