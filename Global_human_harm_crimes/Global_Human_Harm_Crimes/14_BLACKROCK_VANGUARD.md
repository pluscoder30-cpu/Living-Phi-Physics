## 8. Index Fund Ownership Structure

### How Index Funds Control Public Companies
- **Passive strategies**: 39% of top-500 AUM (2024)
- **Top 10 passive providers**: Captured 90%+ of net inflows over past decade
- **US index funds + ETFs**: Majority (52%) of long-term US fund assets at year-end 2025
- **Vanguard + BlackRock + State Street**: Together own 20-25% of virtually every S&P 500 company
- Source: Thinking Ahead Institute World 500 (2025); BCG Global Asset Management Report (2026)

### The "Common Ownership" Effect
- Three firms own significant stakes in competing companies simultaneously
- Example: Vanguard owns ~9% of Lockheed Martin AND ~9% of RTX AND ~9% of Northrop Grumman
- This structural ownership gives index fund managers proxy voting power over:
  - Corporate governance
  - Executive compensation
  - Environmental policies
  - Political spending
  - Shareholder proposals
- Source: Whoistheownerof.com defense analysis (May 2026)

## 11. Summary: The Financial Structure

### What Is Documented (Factual)
1. Four firms manage $39 trillion — 26.5% of global AUM
2. These same firms own 5-10% of every major US defense contractor
3. BlackRock has $16.6M in federal contracts (PBGC) and $73.5B across 151 contracts
4. BlackRock spent $2.84M on lobbying in 2024; 73% of lobbyists are former government officials
5. BlackRock PAC donated $837K in 2024 (bipartisan)
6. Pension funds (US and international) are major clients of these firms
7. Index fund ownership gives these firms proxy voting power over corporate governance

### What Requires Interpretation
- Whether this structure constitutes "control" or is simply a feature of modern capital markets
- Whether passive index investing creates anticompetitive effects
- Whether pension fund investments create conflicts of interest
- The appropriate role of ESG criteria in institutional investing
