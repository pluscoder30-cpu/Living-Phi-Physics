## FILE 24: 1700-1800 Open Questions

### Q1: Total value of Rothschild war financing during Napoleonic Wars

**Answer:** Partially recovered. In 1815 alone, the Rothschilds provided **9.8 million pounds** (approx. 910 million pounds in 2024) in subsidy loans to Britain's continental allies. In 1818, they arranged a **5 million pound** loan to the Prussian government. Total Rothschild war bonds across the 19th century totaled **11.5 million pounds**. The full cumulative total across all five branches during 1803-1815 remains classified/lost in family archives, but historians estimate the Rothschilds financed a substantial share of Britain's entire war effort, making them the most powerful banking family in Europe by 1820.

**Source:** Wikipedia - Rothschild family; Rothschild Archive; Niall Ferguson, *The House of Rothschild*

### Q3: Total African lives lost in slave voyages financed by documented banking entities

**Answer:** The Trans-Atlantic Slave Trade Database estimates **12.5 million Africans** were loaded onto slave ships between 1501-1866. Approximately **1.8 million (14.4%) died during the Middle Passage**. Historian Patrick Manning estimates **4 million died inside Africa after capture**. A complete banking-entity-specific death toll requires cross-referencing individual bank records with the slavevoyages.org voyage database -- a task not yet completed.

**Source:** slavevoyages.org estimates database; Wikipedia - Atlantic slave trade; NEH/Emory Trans-Atlantic Slave Trade Database; Patrick Manning, *The Slave Trade*

### Q4: How opium trade profits were specifically reinvested in British industrial development

**Answer:** The opium trade generated a triangular system: India grows opium, China pays silver for opium, silver used to purchase tea for Britain. Opium constituted up to **17% of British India's tax revenues** in the 19th century. Profits flowed through: (a) EIC tea trade financing -- opium profits financed Britain's tea demand, which became a working-class staple fueling industrial labor; (b) Banking capital -- Liverpool, Bristol, Manchester regional banks built on colonial trade wealth; (c) When cotton prices crashed in 1826, 5 London banks collapsed and the Bank of England rescued them with emergency loans -- demonstrating the direct link between colonial trade profits and British financial stability.

**Source:** BBC (Aug 2019); Britannica - opium trade; Bank of England Museum Slavery Guide; Economic History Association

### Q5: Connection between VOC dissolution and formation of modern Dutch East Indies exploitation

**Answer:** When the VOC was dissolved on December 31, 1799, its **assets and territories were nationalized as the Dutch East Indies** under direct state control. The Batavian Republic absorbed all colonial holdings, transitioning from corporate to governmental imperialism. The colonial infrastructure (forts, plantations, administrative systems, forced labor) was inherited intact by the Dutch state. Spice monopoly exploitation was replaced by broader plantation agriculture (sugar, rubber, coffee, tin). The forced labor cultuurstelsel system (1830-1870) extracted enormous wealth. Dutch colonial rule continued until Indonesian independence in 1945. The VOC's 200 years of extractive infrastructure became the foundation for 146 additional years of Dutch colonial exploitation.

**Source:** Wikipedia - Dutch East India Company; World History Encyclopedia; silencedhistory.org

### Q7: How did the ERO's 750,000+ family pedigrees get used after the office closed?

**Answer:** Chain of custody: (1) Dec 1939: ERO closed, materials put in storage, renamed Genetics Record Office; (2) 1944/1948: Records transferred to Charles Fremont Dight Institute at University of Minnesota -- **23,030 pounds** of material (43,500 family trait folders, 10,000+ pounds of index cards); (3) 1991: Dight Institute closed, material dispersed to Genealogical Society of Utah (filmed), Center for Human Genetics, and American Philosophical Society (non-genealogical); (4) Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory retained the core ERO Collection. The Dight Institute itself supported eugenic positions including possible support of Hitler's eugenics program.

**Source:** CSHL ArchivesSpace; CSHL Archives Blog; Wikipedia - ERO; American Philosophical Society; Embryo Project Encyclopedia

### Q8: What was the exact financial relationship between the Federal Reserve and J.P. Morgan & Co. during 1914-1918?

**Answer:** Deeply intertwined: (a) Morgan was Britain's sole purchasing agent (Jan 1915) and later France's, handling ~$10 million/day at peak; (b) Morgan underwrote ~$3.5 billion in war loans to London; (c) 8.3% commission yielded over $30 million in profits; (d) The NY Fed was designated Treasury's fiscal agent for Liberty Bond sales; the Fed lent to member banks at low rates for bond purchases; (e) In Nov 1916, Wilson ordered the Fed to warn against Allied bonds, threatening to collapse financing before a $500M Morgan bond issue; (f) Henry Davison (Morgan partner) designed the Fed at Jekyll Island (1910), then profited from it. The same banking families who created the Fed profited from the war lending it facilitated.

**Source:** Federal Reserve History; encyclopedia.1914-1918-online.net; Cambridge Business History Review; Chernow, *The House of Morgan*

### Q9: Did the Aldrich-Rockefeller family connection influence Fed design in ways not documented in public records?

**Answer:** The marriage connection is documented: Nelson Aldrich's daughter Abby Aldrich married John D. Rockefeller Jr. in 1901. Aldrich was the primary organizer of the Jekyll Island meeting (1910) and chaired the Senate Finance Committee. While public records show the Aldrich Plan closely resembled the final Federal Reserve Act, the private conversations between Aldrich, Warburg, Davison, and Vanderlip at Jekyll Island were by design secret. Vanderlip stated: "The results of the conference were entirely confidential. Even the fact that there had been a meeting was not permitted to become public." The family connection undeniably placed Rockefeller interests at the table where the Fed was designed, but no "smoking gun" document has surfaced showing explicit quid-pro-quo. The circumstantial evidence is strong: Rockefeller through Aldrich had input into the Fed's design, and Rockefeller banks later profited from the Fed's operations.

**Source:** Federal Reserve History; NPR Planet Money; Wikipedia - Abby Aldrich Rockefeller; Paul Warburg (1930 testimony); Frank Vanderlip (1935 Saturday Evening Post)

### Q10: What happened to the ERO field workers and their training methods after 1939?

**Answer:** 258 field workers were trained between 1910-1924 (primarily women with biology bachelor's degrees). After the ERO closed: (a) Many had already been placed as "eugenics specialists" at hospitals, asylums, and state-run institutions across the country; (b) Their training methods -- family pedigree collection, trait classification, institutional surveys -- were absorbed into mainstream genetics and social work; (c) Harry Laughlin (superintendent) was forced into retirement, became first president of the Pioneer Fund (1937-1941, classified as a hate group by SPLC), which funded distribution of Nazi propaganda film "Erbkrank"; (d) Laughlin died January 26, 1943 in Kirksville, Missouri; (e) The fieldworker methodology of institutional population surveys influenced later government census and public health practices.

**Source:** CSHL Archives; Embryo Project Encyclopedia; Wikipedia - ERO; Amy Sue Bix, "Experiences and Voices of Eugenics Field-Workers" (1997)
