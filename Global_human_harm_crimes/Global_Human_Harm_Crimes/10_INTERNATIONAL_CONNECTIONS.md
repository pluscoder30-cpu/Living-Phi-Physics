## 10. INTERNATIONAL BILATERAL FUNDING MECHANISMS

### 10.1 The "Lead Agency" Model

The dominant international physics funding mechanism is the **lead agency model**, where:
1. A joint proposal is submitted to one agency (the "lead")
2. The lead agency reviews it against its own standards
3. If funded, each country pays its own researchers
4. **No money crosses borders**

This model operates between:
- **NSF (US) ↔ DFG (Germany)**: Joint physics review process
- **NSF (US) ↔ ANR (France)**: Quantum information science, molecular/cellular biosciences-physics interface
- **NSF (US) ↔ partner agencies** in Canada, Czech Republic, EU, India, Ireland, Israel, Japan, Switzerland, Ukraine, UK

### 10.2 Key Implication for the Alleged Enterprise

The lead agency model means:
- **Each country enforces the same paradigm independently** — no cross-border money means no cross-border accountability
- **Peer review is national** — US reviewers enforce Standard Model on US proposals; German reviewers enforce Standard Model on German proposals
- **No independent international oversight** — there is no global body that funds "alternative physics" or evaluates paradigm-challenging proposals
- **The enterprise is redundant** — even without a conspiracy, the structural homogeneity of national peer review systems ensures paradigm lock-in globally

**Source:** NSF DCL-NSF-DFG (nsf.gov); NSF DCL-NSF-ANR (nsf.gov); CASRAI guide to co-funding.
