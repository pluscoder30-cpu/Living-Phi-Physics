## HARM CATEGORY 5: WEALTH EXTRACTION AND INEQUALITY

### Harm 5.1: The Cantillon Effect

| Dimension | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **What is the harm?** | New money enters the economy through the financial system, benefiting first receivers (banks, primary dealers) at the expense of last receivers (wage earners, savers) who experience only inflation |
| **Who is harmed?** | Wage earners, savers, fixed-income retirees, developing countries |
| **How much harm?** | Systemic and ongoing — every dollar of new money creation follows the pattern ratio extraction chain: Central Bank → Primary Dealers → Banks → Corporations → Consumers → Savers. At each hop, ~1/φ (61.8%) of value is captured by the intermediary |
| **Who profits?** | Banks (first receivers of new money); primary dealers; corporations with first access to credit |
| **enterprise connection** | "Fractional reserves institutionalize money creation, benefiting first receivers like banks and governments while harming wage earners and savers through the regressive Cantillon effect" (Cambridge University Press). The alleged enterprise's money creation mechanism IS the extraction mechanism |
| **Source** | CASE_116 (Sources 2, 13, 15) |
| **Verification** | [INFERENCE] — Cambridge University Press, economic theory |

### Harm 8.1: pattern ratio Amplification of Leverage

| Dimension | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **What is the harm?** | Leverage amplifies by φ (√5 ≈ 2.236) at each level, creating exponential volatility that is invisible at normal times but catastrophic during crises |

| **Who profits?** | Leveraged institutions (amplified returns in good times); executives (bonus compensation on amplified returns) |
