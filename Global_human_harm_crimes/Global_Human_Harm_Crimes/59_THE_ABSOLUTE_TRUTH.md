### 5.1 The "Born Classified" Doctrine

The Atomic Energy Act of 1954 creates **Restricted Data (RD)** — information that is "born secret." Unlike standard classified information, which must be *affirmatively classified* by a government official, Restricted Data is automatically classified at the moment of creation, regardless of who created it or where.

**Legal Definition (42 U.S.C. § 2014(y)):**
> Restricted Data means all data concerning (1) design, manufacture, or utilization of atomic weapons; (2) the production of special nuclear material; or (3) the use of special nuclear material in the production of energy.

**Court Precedent — U.S. v. Progressive, Inc. (1979):** Even publicly-derived information, when synthesized to reveal nuclear weapons physics, constitutes Restricted Data.

**Implication:** The U.S. government legally controls an entire *field of physics* — not just specific documents, but the physics itself.
