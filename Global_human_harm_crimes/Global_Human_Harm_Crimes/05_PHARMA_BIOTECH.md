## SECTION 6: THE FDA REVOLVING DOOR

### Scale of the Problem
- **9 out of 10 FDA commissioners** (2006-2019) moved to pharmaceutical companies
- **50%+ of FDA hematology-oncology reviewers** later worked for biopharma industry
- FDA officials involved in COVID-19 vaccine review later joined Moderna

**Source**: BMJ (2024), STAT News (2025), Science Magazine

### The PDUFA Problem
- **Prescription Drug User Fee Act (PDUFA)**: Drug manufacturers pay fees that fund FDA review
- Creates potential conflict: FDA may view pharma as "clients" rather than entities requiring oversight
- **65% of FDA drug review budget** comes from pharmaceutical industry

**Source**: Wisner Baum PR (May 2026), STAT News

### Key FDA Revolving Door Cases
- **Robert Califf** (FDA Commissioner 2016-2017, 2022-2025): Previously paid .7M by Verily (Alphabet)
- **Margaret Hamburg** (FDA Commissioner 2009-2015): Husband's hedge fund (Renaissance Technologies) invested in FDA-regulated companies during her tenure
- **Multiple FDA staff** moved to companies they previously regulated

**Source**: BMJ Investigation (2024), Stanford Law & Policy Review (2023)

## SECTION 7: SUPPRESSION OF ALTERNATIVE MEDICINE

### Regulatory Barriers
1. **FDA approval process**: Designed for pharmaceutical drugs (single molecules), not for complex natural therapies
2. **Patent system**: Natural substances cannot be patented, no profit incentive
3. **Clinical trial requirements**: Extremely expensive (.6B average per drug), prohibitive for natural therapies
4. **Licensing requirements**: Alternative practitioners face significant regulatory hurdles
5. **Public misinformation campaigns**: Natural remedies labeled as "quack" remedies

### Specific Suppressed Therapies (Documented Cases)
| Therapy | Status | Evidence |
|---------|--------|----------|
| Laetrile (amygdalin) | FDA banned | Decades of patient reports; FDA raids on clinics |
| High-dose IV Vitamin C | Limited use | Research shows promise for sepsis, cancer; not standard practice |
| Chelation therapy | FDA restricted | Used for heavy metal detox; FDA labeled "dangerous and ineffective" |
| PEMF therapy | Limited recognition | 2000+ peer-reviewed studies; not mainstream |
| UV blood irradiation | Abandoned | Was frontline treatment for infections; buried |

**Source**: BrightLearn.AI Chapter 4, GoldCare Blog (2025)

### Consumer Awareness
- **49% of Americans** believe in at least one medical conspiracy theory
- **36% believe** FDA prevents access to natural cures
- Those aware of conspiracies **3x more likely** to use herbal supplements
- **35% of believers** in conspiracies use herbal supplements vs. 13% of non-believers

**Source**: JAMA Internal Medicine (University of Chicago study, 2014)

### Medical Physics Research Under Threat
- **NIH funding for medical physics declined** in FY2025
- NCI (primary funder of medical physics) showed "pronounced decline" in new research awards
- Concerns about "long-term vitality and stability of the medical physics research pipeline"
- **Federal funding essential** to sustaining innovation in medical physics

**Source**: AAPM Working Group, arXiv paper (2601.07187)
