- The FDA stated it "does not know how many self-affirmed GRAS substances are on the market"

**Why this matters for the alleged enterprise:** The GRAS loophole allows the food industry to introduce new chemicals without independent review, while the FDA's drug approval process costs $2.6B per drug. The asymmetry means food chemicals face essentially zero regulatory barrier while alternative therapies face insurmountable ones.
