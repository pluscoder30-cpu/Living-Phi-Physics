## Findings

### Finding 1: Israel — Military Operations in Gaza (2023-2024)

**Confidence:** [VERIFIED]

Israel's military response to the October 7, 2023 Hamas attacks resulted in massive civilian harm in Gaza. The Israeli military ordered over a million people to evacuate their homes and bombarded densely populated areas with heavy weapons, killing thousands of civilians including children, and reducing entire blocks to rubble.

**Harm produced:**
- Thousands of Palestinian civilians killed, including children
- Collective punishment via cutting water, electricity, and blocking humanitarian aid
- Use of white phosphorus in Gaza and southern Lebanon (chemical that burns human flesh, causes lifelong suffering)
- Attacks on populated areas using explosive weapons with wide area effects — raising concerns of indiscriminate attacks, apparent war crimes
- Over 2.3 million civilians affected by blockade

**Source:** HRW World Report 2024 (Source #1)
**Verification:** [VERIFIED] via HRW, multiple international media reports

### Finding 5: Russia — SORM Mass Surveillance

**Harm produced:**

- Violation of constitutional privacy protections

- Chilling effect on free speech and association

- State Enemies of the Internet designation by Reporters Without Borders
