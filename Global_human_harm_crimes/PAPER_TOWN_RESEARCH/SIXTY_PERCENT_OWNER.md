# THE 60% OWNER OF LIQUID FUNDING LTD. — INVESTIGATION REPORT

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**There is no single 60% owner.** The 60% non-Bear-Stearns equity in Liquid Funding Ltd. was distributed across **11 holders of Class A Voting Units** in Liquid Funding Holdings, LLC (the Delaware parent). Bear Stearns bought out ALL 11 holders in March 2011 for approximately $265,303 — consolidating 100% control under what was by then JPMorgan Chase.

The 357 pages from Bermuda's Registrar of Companies (the Appleby filings) — **never subpoenaed** — would contain the Private Placement Memorandum with exact equity percentages for each holder. Without those pages, the precise ownership split within the 60% cannot be determined from public records alone.

## KEY FINDINGS

### 1. Epstein's Entity — Financial Trust Company, Inc.

- **Source:** EFTA00284566 (Membership Interest Purchase and Sale Agreement, March 24, 2011)
- Financial Trust Company, Inc. (6100 Red Hook Quarter, B-3, St. Thomas, USVI 00802) was Epstein's largest shell
- It held Class A Voting Units in Liquid Funding Holdings, LLC
- In March 2011, Financial Trust **sold** its units to Bear Stearns Liquid Funding Holdings Inc. for **$265,303**
- The purchase price was paid via JPMorgan account #Q30171005
- **This sale is the proof that Epstein did NOT own a controlling stake** — $265K for units in a $6.7B entity suggests a relatively small position

### 2. UNIQA Insurance Group — Austrian Connection

- **Source:** ORF (Austrian Broadcasting) investigation, April 14, 2026
- **German:** "Die restlichen 60 Prozent teilte sich eine illustre Schar an Investoren, darunter die Firma von Epstein, Financial Trust, Inc., und gleich zwei Gesellschaften der Uniqa-Versicherungsgruppe"
- **English:** "The remaining 60 percent was shared among a illustrious group of investors, including Epstein's firm Financial Trust, Inc., and **two entities of the Uniqa insurance group**"
- UNIQA confirmed: "Unsere damalige Beteiligung war — soweit rekonstruierbar — rein passiver Natur, ohne operative Rolle oder Einflussmöglichkeiten" ("Our participation at the time was — as far as can be reconstructed — purely passive, without operational role or influence")
- UNIQA invested less than $3.5 million by end of 2005
- Marcus Klug (director of Liquid Funding) was UNIQA's representative — later became board member of Bundespensionskasse AG (Austrian pension fund)
- Ernst Krehan (also director) was another Austrian connected to UNIQA

### 3. Bear Stearns 40% — SEC Filing

- **Source:** Bear Stearns Companies Inc. 10-K FY2002, Exhibit 13 (SEC EDGAR, February 28, 2003)
- "At November 30, 2002, the Company had an approximate 40% equity interest in Liquid Funding, Ltd. ('LFL'), a AAA-rated special purpose vehicle established to participate in the repurchase agreement and total return swap markets. A subsidiary of the Company acts as investment manager."
- Bear Stearns disclosed this position **exactly once** — in FY2002. Then 17 years of silence across 19 annual reports.

### 4. The Bear Stearns Buyout — March 2011

- **Source:** EFTA00284566, EFTA00295144 (Epstein Files)
- Bear Stearns Liquid Funding Holdings Inc. (383 Madison Avenue, NYC — JPMorgan's HQ) sent letters to all 11 holders on March 10, 2011
- The letter stated: "We refer to our letter of September 15, 2010, indicating our interest in purchasing all of the Class A Voting Units"
- **All holders were to sell 100% of their Class A Voting Units** — "Purchaser intends to acquire 100% of the other Class A Voting Units (the Other Acquisitions)"
- Closing date: March 31, 2011
- Financial Trust (Epstein) received $265,303 for its units
- Financial Trust's bank account: J.P. Morgan, ABA# 021000021

### 5. The "Mystery" — Wall Street on Parade

- **Source:** Wall Street on Parade, July 21, 2019 (Pam Martens & Russ Martens)
- "Just who it was that owned the remaining 60 percent of Liquid Funding Ltd. is unknown at this time"
- This statement was accurate in 2019, before the Epstein Files release in 2026

### 6. The Private Placement Memorandum — The Missing Key

- **Source:** OffshoreAlert, July 24, 2019
- OffshoreAlert obtained 357 pages from Bermuda's Registrar of Companies
- These pages include: Private Placement Memorandum, registration of mortgage charges, agreement for derivatives trading, liquidation notice
- The PPM would contain the **exact equity percentages** for each of the 11 holders
- **These pages were never subpoenaed by DOJ, SEC, or any law enforcement**
- OffshoreAlert sells these documents — they are the single most important unrecovered evidence

The Austrian insurance giant UNIQA was a passive investor. Financial Trust (Epstein) held a tiny position worth $265,303. The remaining holders — trusts, partnerships, and unknown beneficial owners — may include individuals or families whose identities, if revealed, would complete the picture of who actually controlled a $6.7 billion offshore vehicle chaired by Jeffrey Epstein from 2001 to 2007.

**The 357 Appleby pages are the Rosetta Stone of the Liquid Funding investigation. Without them, the precise ownership within the 60% remains partially opaque.**
