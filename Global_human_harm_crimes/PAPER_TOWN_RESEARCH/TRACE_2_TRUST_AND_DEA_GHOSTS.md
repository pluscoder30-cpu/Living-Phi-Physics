## PART I: THE 1953 TRUST — GRANTOR INVESTIGATION

### A. The Trust Document Evidence

The 1953 Trust is formally titled "THE 1953 TRUST" and was created on August 8, 2019 — two days before Epstein's death. The trust agreement explicitly states:

> "THIS TRUST AGREEMENT is made as of August 8, 2019 ('Agreement'), by and among **JEFFREY E. EPSTEIN, as Grantor** (the 'Grantor'), and DARREN K. INDYKE and RICHARD D. KAHN, as Trustees (the 'Trustees')."

**Source:** EFTA00099303 (DOJ Epstein Files)¹

The trust is an amendment and restatement of the "Jeffrey E. Epstein 2019 Trust dated January 18, 2019." The document states:

> "WHEREAS, Grantor, residing at Little St. James Island, St. Thomas, U.S. Virgin Islands established The Jeffrey E. Epstein 2019 Trust dated January 18, 2019, with Darren K. Indyke, and Richard D. Kahn, as Trustees; and WHEREAS, the Grantor retained the power to amend the trust in whole or in part..."

The trust was renamed: "Further, The Jeffrey E. Epstein 2019 Trust shall henceforth be known as the 'The 1953 Trust'."

The trust is named "1953" — which matches Epstein's birth year (January 20, 1953). However, the investigation files suggest this may not be coincidental:

### C. The Discrepancy Resolution

The trust document itself is unambiguous: **Epstein is the Grantor**. However, the investigation files raise legitimate questions:

1. **The 2-day-before-death amendment** — The trust was amended on August 8, 2019, increasing Indyke's bequest from $0 to $50M and Kahn's from $0 to $25M. The amendment was signed while Epstein was in federal custody.

3. **The naming convention** — "1953" could reference:
   - Epstein's birth year (most likely)
   - The year the trust was originally created (possible if predating Epstein's control)
