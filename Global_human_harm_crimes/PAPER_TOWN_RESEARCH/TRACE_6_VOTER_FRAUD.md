Delaware allows **corporations, LLCs, partnerships, and trusts to vote** in municipal elections if they own property in the municipality. There is no requirement that the entity have actual operations, employees, or physical presence — only property ownership.

### The One Person, One Vote Problem

The Supreme Court established in *Reynolds v. Sims* (1964) that "legislators represent people, not trees or acres. Legislators are elected by voters, not farms or cities or economic interests."

- **One shell company = one vote**
- **One human = one vote**
- **A shell company can be created in 24 hours for $50**
- **A human must be born, grow up, and reside in a place**
- **A shell company can be owned by anyone, anywhere**
- **A human must be a citizen and resident**
