## PART VI: THE REGISTERED AGENT ECOSYSTEM — A SYSTEMIC ANALYSIS

### Why Registered Agents Matter

Registered agents are the **front door** of every shell company. They:
1. Receive legal documents (lawsuits, subpoenas, government correspondence)
2. Forward those documents to the company's actual operators
3. Provide a legal address in the jurisdiction of incorporation
4. Create the appearance of legitimate corporate governance

In a legitimate business, the registered agent is a formality — a necessary legal appointment that has no bearing on the company's operations. In a shell company network, the registered agent becomes the **first line of defense** — the person who absorbs legal process while the true beneficial owner remains invisible.

### The Epstein Registered Agent Pattern

Epstein's registered agents followed a specific pattern:

1. **Individual attorneys** (Hodge) who had personal relationships and provided legal services beyond mere registration
2. **Law firms** (Kellerhals Ferguson) that incorporated entities, provided ongoing legal counsel, and served as registered agents
