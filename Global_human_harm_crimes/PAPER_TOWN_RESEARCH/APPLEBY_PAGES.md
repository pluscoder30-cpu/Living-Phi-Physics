## WHAT IS KNOWN ABOUT THE 60% OWNERSHIP

### Confirmed: Bear Stearns = 40%
- **Primary Source:** Bear Stearns Companies Inc FY2002 10-K, Exhibit 13, filed February 28, 2003
- **Quote:** "At November 30, 2002, the Company had an approximate 40% equity interest in Liquid Funding, Ltd. ('LFL'), a AAA-rated special purpose vehicle established to participate in the repurchase agreement and total return swap markets. A subsidiary of the Company acts as investment manager."
- **SEC EDGAR:** Accession 0000914121-03-000238

### The Remaining 60%
The 60% was held through **Liquid Funding Holdings, LLC** — a Delaware entity. The LLC had **Class A Voting Units** held by **11 holders** (per Pete Lincoln / Substack analysis). The exact list of all 11 holders and their percentages is IN the Private Placement Memorandum within the 357 pages.
