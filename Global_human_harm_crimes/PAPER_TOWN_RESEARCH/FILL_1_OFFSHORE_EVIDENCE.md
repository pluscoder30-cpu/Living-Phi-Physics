## 3. LIECHTENSTEIN â€” The Stiftung Haven

### Overview of Liechtenstein Trusts
Liechtenstein offers two primary vehicles for offshore asset protection:

## 5. COOK ISLANDS â€” The Asset Protection Fortress

### Why Cook Islands
- **International Trusts Act 1984**: Created the modern asset-protection trust
- Cook Islands courts **generally do not recognize or enforce foreign court judgments** against trust assets
- Creditors must bring a new proceeding in Cook Islands under Cook Islands law
- No creditor is known to have recovered assets from a Cook Islands trust through Cook Islands proceedings
- The settlor can be a discretionary beneficiary without undermining asset protection
- Self-settled asset protection trusts expressly authorized by statute

### Epstein's Cook Islands Connection
- **Reed Family Irrevocable Trust**: Registered in Cook Islands (June 3, 1993), linked to "Epstein & Reed" intermediary in ICIJ Offshore Leaks Database â€” Status: Struck/Defunct/Deregistered
- The "Epstein & Reed" intermediary was logged in the original ICIJ database from Portcullis TrustNet
- **Note**: This "Epstein" in the Offshore Leaks database may not be Jeffrey Epstein â€” the entry notes it is "not an offshore entity even though it was logged as such in original database" but rather "an internal account created by the agent to record miscellaneous charges of an officer or intermediary"
- However, the Cook Islands connection through the Reed Family Irrevocable Trust (1993) and Spitfire Trading Company (BVI, 1995) warrants further investigation

### Cook Islands Trust Mechanics
- Creditor must prove settlor's **principal intent was to defraud that specific creditor** (not creditors generally)
- One-year statute of limitations for fraudulent transfer claims
- Assets remain fully visible to tax authorities (transparency-compliant, not secrecy jurisdiction)
