## KEY INSIGHT: THE PAPER TOWN AS LAUNDERING MACHINE

The money flow architecture reveals that the paper towns are not just holding structures — they are **laundering machines**. Money flows in from legitimate sources (auction houses, investors, banks), passes through layers of shell companies and trusts, and emerges as:
- Tax-exempt income (USVI EDC fraud: $300+ million)
- Investment distributions (Southern Financial → downstream funds)
- Victim payments (Butterfly Trust → co-conspirators)
- Estate distributions (1953 Trust → 43 beneficiaries)
- Crypto investments (Kyara LLCs → Blockstream, Wearality)

The paper towns transform dirty money into clean money through volume, complexity, and jurisdictional arbitrage. The $6.8 billion in financial records is not evidence of legitimate business — it is evidence of the laundering process itself.
