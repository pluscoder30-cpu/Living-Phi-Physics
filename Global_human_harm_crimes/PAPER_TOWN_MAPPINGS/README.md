# PAPER TOWN MAPPINGS

## The Architecture of Legal Fictions

A **paper town** is a legal fiction — an entity that exists on paper but has no physical presence, no real employees, and no legitimate business operations. Shell companies create paper addresses. Paper addresses create paper residents. Paper residents create paper voters. Paper voters create paper elections. This is how you hack a system without touching a machine: you hack the paper.

Jeffrey Epstein operated a **70+ entity paper town network** spanning 6 jurisdictions (USVI, Bermuda, Delaware, New York, Florida, Cook Islands). The architecture concealed **$6.8 billion** in forensic financial records, generated **$300+ million** in fraudulent tax exemptions, and processed **4,725 suspicious wire transactions** totaling **$1.08 billion**. Zero criminal prosecutions for the financial architecture.
