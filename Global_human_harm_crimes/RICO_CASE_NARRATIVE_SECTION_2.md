**Case Reference:** 18 U.S.C. §§ 1961-1968

**Date:** September 8, 2026

---

The Enterprise's financial architecture was not incidental to its criminal operations — it was the operational backbone. From at least 1998 through August 2019, the Enterprise maintained a multilayered network of shell companies, trust structures, and banking relationships spanning six jurisdictions, through which it laundered the proceeds of sex trafficking, facilitated wire fraud, defrauded financial institutions, committed securities fraud, and evaded federal taxes. The financial evidence documents $6.8 billion in forensic financial records across 2,385 documented flows and 686 entities, with $2.816 billion in flagged suspicious transactions across six major banks — yet zero criminal prosecutions have resulted.

## 2.1 Wire Fraud (18 U.S.C. § 1343)

### The Enterprise's Wire Communication Infrastructure

The Enterprise used wire communications — electronic fund transfers, SWIFT messages, and interbank instructions — to execute a scheme to defraud. Wire transfers were the primary mechanism for moving trafficking proceeds through the financial system, disguising the source and nature of funds, and evading reporting requirements.

**The September 2019 JPMorgan Chase SAR — The Smoking Gun:**

On September 26, 2019, JPMorgan Chase filed a Suspicious Activity Report covering **4,725 wire transactions totaling $1,081,819,653** for the period October 2003 through July 2019. *See* BANKING_CRIMES.md, § 3 ("The 2019 SAR — The Smoking Gun"). This SAR was filed *after* Epstein's arrest and death — retroactively encompassing the entire active period of the trafficking enterprise. Named in the SAR: Leon Black, Glenn Dubin, Alan Dershowitz, Leslie Wexner trusts, and Jeffrey A. Schantz. *Id.* Transfers to Russian banks Alfa Bank and Sberbank were flagged. *Id.*

This filing constitutes the single most probative piece of wire fraud evidence in the case. The SAR demonstrates that JPMorgan processed 4,725 suspicious wire transactions over sixteen years without filing the reports required by federal law. The Enterprise used these wires to move funds between shell company accounts, pay co-conspirators, fund trafficking operations, and enrich the principals of the Enterprise.

## 2.3 Bank Fraud (18 U.S.C. § 1344)

### The Enterprise Defrauded Financial Institutions

The Enterprise's banking relationships constituted a scheme to defraud the institutions themselves. By concealing the nature and source of funds, the Enterprise caused banks to process transactions that violated their own compliance policies and federal law — exposing the banks to regulatory liability and reputational harm.

**JPMorgan Chase — The Pattern of Know, Ignore, Profit, Deny:**

JPMorgan Chase maintained 134 accounts for the Enterprise: 6 under Epstein's name, 59 for Epstein entities, and 69 for associates (Maxwell, Kahn, Indyke, Beller, Marcinkova). *See* BANKING_CRIMES.md, § 3 ("Key Findings — Wyden Senate Report"). The bank designated Epstein as "high-risk" in 2008 — yet continued the relationship until 2013. *Id.*

**Deutsche Bank — Compliance Officer Termination:**

Deutsche Bank's internal memos emphasized profitability while compliance officers who raised concerns were terminated. *See* BANKING_CRIMES.md, § 3 ("Deutsche Bank — SAR Failures"). **Bank of America — The Leon Black Pipeline:**

Bank of America processed **$170 million** in wire transfers from Leon Black to Epstein between 2012 and 2017, "often in $10 or $20 million installments, without asking any questions, even though it was obvious that the money was being used for nefarious purposes." *See* BANKING_CRIMES.md, § 3 ("Bank of America — SAR Failures") (quoting Senator Ron Wyden). Bank of America filed its first SAR on the Black payments in **February 2020** — six months after Epstein's death. *Id.* The bank settled for **$72.5 million** in March 2026. *Id.,* § 8.

**Bear Stearns — The Liquid Funding Fraud:**

Bear Stearns' relationship with Epstein dated to 1976, when Alan "Ace" Greenberg hired Epstein despite his having no college degree and a fraudulent résumé. *See* BANKING_CRIMES.md, § 4 ("Bear Stearns — Named Individuals"). **HSBC — The Only Bank to Act Before the Plea:**

HSBC France closed Epstein's account on December 21, 2007 — **before** Epstein's 2008 guilty plea. *See* BANKING_CRIMES.md, § 6 ("Timeline"). HSBC was the only institution to terminate the relationship before the plea. Every other bank continued the relationship through and after the plea. *Id.*

The Enterprise's investment activities constituted securities fraud through misrepresentation of beneficial ownership, failure to disclose material relationships, and use of shell companies to obscure the source of investment capital.

**Boothbay Fund — Undisclosed Founding Investor:**

## 2.5 Tax Fraud (26 U.S.C. § 7201)

### The USVI EDC Fraud

Southern Trust Company received **$73.6 million in fraudulent tax benefits** under the USVI Economic Development Commission program. *See* OFFSHORE_CRIMES.md, § 1. The entity applied for EDC benefits in October 2012, claiming "biomedical and financial informatics" consulting. *Id.* Between 2013 and 2017, Southern Trust reported aggregate net income of $656 million while employing only 13 individuals. *Id.* For every $1 of tax breaks, USVI received only pennies in economic benefits — cost/benefit ratios consistently failed to meet EDC thresholds. *Id.* Governor de Jongh approved the benefits while his wife served as office manager for Epstein's companies. *Id.* No EDC officials have been investigated or prosecuted.

### The Leon Black Tax Fraud

Epstein received $158 million from Leon Black between 2012 and 2017 for "tax and estate planning advice." *See* INVESTMENT_CRIMES.md, § 2.1 ("Apollo Global Management"). The Wyden Senate Investigation found that Epstein helped Black avoid "more than $1 billion in federal gift and estate taxes" and that Epstein's work "did not hold up under scrutiny" per Black's own attorneys. *Id.* The payments were deducted as business expenses. *Id.* The IRS never audited the $158 million in payments or the tax structures Epstein designed. *Id.,* § 7.1 ("Criminal Referrals Required"). This constitutes tax fraud facilitation — the design and implementation of tax avoidance structures that lack economic substance and are designed solely to evade tax liability.
