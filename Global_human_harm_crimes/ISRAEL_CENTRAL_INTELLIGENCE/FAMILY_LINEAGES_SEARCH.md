## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This document catalogs publicly documented connections between prominent wealthy families and the Epstein network. Every claim cites a specific public record source. The research covers 10 family lineages, their corporate entities, foundation structures, offshore holdings, and documented relationships to Jeffrey Epstein and/or the "Mega Group" — a secretive consortium of approximately 20-50 wealthy Jewish businessmen co-founded in 1991 by Les Wexner and Charles Bronfman.

**Key Finding:** The Mega Group (also called the "Study Group") connects multiple family lineages through a single organizational structure. FBI documents from 2019 explicitly tracked this group's connection to Epstein. The FBI's own files include a printed Wikipedia page about the Mega Group in Les Wexner's investigative dossier.
