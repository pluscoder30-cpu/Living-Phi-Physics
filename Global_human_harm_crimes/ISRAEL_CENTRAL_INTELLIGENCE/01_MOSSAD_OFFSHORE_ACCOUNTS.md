## I. THE ORA SLUSH FUND (1980s-1990s)

### Primary Source: Ari Ben-Menashe, "Profits of War" (1992)

The most documented Mossad offshore financial operation was the **Ora slush fund**, established from covert arms sales to Iran during the Iran-Iraq War.

**Structure:**
- **Holding group name:** Ora ("light" in Hebrew) — the only name never changed
- **Accounts:** 200+ numbered accounts across European banks
- **Paper companies:** Annual rotation of company names; only Ora remained constant
- **Annual refresh:** Account numbers and company names changed yearly; Ora holding group persisted

**Three main purposes of the slush fund:**
1. **Black operations financing** — Funding Israeli-controlled "Palestinian terrorists" who committed crimes in the name of the Palestinian revolution but were actually unwitting pawns in Israeli propaganda operations
2. **West Bank settlement financing** — Tens of millions of dollars used to build foundations for new Jewish settlements and purchase land from Arabs, circumventing US aid restrictions that prohibited use of funds for West Bank construction
3. **Intelligence community black operations** — Including the Achille Lauro operation (1985) and the Hindawi affair (1986) to discredit Syria

**Key operational details:**
- By 1983, the slush fund was "running like a well-oiled machine"
- Trading companies with various names handled contacts with Iranian weapons manufacturers and brokers
- David Kimche (Mossad spymaster) dealt directly with CIA's Robert Gates on implementation
- The fund operated under a general cooperation agreement secretly reached between PM Begin and CIA Director William Casey in August 1980

**Sources:**
- Ari Ben-Menashe, *Profits of War* (1992), Chapter 8
- Wikipedia: "Israeli support for Iran during the Iran-Iraq War"
- Washington Post: "From Langley to Tel Aviv" (1991)
