### 3.2 Cell-Site Simulator Technology [V]

**Product:** FalcoNet — IMSI-catcher/cell-site simulator
**Alternative Names:** Stingray, cell-site simulator, IMSI catcher

**How Cell-Site Simulators Work:**
1. Device transmits as a fake cell tower
2. Nearby phones connect to it (thinking it's a real tower)
3. Device collects IMSI (International Mobile Subscriber Identity) and IMEI (International Mobile Equipment Identity)
4. Can track location of phones
5. Can intercept communications content (text messages, calls)
6. Collects data from ALL phones in range (not just target)

**Legal Framework:**
- DOJ Policy (2015): Requires warrant supported by probable cause
- DHS Policy Directive 047-02 (2015): Similar requirements
- Pen Register Statute (18 U.S.C. § 3121 et seq.): Governs use
- **Problem:** Multiple agencies found in violation of warrant requirements

**Source:** ACLU (2015), DOJ Policy (2015), DHS Policy Directive 047-02
