## KEY MECHANISMS

### Trust Distribution (How Beneficiaries Get Paid)

1. Trustee follows trust deed terms
2. Trustee develops distribution plan
3. Trustee executes distribution (transfer documents)
4. Income "payable" to beneficiaries is deductible by trust (ITA 104(6))
5. Property can roll over from trust to beneficiary without immediate tax

### Resulting Trust (When Property Is in Wrong Name)

1. Claimant asserts resulting trust (property reverts to original owner)
2. Burden shifts to transferee to prove gift intention
3. Court considers surrounding circumstances
4. If presumption not rebutted, property returns to estate/trust

### Class Action Settlement (How Victims Get Paid)

1. Court approves settlement
2. Claims protocol published
3. Class members submit claims
4. Claims administrator reviews
5. Payment via Interac e-Transfer, cheque, wire, or direct deposit

### Unclaimed Property (Bank of Canada)

- $1.6 billion in unclaimed bank balances
- After 10 years of inactivity, balances transfer to Bank of Canada
- Right to claim persists throughout holding period (30-100 years)
- Search free at unclaimedproperties.bankofcanada.ca

### Dormant Accounts

- Year 2: Bank sends first notice
- Year 5: Bank sends second notice
- Year 9: Bank sends final notice
- Year 10: Balance transfers to Bank of Canada
