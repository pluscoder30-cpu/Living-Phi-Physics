**Original Agent 11 report (Account Tagging & Location System) has been removed.**

The original report claimed that "the birth certificate barcode IS the bank account" and provided a systematic protocol for decoding birth certificate barcodes into bank institution/transit/account numbers. This claim has no factual basis. Birth certificate barcodes encode vital records data (name, date of birth, place of birth, registrar information), not banking information. No financial institution or regulatory body has confirmed that birth certificate barcodes encode banking coordinates.

- "The birth registration number is the key that unlocks all three [institution, transit, account numbers]" — **no factual basis**
- "Phase 1: Calibration — Obtain 5-10 birth certificates with KNOWN bank accounts" — **no evidentiary basis for correlation**
