## 2. Key Statutes and Regulations Cited

### 2.1 46 CFR Part 67 — Vessel Documentation

**Mainstream:** Governs the documentation and registration of vessels under U.S. flag. Establishes the vessel's nationality, ownership chain, and lien priority.

**Alternative claim:** The ALL-CAPS PERSON is treated as a movable maritime commercial entity. The birth certificate functions as a "Certificate of Documentation" — the vessel's registration with the flag state (the U.S. government). The name on the certificate IS the vessel name; the official number (SSN) parallels a vessel's official number; the title chain = vital records.

**Reality:** 46 CFR 67 applies exclusively to watercraft (vessels). It contains no provisions for human beings, birth certificates, or trusts created from birth registration. No court has ever accepted this analogy as legally operative.

### 2.2 31 CFR Part 363 — Treasury Book-Entry Securities

**Mainstream:** Governs how the U.S. Treasury maintains book-entry securities (Treasury bills, notes, bonds). Defines how Treasury maintains records of beneficial ownership.

**Alternative claim:** 31 CFR 363 "standardizes how the PERSON is treated as a custodial financial account." Everything downstream — IRS enforcement, court bonds, CUSIP assignment, child support intercepts, FRB credit creation — becomes possible because of this standardization.

**Reality:** 31 CFR 363 governs Treasury securities (bills, notes, bonds), not people. It does not create accounts named after individuals from birth certificates.

### 2.3 CSA 2001 (Civil Service Act / Companies Act)

The reference to "CSA 2001" varies by jurisdiction. In the US context, alternative researchers sometimes reference the **UCC Revised Article 9** (effective July 1, 2001), which required re-filing of UCC-1 financing statements in the state of birth by June 30, 2002.

**Alternative claim:** Under revised Article 9, the UCC-1 secured party filing had to be refiled in the debtor's state of birth. Failure to refile resulted in loss of original filing date and secured party status.

### 2.4 18 USC §9 — Definition of "Vessel"

**Mainstream:** 18 U.S.C. §9 defines "vessel" for federal criminal law purposes as "every description of watercraft or other artificial contrivance used, or capable of being used, as a means of transportation on water."

**Alternative claim:** This definition is broader than it appears and encompasses any "artificial contrivance" — including legal persons created through birth registration.

**Reality:** The statute explicitly references watercraft and transportation on water. No federal court has accepted the argument that a legal person constitutes a "vessel" under this definition.

### 2.5 Sheppard-Towner Maternity Act (1921)

**Alternative claim:** This Act forced all births to be registered with the state "under the excuse that it was for health benefits for the people." This is presented as the mechanism that began the systematic registration of births as commercial events.

**Reality:** The Sheppard-Towner Act provided federal funding for maternal and infant healthcare. It did not mandate birth registration (which was already state-level practice). It was repealed in 1929.

### 2.6 HJR-192 (1933)

**Alternative claim:** House Joint Resolution 192 took the U.S. off the gold standard and effectively declared bankruptcy. This switched the country from common law to maritime admiralty law because citizens could no longer pay with real money (gold/silver), only Federal Reserve Notes (negotiable instruments in admiralty jurisdiction).

**Reality:** HJR-192 suspended the gold standard temporarily. It did not create admiralty jurisdiction over citizens, nor did it convert people into vessels or create trusts from birth certificates.

## 3. The Cestui Que Vie Act 1666

### 3.1 What the Act Actually Says

The Cestui Que Vie Act 1666 (18 & 19 Cha. 2 c. 11) is a real English statute, still on the statute books. Its full title:

> "An Act for Redresse of Inconveniencies by want of Proofe of the Deceases of Persons beyond the Seas or absenting themselves, upon whose Lives Estates doe depend."

**Section I:** If a person for whose life an estate was granted remains beyond the seas or absent themselves for seven years, and no sufficient proof of their lives is provided, the judge in any action shall direct the jury to give their verdict as if the person were dead.

**Section IV:** If the supposed dead person proves to be alive, the title is revested and they can recover mean profits with interest.

### 3.2 Historical Context

- Enacted during the Great Plague (1665) and Great Fire of London (1666)
- Massive population displacement and death made it impossible to prove whether absent persons were alive or dead
- Land owners holding life estates based on absent persons' lives couldn't recover their property
- The Act solved a specific property law problem: land tenure when the measuring life disappears

### 3.3 Modern Legal Status in the UK

- **Still on the statute books** — legislation.gov.uk shows no outstanding effects
- **Superseded in practice** by the Presumption of Death Act 2013 (fully in force October 1, 2014)
- The 2013 Act created a single comprehensive procedure covering property, finances, and marriage/civil partnership
- The original 1666 Act dealt ONLY with land tenure in life estates — it did NOT:
  - Create trusts over people
  - Assign financial value to birth certificates
  - Relate to admiralty law, the Federal Reserve, or personal sovereignty
  - Create a mechanism for "declaring people dead at birth"

### 3.4 Modern Legal Status in the US

- The seven-year absence presumption of death was adopted into various US codes:
  - **38 USC §108** — Seven-year absence presumption of death (veterans' benefits)
  - **43 CFR 30.124** — When a judge may presume the death of an heir
  - **New York EPTL 2-1.7** — Presumption of death from absence
  - **Social Security POMS §GN 00304.050** — Presumption of death of a missing person
- The presumption is always **rebuttable** — evidence the person is alive defeats it
- The Act has NEVER been used in US courts to create a trust from birth registration

### 3.5 Court Rejection of the CQV Trust Theory

The most recent and comprehensive rejection:

**Doughty v. [Defendants], D. Maine, No. 1:25-cv-00400 (2025)**
- Plaintiff claimed "Settlement of the Cestui Que Vie Trust and Estate"
- Magistrate Judge recommended dismissal for lack of subject matter jurisdiction
- Finding: "the Cestui Que Vie Act of 1666, or a 'cestui que vie' trust" was used "as support for their arguments in court" — rejected as "legal fiction... not based in law but in the fantasies of the sovereign citizen movement"
- **Date:** August 21, 2025 — one of the most recent federal court rejections

**Webb v. People of the State of Kentucky, W.D. Ky., No. 3:2025cv00492 (2026)**
- Court identified CQV trust arguments as a "hallmark" of sovereign citizen filings
- Case dismissed

## 4. The Remedy Pathways: Detailed Analysis

### 4.1 Notice of Live Birth (Steve Counsell)

**Proponent:** Steve Counsell — UK-based researcher and content creator

**Claimed mechanism:**
- The state registers your birth and creates a legal fiction (strawman)
- By filing a "Notice of Live Birth," you provide notice to the state that you are a living being, not the legal fiction
- This allegedly separates the living man/woman from the ALL-CAPS PERSON
- Once separated, you can claim the trust beneficial interest

**Practical steps (as described by Counsell):**
1. Obtain a certified copy of the birth certificate
2. Create a Notice of Live Birth document
3. File the notice with relevant government offices
4. Maintain the distinction between the living being and the legal fiction in all interactions

**Court outcome:** No documented court success. The Notice of Live Birth has no legal standing in any jurisdiction. Courts treat it as a sovereign citizen filing.

### 4.2 Conditional Acceptance + Accepted for Value (A4V)

**Proponents:** Doug Riddle (2008 viral Talkshoe recordings), Winston Shrout, various "Patriot" researchers

**Claimed mechanism:**
1. Receive a claim (tax bill, citation, debt notice) addressed to the ALL-CAPS NAME
2. The ALL-CAPS NAME is the debtor (strawman) — you are the secured party creditor
3. Write "Accepted for Value, Returned for Value, Exempt From Levy" on the instrument
4. Send it to the IRS (Stop 4440, Ogden, Utah) or other creditor
5. The creditor allegedly accepts it and discharges the debt against the Treasury account
6. The balance is adjusted to zero

**The Doug Riddle Method (2008):**
- Write: "Accepted for Value Returned for Value Exempt From Levy, Deposit to the U.S. Treasury and Charge the Same to [ALL-CAPS NAME] 123-45-6789 ADJUST THE BALANCE TO ZERO"
- Mail to IRS Criminal Investigation Division
- Initially appeared to work for a few months

**Court outcomes and IRS response:**
- **Initial period (2008-2009):** Some apparent successes — bills were not pursued for a period
- **After ~2009:** Rate of success dropped to zero
- **Current status:** The IRS has specifically designated A4V filings as **Frivolous Tax Arguments** under 26 CFR §1.6662-10
- **IRS penalty:** Filing a frivolous return based on A4V theory can result in a **$5,000 penalty** (IRC §6702)
- **Criminal prosecution:** Multiple convictions for using A4V to avoid taxes:
  - *United States v. Regular* (redemption theory)
  - *United States v. Waalee* (3d Cir. 2005) — "Redemption theory... appears to hold that a birth certificate is a negotiable instrument"
  - *United States v. Prestonwood Properties* (N.D. Tex. 2001) — frivolous UCC filings

**Assessment:** A4V was briefly effective as an administrative oversight exploit, not as a legal remedy. The IRS closed the loophole. Current use exposes filers to $5,000 penalties and potential criminal prosecution.

### 4.3 Land Registry RX1 Restriction (UK — Steve Counsell)

**Proponent:** Steve Counsell

**Claimed mechanism:**
- File Form RX1 with HM Land Registry
- Enter a restriction against your property preventing it from being dealt with (sold, mortgaged) without your consent
- This allegedly protects against the "strawman" accessing or encumbering the property

**Actual RX1 function (mainstream):**
- RX1 is the official form to apply to enter a restriction on a registered title in England and Wales
- A restriction **controls how a property can be dealt with** — it prevents certain transactions without a specified condition being met
- Cost: £40 per title
- Processing time: 2-4 months
- Common uses: protecting co-owners' interests, preventing fraud, developer plot control

**Legal reality:**
- The RX1 is a legitimate, widely-used legal instrument
- However, the grounds for applying must be lawful — "protecting against the strawman" is not a recognized ground
- Applying against someone else's property without proper legal basis exposes the applicant to **liability for damages under Section 77 of the Land Registration Act 2002**
- The restriction does NOT "separate" the living person from the legal fiction — it is a property protection tool

**Assessment:** The RX1 is a real legal instrument being used for an unintended purpose. It may provide practical protection against fraud but does not achieve the claimed legal status correction.

### 4.4 UCC-1 Financing Statement (LearnToBeFree / Redemptionists)

**Proponents:** Dario Busch, Winston Shrout, "Redemption" movement

**Claimed mechanism:**
1. File a UCC-1 Financing Statement listing the ALL-CAPS NAME as debtor and the living person as secured party
2. Describe the collateral as "all assets, rights, and interests associated with the legal fiction"
3. File with the Secretary of State in the state of birth
4. This "perfects a security interest" in the strawman
5. As secured party creditor, you have priority over all subsequent claims
6. You can then discharge debts using "Accepted for Value" against the strawman's accounts

**The claim (from SPC University):**
> "A UCC-1 Financing Statement is a legal tool under Article 9 of the Uniform Commercial Code that allows you to perfect a security interest in property—real or personal, tangible or intangible. When applied to your strawman: It creates a recorded claim showing that you, as the secured party, have a priority interest in the legal fiction."

**Mainstream legal reality:**
- A UCC-1 Financing Statement is a real legal document used in secured transactions
- Article 9 of the UCC requires: a debtor, a secured party, a security agreement, and **eligible collateral**
- **A birth certificate is not collateral** — it is a vital records document
- **The ALL-CAPS NAME is not a separate entity** that can be encumbered by UCC filing
- Filing a UCC-1 against your own name with your own birth certificate as collateral is legally meaningless
- Multiple legal guides confirm: "A UCC-1 filing is not a vehicle to attach a lien to a birth certificate" (Catalyst Legal, Bridge Legal, The Legal Guide)

**Court outcomes:**
- **United States v. Benabe** (7th Cir. 2007): "No federal court has recognized the validity of a secured party creditor filing against a straw man"
- **United States v. Snead** (6th Cir. 2010): "The redemption theory... is a legally frivolous argument"
- **United States v. Bell** (E.D. Mich. 2010): Conviction for filing false UCC statements to discharge tax debts
- The Treasury Department Analysis and Control Division has confirmed that "the birth certificate does not have a Commercial Value"

**Assessment:** Filing a UCC-1 against yourself is legally void. It does not create a security interest, does not establish priority, and does not enable debt discharge. It may create legal exposure.

## 6. Admiralty Law vs. Common Law in This Context

### 6.1 The Claimed Transition

The alternative framework claims that the US switched from common law to maritime admiralty law through a series of events:

| Year | Event | Alleged Effect |
|------|-------|----------------|
| 1910 | Jekyll Island meeting | Central banking scheme devised |
| 1913 | Federal Reserve Act | Put the US "into Maritime Admiralty law" |
| 1921 | Sheppard-Towner Maternity Act | Forced birth registration |
| 1933 | HJR-192 / Gold seizure | Switched from common law to admiralty; all money became "negotiable instruments" |

**The claim:** "This whole process switched the country from common law, to maritime admiralty law because we were no longer allowed to pay for anything with real money (gold or silver). We now walk around exchanging negotiable instruments, which is done only in maritime admiralty law."

### 6.2 Mainstream Legal Reality

**Admiralty law** (maritime law) governs:
- Disputes arising on navigable waters
- Ship collisions, cargo damage, salvage
- Seafarer employment contracts
- Maritime liens and mortgages on vessels

**Common law** governs:
- Civil disputes between individuals
- Criminal law
- Property law
- Contract law (non-maritime)
- Constitutional rights

**The two systems coexist** — they are not in competition or substitution. The US Constitution (Article III, Section 2) grants federal courts jurisdiction over "Cases of Admiralty and Maritime Jurisdiction" alongside other federal question jurisdiction.

**Critical point:** The transition from gold-backed currency to fiat currency did NOT create admiralty jurisdiction over citizens. Currency type has no bearing on which court system applies to a person. A traffic ticket in Kansas is still common law. A tax return is still statutory law. Neither is "admiralty law."

### 6.3 The Maritime Terminology Paradox

Alternative researchers point to maritime terminology used in everyday legal and financial contexts:

| Term | Maritime Meaning | Everyday Usage |
|------|------------------|----------------|
| "Born" | Mooring a vessel | Birth of a child |
| "Berth" | Docking space | "Birth certificate" |
| "Currency" | Current-sea (flow of money) | Money |
| "Dock" | Ship's berth | Court appearance |
| "Tonnage" | Ship's capacity | Tax on vessels |
| "Consideration" | Cargo loading | Payment |

**The claim:** These linguistic parallels prove that everyday life operates under admiralty law.

**Reality:** These are etymological coincidences. The English language evolved from maritime trade — many commercial terms have nautical origins. This does not mean that all commerce is admiralty jurisdiction. The etymology of "salary" comes from "sal" (salt) — this does not make employment law a salt-trade regulation.

## 7. The "Personage" Concept

### 7.1 Anna von Reitz's Definition

**Personage** = the crime of impersonation / identity theft
**Barratry** = the crime of knowingly bringing false claims into court

**The claim (from Anna von Reitz):**

> "The corollary crime routinely practiced by attorneys and barristers is known as 'barratry' — knowingly bringing charges against these corporate proxies 'as if' they were the same as the living people they are named after."

**The mechanism as described:**
1. State registers birth → creates legal person (ALL-CAPS NAME)
2. Courts treat living person AS IF they ARE the legal person
3. This is "personage" — impersonating a living being as a corporation
4. Charges brought against the legal person are "barratry" — false claims against a dead entity
5. When you answer to the ALL-CAPS name, you "consent" to the impersonation

### 7.2 Legal Analysis

**In mainstream law:**
- "Personage" (impersonation) IS a crime — but it means pretending to be a police officer, doctor, etc., not "being treated as your legal name"
- "Barratry" IS a crime — but it means maintaining frivolous litigation, not "bringing charges in court"
- Courts treat the name on documents as referring to the same person regardless of capitalization
- The ALL-CAPS formatting is a typographical convention in government databases, not evidence of a separate entity

**Key distinction:** Anna von Reitz describes the crime as the STATE impersonating individuals. Courts describe the CRIME as individuals impersonating officials. These are opposite claims.

### 7.3 The "Lawful Person" vs. "Legal Person" Framework

From Anna von Reitz:
- **Lawful Person** = living man/woman on the land and soil jurisdiction → has Natural and Unalienable Rights
- **Legal Person** = corporate entity in the international jurisdiction of the sea → has only Civil Rights (privileges)

> "Lawful Persons 'record' their property assets and interests and retain ownership of their assets. Legal Persons 'register' their interests and give them to their Masters in Trust."

**The claimed remedy:** Record (land jurisdiction) vs. Register (sea jurisdiction). By recording documents instead of registering them, you allegedly remain in the Lawful Person status.

**Legal reality:** There is no recognized legal distinction between "recording" and "registering" that changes a person's rights or jurisdiction. Both are filing mechanisms.

## 9. Synthesis: Connecting to the Enterprise System

### 9.1 What Is Real in the Legal Chain

Despite the court rejections of the specific remedy pathways, several structural observations from the research are noteworthy:

1. **Birth registration IS a commercial event** — The state uses vital records for administrative purposes beyond mere recording. Social Security numbers DO function as account identifiers within the federal financial system.

2. **Legal persons ARE separate from natural persons** — This is mainstream legal doctrine, not a conspiracy theory. Corporations, trusts, and other legal entities are "persons" in law. The question is whether the birth-created legal person functions as described.

3. **The ALL-CAPS formatting IS a real convention** — Government databases DO use ALL-CAPS formatting. This IS a typographical convention. But it IS also a convention that creates a distinguishable entity in records systems.

4. **Birth certificate bonds ARE historical** — There is evidence that birth certificates were used as collateral in the early 20th century (especially during the 1933 gold seizure). Whether this practice continues is debated.

5. **The maritime/admiralty terminology IS real** — Many commercial terms DO have nautical origins. This reflects the historical centrality of maritime trade to English commercial law. But etymology ≠ jurisdiction.

### 9.2 What Is NOT Real
