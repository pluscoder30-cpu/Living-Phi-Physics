## KEY FINDINGS

### 1. Crown Bay Marina v. Epstein Established Maritime Lien Precedent
Big N Barge (70' transport barge) caused $230,473 damage. Maritime lien attached automatically under 46 U.S.C. § 31342.

### 2. Maritime Liens Travel with Property
Liens follow the vessel regardless of ownership transfer. Even bona fide purchasers cannot escape enforcement.

### 3. GVI v. Estate Targeted Maritime Assets
Nautilus Inc. (Little St. James, $63.9M), Great St. Jim LLC, The 1953 Trust. $105M settlement resolved claims but liens can still reach remaining assets.

### 4. CICO Criminal Activity Liens Complement Maritime Liens
Territorial liens for criminal activity can be used alongside maritime liens for asset recovery.

### 5. Asset Recovery Potential Is Significant
Maritime liens could reach: remaining vessels/aircraft ($18.5M), real property through shell companies, offshore assets through in rem jurisdiction.

### 6. Trust System Interacts with Maritime Liens
The birth certificate trust system creates a parallel maritime trust structure where the "vessel" (person) can be liened through admiralty jurisdiction.

### 7. Maritime Liens Survive Settlements
"A maritime lien arises by operation of law and is unaffected by a settlement agreement." — Addax Energy SA v. M/V Yasa H. Mulla.

### 8. The Liens Could Still Be Enforced
Any maritime liens that existed before the settlement remain enforceable against remaining assets.
