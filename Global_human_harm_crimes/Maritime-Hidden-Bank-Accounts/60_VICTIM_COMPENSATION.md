EVCP ($121-125M), Estate settlements ($164-170M), JPMorgan ($290M), Deutsche Bank ($75M), Bank of America ($72.5M), USVI ($105M).

### 2. Net to Survivors: ~$520-540M
After ~30% attorney fees on settlements.

### 3. 150-650+ Victims Remain Uncompensated
Out of estimated 500-1,000+ total victim population. EVCP only paid ~135-150 survivors.

### 4. NYC GMVA Lookback Window Is Critical
18-month window (January 29, 2026 – July 29, 2027) for gender-motivated violence claims. Time-sensitive.

### 5. $112M Tax Refund Controversy
Estate executors may benefit instead of victims. Challenge asset valuations during EVCP.

### 6. Institutional Claims Beyond Banks Remain Unexplored
Airlines, hotels, security firms, accounting firms — all potential recovery targets.

### 7. Broad Releases Limit Future Claims
But releases do NOT cover claims against banks, institutions not named, or criminal proceedings.

### 8. Prevention Requires Structural Reform
Beneficial ownership transparency, mandatory SAR filing, cross-border asset recovery protocols.
