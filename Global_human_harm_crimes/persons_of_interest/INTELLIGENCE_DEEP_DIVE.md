- Chabad/Kushner references

### Reliability Assessment
**Mixed** — The CHS had apparent access to Dershowitz and knowledge of the Epstein-Maxwell orbit, but the Leon Black/Blackwater claim was factually incorrect. FBI closure codes indicated:
- "Contains Open Source Information"
- "Addresses a validated field office collection gap"
- "Addresses NHCD or USIC requirement"

### Significance
This is the **only document in the FBI corpus** showing Epstein as subject of a foreign intelligence case. Filed under case **813B-NY-2928278** — an intelligence division case (not criminal), classified **SECRET//NOFORN**, with 25-year declassification timeline (until 2042).
