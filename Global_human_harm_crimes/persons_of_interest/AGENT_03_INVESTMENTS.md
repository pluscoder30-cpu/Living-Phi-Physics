## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Jeffrey Epstein maintained a diversified investment portfolio spanning cryptocurrency, venture capital, hedge funds, wearable technology, and speculative startup ventures. Despite being a convicted sex offender since 2008, Epstein continued to deploy capital into Silicon Valley startups and crypto firms through 2019, amassing over $600 million in total assets. His investment activity was facilitated by a network of venture capitalists, entrepreneurs, and financial intermediaries who brokered deals and provided access to deal flow. At least $120 million in confirmed investment capital was deployed across direct startup investments, venture fund limited partnerships, and hedge fund allocations. The portfolio appreciated significantly, with paper gains exceeding $250 million by the time of his 2019 arrest.
