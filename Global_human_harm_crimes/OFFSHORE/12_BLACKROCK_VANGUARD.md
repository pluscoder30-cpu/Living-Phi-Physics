### Finding 11: The CFC Tax Strategy - IRS Private Letter Ruling

**Confidence:** [VERIFIED]

Fidelity Cayman subsidiaries are structured specifically to exploit a tax loophole via a private IRS letter ruling:

**The Mechanism:**
1. U.S. mutual funds must derive 90% of gross income from qualifying income (dividends, interest, gains from securities) under Subchapter M of the Internal Revenue Code
2. Income from commodities (gold, oil, derivatives) is not qualifying income
3. Fidelity obtained an IRS private letter ruling concluding that income from investments in CFCs that invest in commodities constitutes qualifying income
4. The fund invests up to 25% of assets in a wholly-owned Cayman subsidiary
5. The subsidiary invests in commodities, gold, crypto derivatives
6. The fund looks through the CFC and aggregates holdings for 1940 Act compliance
7. The CFC is not registered under the 1940 Act and not subject to investor protections

**The Tax Benefit:**
- Without the CFC: Fund cannot invest in commodities and maintain RIC (Regulated Investment Company) status
- With the CFC: Fund gains commodity exposure while preserving RIC tax status
- The CFC pays a separate management fee (0.30% annually) to Geode Capital Management
- The CFC subpart F income is included in gross income but treated as ordinary income regardless of character

**Evidence:**
- Source 8: SEC No-Action Letter - Gold Portfolio relies on a private letter ruling from the IRS ruling that income from investment in the Subsidiary will constitute qualifying income
- Source 9: SEC Fidelity CFC Disclosure - The fund will look through its controlled foreign corporation (CFC) and will aggregate the CFC individual holdings together with direct holdings of the fund

The naming convention is telling: Fidelity has an entity literally named FDOF Cayman Tax Subsidiary LP - the word Tax in the entity name explicitly identifies its purpose.

### Finding 13: Left.eu Study - BlackRock European Tax Avoidance

**Confidence:** [VERIFIED]

**Key Findings:**
