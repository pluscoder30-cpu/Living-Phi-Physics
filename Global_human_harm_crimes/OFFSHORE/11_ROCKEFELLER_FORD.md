## Findings

### Finding 1: The Foreign Blocker Corporation Mechanism

**Confidence:** [VERIFIED]

All three foundations invest through offshore blocker corporations in zero-tax jurisdictions (Cayman, Bermuda, BVI).

**How it works:**
1. Foundation allocates to hedge funds/PE/VC
2. These funds generate UBTI through debt-financed income
3. Direct investment = 35%+ tax on UBTI
4. Offshore blocker corp (0% tax) receives income, pays tax-free dividends to foundation
5. Blocker prevents look-through rule from exposing UBTI

**Money trail:** Foundation endowment -> Offshore blocker corp (Cayman/Bermuda) -> Hedge fund/PE fund -> Tax-free dividends

**Date(s):** 1969 (Revenue Act) � present

**Verification:** [VERIFIED] via Sources 1, 2, 3, 4, 17, 18

**Evidence:**
- NYT (2007): Rockefeller Foundation's general counsel said 21% of $3.7B endowment in hedge funds, 12% in private equity. "He did not know what portion was offshore." � Source 1
- BYU Law Review (2013): "Tax-exempt organizations are able to use the foreign blocker corporation loophole" � Source 2
- Joint Committee on Taxation (2007): "Such corporations may be established offshore in low-tax or zero-tax jurisdictions" � Source 4

## Verdict

The Rockefeller, Ford, and Carnegie foundations systematically invest through offshore blocker corporations in zero-tax jurisdictions (Cayman Islands, Bermuda, British Virgin Islands). This is legal but opaque. The combined estimated annual UBTI tax avoidance across these three foundations is ~$1.9B/yr. Multiple Rockefeller-associated entities appear in the ICIJ Offshore Leaks Database (Bahamas Leaks, Paradise Papers). The Rockefeller Brothers Fund investment policy explicitly permits offshore fund vehicles. Carnegie International Corporation was involved in an SEC fraud case involving offshore trust share sales. Rockefeller Philanthropy Advisors operates as a global fund flow hub across 70+ countries.
