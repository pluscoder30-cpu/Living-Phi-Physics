## Executive Summary

This document maps the offshore and foreign subsidiary structures of six major defense and government services entities. All data sourced from SEC Form 10-K Exhibit 21 filings. GE has the most complex offshore structure with subsidiaries in 30+ countries. The defense entities maintain strategic offshore subsidiaries in key operational jurisdictions (UK, Australia, Singapore, Middle East) rather than traditional tax-haven structures.
