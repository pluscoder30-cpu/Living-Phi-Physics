## Findings

### Finding 1: Pfizer - 100% Offshore Income, 5.4% Effective Tax Rate

**Confidence:** [VERIFIED]

Pfizer carried out potentially the largest tax-avoidance structure in the history of big pharma. In 2019, despite selling 20 billion in drugs to US customers, Pfizer reported 0 in taxable US profits - 100% of income was booked in offshore Controlled Foreign Corporations (CFCs). [Source 1, 26]

**Money trail:** US drug sales (20B) -> booked entirely in offshore CFCs -> taxed at GILTI rate 10.5% (not 21% US rate) -> further reduced via Puerto Rico (tax holiday until 2053) and Singapore (incentive rates through 2048)

**Effective tax rates (post-2017 TCJA):**
- 2019: **5.4%**
- 2020: **5.3%**
- 2021: **7.6%**
- 2022: **9.6%**

**US losses reported while recording profits offshore:**
- 2018: 7.97 billion US loss
- 2019: 1.29 billion US loss
- 2020: 0.62 billion US loss (while 9.9B foreign profit on comparable revenue)
- 2023: 4.4 billion US loss on 27B US sales; 5.4B profit on 31B foreign sales

**Key offshore jurisdictions:** Ireland (~dozen entities), Singapore, Puerto Rico, Luxembourg, Bermuda, Netherlands

**Pfizer Irish subsidiaries:** Pfizer Healthcare Ireland Unlimited, Pfizer Investment Capital Unlimited, Pfizer Ireland Pharmaceuticals Unlimited, Pfizer Ireland Ventures Unlimited, Pfizer Leasing Ireland, Pfizer Manufacturing Ireland Unlimited, Pfizer Manufacturing Services Unlimited, Pfizer Service Company Ireland Unlimited, Pfizer Shared Services Unlimited, Pfizer Worldwide Services Unlimited

**NDA secrecy:** Pfizer signed NDAs with Singapore and Puerto Rico governments on tax deals, preventing disclosure to Congress [Source 1, 26]

**CP Pharmaceuticals International B.V. (Netherlands):** Dutch limited partnership that owns majority of Pfizer international subsidiaries. The Irish EPA facility licence reveals CPPI/CV recorded net profits exceeding 13 billion USD in 2009 alone [Source 28].

**Verification:** [VERIFIED] via Senate Finance Committee (Sources 1, 26), SEC filings (Source 2, 27), Reuters (Source 3)

**Harm:** Billions lost US tax revenue; US taxpayers subsidize Pfizer drug development while paying highest drug prices globally

### Finding 3: Google/Alphabet - Double Irish Dutch Sandwich (75.4bn in One Year)

**Confidence:** [VERIFIED]

Google used the Double Irish, Dutch Sandwich for over a decade, achieving single-digit effective tax rates on non-US profits - roughly 25% the average overseas tax rate. [Source 8, 9]

**Structure:**
Google Inc (US) -> shares IP costs with -> Google Ireland Holdings Unlimited (Irish-incorp, Bermuda-tax-resident) -> royalties to -> Google Netherlands Holdings BV (Dutch shell, 0 employees) -> royalties to -> Google Asia Pacific / Google Ireland (operating)

**Amounts shifted to Bermuda:**
- 2014: EUR 10.7bn
- 2017: EUR 19.9bn (23bn)
- 2018: EUR 21.8bn (24.5bn)
- 2019: **75.4bn** (EUR 63bn) shifted out of Ireland via dividends

**Google Ireland Holdings Unlimited:**
- Incorporated Ireland, tax-domiciled Bermuda (0% rate)
- Zero employees
- 13bn pretax profit 2019 - effectively tax-free
- 50.8bn dividends paid over 2018-2019

**Resolution (2020):** IP consolidated to US. EUR 345m tax settlement with Irish Revenue for prior years. [Source 30]

**Verification:** [VERIFIED] via Irish Times (Source 8), Reuters (Source 9), Irish filings

### Finding 4: Apple - Stateless Entities and the Green Jersey

**Confidence:** [VERIFIED]

Apple created three Irish-incorporated subsidiaries that were stateless for tax purposes - called the holy grail of tax avoidance by Senator Carl Levin. [Source 11]

**The three stateless entities:**
1. **Apple Operations International (AOI):** 30bn income 2009-2012; zero tax any government for 5 years
2. **Apple Sales International (ASI):** 74bn income 2009-2012; 0.005% effective rate 2011
3. **Apple Operations Europe (AOE):** Irish manufacturing

**EU State Aid Ruling (2016, upheld 2024 by ECJ):**
- Ireland gave Apple illegal tax benefits worth up to **EUR 13 billion** + interest [Source 10]
- Effective rate declined from 1% (2003) to **0.005% (2014)**
- 2011: ASI 22B profit; only EUR 50M taxable in Ireland

**Green Jersey transition (2014-2015):** [Source 12, 13]
- December 2014: AOI/ASI relocated tax residency to Jersey (0% corporate tax)
- AOE relocated to Ireland; purchased IP from ASI via capital allowances
- AOE borrowed from Jersey subsidiary; interest deductible in Ireland
- Apple onshored ~300B of IP from Jersey Q1 2015 - the largest recorded BEPS action in history

**Offshore cash hoard:** 252 billion (as of 2017), 94% held offshore

**Verification:** [VERIFIED] via EU Commission (Source 10), US Senate (Source 11), Social Europe (Source 12), Paradise Papers/Guardian (Source 13), CFR (Source 24)

### Finding 5: Amazon - Luxembourg Empty Shell Hiding 75% of EU Profits

**Confidence:** [VERIFIED]

Amazon structured its European operations around Amazon Europe Holding Technologies SCS (LuxSCS), described by the EU Commission as an empty shell with no employees, no offices, and no business activities. [Source 14, 15]

**The structure (2006-2014):**
Amazon.com Inc (US) -> partners in -> LuxSCS (Luxembourg SCS, tax-exempt) -> IP license -> Amazon EU Sarl (LuxOpCo) -> runs European websites -> European customers

**How it worked:**
- LuxSCS held all European IP rights
- LuxOpCo licensed IP from LuxSCS, paying royalties
- Royalties exceeded **90% of LuxOpCo's operating profits**
- LuxSCS was a transparent entity (not subject to corporate tax)
- Result: ~75% of Amazon's EU profits were untaxed

**EU Commission findings:**
- Tax ruling issued Nov 6, 2003, prolonged 2011
- Reduced Amazon's taxable profits to **one-quarter** of reality
- Ordered Luxembourg to recover **EUR 250 million** + interest (2017) [Source 14]
- General Court annulled in 2021; ECJ upheld annulment Dec 2023 (procedural grounds)
- New Commission decision Nov 2024 found no State aid (reversal) [Source 15]

**Verification:** [VERIFIED] via EU Commission (Source 14, 15), Reuters (Source 16), Fortune

## Verdict

The investigation establishes that all six target companies - Pfizer, Johnson & Johnson, Google, Apple, Amazon, and Microsoft - maintain extensive offshore structures designed to minimize tax liabilities. The combined scale is staggering:

**Estimated annual tax avoidance (conservative):**
- Pfizer: Billions (100% offshore income, 5.4% effective rate vs 21% statutory)
- J&J: Billions (93 tax haven subsidiaries across 9 jurisdictions)
- Google: Tens of billions over decade (single-digit effective rate on foreign profits)
- Apple: EUR 13B+ (EU state aid ruling upheld by ECJ 2024)
- Amazon: EUR 250M+ (EU state aid ruling, though annulled on procedural grounds)
- Microsoft: Tens of billions (Round Island One: 314.7bn profit, zero tax)

**Common structural elements:**
1. Ireland as primary hub (all 6 companies have Irish subsidiaries)
2. IP shifting to low/no-tax jurisdictions
3. Treasury/finance subsidiaries for intra-group loans
4. Use of tax-transparent entities (Luxembourg SCS, Dutch BV)
5. Exploitation of Double Irish / Green Jersey / GILTI loopholes
6. NDAs and opacity to prevent oversight

**Systemic finding:** The 2017 Tax Cuts and Jobs Act (TCJA) created the GILTI system with global blending, which provided a lower tax rate (10.5%) on foreign income, creating massive incentives for exactly these offshore structures. The average effective tax rate for large pharmaceutical companies fell from 19.6% (2014-2016) to 11.6% (2019-2020) - a 40% reduction. [Source 24, 25]
