### Finding 1: RTX Maintains Extensive Offshore Subsidiary Network â€” Multiple Tax Haven Jurisdictions

**Confidence:** [VERIFIED]

RTX Corporation (and its predecessor Raytheon Company) maintains a multi-jurisdictional offshore subsidiary network spanning at minimum: Bermuda, Ireland, Netherlands, Singapore, United Kingdom, and multiple other jurisdictions. SEC Exhibit 21 filings list only "significant subsidiaries" per SEC Regulation S-X Rule 1-02(w), meaning the vast majority of subsidiaries are not disclosed in SEC filings.

**Post-Merger (RTX Corporation):**
- SEC Exhibit 21 (FY 2022-2024) filings identify significant subsidiaries in Netherlands, Ireland, Bermuda, Singapore, UK, Germany, Australia, Canada, India, Japan, and Saudi Arabia.
- ICIJ Paradise Papers (2017) documented Raytheon Bermuda entities incorporated in 1998.

## Verdict

**RTX Corporation (Raytheon) maintains a multi-jurisdictional offshore subsidiary network spanning Bermuda, Ireland, Netherlands, Singapore, UK, and other jurisdictions, consistent with the broader defense contractor offshore tax optimization pattern documented across Boeing (CAGE_022), Lockheed Martin (CAGE_021), and other major defense contractors.**

**The offshore structure is confirmed by:**

- SEC Exhibit 21 filings (2022-2024) listing significant post-merger RTX subsidiaries
- ICIJ Paradise Papers (2017) documenting Raytheon Bermuda entities

**The $950M+ settlement (October 2024) for bribery, fraud, and export control violations establishes a pattern of financial misconduct that intersects with the offshore structure â€” the Qatari bribery scheme used sham subcontracts that likely routed through international entities.**

**Confidence:** HIGH on documented entities; MEDIUM on full subsidiary count and specific tax savings.
