## Critical Pattern: Warburg Pincus Bermuda Convergence

The single most significant finding is the **convergence of defense and pharmaceutical pension fund investments in a single Bermuda-registered private equity vehicle**:

| Pension Fund | Trustee | Investment Vehicle | Jurisdiction | Date |
|-------------|---------|-------------------|--------------|------|
| Boeing Employee Retirement Plan | JPMorgan Chase Bank | Warburg Pincus (Bermuda) Private Equity X, L.P. | Bermuda | 12-JUN-2008 |
| Northrop Grumman Pension Master Trust | State Street Bank | Warburg Pincus (Bermuda) Private Equity X, L.P. | Bermuda | 12-JUN-2008 |
| Pfizer Inc. (Master Trust) | Northern Trust Company | WPPEXB, L.P. | Bermuda | 12-JUN-2008 |

**Three of the largest U.S. defense and pharmaceutical corporations channeled employee retirement funds into the same Bermuda-registered private equity vehicle on the same date (June 12, 2008), through three different trustee banks (JPMorgan, State Street, Northern Trust).**

The "WPPEXB" prefix in Pfizer's vehicle almost certainly stands for "Warburg Pincus Private Equity X (Bermuda)" — confirming all three investments are in the same fund.
