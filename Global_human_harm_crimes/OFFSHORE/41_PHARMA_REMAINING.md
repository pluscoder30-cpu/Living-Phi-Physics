## Executive Summary

This document catalogs publicly disclosed offshore and international subsidiary structures for 10 major pharmaceutical companies, sourced from SEC EDGAR filings (10-K, 20-F, EX-21 exhibits). All data is from publicly filed documents. No private or confidential information is included.

**Key Finding:** Ireland, Bermuda, Cayman Islands, Switzerland, Singapore, Puerto Rico, and the Netherlands are the primary jurisdictions used by these companies for international holding structures, IP management, and financing operations.

## Verdict

**[VERIFIED]** All offshore structures documented are from publicly filed SEC documents (10-K, 20-F, EX-21). These are legally required disclosures.

**Key Observations:**

1. **Ireland** is the dominant offshore hub (9 of 10 companies have significant Irish presence)
2. **Unlimited Company** designation used in Ireland provides privacy (no public financial statements required)
3. **Cayman Islands** used primarily for investment vehicles and reinsurance
4. **Bermuda** used for holding companies and finance entities
5. **Switzerland** used for export hubs and IP management
6. **Puerto Rico** provides US tax territory treatment with local incentives
7. **Netherlands** used for finance and holding structures
