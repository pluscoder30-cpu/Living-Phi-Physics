## Executive Summary

BlackRock, Inc. (NYSE: BLK) is the world's largest asset manager with **$15.3 trillion AUM** (2026). The company operates through **200+ subsidiaries** across **30+ countries**. This document maps every offshore, tax-haven, and international subsidiary identified in the FY2024 10-K filing (EX-21.1), tagged to jurisdiction, entity type, and parent relationship.

**Key Finding:** BlackRock maintains **21 Cayman Islands entities**, **3 British Virgin Islands entities**, **1 Barbados entity**, **9 Jersey entities**, **8 Luxembourg entities**, and **5 Singapore entities** — forming a multi-layered offshore holding and fund administration infrastructure.

## Offshore Structure Analysis

### The Cayman Layer Cake

BlackRock's Cayman structure follows a **4-tier architecture**:

```
Tier 1: BlackRock, Inc. (Delaware parent)
  │
  ├── Tier 2: BlackRock Cayman Capital Holdings Limited
  │     ├── BlackRock Cayman Finco Limited
  │     ├── BlackRock Cayman Finco 2 Limited
  │     ├── BlackRock Cayman Finco 3 Limited
  │     └── BlackRock Cayman West Bay Finco Limited
  │
  ├── Tier 2: BlackRock Holdco 8-12, Ltd (5 entities)
  │     └── [Sub-holdings for fund vehicles]
  │
  ├── Tier 2: BlackRock Cayman 1 LP / 2 LP
  │     └── [Fund vehicles — likely blocker corp structures]
  │
  └── Tier 2: BlackRock Cayco Limited
        └── [Cayman operating entity]
```

**Purpose of Cayman LPs (Blocker Structures) [INFERENCE]:**
- Cayman LPs are commonly used as "blocker corporations" to prevent UBTI (Unrelated Business Taxable Income) from flowing to tax-exempt investors (pensions, endowments)
- The LP structure allows BlackRock to isolate income types while maintaining consolidated control
- Multiple Finco entities suggest intercompany lending/bond structures for tax-efficient profit repatriation

### The BVI-Barbados Connection

```
BlackRock Inc.
  ├── BlackRock (BVI) Holdco 1, Ltd.
  ├── BlackRock (BVI) Holdco 2, Ltd.
  ├── BlackRock (BVI) Finco 1, Ltd.
  └── BlackRock (Barbados) Finco 1 SRL
```

**BVI entities** typically serve as:
- Holding companies for Caribbean/Latin American operations
- Intercompany financing vehicles (note the Finco structure)
- Entity isolation for liability purposes

**Barbados SRL** serves as:
- Intercompany financing vehicle (Barbados has favorable tax treaties)
- Caribbean regional treasury function

### The Jersey Hub

Jersey serves as BlackRock's **alternative investment administration center**:
- Mercury Private Equity MUST 3 — private equity fund vehicle
- Kreos Capital Management — private credit (acquired 2023)
- St. Albans House Nominees — nominee/custody structure
- BR Jersey International Holdings LP — international holdings

### Luxembourg — The European Gateway

Luxembourg is BlackRock's **primary European fund domicile**:
- UCITS fund management (BlackRock Fund Management Company S.A.)
- Aladdin technology licensing (BlackRock Aladdin Luxembourg S.à.r.l.)
- EMEA capital markets operations
- Property holdings (BlackRock Property Lux S.à.r.l.)

### Aladdin IP Licensing [INFERENCE]

BlackRock Aladdin Luxembourg S.à.r.l. is significant:
- Aladdin is BlackRock's $1B+ technology platform
- Luxembourg Sàrl structure suggests IP licensing/royalty arrangement
- Technology income routed through Luxembourg (favorable IP regime)
- This is a **major tax optimization structure** — Aladdin revenue flows from global clients through Luxembourg entity

## Verdict

**[VERIFIED]** BlackRock maintains **54+ offshore/international entities** across Cayman Islands (21), BVI (3), Barbados (1), Jersey (9), Luxembourg (8), Ireland (2), Singapore (5), Hong Kong (4), Guernsey (1), and Netherlands (3). The structure follows a multi-tiered architecture with distinct functions:

1. **Cayman LPs** — Likely blocker corp structures for tax-exempt investor UBTI isolation
2. **Cayman/BVI Finco entities** — Intercompany financing vehicles
3. **Luxembourg Sàrls** — European fund administration and Aladdin IP licensing
4. **Jersey entities** — Alternative investment fund administration and nominee structures
5. **Barbados SRL** — Caribbean intercompany financing
6. **Singapore/HK/Guernsey** — APAC/infrastructure regional operations

BlackRock explicitly states foreign earnings remain **"indefinitely reinvested"** offshore, and Ireland is identified as a **significant low-tax jurisdiction** for the company.
