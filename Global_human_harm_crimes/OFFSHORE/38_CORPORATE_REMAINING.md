**Finding 1: Luxembourg Finance Shell — €135M Tax Evasion [VERIFIED]**
Finmeccanica Finance SA was incorporated in Luxembourg but **actually managed and administered in Italy** (a practice called "esterovestizione"). The company was used to hide €490 million in taxable wealth from 2010 to 2015, evading over €135 million in Italian taxes. 82 Finmeccanica executives and employees received notices of investigation. - **Confidence:** [VERIFIED]
- **Source:** Il Sole 24 Ore (2017), Il Tempo (2017), Guardia di Finanza investigation

**Finding 2: Panama Bribery Network — Agafia Shell [VERIFIED]**
A shell company named **Agafia** in Panama was created as part of a $250 million helicopter/radar contract with the Panamanian government (2010). Agafia was to receive $25 million (10% commission). Investigations revealed ties between the shell company and former Panamanian President Martinelli's circle. Former Finmeccanica director Paolo Pozzessere and Italian businessman Valter Lavitola were charged. The case was ultimately dismissed due to statute of limitations.
- **Confidence:** [VERIFIED]
- **Source:** Irish Times (2016), La Prensa Panama (2021)

**Finding 3: Dublin/Offshore Commission Network [VERIFIED]**
Mossack Fonseca leaked files revealed that Intertrade Projects Consultants Ltd (Dublin) negotiated sales promotion agreements with Finmeccanica subsidiaries for military aircraft, torpedoes, and electronic warfare equipment over two decades. Intertrade entered subagency agreements with entities in **BVI, Bahamas, Philippines, Hong Kong, and the US**, with large percentages of commissions routed offshore. The agreements contained secrecy clauses. Intertrade was linked to a parent company in the Bahamas and a trust in **Niue** (Pacific tax haven).
- **Confidence:** [VERIFIED]
- **Source:** Irish Times (2016), Mossack Fonseca files

**Finding 4: Google Payments Bribery Scheme [VERIFIED]**
Between 2012 and 2018, Leonardo executives allegedly received bribes disguised as extra monthly/annual compensation, fuel vouchers, purchase vouchers, and luxury goods. The scheme used **Google Ireland and Google Payments** as intermediaries, with offshore companies in **Panama, UK, and Ireland** to erase traces. An estimated €6 million flowed from Italy to foreign accounts. Milan prosecutor Gaetano Ruta opened proceedings for money laundering, tax crimes, and private-sector corruption.
- **Confidence:** [VERIFIED]
- **Source:** La Prensa Panama (2021)

**Money trail:** Italian government defense contracts → Finmeccanica/Leonardo → Luxembourg Finance SA (€490M hidden) → Panama (Agafia, $25M commission) → Dublin (Intertrade) → BVI/Bahamas/Philippines sub-agencies → bribery payments via Google Payments
