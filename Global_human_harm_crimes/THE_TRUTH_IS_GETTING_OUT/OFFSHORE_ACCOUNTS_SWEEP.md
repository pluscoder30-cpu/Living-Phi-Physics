- **Created**: December 27, 2006 (one month after FBI began interviewing witnesses)

- **Trustee**: Erika Kellerhals (Epstein's USVI attorney)
- **Closed**: July 9, 2019 (3 days after Epstein arrest)
- **Source**: Fortune 2026, NYDFS consent order 2020

- **Source**: DOJ EFTA releases, USVI court filings
