## I. THE THESIS

Credit unions are chartered financial institutions that operate on trust — member-owned, locally governed, and built on the fiction that their charters serve their communities. The Epstein network exploited this architecture by converting financial institutions into paper towns: entities with charters, balance sheets, and employees that existed on paper but operated as fronts for money laundering, intelligence operations, and synthetic identity management.

**The key insight:** Every bank in the Epstein network functioned as a credit union in the worst sense — not as a community institution, but as a locally-chartered entity that processed money without meaningful oversight because someone with authority decided not to look.
