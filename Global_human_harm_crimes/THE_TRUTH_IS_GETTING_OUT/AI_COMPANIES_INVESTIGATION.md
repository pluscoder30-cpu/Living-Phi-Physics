## 1. OPENAI (GPT, ChatGPT)

### Copyright & Data Scraping
- **NYT v. OpenAI** (2024-present): Judge allowed main copyright claims to proceed. NYT alleges OpenAI scraped millions of articles without permission. Search referral traffic to midsize publishers declined 47% YoY by Dec 2025
- **Ziff Davis v. OpenAI** (2025): $1.1B+ lawsuit. OpenAI used GPTBot to scrape content despite robots.txt directives. 9 causes of action including copyright infringement and DMCA violations
- **Seattle Times lawsuit** (Sep 2026): New suit joined by Newsday, accusing OpenAI of scraping published work to train LLMs
- **Local news publishers lawsuit** (Jun 2026): Group of local news outlets sued over scraping copyrighted content
- **MDL consolidation** (Apr 2025): 12 cases consolidated in S.D.N.Y. — largest AI copyright litigation in history
- **India ANI lawsuit**: OpenAI told Indian court deleting training data would "breach US legal obligations"

### Military & Defense
- **$200M Pentagon contract** (Jun 2025): First military contract for "proactive cyber defense" and "warfighting domains"
- **Anduril partnership** (Dec 2024): AI integrated into counterdrone systems for military
- **Classified systems deal** (Feb 2026): Hours after Trump banned Anthropic, OpenAI signed deal for classified military networks
- **Altman to employees** (Mar 2026): "You don't get to make operational decisions" about military use of AI
- **Robotics head resigned** (Mar 2026): Caitlin Kalinowski quit over surveillance/autonomous weapons concerns
- **Original ToS flip**: OpenAI originally banned military/warfare use, then quietly removed the prohibition

### Surveillance
- **Pentagon surveillance loopholes** (Mar 2026): Deal initially allowed "any lawful purpose" — experts warned this enabled mass surveillance through data broker purchases
- **EO 12333 loophole**: Existing authority allows NSA to collect communications incidentally containing US persons' data
- **Amended contract**: After backlash, added "shall not be intentionally used for domestic surveillance" — but critics say "intentional" is a loophole
- **Chinese surveillance tool uncovered** (Feb 2025): OpenAI discovered Chinese operation building surveillance tool to identify anti-Chinese posts on Western social media
- **Iranian election interference** (Aug 2024): Blocked accounts using ChatGPT to generate election influence content

### Political & Bias
- **Election safeguards 2026**: Banned scaled campaign messaging, but allows internal campaign use
- **California ballot initiative** (Dec 2025): Launched own ballot measure to control AI regulation — critics called it "cynical hijacking"
- **Political PACs**: Not directly funding PACs, but executives fund "Leading the Future" ($125M+ super PAC)
- **Hallucination problem worsening** (May 2025): OpenAI's own tests showed hallucinations increasing — classified as "AI Incident" by OECD

### Harm to Workers/Users
- **800M weekly active users** by April 2025 — massive scale of potential harm
- **Children's safety lawsuits**: SB 243 in California was response to minors harmed after chatbot interactions
