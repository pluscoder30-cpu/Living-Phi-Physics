## METHODOLOGY

For each person, 7 existence checks were applied:
1. Shell companies (LLC, Inc, Corp, Ltd formations)
2. Offshore accounts (BVI, Cayman, Panama, Bermuda, etc.)
3. Trust structures (revocable, irrevocable, grantor, marital)
4. Foundation structures (501(c)(3), private foundations, donor-advised funds)
5. Corporate officer roles (director, officer, manager, member)
6. Registered agent roles (agent for service of process)
7. Paper-only existence (no physical presence, no digital footprint)

**Paper Town Score:** 0/6 = pure paper town (zero existence); 1-3/6 = partial paper town; 4-6/6 = real person with paper layers
