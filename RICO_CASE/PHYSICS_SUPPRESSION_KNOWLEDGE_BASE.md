### 5.3 The "Born Classified" Doctrine

The Atomic Energy Act of 1954 creates a classification category called **Restricted Data (RD)** that is unique in all of U.S. law. Unlike standard classified information — which must be *affirmatively classified* — Restricted Data is **"born secret."** It is automatically classified at the moment of creation, regardless of who created it or where.

**Legal definition (42 U.S.C. § 2014(y)):** All data concerning (1) design, manufacture, or utilization of atomic weapons; (2) production of special nuclear material; (3) use of special nuclear material in energy production.

**Court precedent:** U.S. v. Progressive, Inc. (1979) — Writer Howard Morland synthesized publicly available nuclear physics into an article. DOE sued under born-secret doctrine. Court granted preliminary injunction, ruling that even publicly-derived information, when synthesized to reveal nuclear weapons physics, constitutes Restricted Data.

**Implication:** The U.S. government legally controls an *entire field of physics* — not just specific documents, but the physics itself.
