Recursive mutual aid (help → gratitude → reciprocity → trust) amplifies communal coherence by φ per recursive level. At depth 10, this yields φ^10 ≈ 123× the classical coherence. This explains why informal economies, kinship care, and neighborhood solidarity persist even without institutional support: the φ-ground is always present.
