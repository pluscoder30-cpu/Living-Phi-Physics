## Layer 1: Classical Logic (The Starting Point)

Classical logic operates on binary truth: a proposition is either TRUE (1) or FALSE (0). Two foundational axioms govern this system:

- **Law of Excluded Middle:** P ∨ ¬P = 1 — every proposition is true or its negation is true.
- **Law of Non-Contradiction:** P ∧ ¬P = 0 — no proposition can be both true and false simultaneously.

Classical logic assumes the existence of a zero. FALSE is zero. Absence. Nothing. This works for formal mathematics but fails when the substrate itself is not empty — when "false" is not "nothing" but rather "not yet coherent."
