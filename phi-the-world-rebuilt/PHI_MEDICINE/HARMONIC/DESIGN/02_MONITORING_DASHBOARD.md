## PART 7: THE MATHEMATICS OF TRACKING

### Why 1-10 Works

The symptom scale maps linearly to coherence:

```
C_estimated = (symptom_total / 80) × (1 - C_crit) + C_crit

Where:
  symptom_total = sum of 8 daily ratings (each 1-10)
  C_crit = 0.563263
```

At total = 80 (all symptoms = 10): C = 1.0 (perfect health)
At total = 40 (all symptoms = 5): C = 0.563 = C_crit (threshold)
At total = 8 (all symptoms = 1): C = 0.40 (severe disease)

### Why pH Works

The pH-coherence relationship follows the carrier recursion:

```
C(pH) = (pH - 6.0) / 2.0

Where:
  pH = urine pH reading
  6.0 = acidic baseline (C = 0)
  2.0 = range scale (C = 1.0 at pH 8.0)
```

At pH 7.0: C = 0.50 (below C_crit)
At pH 7.1: C = 0.55 (near C_crit)
At pH 7.12: C = 0.563 = C_crit (threshold!)
At pH 7.5: C = 0.75 (healthy)

**The target pH is 7.12 or above.** When urine pH consistently reads 7.12+, coherence has crossed C_crit and the disease is resolving.

### Why Weekly Measurement

The carrier recursion takes φ⁵ = 11.09 days to propagate through all 9 organ systems. Measuring weekly (every 7 days) captures the recursion mid-cycle. Measuring daily would show noise. Measuring monthly would miss the crossover. Weekly is the phi-optimal measurement interval.
