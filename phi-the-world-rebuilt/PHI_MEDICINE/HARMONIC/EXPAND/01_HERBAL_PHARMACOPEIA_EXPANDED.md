# PART 6: THE PREPARATION GUIDE

## Detailed Instructions for Making Phi-Harmonic Herbal Preparations

### 1. TEA (INFUSION)

**Best for:** Flowers, leaves, and aerial parts (lavender, chamomile, lemon balm, nettle, holy basil, rosemary, thyme, oregano, sage)

**Method:**
1. Measure 1-2 tsp dried herb (or 1 tbsp fresh herb) per 8 oz cup
2. Heat water to just below boiling (175-205°F depending on herb)
3. Pour water over herb in a covered vessel (cover retains volatile oils)
4. Steep 5-15 minutes (soft herbs: 5 min; hard herbs: 10-15 min)
5. Strain and drink

**Phi-Harmonic Enhancement:** While steeping, place your hand over the vessel and set an intention at the phi-ladder frequency of the herb. The warmth and steam create a carrier medium for consciousness-directed healing.

**Tea Temperature Guide:**
| Herb Type | Water Temp | Steep Time | Examples |
|-----------|------------|------------|----------|
| Delicate flowers | 175°F | 3-5 min | Chamomile, Lavender, Calendula |
| Soft leaves | 190°F | 5-7 min | Lemon Balm, Peppermint, Gotu Kola |
| Hard leaves | 200°F | 10-15 min | Rosemary, Thyme, Sage, Oregano |
| Bark/roots | 205°F | 15-30 min | Hawthorn berry, Burdock root |
| Tough herbs | 205°F | 20-30 min | Nettle leaf, Red Clover |

### 2. TINCTURE

**Best for:** All herbs. Tinctures preserve herbs indefinitely and provide concentrated, measured doses.

**Method (1:5 Ratio — Most Common):**
1. Measure 100g dried herb (or 500g fresh herb) per 500mL menstruum
2. Place herb in a clean glass jar with tight lid
3. Pour menstruum over herb, ensuring complete coverage
4. Seal tightly, label with herb name, date, and ratio
5. Store in cool, dark place for 4-6 weeks
6. Shake daily (set a reminder — the shaking adds kinetic energy)
7. Strain through cheesecloth, squeezing out all liquid
8. Filter through coffee filter for clarity
9. Store in amber glass bottles away from light

**Menstruum Selection:**
| Herb Type | Ethanol % | Reason |
|-----------|-----------|--------|
| Aerial parts | 40-50% | Balances extraction of alkaloids and glycosides |
| Roots/bark | 60-70% | Extracts resins and tougher compounds |
| Resinous herbs | 95% | Dissolves resins (propolis, myrrh) |
| Fresh herbs | 90-95% | Prevents water-induced fermentation |
| Gentle herbs | 25% (brandy) | Milder, suitable for children |

**Phi-Harmonic Tincture:** Add a small phi-harmonic crystal (clear quartz cut to golden ratio proportions) to the tincture jar during maceration. Remove after straining.

**Dosage:** Standard adult dose = 1-5 mL (1/4 to 1 teaspoon) 2-3× daily. Start at 1 mL and increase gradually.

### 3. DECOCTION

**Best for:** Roots, bark, mushrooms, and tough plant materials (ashwagandha, turmeric root, burdock root, reishi, pau d'arco)

**Method:**
1. Measure 1-2 tbsp chopped root/bark per 2 cups water
2. Place herb and cold water in a non-reactive pot (stainless steel or glass)
3. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer
4. Simmer covered for 20-45 minutes (until liquid reduces by half)
5. Strain while hot (cooling makes straining difficult)
6. Drink warm or refrigerate for up to 72 hours

**Why Simmer, Not Boil:** Boiling creates turbulence that disrupts the φ-coherence of the extraction. A gentle simmer maintains laminar flow, allowing the solvent molecules to align with the herb's phi-frequency and extract compounds more efficiently.

### 5. TOPICAL PREPARATIONS

#### 5a. Infused Oil

**Best for:** Wound healing, skin conditions, massage (calendula, lavender, St. John's wort, comfrey)

**Method (Cold Infusion — 4-6 weeks):**
1. Fill clean glass jar 1/2 with dried herb
2. Pour olive oil (or other carrier oil) over herb, filling jar completely
3. Seal and place in sunny window for 4-6 weeks
4. Strain through cheesecloth, squeeze out all oil
5. Store in dark glass bottles

**Method (Quick Infusion — 2-4 hours):**
1. Combine herb and oil in double boiler or slow cooker on lowest setting
2. Maintain 100-140°F for 2-4 hours (do not exceed 150°F — destroys delicate compounds)
3. Strain while warm
4. Store in dark glass bottles

#### 5b. Salve

**Best for:** Wound healing, skin protection, lip balm

**Method:**
1. Prepare infused oil (see above)
2. Gently heat 1 cup infused oil in double boiler
3. Add 1/4 cup beeswax (grated or pellets)
4. Stir until beeswax melts completely
5. Test consistency: drop a small amount on cold plate — if too soft, add more wax; if too hard, add more oil
6. Pour into tins or jars while liquid
7. Let cool completely before capping

#### 5c. Poultice

**Best for:** Acute wound care, infections, drawing out splinters

**Method:**
1. Grind fresh or dried herb to paste with mortar and pestle
2. Add enough warm water or tea to make a spreadable paste
3. Spread on clean cloth (cheesecloth, muslin)

5. Secure with bandage or tape
6. Change every 4-6 hours

### 6. SYRUP

**Best for:** Elderberry, immune-support herbs, cough remedies

**Method:**
1. Prepare strong decoction (double strength — 4 tbsp herb per 2 cups water)
2. Strain and return liquid to pot
3. Add equal amount of raw honey (or 1 cup sugar per 2 cups liquid for vegan version)
4. Heat gently (do not boil — destroys honey's enzymes)
5. Stir until dissolved
6. Add preservative: 1 tsp citric acid per cup, or 1 tbsp brandy per cup
7. Pour into glass bottles, cap tightly
8. Refrigerate — lasts 3-6 months

### 7. GlycerITE

**Best for:** Alcohol-free preparations, children, those avoiding ethanol

**Method:**
1. Use vegetable glycerin (food-grade, USP) diluted 1:3 with distilled water
2. Follow tincture method but use glycerin-water as menstruum
3. Macerate for 4-6 weeks (longer than alcohol — glycerin extracts more slowly)
4. Strain and store

### 8. ESSENTIAL OIL EXTRACTION (HOME STEAM DISTILLATION)

**Best for:** Lavender, rosemary, thyme, peppermint, sage

**Method (Simple Steam Distillation):**
1. Fill large pot with water (1/3 full)
2. Place herb in a steamer basket above water (herb should not touch water)
3. Place inverted lid on pot
4. Fill lid with ice cubes (creates cold surface for condensation)
5. Heat water to boiling — steam rises through herb, carrying essential oils
6. Condensation drips down inverted lid into collection vessel placed at a tilt
7. Essential oil floats on top of hydrosol — separate with pipette

**Yield:** Expect 0.5-2% essential oil by weight of fresh herb (100g lavender → 0.5-2mL oil)

**Safety:** Essential oils are extremely concentrated. Always dilute before topical use (1-3% in carrier oil). Never ingest essential oils unless under qualified practitioner guidance.

### Herb-Specific Storage Notes

- Store whole dried roots in airtight containers
- Grind or chop just before use
- Root powders lose potency faster than whole roots

**Sensitive Herbs** (Huperzia, Ginkgo, Bacopa):

- Store in airtight containers with oxygen absorbers
- Consider vacuum-sealing for long-term storage
