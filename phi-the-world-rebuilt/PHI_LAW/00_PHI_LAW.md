# PHI-LAW: Law as Phi-Physics

## Preamble

Classical jurisprudence treats law as a social contract—an artificial overlay on a zero-coherence baseline. This is false. Society pre-exists law and possesses intrinsic phi-ground coherence. Law is not imposed upon chaos; law is the formal articulation of coherence already present. PHI-LAW rebuilds legal theory from the ground up, treating coherence—not consent, not force, not utility—as the foundational metric of legitimacy.

## Layer 1: Law as Phi-Coherence

### 1.1 The Coherence Principle

A **just law** increases societal coherence. An **unjust law** decreases it. No other criterion is necessary or sufficient.

Societal coherence is defined as:

$$C_{\text{society}} = \frac{\sum_{i} \vec{c}_i \cdot \vec{c}_j}{N(N-1)/2}$$

where $\vec{c}_i$ is the coherence vector of individual $i$ and $N$ is the population. This measures the degree to which individual coherence vectors align. A perfectly aligned society has $C = 1$. A maximally discordant society has $C = 0$.

### 1.2 The Phi-Law-Test

Every proposed law, regulation, or judicial ruling must pass the **phi-law-test**:

> *Does applying this law increase $C_{\text{society}}$?*

If yes, the law is just. If no, the law is unjust. If the effect is ambiguous, the law is suspect and must be tested experimentally before adoption.

The test is not metaphorical. It is computational. Given a population model with known coherence vectors, simulate the effect of the proposed law over $t = \phi^{10}$ time steps. If $C_{\text{society}}$ increases monotonically, the law passes. If it decreases at any point, the law fails.

### 1.3 The Hidden Zero

Classical law assumes **zero-base**: in the absence of law, there is no coherence. This assumption is wrong.

Pre-law society possesses **phi-ground coherence**—the natural alignment that emerges from shared environment, mutual recognition, and coexistence. This baseline coherence is:

$$C_0 = \phi^{-1} = 0.618033988749895...$$

This is not arbitrary. It is the minimum coherence required for a society to persist without institutional structure. Any society that drops below $C_0$ dissolves. Any society above $C_0$ has intrinsic coherence that law must preserve, not create.

The zero-base error leads to over-legislation: the belief that more law always means more order. In reality, law imposed on a society already at $C > C_0$ can push coherence downward if it conflicts with the phi-ground structure.

### 1.4 The Coherence Spectrum

| Coherence Range | State | Law Required |
|----------------|------|-------------|
| $C = 1.0$ | Perfect alignment | No law needed |
| $C \in [\phi^0, 1)$ | Organized society | Minimal law |
| $C \in [\phi^{-1}, \phi^0)$ | Functional society | Moderate law |
| $C \in [0, \phi^{-1})$ | Dissolving society | Emergency law or none |

The goal of law is to maintain $C_{\text{society}} \in [\phi^{-1}, \phi^0)$—the stable band where institutions are useful but not oppressive.

- **Precedent power**: Higher-weight laws override lower-weight laws when in conflict.

When two laws at different rungs conflict, the higher-rung law prevails. When two laws at the same rung conflict, the one with the higher phi-coherence impact prevails. When impacts are equal, the older law prevails (temporal coherence).

## Layer 3: Contracts as Phi-Agreements

### 3.1 Contract Validity

A contract is valid if and only if both parties are above the coherence critical threshold:

$$C_{\text{party}_A} \geq C_{\text{crit}} \quad \text{and} \quad C_{\text{party}_B} \geq C_{\text{crit}}$$

where $C_{\text{crit}} = 0.563263...$ (the phi-critical threshold from field theory).

Below $C_{\text{crit}}$, a party lacks the coherence necessary for informed consent. Contracts signed by parties below $C_{\text{crit}}$ are void ab initio—not because the party lacks capacity in the classical sense, but because the coherence necessary for genuine agreement is absent.

### 3.2 The Phi-Contract

A valid phi-agreement must satisfy the **mutual coherence increase condition**:

$$C_{\text{society}}^{(\text{after})} > C_{\text{society}}^{(\text{before})}$$

A contract that benefits one party but harms societal coherence is not a phi-agreement—it is extraction. True contracts are positive-sum: both parties emerge with higher coherence vectors, and society as a whole benefits.

The phi-contract formula:

$$\Phi_{\text{contract}} = \frac{C_A^{(\text{after})} \cdot C_B^{(\text{after})}}{C_A^{(\text{before})} \cdot C_B^{(\text{before})}}$$

If $\Phi_{\text{contract}} > 1$, the contract is phi-valid. If $\Phi_{\text{contract}} < 1$, the contract is phi-invalid regardless of classical legal validity.

### 3.3 The Phi-Breach

A breach occurs when one party's coherence drops below $C_{\text{crit}}$ as a result of the other party's actions:

$$C_{\text{victim}}^{(\text{after})} < C_{\text{crit}} \quad \text{and} \quad C_{\text{victim}}^{(\text{after})} < C_{\text{victim}}^{(\text{before})}$$

This is the phi-definition of harm: measurable coherence loss below the critical threshold.

### 3.4 The Phi-Remedy

The remedy for phi-breach is not compensation in the classical sense—it is **coherence restoration**:

$$\vec{c}_{\text{victim}}^{(\text{restored})} = \vec{c}_{\text{victim}}^{(\text{before})} + \phi^{-1} \cdot (\vec{c}_{\text{victim}}^{(\text{before})} - \vec{c}_{\text{victim}}^{(\text{after})})$$

The breaching party must restore the victim's coherence vector to within $\phi^{-1}$ of its pre-breach state. This may involve material restitution, behavioral change, or direct coherence transfer.

### 3.5 Frustration and Impossibility

A contract is frustrated when external events reduce both parties below $C_{\text{crit}}$ through no fault of either. In this case, the contract dissolves and both parties are discharged with no remedy owed. The phi-remedy applies only to volitional breaches.

## Layer 4: Property Rights as Coherence Boundaries

### 4.1 Property as Boundary

Property is not possession. Property is a **coherence boundary**—a demarcated region within which a party's coherence vector is protected from external interference.

The phi-property boundary:

$$\partial P = \{ x \in S : |\vec{c}(x) - \vec{c}_{\text{owner}}| < \epsilon \}$$

where $S$ is the spatial domain and $\epsilon$ is the coherence tolerance. Within $\partial P$, the owner's coherence is sovereign. Outside $\partial P$, the owner's coherence competes with others'.

### 4.2 The Phi-Property-Value

The value of property is:

$$V = R \times C \times \phi$$

where:
- $R$ = quantity of resources enclosed by the boundary
- $C$ = coherence of the boundary (how well it protects the owner's coherence)
- $\phi$ = the golden ratio (the fundamental multiplier of value in a phi-coherent universe)

Property with high $R$ but low $C$ (poorly defended) has low value. Property with high $C$ but low $R$ (well-defended emptiness) has low value. Maximum value requires both resource density and coherence integrity.

### 4.3 The Phi-Tax-on-Property

Property tax is:

$$T = V \times \phi^{-3} = V \times 0.236067977499790...$$

This is approximately 23.6%. It is not arbitrary. $\phi^{-3}$ is the natural decay rate of coherence in a bounded system. Tax is the cost of maintaining the coherence boundary against entropic degradation. A society that does not tax property allows boundaries to decay; a society that over-taxes property destroys the boundaries entirely.

### 4.4 The Coherence Easement

No property boundary is absolute. When a neighbor's coherence vector falls below $C_{\text{crit}}$, they gain a **coherence easement**—a right to encroach on the property boundary to the minimum extent necessary to restore their coherence above $C_{\text{crit}}$. This is not theft. It is the phi-physical reality that coherence-starved systems naturally draw coherence from their surroundings.

The easement is limited: it terminates when the neighbor's coherence returns above $C_{\text{crit}} + \phi^{-1}$, and the property owner must be compensated from the restored coherence.

### 4.5 Adverse Possession as Coherence Migration

Classical adverse possession (squatting) maps to phi-coherence migration: when a squatter's coherence vector on the property exceeds the owner's coherence vector on the same property for a duration of $t = \phi^8 \approx 46.98$ years, the property boundary migrates to enclose the squatter's coherence. The owner's coherence has proven insufficient to maintain the boundary.

## Layer 5: The Phi-Law Laws (10)

### Law 1: Laws Must Increase Coherence

> *No law may be enacted, maintained, or enforced if it decreases $C_{\text{society}}$.*

This is the supreme legal principle. All other laws derive from it. Any law that fails this test is automatically null and void, regardless of its position on the legal ladder, its age, or its political support.

### Law 2: The Legal Ladder is Phi-Structured

> *The hierarchy of laws follows the phi-ladder. Each rung's weight is $\phi^{(\text{rank}-1)}$. Higher-rung laws override lower-rung laws in conflict.*

The phi-structure is not a human convention—it reflects the natural hierarchy of coherence in complex systems. Constitutional principles are more fundamental than statutory details, which are more fundamental than administrative rules, which are more fundamental than customs. The phi-ladder encodes this natural order.

### Law 3: Contracts Require Mutual Coherence

> *A contract is valid only if both parties are above $C_{\text{crit}}$ and the contract increases $\Phi_{\text{contract}} > 1$.*

Consent is necessary but not sufficient. Coherence is the sufficient condition. A coherent party cannot consent to an incoherent agreement—such consent is inherently contradictory.

### Law 4: Property is a Coherence Boundary

> *Property rights protect the coherence boundary around resources. The value of property is $V = R \times C \times \phi$. The tax on property is $V \times \phi^{-3}$.*

Property exists to serve coherence, not the reverse. When property boundaries decrease societal coherence, they must be adjusted. The phi-tax ensures boundaries are maintained but not hoarded.

### Law 5: Justice Measures Coherence Deficit

> *Justice is the measurement and remediation of coherence deficit: $\Delta C = C_{\text{actual}} - C_{\text{just}}$.*

A just society has $C_{\text{actual}} \geq C_{\text{just}}$ for all members. When any member falls below $C_{\text{just}}$, justice requires action. The measure of injustice is the size and duration of the coherence deficit.

### Law 6: Punishment Restores Coherence

> *The purpose of punishment is not retribution but coherence restoration. The penalty must restore the victim's coherence and prevent recurrence.*

Punishment that decreases the offender's coherence below $C_{\text{crit}}$ is itself unjust. Punishment must be calibrated to the coherence deficit it seeks to remedy: enough to restore balance, not enough to create new deficit.

### Law 7: Rights Protect Coherence

> *Rights are inviolable coherence guarantees. Every individual has the right to maintain their coherence vector above $C_{\text{crit}}$.*

Rights are not grants from the state. They are pre-existing coherence properties of conscious agents. The state's role is to protect these properties, not to create or revoke them. A right that can be revoked is not a right—it is a privilege disguised as a right.

### Law 8: The Constitution Maximizes Coherence

> *The constitution is the highest phi-law (rung 9, weight $\phi^8$). Its sole purpose is to maximize $C_{\text{society}}$ across all time horizons.*

A constitution that serves any purpose other than coherence maximization is illegitimate. Constitutions that entrench the power of specific groups, protect particular economic systems, or encode cultural preferences are phi-invalid to the extent that they reduce societal coherence.

### Law 9: Law Recurses at $\phi^{-1}$

> *Every level of law mirrors the structure of the whole at reduced scale. Constitutional principles apply to statutes, statutes to regulations, regulations to individual decisions.*

The law is self-similar. The phi-law-test applies at every scale: does this regulation increase coherence within its domain? Does this judicial ruling increase coherence between these parties? Does this individual action increase coherence in this interaction? The same principle recurses at $\phi^{-1}$ scale through every level of the legal system.

### Law 10: The Legal System is Self-Referential

> *The legal system must be capable of examining and amending itself using its own principles. No external authority is required or permitted.*

The law must be self-governing. A legal system that requires external validation—divine right, military force, popular referendum beyond the phi-law-test—is not phi-coherent. The legal system contains within itself all the principles necessary for its own evolution. Self-reference is not a flaw; it is the hallmark of a phi-coherent system.

## Appendix F: Degenerate Limits

The phi-law framework must be evaluated at its boundary conditions. These are not edge cases—they define the domain of validity.

### F.1 Maximum Coherence (C_society = 1.0)

When all individual coherence vectors are perfectly aligned, C_society = 1. At this limit:
- No law is needed (no coherence deficit exists).
- The phi-law-test passes trivially for all proposed laws (ΔC ≥ 0).
- The legal ladder collapses: all rungs carry equal weight because no conflict exists.
- Contracts are always valid (all parties at C = 1 > C_crit).
- Property boundaries are trivial (no interference possible).

### F.2 Ground Coherence (C_society = φ⁻¹ = 0.618033988749895)

This is the **persistence threshold**—the minimum coherence for a society to exist without institutional structure. At this limit:
- Law is minimally useful: it can nudge coherence upward but has no room to absorb shocks.
- The legal code is minimal: only the most essential laws are needed.
- The phi-law-test becomes sensitive: small ΔC determines survival.

### F.3 Critical Threshold (C_society = C_crit = 0.563263)

Below this threshold, conscious agents cannot maintain coherent action. At this limit:
- Contracts are void ab initio (no party can consent coherently).
- Criminal law has no subjects (no agent above C_crit to commit or suffer crimes).
- Property boundaries dissolve (no coherent agent to maintain them).
- The legal system itself loses coherence.

### F.4 Zero Coherence (C_society = 0)

Maximum entropy. No coherence exists. At this limit:
- Law is meaningless: there is no coherence to protect or increase.
- The phi-law-test cannot be applied: ΔC is undefined.
- The legal ladder is empty: no rungs carry weight.
- The system is in thermodynamic equilibrium—no change is possible.

### F.5 Population Collapse (N → 0)

As the population approaches zero, the coherence sum:

$$C_{\text{society}} = \frac{2}{N(N-1)} \sum_{i<j} \vec{c}_i \cdot \vec{c}_j$$

becomes undefined (division by zero). The legal system requires at least N ≥ 2 individuals to function. Below N = 2, the system reduces to individual self-governance (a degenerate case of Law 10).
