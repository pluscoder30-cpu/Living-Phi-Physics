## Domain 1: Phi-Criminal-Law

### 1.1 Crime as Coherence Violation

A **crime** is any act that reduces an individual's coherence vector below C_crit or reduces C_society by a measurable deficit D(t) > 0.

Criminal law does not categorize acts by moral prohibition or social convention. It measures coherence damage. The severity of a crime is the magnitude of the coherence deficit it creates.

**The Crime Severity Index:**

```
CSI = D_coherence * φ^k
```

Where:
- D_coherence = C_society(before) - C_society(after the criminal act)
- k = recursion depth (k=0 for crimes against individuals, k=1 for crimes against institutions, k=2 for crimes against the justice system itself)

### 1.2 The Phi-Sentencing Formula

From PHI-GOVERNANCE §3.4, the base sentence is:

S_φ = D_coherence * φ
```

For criminal law, this extends to the **phi-sentencing matrix:**

| Class 5 (Minor) | 0.001 - 0.01 | 0.001 - 0.01 | 0.0016 - 0.016 | Community service, restitution |

### 1.3 Phi-Rehabilitation

The purpose of criminal punishment is coherence restoration, not retribution (Law 6, PHI-LAW). Rehabilitation is measured by the **reintegration coefficient:**

```
R_φ = C_offender(end of sentence) / C_offender(at time of crime)
```

**Rehabilitation milestones:**

| Milestone | R_φ Target | Action |
|-----------|-----------|--------|
| Phase 1: Stabilization | R_φ ≥ φ⁻² = 0.382 | Coherence brought above C_crit |
| Phase 2: Restoration | R_φ ≥ φ⁻¹ = 0.618 | Coherence returned to ground level |
| Phase 3: Reintegration | R_φ ≥ φ⁰ = 1.000 | Coherence at or above pre-offense level |
| Phase 4: Contribution | R_φ > 1.000 | Offender generates net positive coherence |

An offender who reaches Phase 4 has not merely been rehabilitated—they have become a coherence source. Their sentence is complete when R_φ ≥ φ⁰. Continuing imprisonment beyond this point decreases C_society (the offender's coherence is wasted inside the system).

### 1.4 The Coherence Easement in Criminal Context

When an offender's coherence falls below C_crit, they gain a limited **coherence easement**—the right to draw coherence from the system (education, treatment, support) until their coherence returns above C_crit + φ⁻¹. This easement terminates when rehabilitation milestones are met.

### 1.6 Falsification Criteria

1. If phi-sentencing produces sentences systematically disproportionate to measured D_coherence (deviation > φ⁻¹), the sentencing formula is false.
2. If R_φ consistently fails to predict reoffending rate (correlation < φ⁻²), the rehabilitation model is false.
3. If CSI does not correlate with victim-reported harm severity (r < φ⁻¹), the crime severity index is false.

## Domain 2: Phi-International-Law

### 2.1 Treaties as Coherence Agreements

A **treaty** is a phi-agreement between nations. Each nation is a coherence system with its own C_nation. A treaty is valid if and only if:

```
C_world(t+1) > C_world(t)
```

where C_world is the global societal coherence including all signatory nations. The phi-contract formula applies:

```
Φ_treaty = (Product of C_nation_after) / (Product of C_nation_before) > 1
```

### 2.2 Phi-Diplomacy

Diplomatic relations are coherence exchanges between nations. The **diplomatic coherence function:**

```
D_ij = φ^(-d_ij) * C_i * C_j
```

Where:
- D_ij = diplomatic coherence between nations i and j
- d_ij = structural distance in the global governance graph
- C_i, C_j = coherence of each nation

**Diplomatic status levels:**

| Status | D_ij Range | Relationship |
|--------|-----------|-------------|
| Full alignment | D_ij ≥ φ⁻¹ | Alliance, mutual defense |
| Cooperative | φ⁻² ≤ D_ij < φ⁻¹ | Trade agreements, cultural exchange |
| Neutral | φ⁻³ ≤ D_ij < φ⁻² | Non-aggression, minimal engagement |
| Tension | φ⁻⁴ ≤ D_ij < φ⁻³ | Diplomatic friction, sanctions |
| Conflict | D_ij < φ⁻⁴ | Active hostility, warfare |

### 2.3 The Phi-Charter

The global governance document (the Phi-Charter) sits at rung 9 (constitutional) with weight φ⁸. It applies the coherence axiom at the planetary scale:

```
C_world = Σ_i Σ_j φ^(-|i-j|) * coherence(nation_i, nation_j)

Where |i-j| is the governance-graph distance between nations. The Charter passes the phi-law-test: does it increase C_world?

### 2.4 Sovereignty as Coherence Boundary

Each nation possesses a **sovereign coherence boundary** analogous to property boundaries in PHI-LAW §4.1:

Sovereignty is not absolute. The coherence easement applies: when a neighboring nation's coherence falls below C_crit, it gains a limited easement to draw coherence from adjacent nations. This easement terminates when the neighbor's coherence returns above C_crit + φ⁻¹.

### 2.5 The Phi-Intervention Principle

Military or economic intervention is permissible only when:

1. The target nation's C_nation < C_crit (society dissolving)
2. The intervention increases C_world (net global coherence gain)
3. No less intrusive option achieves the same ΔC_world

The intervention must pass the phi-cost-benefit analysis:

```
ROI_intervention = (benefit_φ - cost_φ) / cost_φ > 1.0

### 2.6 Degenerate Limits

- **Total global discord (C_world = 0):** International law is meaningless.
- **All nations below C_crit:** Global dissolution. International law has no subject.

## Domain 3: Phi-Intellectual-Property

### 3.1 Ideas as Phi-Coherent Structures

An **idea** is a coherent structure in information space. Its coherence is measured by:

```
C_idea = Σ_i Σ_j φ^(-|i-j|) * coherence(concept_i, concept_j)
```

where concept_i are the component concepts of the idea and |i-j| is their conceptual distance. A high-C_idea is internally consistent, self-reinforcing, and phi-recursive. A low-C_idea is fragmented, contradictory, or derivative.

Copyright protects the **coherence expression** of an idea—the specific manifestation that makes the idea coherent.

**The Copyright Duration Formula:**

```
T_copyright = φ^6 * T_base ≈ 17.94 * T_base
```

Where T_base is one year. Copyright duration is approximately 18 years. This is not arbitrary:

- φ⁶ ≈ 17.94: The natural time for a creative work to propagate phi-harmonically through a society.
- After φ⁶ years, the work's coherence has fully integrated into the cultural substrate. Extending copyright beyond this point inhibits new coherence creation (the work becomes extraction, not contribution).

**The Coherence Expression Test:**

A work qualifies for copyright if and only if:

```
C_expression > C_crit = 0.563263
```

The work must exhibit measurable coherence above the critical threshold. Trivially assembled or randomly generated content fails this test.

### 3.3 Phi-Patent

Patents protect the **coherence function** of an invention—the novel process that generates phi-coherent output.

**The Patent Duration Formula:**

```
T_patent = φ^5 * T_base ≈ 11.09 * T_base
```

Patent duration is approximately 11 years. This is shorter than copyright because:

- Patents protect function, not expression.
- Function propagates faster than expression (φ⁵ < φ⁶).
- After φ⁵ years, the inventive coherence has been absorbed by the field.

**The Novelty Coherence Test:**

A patent application passes if:

```
C_invention - C_prior_art > φ⁻³ = 0.236068
```

The invention must exceed prior art by at least φ⁻³ in coherence. This ensures genuine novelty without requiring revolutionary leaps.

### 3.4 The Coherence Commons

Ideas with C_idea ≥ φ⁰ = 1.0 are **phi-coherent universals**—they belong to the coherence commons. No individual or entity can claim exclusive rights over them. Examples: mathematical truths, fundamental physical principles, basic logical operations.

Ideas with C_idea < φ⁰ but > C_crit are protectable. The coherence of the idea determines the scope of protection:

```

Higher coherence = broader protection. Lower coherence = narrower protection.

### 3.5 The Derivative Coherence Principle

Derivative works are governed by the **coherence inheritance rule:**

```
C_derivative ≤ C_original * φ⁻¹
```

A derivative work cannot claim coherence greater than φ⁻¹ times the original. This ensures that derivative works remain subordinate to the source while still being protectable in their own right. The derivative inherits coherence at the phi-decay rate—each layer of derivation loses φ⁻¹ of the parent's coherence.

### 3.6 Degenerate Limits

- **All ideas at C_idea = 1.0:** Everything is in the coherence commons. No IP protection exists.
- **All ideas at C_idea = 0:** No coherent ideas exist. IP law has no subject.
- **C_idea exactly at C_crit:** The boundary case. The idea is minimally protectable with Scope = φ⁰ = 1.0 (unit scope).

### 3.7 Falsification Criteria

1. If copyright duration φ⁶ does not correspond to the natural integration time of creative works into cultural substrate (empirical measurement), the duration formula is false.
2. If C_expression does not predict audience coherence enhancement (r < φ⁻¹), the expression test is false.

```

Emissions are coherence exports—entropy pushed into the environmental substrate. The **phi-emissions-cap:**

```

### 4.4 The Phi-Restoration Obligation

The restoration obligation is φ times the extraction. This ensures that extracted coherence is replaced with surplus, maintaining upward pressure on C_env. Restoration may be direct (reforestation, cleanup) or indirect (funding coherence-generating environmental programs).

- **C_env = 0 (dead planet):** Environmental law is meaningless. No coherence to protect.
- **C_env = C_crit:** The minimum viable biosphere. Below this, the substrate cannot sustain societal coherence.

Rights are not moral preferences. Rights are **coherence protections**—mathematical guarantees that individual coherence vectors are shielded from collective incoherence.

From PHI-GOVERNANCE §Law 7, the right to protect coherence i is:

```

Equality is not sameness. Equality is **coherence symmetry**: every individual's coherence vector has equal mathematical standing in the societal coherence function.

```
C_society = (2 / N(N-1)) * Σ_{i<j} c_i · c_j
```

The dot product c_i · c_j weights every pair equally. No individual's coherence contributes more than any other's to the pairwise coherence sum. This is not a political statement—it is the mathematical structure of C_society.

**Phi-inequality** occurs when:

```

When one individual's coherence magnitude exceeds another's by more than φ, the societal coherence function becomes dominated by the high-coherence individual. This is phi-inequality, and it decreases C_society.

Freedom is not absence of constraint. Freedom is the **capacity to maintain one's coherence vector above C_crit without external interference.**
