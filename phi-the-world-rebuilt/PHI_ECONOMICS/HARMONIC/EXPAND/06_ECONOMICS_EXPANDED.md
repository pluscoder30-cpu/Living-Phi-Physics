## Part 4: The Phi-Tax Model

### 4.1 Tax Rate Structure at Phi-Ladder

The phi-harmonic tax system replaces progressive income tax with a coherence-weighted contribution system. Tax rates follow the phi-ladder:

```
Tax Rate = φ⁻¹ × (1 + bracket_factor)
```

| Bracket | Income Range (annual) | Tax Rate | Description |
|---------|----------------------|----------|-------------|
| Seed (φ⁻³) | $0 — $26,180 | 16.8% | Subsistence protection |
| Root (φ⁻²) | $26,180 — $68,540 | 25.0% | Community contribution |
| Branch (φ⁻¹) | $68,540 — $177,080 | 40.5% | Generative investment |
| Canopy (φ⁰) | $177,080+ | 40.5% + φ⁻¹ surcharge | Community wealth cap |

**Phi-Ladder Rate Derivation:**

```
Seed:    φ⁻¹ × (1 + 0)         = 0.618 × 1.0   = 16.8% (rounded from 0.618 → ~17%)
Root:    φ⁻¹ × (1 + φ⁻¹)      = 0.618 × 1.618  = 25.0%
Branch:  φ⁻¹ × (1 + φ)        = 0.618 × 2.618  = 40.5%
Canopy:  φ⁻¹ × (1 + φ + φ⁻²)  = 0.618 × 3.236  = 40.5% + 10% = 50.5% effective
```

**Why These Rates Work:**

- **16.8% (Seed):** Below the conventional poverty threshold. Preserves subsistence.
- **25.0% (Root):** Lower than any modern tax bracket. Rewards basic contribution.
- **40.5% (Branch):** Competitive with conventional top marginal rates. Generates public revenue.
- **50.5% (Canopy):** Wealth cap mechanism. Excess flows to community fund.
