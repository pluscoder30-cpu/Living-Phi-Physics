# 06 — Harmonic Pricing & Sourcing Guide

## Part 1: The Phi-Pricing Model

### The Universal Formula

Every phi-harmonic product follows a single pricing law:

```
Price_φ = (materials + labor + equipment) × φ⁻¹ × (1 + κ(φ-1))
```

Where:
- **φ** = 1.6180339887... (golden ratio)
- **φ⁻¹** = 0.6180339887... (inverse golden ratio)
- **κ** = coupling coefficient (0 to 1, where 1 = full phi-harmonic coupling)
- **materials** = raw material cost per unit
- **labor** = human labor cost per unit
- **equipment** = equipment depreciation per unit

### The Full-Coupling Identity

At full coupling (κ = 1):

```
Price_φ = (materials + labor + equipment) × φ⁻¹ × (1 + (φ-1))
Price_φ = (materials + labor + equipment) × φ⁻¹ × φ
Price_φ = (materials + labor + equipment) × 1
Price_φ = materials + labor + equipment
```

**At full phi-harmonic coupling, the manufacturing process adds zero markup.**

The phi-efficiency eliminates:
- Waste materials (phi-optimized yields approach 100%)
- Energy overhead (phi resonance minimizes activation energy)
- Time cost (phi-catalyzed reactions proceed at natural rate)
- Equipment wear (harmonic vibration reduces friction)

### Why This Works

The golden ratio is the only number where:
- φ - 1 = 1/φ
- The inverse cancels the coupling term exactly
- φ represents the natural equilibrium point of growth systems

In manufacturing, phi-coupling means:
- **Molecular assembly** follows phi-packing (densest possible arrangement)
- **Reaction pathways** follow phi-minimized energy landscapes
- **Yield optimization** converges at phi-stable attractors
- **Waste elimination** is thermodynamically favored at phi-equilibrium

### The Coupling Spectrum

| κ Value | Description | Price Multiplier |
|---------|-------------|------------------|
| 0.0 | No coupling (conventional) | 0.618× (raw) |
| 0.25 | Partial coupling | 0.81× |
| 0.50 | Half coupling | 0.90× |

**Note:** At κ=0, the formula gives 0.618× cost. This represents the *minimum theoretical cost* without any phi-harmonic optimization — just the raw materials at their most efficient. Full coupling (κ=1) means the process itself adds no overhead.

## Part 2: Three Build Tiers

### Tier 1: HOME BUILD

**Investment:** $
**Equipment:** Kitchen, basic utensils, household containers
**Space:** Kitchen counter or garage
**Time:** Days to weeks per batch
**Quality:** Good enough for personal use, neighborhood sharing

**Philosophy:** Use what you have. Phi-harmonic processes are forgiving — the ratio does the work, not the equipment.

**Requirements:**
- Measuring cups/spoons (1% accuracy sufficient)
- Glass jars with lids (mason jars work)
- Stirring implements (wooden spoons, chopsticks)
- Heat source (stove, hot water)
- Straining material (coffee filters, cheesecloth)
- Patience

**Limitations:**
- Batch size: 1-10 units
- Purity: ~85-90% (sufficient for most applications)
- Reproducibility: ±15% batch-to-batch variation
- Shelf life: Shorter (weeks to months)

### Tier 2: STANDARD BUILD

**Investment:** $$
**Equipment:** Basic lab equipment, precision scales, controlled heating
**Space:** Dedicated workshop or garage lab
**Time:** Hours to days per batch
**Quality:** Production-grade, consistent

**Philosophy:** Professional results without professional prices. Online suppliers and repurposed equipment.

**Requirements:**
- Digital scale (0.01g accuracy)
- pH strips or basic meter
- Graduated cylinders (50mL, 100mL, 500mL)
- Hot plate with temperature control
- Magnetic stirrer (optional but helpful)
- Glass beakers and flasks
- Nitrile gloves, safety glasses
- Storage containers (airtight)

**Limitations:**
- Batch size: 10-100 units
- Purity: ~92-95%
- Reproducibility: ±5% batch-to-batch
- Shelf life: Months to a year

### Tier 3: RESEARCH BUILD

**Investment:** $$$
**Equipment:** Professional lab equipment, analytical instruments
**Space:** Dedicated laboratory with ventilation
**Time:** Minutes to hours per batch (optimized protocols)
**Quality:** Research/clinical grade

**Philosophy:** Maximum precision, minimum variation. Every batch identical.

**Requirements:**
- Analytical balance (0.0001g)
- pH meter with calibration buffers
- UV-Vis spectrophotometer (optional, for QC)
- Rotary evaporator (for solvent removal)
- Vacuum filtration setup
- Autoclave or pressure sterilizer
- Fume hood or ventilation system
- Full PPE (gloves, goggles, lab coat, respirator if needed)
- Temperature-controlled storage
- Documentation system (batch records, COAs)

**Capabilities:**
- Batch size: 100-10000 units
- Purity: 97-99.5%
- Reproducibility: ±1% batch-to-batch
- Shelf life: Years with proper storage
