- Put it on food (curry, eggs, rice)

**How to use it:**

## Chapter 6: How to Grow These Herbs 🌱

### Why Grow Your Own?

- It's FREE after the first cost 💰
- You know it's fresh ✨
- It's fun to watch things grow 🌱
- You'll feel like a wizard 🧙‍♂️

### Turmeric 🌿

**What you need:**
- A turmeric root (from the grocery store)
- A big pot
- Potting soil
- Water
- Sunshine

**Steps:**
1. Buy a fresh turmeric root (the more knobs, the better)
2. Cut it into pieces (each piece needs at least one bump)
3. Fill a big pot with soil
4. bury the pieces 2 inches deep
5. Water well
6. Put in a warm, sunny spot
7. Water when soil feels dry
8. Wait 8-10 months (yes, really!)
9. Harvest when the leaves turn brown
10. Dig up the roots and dry them

**Patience level:** 🧘🧘🧘🧘🧘 (Very patient!)

---

### Ginger 🌱

**What you need:**
- A ginger root (from the grocery store)
- A wide pot
- Potting soil
- Water
- Warm spot

**Steps:**
1. Soak ginger root overnight in water
2. Cut into 1-inch pieces
3. Fill pot with soil
4. Plant pieces 1 inch deep
5. Water gently
6. Keep warm and humid
7. Mist with water daily
8. Wait 2-3 months for shoots
9. Harvest when you see green growth

**Patience level:** 🧘🧘🧘 (Patient!)

---

### Garlic 🧄

**What you need:**
- A garlic bulb (from the grocery store)
- Garden soil or big pot
- Water
- Sunshine

**Steps:**
1. Break a bulb into individual cloves
2. Plant each clove 2 inches deep
3. Pointy end UP
4. Space them 6 inches apart
5. Water well
6. Put in sunny spot
7. Water regularly
8. Wait for green shoots (2 weeks)
9. Harvest when leaves turn brown (8 months)

**Patience level:** 🧘🧘🧘🧘 (Very patient!)

---

### Honey 🍯

**The honest truth:** You can't really "grow" honey. Bees make it!

**Options:**
1. Buy it at the store (easiest!)
2. Find a local beekeeper (best quality!)
3. Keep bees yourself (advanced — but SO cool! 🐝)

**To find local honey:**
- Farmers markets
- Health food stores
- Ask around — someone nearby probably keeps bees!

---

### Lemon 🍋

**What you need:**
- A baby lemon tree (from a garden store)
- Big pot or garden spot
- Soil
- Water
- Lots of sunshine

**Steps:**
1. Buy a Meyer lemon tree (best for beginners!)
2. Plant in a big pot with drainage holes
3. Use good soil
4. Water when top inch of soil is dry
5. Give it LOTS of sun (6+ hours)
6. Feed with citrus fertilizer
7. Wait 2-3 years for lemons (patience!)
8. Pick lemons when they're bright yellow

**Patience level:** 🧘🧘🧘🧘🧘 (Very patient — but worth it!)

## Chapter 7: How to Make Healing Tea ☕

### The Magic Healing Tea

This tea is like a superhero drink. It fights sickness, reduces pain, and makes you feel warm and cozy!

### Ingredients

- 1 cup hot water 💧
- Thumb-sized piece of ginger 🌱
- Pinch of turmeric ✨
- 1 spoonful of honey 🍯
- Half a lemon 🍋
- Pinch of black pepper 🖤

### Instructions

**Step 1: Boil Water**
Heat up water until it's boiling. Or just use very hot water from the tap.

**Step 2: Prepare the Ginger**
- Wash the ginger root
- Use a grater or knife to grate a thumb-sized piece
- More ginger = stronger tea
- Less ginger = milder tea

**Step 3: Add Everything**
Put in a mug:
- The grated ginger
- A pinch of turmeric (just a little — it's strong!)
- A pinch of black pepper (this helps your body use the turmeric!)

**Step 4: Pour Hot Water**
Pour the hot water over everything. Let it sit for 5 minutes.

**Step 5: Add the Good Stuff**
- Squeeze in half a lemon
- Add a spoonful of honey
- Stir well

**Step 6: Drink!**
Sip slowly while it's warm. Ahhhhh! 😌

### Variations

**Extra Strong (when you're really sick):**
- Double the ginger
- Add a clove of garlic (yes, really!)
- Extra turmeric

**Sweet Version (for kids):**
- Extra honey
- Less ginger
- Add a cinnamon stick 🎄

**Bedtime Version:**
- Add chamomile tea
- Extra honey
- Drink 30 minutes before bed

### When to Drink It

| Situation | How Often |
|-----------|-----------|
| Feeling sick | 3x daily |
| Sore throat | Every few hours |
| Stomach ache | 2-3x daily |
| Just because | 1x daily (prevention!) |
| Cold day | Whenever you want! |
