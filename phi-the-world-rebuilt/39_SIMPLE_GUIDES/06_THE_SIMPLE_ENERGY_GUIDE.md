## ⚡ PART 1: MAKE YOUR OWN ELECTRICITY

### ☀️ Solar Panels at Phi-Angles

Most people put solar panels flat on their roof. That's okay. But you know what's better?

**Angle them like nature would.** 🌿

The golden ratio (phi = 1.618) shows up everywhere — in sunflowers, pinecones, hurricanes. It also shows up in the *best* angle for catching sunlight.

**The Phi-Angle Rule:**
- Tilt your panels at **34.7°** from the ground (that's 90° ÷ φ² ≈ 34.7°)
- Face them true south (in the northern hemisphere) or true north (in the southern)
- Space them so one panel's shadow doesn't touch the next

**Why does this work?** Because the sun moves in an arc. At 34.7°, your panel catches the *most total light* across the whole day — morning, noon, and evening — not just the midday peak. It's like the difference between cupping your hands flat versus cupping them at just the right angle to catch rain.

**Step-by-Step:**

1. **Pick your spot** — South-facing roof, deck, or yard. No shade from trees.
2. **Get panels** — A 100-watt panel costs ~$50-80 online. Start with ONE to learn.
3. **Build the frame** — Untreated wood (2×4s), cut to make a triangle frame. The hypotenuse is where the panel sits.
4. **Set the angle** — 34.7° from flat. Use a cheap angle finder app on your phone.
5. **Wire it up** — Panel → charge controller → battery → inverter → your stuff.

```
         ☀️ SUN ☀️
           ↓ ↓ ↓
    ┌─────────────────┐
    │  ☀️ SOLAR PANEL  │  ← Tilted at 34.7°
    │    (100 watt)    │
    └────────┬────────┘
             │
    ┌────────▼────────┐
    │ Charge Controller│  ← $15-25
    └────────┬────────┘
             │
    ┌────────▼────────┐
    │    BATTERY       │  ← Stores the power
    │  (12V, 50Ah+)   │
    └────────┬────────┘
             │
    ┌────────▼────────┐
    │    INVERTER      │  ← Turns battery power into
    │  (300W+ is fine) │     wall-outlet-style power
    └────────┬────────┘
             │
         💡 LIGHT!
```

**Cost for a starter system:** $150-250. Enough to charge phones, run lights, and power a small fan.

### 🌬️ Simple Wind Turbine

If you live somewhere windy — coast, hill, open plains — wind can work even when the sun doesn't shine.

**The Simplest Build:**

You need:
- A car alternator (junkyard, ~$20-30)
- PVC pipes (4 buckets, 24" long, cut in half lengthwise)
- A tail fin (plywood, shaped like an arrow)
- A tower (old TV antenna pole, or a strong wooden post)
- A battery and charge controller

**How it works:**
The wind pushes the PVC "scoops" (called a Savonius rotor). They spin. The alternator turns that spin into electricity. The battery stores it.

```
        ┌─── PVC Scoop ───┐
        │                  │
   ┌────┤   ╔═══════╗     │
   │    │   ║ ALTERN║     │
   │    └───║ ORATOR║─────┤
   │        ╚═══╤═══╝     │
   │            │         │
   └─── PVC ───┤         │
               │         │
          ┌────▼────┐    │
          │ BATTERY │    │
          └─────────┘
               │
    ┌──────────▼──────────┐
    │   TAIL FIN          │
    │   (points into wind)│
    └─────────────────────┘
```

**Sweet spot:** Your turbine should start spinning in **8 mph wind** and produce full power at **25 mph**.

**Pro tip:** Combine solar AND wind. Sun shines when it's calm. Wind blows when it's cloudy. They're perfect partners. 🤝

## 🔋 PART 2: STORE YOUR ENERGY

Batteries are your bank account. Sunlight and wind are your paycheck.

### Battery Basics (No PhD Required)

| Battery Type | Cost | Lifespan | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lead-acid** (car batteries) | Cheap! $50-100 | 3-5 years | Getting started |
| **LiFePO4** (lithium iron phosphate) | Medium $200-400 | 10+ years | Long-term savings |
| **Salt-water** | DIY ~$100 | 5+ years | No-toxicity homes |

**The Golden Rule of Batteries:**
- Never drain below 50% (for lead-acid) or 20% (for lithium)
- Keep them charged — a dead battery is a sad battery
- Store in a cool, dry place (not freezing, not blazing hot)

**Size Your Battery Bank:**

A family of four typically uses:
- ~5 kWh per day (modest home, LED lights, efficient appliances)
- ~15 kWh per day (normal American home with AC)

For a modest setup: **100Ah × 12V = 1,200Wh = 1.2 kWh**

That's about 1/4 of a modest daily use. Scale up as needed!

## 🍳 PART 4: COOK WITHOUT GAS OR ELECTRICITY

### The Solar Cooker ☀️🍕

Yes, you can cook a pizza with sunlight. No, this is not a joke.

**Build a Box Solar Cooker (costs ~$20):**

You need:
- A cardboard box (big enough for a pot)
- Aluminum foil
- Black paint or black construction paper
- Clear plastic wrap or an old glass picture frame
- A ruler and scissors

**How to build it:**

```
    SUNLIGHT ☀️
        ↓ ↓ ↓
   ┌────▼▼▼▼▼────┐
   │  CLEAR LID   │ ← Traps heat (greenhouse effect)
   │ ┌──────────┐ │
   │ │ BLACK POT│ │ ← Black absorbs ALL the heat
   │ │ (inside) │ │
   │ └──────────┘ │
   │ FOIL WALLS   │ ← Reflects extra light onto the pot
   └──────────────┘
```

1. Line the inside walls with aluminum foil (shiny side in)
2. Paint the bottom black (or lay black paper inside)
3. Make a clear lid with plastic wrap stretched over a frame
4. Cut a reflector flap — a cardboard flap covered in foil that folds UP to bounce more light in
5. Prop the reflector at an angle to catch more sun

**What it can do:**
- On a sunny day: **250-350°F** (120-175°C)
- Bakes bread 🍞
- Cooks rice 🍚
- Roasts vegetables 🥕
- Heats water for tea ☕
- Sterilizes water for drinking 💧

**Cook time:** 1.5-3 hours (slow cooking — like a crockpot powered by the sun)

**Fuel cost: $0.00 forever.**

### Other No-Electricity Cooking Methods:

- **Rocket stove** — Burns tiny sticks efficiently. Cooks a meal with 5 twigs.
- **Haybox (retained heat)** — Bring food to boil, then insulate in a pillow/blanket box. It finishes cooking with no extra energy.
- **Earth oven** — Dig a hole, line with hot rocks, bury your food. Ancient, effective, and delicious.

## 🌍 PART 6: THE PHI-HARMONIC ENERGY INSIGHT

Here's the beautiful part that most people miss:

**Energy systems work best when they follow natural ratios.**

The golden ratio (φ = 1.618) appears in:

- **Panel spacing** — Space panels at φ-ratios of their width for minimum shadow overlap
- **Battery bank sizing** — A 60/40 split (capacity/backup) mirrors φ proportions
- **Energy use balance** — The ideal split between production and storage follows φ
- **Wind turbine blade length** — Blade ratios of 1:1.618 are aerodynamically optimal

Nature figured out efficiency billions of years ago. We just have to listen.
