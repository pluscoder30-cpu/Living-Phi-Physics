# Part 1: Why Your House Should Use the Magic Number

## Chapter 1: The Golden Ratio in Buildings

You know what's cool? The most beautiful buildings in the entire world — the pyramids, the Parthenon, even modern skyscrapers — they ALL use the same number.

**That number is 1.618.**

It's called the **Golden Ratio** (or **phi**, pronounced "fy"). It shows up everywhere in nature: in flowers, seashells, hurricanes, even your own face.

And guess what? It works in houses too.

### The Golden Rule of Rooms

Here's the rule: **If a room is 10 feet wide, it should be 16 feet long.**

```
         10 feet
    ┌──────────────┐
    │              │
    │              │  16 feet
    │              │
    │              │
    └──────────────┘

    10 × 1.618 = 16.18  (close enough to 16!)
```

That's it. That's the ratio. Your eyes see this shape and go: "Yep. That's nice." You don't know why. You just feel it.

### Why It Works

Nobody knows for 100% sure. But here's what we think:

- Your eyes naturally look for patterns
- The golden ratio appears in things we find beautiful (faces, flowers, waves)
- When a room follows this ratio, your brain goes "ahhhh" instead of "hmmmm"
- It feels balanced without being boring

### Other Golden Ratio Tricks

- **Doors:** Make your door 1.618 times taller than it is wide. A 3-foot door should be about 5 feet tall.
- **Windows:** Make them golden ratio rectangles too.
- **The whole house:** If your house is 20 feet wide, try making it 32 feet long.

```
    THE GOLDEN HOUSE

         20 feet
    ┌────────────────────┐
    │                    │
    │   ┌──┐    ┌──┐    │
    │   │  │    │  │    │  32 feet
    │   │  │    │  │    │
    │   └──┘    └──┘    │
    │        ┌──┐       │
    │        │  │       │
    └────────┴──┴───────┘
              door

    20 × 1.618 = 32.36  (close enough to 32!)
```

## Chapter 4: How to Make Adobe Bricks

Adobe bricks are just dried mud. Humans have been making them for 10,000 years. You can too.

### What You Need
- Dirt (clay is best)
- Water
- Straw or dried grass
- A wooden box (your mold)
- Sun

### Steps

```
STEP 1: DIG
    🏜️ → 🕳️

Dig up some dirt. Clay-heavy dirt works best.
If you squeeze it and it holds its shape, perfect.


STEP 2: MIX
    Dirt + Water + Straw → MUD!

Add water slowly. You want thick mud, not soup.
Add straw. Mix it up with your hands or feet.
(Stomping in the mud is actually the fun part!)


STEP 3: MOLD
    ┌─────┐
    │░░░░░│  ← Pack mud into a wooden box
    │░░░░░│     (open on top)
    └─────┘


STEP 4: DRY
    ☀️☀️☀️☀️

Leave bricks in the sun for 3-5 days.
Flip them once after 2 days.
They're ready when they're hard.


STEP 5: STACK AND BUILD!
    ┌──┐┌──┐┌──┐
    ┌──┐┌──┐┌──┐
    ┌──┐┌──┐┌──┐

    That's a wall! (almost)
```

### Brick Size

A good brick size:
- 10 inches long
- 5 inches wide  
- 4 inches tall

Make your mold this size and you're good.

### How Many Do You Need?

For a small house (10 ft × 16 ft):
- Walls: about 400-600 bricks
- You can make 20-30 per day
- That's about 2-3 weeks of brick-making

**Pro tip:** Get friends to help! More stomping = more fun.

```
    │                             │
    │                             │
    └─────────────────────────────┘

STEP 2: STACK THE BALES

    │░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░│
```

### Why Straw Bales Are Awesome

| Feature | Why It's Good |
|---------|---------------|
| Warm in winter | Straw traps air, like a blanket |
| Cool in summer | Same reason — it insulates both ways |
| Cheap | $3-5 per bale, you need maybe 100 |
| Strong | When plastered, they can hold up a roof |
| Fire resistant | Plastered straw bales are hard to burn |

### Window Herbs 🌿

Every window gets a planter box:

- Mint (makes tea wonderful)
- Rosemary (makes everything taste better)
- Chives (easy to grow, never dies)

### Kitchen Fish Tank (Aquaponics) 🐟

```
    ┌──────────────┐
    │  PLANTS 🌱   │  ← Plants grow here
    ├──────────────┤
    │ ~~~~~~~~~~~~ │
    │ ~~~ FISH 🐟 ~│  ← Fish live here
    │ ~~~~~~~~~~~~ │
    └──────┬───────┘
           │
    Water flows: fish waste → plants → clean water → fish
    It's a circle! The fish feed the plants, the plants clean the water.
```

**How to set it up:**
1. Get a big tank (old aquarium works)
2. Fill it with water
3. Add fish (tilapia eat anything, goldfish work too)
4. Put a basket of plants on top (roots hang in the water)
5. The fish poop feeds the plants!
6. The plants clean the water for the fish!
7. You eat the fish AND the plants!

## Chapter 8: The House That Makes Energy ☀️

### Solar Panels Made Simple

Solar panels turn sunlight into electricity. Here's how to set them up:

### The Magic Angle

**Tilt your panels at: YOUR LATITUDE × 0.618**

If you live at 40° latitude:
```
40 × 0.618 = 24.7°

So tilt your panels about 25 degrees from flat.
```

### Direction

- **Northern hemisphere** (USA, Europe, most of Asia): Face panels **SOUTH**
- **Southern hemisphere** (Australia, South America): Face panels **NORTH**

### What One Panel Can Do

A single $100-200 solar panel can:
- Power 10 LED lights
- Charge 4-5 phones per day
- Run a small fan
- Power a radio

That's real electricity for $100.

### Simple Solar Setup

```
    ☀️ SUN ☀️
        ↓
    ╔═══════════╗
    ║ SOLAR     ║  ← Panel (tilted at your latitude × 0.618)
    ║ PANEL     ║
    ╚═════╤═════╝
          │
          ▼
    ┌──────────┐
    │ BATTERY  │  ← Stores the energy (old car battery works!)
    └────┬─────┘
         │
    ┌────┴─────┐
    │ INVERTER │  ← Changes battery power to regular wall power
    └────┬─────┘
         │
         ▼
    ┌─────────┐
    │ LIGHTS  │  ← Your stuff!
```
