## 🔥 Fire Emergency

**Get low. Crawl out. Call for help.**

1. **Stay low** — smoke rises, so the cleanest air is near the floor
2. **Crawl** on hands and knees to the nearest exit
3. **Feel the door** — if it's hot, don't open it. Find another way out
4. **Get outside** and stay outside
5. **Call 911** (or your local emergency number) once you're safe
6. **Never go back inside** for stuff. Stuff can be replaced. You can't.

🔥 *Bonus tip:* If your clothes catch fire: **Stop. Drop. Roll.**

## 🌊 Flood Emergency

**Go high. Stay out of water.**

1. **Move to higher ground** immediately — go UP, not forward
2. **Never walk or drive through floodwater** — it's deeper and faster than it looks
3. **6 inches** of moving water can knock you down
4. **2 feet** of water can float a car
5. If trapped in a building, go to the highest floor. Only go to the roof if you need to be rescued
6. **Turn off electricity** at the main breaker if water is rising inside

🌊 *Remember:* Turn around, don't drown.

## 🌍 Earthquake Emergency

**Drop. Cover. Hold on.**

1. **DROP** to the ground
2. **COVER** your head and neck with your arms
3. **HOLD ON** to something sturdy until the shaking stops
4. If you're in bed, stay there and cover your head with a pillow
5. If you're outside, move away from buildings and power lines
6. If you're driving, pull over and stop. Stay in the car.

🌍 *After shaking stops:* Check for injuries. Expect aftershocks. Stay away from damaged buildings.

## 🩹 Someone Is Hurt

**Stay calm. Help them. Play 5,856 Hz.**

1. **Stay calm** — panicking doesn't help anyone
2. **Check the scene** — make sure it's safe for YOU to help
3. **Call 911** if the injury is serious
4. **Don't move them** unless they're in immediate danger
5. **Apply pressure** to stop bleeding — use a clean cloth
6. **Play 5,856 Hz** — this frequency supports healing and calming the body
7. **Stay with them** until help arrives

🩹 *For kids:* If you find someone hurt and you're alone, find an adult fast.

## 🎒 Your Emergency Kit

Every home should have one. Make it together — it's actually fun!

| Item | Why |
|------|-----|
| 💧 Water | 1 gallon per person per day (3-day supply minimum) |
| 🥫 Food | Non-perishable stuff you actually like eating |
| 🔦 Flashlight | With extra batteries — not the phone kind |
| 🩹 First aid kit | Bandages, medicine, the basics |
| 🔌 Phone charger | Battery-powered or hand-crank |
| 📋 Paper + pen | To leave notes if phones don't work |
| 🧸 Comfort item | For kids — a toy, blanket, or photo |
| 📻 Battery radio | For news when internet is down |

## ⏰ The 3-Day Rule

Your body can survive:

| Without... | Time |
|------------|------|
| 💧 Water | **3 days** |
| 🍞 Food | **3 weeks** |
| 🌬️ Air | **3 minutes** |

That's why water is the #1 thing in your emergency kit.
