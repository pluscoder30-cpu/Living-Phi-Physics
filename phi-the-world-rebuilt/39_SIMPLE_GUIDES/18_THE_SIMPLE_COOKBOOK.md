# 🌅 BREAKFAST RECIPES (1-10)

## 1. 🥣 BASIC OATMEAL
⏱ 5 minutes · 💰 $

**Ingredients:**
- 1 cup rolled oats
- 2 cups water (or milk)
- Pinch of salt
- 1 tablespoon honey or sugar
- ½ cup fruit (banana, berries, whatever you have)

**Steps:**
1. Put oats, water, and salt in a pot
2. Turn heat to medium, stir every 30 seconds
3. When it's thick and bubbly (about 3 minutes), turn off heat
4. Pour into a bowl
5. Add honey and fruit on top — done!

---

## 2. 🍳 SCRAMBLED EGGS
⏱ 5 minutes · 💰 $

**Ingredients:**
- 2-3 eggs
- 1 tablespoon butter or oil
- Salt and pepper
- Optional: cheese, veggies, hot sauce

**Steps:**
1. Crack eggs into a bowl, add salt, and whisk with a fork
2. Heat butter in a pan on medium
3. Pour in eggs, stir gently with a spatula
4. When eggs look almost done but still a little wet, take them off heat (they keep cooking!)
5. Add cheese or whatever you like

---

## 3. 🥞 EASY PANCAKES
⏱ 15 minutes · 💰 $

**Ingredients:**
- 1 cup flour
- 1 egg
- 1 cup milk
- 2 tablespoons butter (melted)
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- Pinch of salt

**Steps:**
1. Mix all dry stuff in a bowl
2. Add milk, egg, and melted butter — stir until smooth (lumps are okay!)
3. Heat a pan on medium, add a little butter
4. Pour ¼ cup batter, flip when bubbles appear on top (about 2 minutes each side)
5. Stack 'em up, add syrup!

---

## 4. 🍌 ONE-INGREDIENT BANANA PANCAKES
⏱ 8 minutes · 💰 $

**Ingredients:**
- 1 ripe banana
- 2 eggs

**Steps:**
1. Mash banana in a bowl with a fork
2. Crack in eggs, mix together
3. Heat a pan with a little butter on medium
4. Pour small circles, cook 1-2 minutes per side
5. They're thin and delicate — be gentle flipping! Top with whatever you want

## 5. 🥤 GREEN POWER SMOOTHIE
⏱ 3 minutes · 💰 $

**Ingredients:**
- 1 banana (frozen works great)
- 1 handful spinach (you won't taste it, promise!)
- ½ cup frozen fruit (mango, pineapple, berries)
- 1 cup milk or water
- 1 tablespoon peanut butter (optional)

**Steps:**
1. Put everything in a blender
2. Blend on high for 30-60 seconds
3. If too thick, add more liquid. If too thin, add more frozen fruit
4. Pour and drink immediately
5. Feel like a superhero 💪

## 8. 🍳 TOAD IN THE HOLE (EGG IN BREAD)
⏱ 5 minutes · 💰 $

**Ingredients:**
- 1 slice bread
- 1 egg
- Butter
- Salt and pepper

**Steps:**
1. Butter both sides of bread
2. Cut a hole in the center (use a cup or cookie cutter)

## 10. 🧇 2-INGREDIENT WAFFLES
⏱ 10 minutes · 💰 $

**Ingredients:**
- 1 cup self-rising flour (or regular flour + 2 tsp baking powder)
- 1 cup Greek yogurt

**Steps:**
1. Mix flour and yogurt until you get a thick dough
2. If too thick, add a splash of milk
3. Heat waffle iron, grease it
4. Pour batter in, cook until golden (3-5 minutes)
5. Top with syrup, fruit, or whipped cream!

## 22. 🍛 EASY FRIED RICE
⏱ 12 minutes · 💰 $

**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups cooked rice (day-old is best!)
- 2 eggs
- 1 cup frozen peas and carrots
- 3 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon oil
- 2 green onions, sliced

**Steps:**
1. Heat oil in a big pan or wok on HIGH
2. Add veggies, stir-fry 2 minutes
3. Push to side, scramble eggs on other side
4. Add rice, break up clumps, stir everything together
5. Pour soy sauce around edges, add green onions — DONE!

# 📝 COOKING TIPS FOR BEGINNERS

🍳 **ALWAYS** wash your hands before cooking
🔥 **NEVER** leave a stove unattended
🧼 **CLEAN** as you go — makes the end easier
👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 **ASK** an adult for help with sharp knives and hot ovens
⏱ **TIMERS** are your best friend — set them every time
👃 **SMELL** and **TASTE** as you go — adjust seasonings!
🔄 **MISE EN PLACE** — read the whole recipe before starting, get all ingredients out first

---

# 🎯 PHI-RATIO COOKING

Some recipes use the phi ratio (φ = 1.618) for ingredient balancing:

- **Sweet:Savory** — Use 1.618 parts sweet to 1 part savory
- **Creamy:Crunchy** — 1.618 parts creamy to 1 part crunchy
- **Salt:Fat** — 1 part salt to 1.618 parts fat

Don't overthink it! Just eyeball it and trust your taste buds. Cooking is about feeling, not measuring.
