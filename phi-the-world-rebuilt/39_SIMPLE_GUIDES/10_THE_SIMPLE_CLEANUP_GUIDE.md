## 🌊 HOW TO CLEAN DIRTY WATER

### ΦWater-1: The Simple Filter

**What you need:**
- A 2-liter plastic bottle (cut the bottom off)
- Baking soda — 2 tablespoons 🧂
- White vinegar — 1/4 cup 🍶
- Sand — clean, fine sand 🏖️
- Gravel — small pebbles 🪨
- Cotton balls or clean cloth 🧻

**How to build it:**

1. Cut the bottom off the bottle. Flip it upside down (cap side down).
2. Put cotton balls in the neck — this is your first layer 🧻
3. Add sand on top of the cotton — about 3 inches thick 🏖️
4. Add gravel on top of the sand — about 2 inches thick 🪨
5. Pour the dirty water through! 🌧️

**The magic trick — ΦWater-1:**

For really dirty water, add magic first:
- Add 2 tablespoons of baking soda to the dirty water
- Stir it up! It will fizz! 🫧
- Then add 1/4 cup of vinegar — MORE fizzing! 💥
- Let it sit for 10 minutes
- The gunk settles to the bottom
- Pour the clean water through your filter

**Why it works:** Baking soda + vinegar = CO₂ bubbles that grab dirt particles and pull them down. The sand and gravel catch everything else. The cotton catches the tiny stuff. Clean water comes out the bottom! ✨

## 🗑️ HOW TO CLEAN UP TRASH

### Recycling + Composting: The Trash Tamer

**The 3-Bin System:**

Set up 3 bins in your house or yard:

```
🟢 GREEN BIN = Compost (food, paper, yard waste)
🔵 BLUE BIN = Recyclables (plastic, glass, metal, cardboard)
⚫ BLACK BIN = Trash (everything else — try to make this one almost empty!)
```

**What goes where:**

### 🟢 COMPOST (Green Bin)
- Vegetable and fruit peels 🥕
- Eggshells 🥚
- Coffee grounds and filters ☕
- Paper towels and napkins 🧻
- Dead leaves 🍂
- Grass clippings 🌿
- Hair and fur 💇
- Wooden toothpicks 🪥

### 🔵 RECYCLE (Blue Bin)
- Plastic bottles (rinse them!) 🧴
- Glass jars (rinse them!) 🫙
- Aluminum cans (rinse them!) 🥫
- Cardboard boxes (flatten them!) 📦
- Paper (no food stains!) 📄
- Metal (rinsed!) 🔩

### ⚫ TRASH (Black Bin — try to keep this EMPTY!)
- Broken glass (wrap it first!) 💎
- Styrofoam 🤷
- Dirty diapers 🍼
- Greasy pizza boxes 🍕

**The goal:** Make your GREEN and BLUE bins HUGE and your BLACK bin TINY. If your trash bin is full every week, you're doing it wrong! If it's nearly empty — you're a recycling champion! 🏆
