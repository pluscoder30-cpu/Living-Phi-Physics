## 1. HOW MUCH WATER YOU NEED

### Daily Minimums

```
┌─────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────┐
│ Person (resting)    │ 1 gallon (3.8L) / day        │
│ Person (active)     │ 1.5-2 gallons / day          │
│ Child               │ 0.5-1 gallon / day           │
│ Pregnant/nursing    │ 1.5 gallons / day            │
│ Hot climate         │ 2+ gallons / day             │
└─────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────┘
```

### Quick Math: Family of 4

```
4 people × 2 gallons = 8 gallons/day
8 gallons × 7 days  = 56 gallons/week
56 gallons × 4 weeks = 224 gallons/month
```

**Store at minimum 2 weeks supply for your family.**

## 2. HOW TO FIND WATER

### Natural Sources (Best → Worst)

```
★★★★★  Running streams/rivers (moving water = cleaner)
★★★★☆  Springs (natural filtration through rock)
★★★★☆  Rainwater collection
★★★☆☆  Ponds/lakes (stagnant, needs treatment)
★★★☆☆  Dew collection (morning only)
★★☆☆☆  Solar still from ground moisture
★★☆☆☆  Water from plants (transpiration bag)
★☆☆☆☆  Muddy puddles (last resort, heavy treatment)
```

### Finding Hidden Water

- **Listen** — Running water makes noise. Follow downhill slopes.
- **Follow animals** — Birds, bees, and tracks lead to water.
- **Look for green** — Lush vegetation means underground water.
- **Valleys and ravines** — Water flows downhill. Check lowest points.

### Water From Plants

```
SAFE:                          AVOID:
• Coconut water                • Milky sap plants (poisonous)
• Cactus fruit juice           • Plants near roads
• Tree sap (maple, birch)      • Algae-covered water
• Vine stems (cut and drink)   • Water with chemical smell
• Morning dew on grass
```

## 3. HOW TO TELL IF WATER IS SAFE

### The Five Senses Test

```
LOOK:
  ✓ Clear, not cloudy
  ✓ No green/brown color
  ✓ No floating particles
  ✗ Milky/white = unsafe
  ✗ Brown/rusty = needs heavy filtration

SMELL:
  ✓ Odorless
  ✗ Rotten egg = sulfur bacteria (boil it)
  ✗ Chemical smell = contamination (avoid)

TASTE (tiny sip only if desperate):
  ✓ Neutral taste
  ✗ Salty = undrinkable (needs desalination)
  ✗ Metallic = heavy metals (filter + boil)

LOCATION:
  ✓ Away from industrial areas
  ✓ Upstream from human activity
  ✗ Near farms = pesticide runoff
  ✗ Near roads = chemical runoff
```

### Warning Signs — NEVER drink water that:
- Has dead animals nearby
- Has blue-green algae (toxic)
- Comes from mining areas
- Has chemical smell/taste
- Is near industrial sites
- Has white foam that doesn't dissipate

### A. BOILING (Most Reliable)

```
    ╔══════════════════════════════════════╗
    ║         WATER BOILING METHOD         ║
    ╚══════════════════════════════════════╝

    Step 1: Pre-filter through cloth
            Dirty water ──→ 🧻cloth ──→ Cleaner water

    Step 2: Bring to rolling boil
            🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥
            ╔═══════════╗
            ║  ♨️ ♨️ ♨️  ║  ← Rolling boil
            ║  ~~~~     ║
            ╚═══════════╝

    Step 3: Boil for:
            • 1 minute at sea level
            • 3 minutes above 6,500 ft
            • 5 minutes above 10,000 ft

    Step 4: Let cool naturally

    Cost: Fuel only
    Time: 5-10 minutes
    Effectiveness: 99.9%
```

### B. ΦWater-1 (Chemical Treatment)

```
    ╔══════════════════════════════════════╗
    ║        ΦWATER-1 TREATMENT           ║
    ╚══════════════════════════════════════╝

    Ingredients:
    • 1 gallon clean water
    • 8 drops unscented liquid chlorine bleach
      (sodium hypochlorite 5.25-8.25%)

    Method:
    ┌─────────────────────────────────┐
    │  1. Filter water through cloth  │
    │  2. Add 8 drops bleach/gallon   │
    │  3. Stir and let stand 30 min   │
    │  4. Should smell faintly of     │
    │     chlorine                    │
    │  5. If no smell, repeat and     │
    │     wait 15 more min            │
    │  6. Safe to drink               │
    └─────────────────────────────────┘

    ⚠️  Use UNSCENTED household bleach ONLY
    ⚠️  NOT splash-less, NOT color-safe

    Cost: ~$0.01 per gallon
    Time: 45 minutes
    Effectiveness: 99.9%
```

### Build Instructions

```
    Step 1: Drill/poke 15-20 small holes in bottom of top bucket
    ┌───────────────────────┐
    │ ○ ○ ○ ○ ○ ○ ○ ○ ○ ○ │  ← 1/8 inch holes
    │ ○ ○ ○ ○ ○ ○ ○ ○ ○ ○ │     spaced evenly

    ═══════════════════════════════════════════════

    ┌───────────────────────┐
    │ ░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░ │ ← Gravel (1/2 inch)

    │ ███████████████████████│ ← Activated charcoal (2 inches)
    │ ▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒ │ ← Fine sand (2 inches)
    │ ░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░ │ ← Gravel (1/2 inch)
    │ ══════════════════════│ ← Coffee filter/cloth
    │ ○ ○ ○ ○ ○ ○ ○ ○ ○ ○ │ ← Holes in bucket
    └───────────┬───────────┘
                │ drip
                ▼
    BOTTOM BUCKET:
    ┌───────────────────────┐
    │                       │
    │       💧💧💧💧       │ ← Clean water collects here
    │                       │
    └───────────────────────┘

    Step 4: Pour dirty water SLOWLY into top
    Step 5: Let drip through (30-60 min per gallon)
    Step 6: REPEAT filtration 3 times for best results
    Step 7: STILL boil or treat the filtered water!

    ⚠️  This removes dirt, sediment, and some chemicals
    ⚠️  This does NOT kill all bacteria/viruses
    ⚠️  ALWAYS follow with boiling or chemical treatment
```

## 8. HOW TO STORE WATER

### Container Rules

```
USE:                              NEVER USE:
✓ Food-grade plastic (HDPE, #2)  ✗ Milk containers (protein residue)
✓ Glass jars (tight lids)        ✗ Juice containers (sugar)
✓ Commercial water jugs          ✗ Chemical containers
✓ Clean milk jugs (ONE-TIME)     ✗ Cardboard
                                 ✗ Metal (rusts)
```

### Storage Rules

```
✓ Cool (50-70°F / 10-21°C)
✓ Dark (no sunlight — algae grows in light)
✓ Away from chemicals (gasoline, pesticides)
✓ Off concrete floors (use pallets or shelves)

ROTATION SCHEDULE:
• Commercial bottled water: 2-5 years
• Tap water in clean container: 6 months
• Treated water (bleach): 1 year max

### How to Test

```
1. Buy pH test strips ($5-10 for 100 strips)
2. Dip strip in water for 1 second
3. Wait 30 seconds
4. Compare color to chart

┌─────┬─────┬──────────────────────────────┐
│ pH  │Good?│ Fix                          │
├─────┼─────┼──────────────────────────────┤
│ 5.5 │ NO  │ Add baking soda (1 tsp/gal) │
│ 6.0 │ NO  │ Add baking soda (1/2 tsp/gal)│
│ 6.5 │ YES │ —                            │
│ 7.0 │ YES │ — (ideal)                   │
│ 7.5 │ YES │ —                            │
│ 8.0 │ NO  │ Add lemon juice (1 tsp/gal) │
│ 8.5 │ NO  │ Add lemon juice (1 tbsp/gal)│
└─────┴─────┴──────────────────────────────┘
```

### Turbidity Test

```
Fill clear glass, hold over text:

✓ CLEAR: Can read text through it = Good
△ SLIGHT: Text blurry but visible = Filter first
✗ CLOUDY: Cannot see text = Heavy treatment needed
```

## 12. EMERGENCY QUICK REFERENCE

### The 4-3-2-1 Rule

```
Without water, you can survive:

┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│  4 MINUTES without air             │
│  4 HOURS without shelter (extreme) │
│  4 DAYS without water              │
│  4 WEEKS without food              │
└─────────────────────────────────────┘

WATER IS PRIORITY #1 AFTER SHELTER.
```

### Emergency Actions (First 24 Hours)

```
HOUR 1:
  □ Fill every container with tap water (if running)
  □ Fill bathtubs, sinks, pots, bottles
  □ Gather any water bottles in house

HOUR 2:
  □ Locate nearest water source (river, lake, pond)
  □ Gather collection materials (buckets, jugs)

HOUR 3-6:
  □ Set up rainwater collection if rain expected
  □ Build solar still if near moist ground
  □ Collect dew early morning if possible

HOUR 6-12:
  □ Set up sand/charcoal filter
  □ Begin boiling/treating collected water
  □ Start water rotation schedule

HOUR 12-24:
  □ Complete full water system
  □ Establish daily routine
  □ Calculate 7-day water needs
```

### Last Resort: No Equipment Purification

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║     DESPERATION: NO EQUIPMENT PURIFICATION          ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

If you have NOTHING:

1. FIND the cleanest water possible
   (running water upstream > stagnant)

2. STRAIN through cloth
   (removes bugs, leaves, large particles)

3. SOLAR HEAT
   • Fill clear container
   • Leave in sun 6+ hours
   • UV kills bacteria

4. or FIRE HEAT
   • Boil in any metal container
   • Any boil is better than no boil

⚠️  DIRTY WATER IS BETTER THAN NO WATER (if you treat it)
⚠️  IF YOU MUST DRINK UNTREATED: Take small sips only
