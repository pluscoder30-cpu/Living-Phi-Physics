# MENTAL HEALTH IN A COLLAPSE

---

## Why Mental Health Matters

- If you're scared, you make bad decisions
- If you're lonely, you get sick
- If you're hopeless, you stop trying
- Mental health IS survival

---

## Common Feelings in a Collapse

| Feeling | What It Feels Like | Why It Happens |
|---------|-------------------|----------------|
| **Fear** | Racing heart, can't sleep, always alert | Your body is protecting you |
| **Grief** | Sadness, emptiness, missing what was | You lost things that mattered |
| **Anger** | Frustration, wanting to lash out | You feel powerless |
| **Loneliness** | Missing friends, family, normal life | The world changed overnight |
| **Hopelessness** | "Nothing will ever be normal again" | Your brain can't see a future yet |

---

## How to Deal With Each Feeling

### Fear
- Breathe slowly: 4 counts in, 7 hold, 8 out
- Play 40,135 Hz frequency
- Remind yourself: fear kept you alive this long

### Grief
- Cry if you need to — it's not weakness, it's processing
- Talk to someone about what you lost
- Write in a journal — even one sentence a day

### Anger
- Walk away before you say something you'll regret
- Squeeze a pillow, push against a wall, do push-ups
- Play 528 Hz — the love frequency

### Loneliness
- Find people — even one person to talk to
- Talk to neighbors you never talked to before
- Build community — you were never meant to do this alone

### Hopelessness
- Remember: you survived the collapse
- You can survive anything
- Write down one small thing you're grateful for today

## Emergency Breathing Exercise

When fear or panic hits, do this:

```
  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │              4-7-8 BREATHING EXERCISE                       │
  │                                                             │
  │   STEP 1: BREATHE IN                                      │
  │   ════════════════════                                      │
  │   ┌─────────────────────────────┐                          │
  │   │ IN: 1... 2... 3... 4...    │  ← 4 counts              │
  │   │                             │                          │
  │   │     ░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░     │                          │
  │   └─────────────────────────────┘                          │
  │                                                             │
  │   STEP 2: HOLD                                             │
  │   ════════════════════                                      │
  │   ┌─────────────────────────────┐                          │
  │   │ HOLD: 1... 2... 3... 4...  │  ← 7 counts              │
  │   │         5... 6... 7...     │                          │
  │   │     ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓    │                          │
  │   └─────────────────────────────┘                          │
  │                                                             │
  │   STEP 3: BREATHE OUT                                     │
  │   ════════════════════                                      │
  │   ┌─────────────────────────────┐                          │
  │   │ OUT: 1... 2... 3... 4...   │  ← 8 counts              │
  │   │         5... 6... 7... 8...│                          │
  │   │     ······················· │                          │
  │   └─────────────────────────────┘                          │
  │                                                             │
  │   REPEAT 4 TIMES. Your body will calm down.               │
  └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Step 1:** Breathe IN through your nose for **4 counts**
**Step 2:** HOLD your breath for **7 counts**
**Step 3:** Breathe OUT through your mouth for **8 counts**
**Repeat 4 times.**

This activates your parasympathetic nervous system — the "rest and digest" mode. It's free medicine.
