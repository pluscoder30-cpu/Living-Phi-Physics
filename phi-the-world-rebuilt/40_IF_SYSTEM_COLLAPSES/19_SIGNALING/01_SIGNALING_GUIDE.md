# SIGNALING FOR RESCUE

When you need help, you need to send a clear signal that someone can see or hear. This guide shows you how to signal for rescue using simple tools and methods.

## HOW TO SIGNAL FOR HELP

### Ground Signals (Visible from Air)

Ground signals are large symbols made on the ground so planes or helicopters can see them from above.

**SOS**
Three short, three long, three short. Make this with rocks, sticks, sand, or anything that stands out against the ground.

```
  SOS Ground Signal

    O     O     O
   / \   / \   / \
  O---O O---O O---O
   \ /   \ /   \ /
    O     O     O

   Short  Long  Short
```

**X**
Means "need medical help." Make a big X that can be seen from the air.

**V**
Means "require assistance." Make a large V shape.

**Arrow**
Means "going this direction." Point the arrow the direction you are traveling.

### Fire Signals

Fire is one of the best signals because it can be seen day and night.

**3 fires in a triangle**
This is the universal distress signal. Space them about 100 feet apart in a triangle.

**Fire + green leaves**
Add green leaves or wet grass to a hot fire. This creates thick white smoke that can be seen for miles.

**Fire at night**
At night, fire is visible from much farther away. Keep the fire burning bright.

```
  Fire Triangle Signal

          🔥
         /  \
        /    \
       /      \
      🔥------🔥

  3 fires, 100 ft apart
  Visible from air
```

### Mirror/Reflective Signals

A mirror or any shiny surface can reflect sunlight to signal for help.

**How to aim:**
1. Hold the mirror close to your face.
2. Look through the hole or at the edge.
3. When you see the flash of light on the mirror, aim it at the aircraft or person.
4. Move the mirror slightly to flash the light at them.

**3 flashes = distress signal**

**The phi-flash**
Flash at phi-intervals (1.618 seconds between flashes). This pattern is unique and less likely to be mistaken for random light.

```
  Mirror Flash Technique

    Sun ☀️
      \
       \  →  Reflected light
        \
   You 👤 → Aim at aircraft
      |
   Mirror 🪞

  Flash pattern: 1.618 sec between flashes
```

### Sound Signals

Sound signals work when you cannot be seen but can be heard.

**3 whistle blasts = distress**

**3 shouts = help needed**

**Bang metal objects together**
Hit two rocks or pieces of metal together. The sharp sound carries far.
