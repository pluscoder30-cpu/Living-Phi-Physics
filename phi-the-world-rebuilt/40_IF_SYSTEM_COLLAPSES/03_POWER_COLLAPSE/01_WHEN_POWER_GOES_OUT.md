## PHASE 1: The First Hour (0–60 Minutes)

**Priority:** SAFETY. Nothing else matters right now.

### What Happens

The power goes out. Everything stops. The refrigerator hums into silence. The TV goes dark. Your phone still works — for now.

### What to Do

#### Step 1: Don't Panic
- Take a deep breath. Count to 10.
- This happens to millions of people every year. You will be fine.
- Say out loud: "I am prepared. I know what to do."

#### Step 2: Find Light
```
  FLASHLIGHT CHECKLIST
  ══════════════════════
  [ ] Flashlight (one per person)
  [ ] Extra batteries (stored WITH the flashlight)
  [ ] Candles (at least 10)
  [ ] Lighters or matches (stored in waterproof bag)
  [ ] Glow sticks (backup — no fire risk)
  
  COST: $15–30 for a basic kit
  PRIORITY: ★★★★★ (CRITICAL)
```

- Flashlights first — they are safer than candles.
- Candles next — they last longer and give more light.
- Place candles in safe spots: away from curtains, on stable surfaces.
- **NEVER leave candles unattended.**

#### Step 3: Unplug Sensitive Electronics
```
  UNPLUG LIST
  ══════════════
  [ ] Computers and laptops
  [ ] TVs and gaming consoles
  [ ] Modems and routers
  [ ] Microwave
  [ ] Washing machine
  [ ] Dishwasher
  
  WHY?  Power comes back in SURGES.
        A surge can fry your electronics.
        Unplugging = free insurance.
  
  COST: $0 (free protection)
  PRIORITY: ★★★★★ (CRITICAL)
```

#### Step 4: Fill the Bathtub
```
  WATER PRESERVATION
  ════════════════════
  
  ┌─────────────┐
  │  BATHTUB     │ ← Fill this NOW
  │  ████████░░  │   (40-80 gallons)
  │  ████████░░  │   
  │  ████████░░  │   Water may stop in
  │  ████████░░  │   1-24 hours.
  └─────────────┘
  
  Also fill:
  [ ] All sinks (plug them)
  [ ] Large pots and buckets
  [ ] Water bottles
  [ ] Coolers
  
  COST: $0 (free water storage)
  PRIORITY: ★★★★★ (CRITICAL)
```

- One bathtub = 40–80 gallons of water.
- One person needs 1 gallon per day minimum.
- A family of 4 with a full bathtub has 10–20 days of water.

#### Step 5: Check the Refrigerator
- Open it as little as possible.
- Food stays cold for about 4 hours if you don't open it.
- A full freezer stays cold for about 48 hours.
- Move ice to the fridge to keep it cold longer.
