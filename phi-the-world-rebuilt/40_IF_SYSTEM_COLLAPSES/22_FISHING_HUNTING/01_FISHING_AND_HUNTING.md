# FISHING AND HUNTING WITHOUT MODERN EQUIPMENT

---

## Fishing Methods

### 1. Hand Line
Simplest method. You need:
- A hook
- Line tied to the hook
- Bait on the hook
- Cast it from shore into the water

Wait. Feel for a tug. Pull it in. That's it.

### 2. Spear Fishing
Works best in shallow, clear water.
- Sharpen a stick to a point
- Stand still in the water
- Wait for fish to swim near
- Thrust down fast

Patience wins here. Move slow. Be part of the water.

### 3. Fish Trap
Weave a funnel-shaped basket from sticks or reeds.
- Wide opening faces upstream
- Funnel narrows toward the inside
- Fish swim in with the current
- They can't figure out how to swim back out

Place it in a stream and check it later.

### 4. Gorge Hook
A backup when you have no real hooks.
- Take a small stick
- Sharpen both ends
- Tie your line to the middle
- Bait it so the stick goes sideways in the fish's throat
- When you pull, the stick turns and catches

Primitive but it works.

### 5. Net Fishing
Weave a net from plant fibers or thin roots.
- Twist fibers into cord
- Tie knots in a grid pattern
- Stretch between two sticks
- Dip into water to catch small fish

Takes time to make. But reusable forever.

## The Phi-Fish-Trap

Here's where geometry makes you a better fisherman.

```
      FISH TRAP - TOP VIEW
      
      Water Flow Direction
      ===================>
      
      ╔══════════════════╗
      ║                  ║
      ║   WIDE OPENING   ║  ← Entrance: width = φ × exit width
      ║   (φ × exit)     ║
      ║                  ║
      ╚══════════════════╝
              ║
              ▼
         ╔══════════╗
         ║          ║
         ║ FUNNEL   ║  ← Walls angle inward at φ ratio
         ║ INWARD   ║
         ║          ║
         ╚══════════╝
              ║
              ▼
         ╔════════╗
         ║ SMALL  ║  ← Exit: narrow, fish can't find way back
         ║ EXIT   ║
         ╚════════╝
              ║
              ▼
        ╔═══════════╗
        ║  HOLDING  ║  ← Fish get trapped here
        ║  CHAMBER  ║
        ╚═══════════╝
```

### Why Phi Works

- Entrance is exactly φ (1.618) times wider than exit
- Fish see the wide opening and swim in
- The narrowing funnel feels natural at first
- By the time they realize it's a trap, the exit is too small to navigate
- Place traps at phi-spaced intervals along the stream (every 1.6 meters, then 1, then 0.6)

### Placement Rules
- Put traps where fish already travel (near rocks, bends, shade)
- Check every few hours
- Bait the entrance with crushed insects or plant matter
- Multiple traps = more food

## Hunting Without Weapons

### 1. Snare
A loop that catches animals by the neck or leg.
- Make a rope loop (use plant fibers if no rope)
- Set it on a path animals use
- Tie the other end to a bent sapling or heavy rock

Animal walks through, loop tightens, spring pulls it up.

```

           ╱╲
          ╱  ╲
         ╱    ╲

                   ╲
                    ○ ← Rope hangs down
                    │
                    │
                    ╭──╮
                    │░░│ ← Loop on ground
                    ╰──╯
    ═══════════════════════════
         Animal Path
```

**Tips:**
- Set snares on trails where you see footprints
- Make the loop about fist-sized for rabbits
- Check snares twice a day
- More snares = more chances

### 2. Deadfall
A heavy rock propped up, waiting to fall on food.
- Find a flat rock (heavy enough to crush)
- Prop it up with a stick
- Tie bait to the stick
- Animal touches bait, stick moves, rock falls

```
      DEADFALL TRAP - SIDE VIEW
      
      ╔════════════════════╗
      ║  HEAVY FLAT ROCK   ║
      ╚════════════════════╝
                 │╲
                 │  ╲
                 │    ╲ ← Support stick (angled)
                 │      ╲
                 │        ╲
                 │          ○ ← Pivot point
                 │
      ───────────────────────────
                 │
              BAIT here
```

**Tips:**
- Test it with a stick first (don't trap your hand)
- Place where you see animal droppings
- Works for rats, rabbits, anything small enough

### 3. Pit Trap
Dig a hole. Cover it. Wait.
- Dig a hole about 3 feet deep and 2 feet wide
- Cover the top with thin sticks
- Layer leaves and dirt on top
- Animal steps on it and falls in

**Tips:**
- Place on animal trails
- Make the walls smooth so nothing climbs out
- Check daily

### 4. Tracking
No trap needed. Just your eyes and brain.
- Look for footprints in soft mud or sand
- Follow droppings (fresh ones mean recent animal)
- Look for bent grass or broken twigs
- Follow worn paths on the ground
- Listen at dawn and dusk (animals are most active)

Track the animal to its water source or feeding area. Then set your trap there.

## Insects as Food (Yes, Really)

This sounds gross. But insects are some of the best survival food on Earth.

### What to Eat

| Insect | Where to Find It | Taste | Nutrition |
|--------|------------------|-------|-----------|
| Crickets | Under rocks, in grass | Like nuts (when roasted) | Very high protein |
| Grasshoppers | Tall grass, fields | Same as crickets | High protein, low fat |
| Ants | Any ant hill | Lemon-y | Good protein |
| Grubs | Inside rotting logs | Rich, fatty | High fat, high energy |
| Caterpillars | On plants and trees | Varies | Moderate protein |
| Beetles | Under bark, at lights | Crunchy, nutty | Good protein |

### How to Eat Them

1. **Catch them** — scoop with hands, use a jar, or shake branches over a cloth
2. **Clean them** — pull off wings and legs (they're too thin to be worth eating)
3. **Cook them** — roast over fire or hot coals until crispy
4. **Eat them** — seriously, they taste fine when cooked

Cooking kills parasites and bacteria. **Never eat insects raw.**

### Cricket Flour
- Roast crickets until crispy
- Crush them into powder with a rock
- Mix into any flour or dough
- You can't taste them but you get the protein

This is how many cultures around the world have eaten for thousands of years.

## The $3 Fishing Kit

You can build a functional fishing kit for almost nothing.

| Item | Where to Get It | Cost |
|------|-----------------|------|
| Hooks (10 pack) | Dollar store, tackle shop | $1 |
| Fishing line (50 ft) | Dollar store, tackle shop | $2 |
| Sinker weights (3 pack) | Dollar store, tackle shop | $1 |
| **TOTAL** | | **$4** |

### How to Use It
1. Tie the hook to one end of the line
2. Tie a sinker 12 inches above the hook
3. Bait the hook (worm, bread, insect)
4. Cast into water
5. Wait for a bite
6. Pull in slowly, steadily

**Free upgrades:**
- Make a bobber from a small stick and a feather
- Make a rod from a sturdy branch
- Use earthworms as free bait

## Remember

- **Patience beats everything.** Fish and animals don't rush. Neither should you.
- **Check traps often.** A caught fish or animal means nothing if it escapes.
- **Cook everything.** Parasites are real. Fire is your friend.
- **Use phi geometry.** The trap shapes work because nature follows phi patterns.
- **Practice now.** Don't wait until you're hungry to learn.
