## IMMEDIATE SHELTER (FIRST NIGHT)

You have 2 hours of daylight left. Here's what you do.

### Tarp + Rope = Instant Shelter

The fastest shelter you can build. Find two trees 10 feet apart. Tie a rope between them at chest height. Drape the tarp over the rope. Stake the corners with rocks or stakes.

### Lean-To

Find a fallen tree or a wall. Lean branches against it at a 45-degree angle. Cover with leaves, grass, or your tarp.

### Debris Hut

Pile branches in a cone shape. Cover with leaves 2 feet thick. Crawl inside. Your body heat warms the small space.

### Rule #1: STAY OFF THE GROUND

The ground steals your body heat. Always put insulation under you.

- Pile leaves 6 inches deep
- Lay branches side by side
- Use a log as a back rest

## BETTER SHELTER (FIRST WEEK)

You survived the night. Now build something that lasts.

### Adobe Bricks

Mud + straw + sun = brick. Mix 3 parts dirt, 1 part straw, and enough water to make it moldable. Pack into a wooden frame (2 feet × 1 foot × 6 inches). Let dry 3 days in sun.

```
  ADOBE BRICK MAKING
  ══════════════════

  MIX:  3 parts DIRT
        + 1 part STRAW
        + WATER until moldable

  ┌─────────────┐
  │  ┌───────┐  │  ← WOODEN FRAME
  │  │ MUD   │  │
  │  │ +STRAW│  │  ← PACK IN MIX
  │  └───────┘  │
  └─────────────┘

  TURN OUT ON SUNNY ROCK
  DRY 3 DAYS (flip once)
```

### Straw Bale Walls

Stack straw bales like giant bricks. Plaster with mud to make them waterproof. They insulate incredibly well.

### Wattle and Daub

Weave thin sticks through upright posts (the "wattle"). Plaster both sides with mud mixed with straw (the "daub"). Let dry.

```
  WATTLE AND DAUB
  ═══════════════

  │ │ │ │ │ │ │   ← UPRIGHT STICKS
  ═══════════════  ← WOVEN STICKS (wattle)
  │ │ │ │ │ │ │
  ═══════════════
  │ │ │ │ │ │ │

  Then plaster with mud:
  ┌─────────────────┐
  │▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓│  ← MUD PLASTER (daub)
  │▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓│
```

## BUILDING WITHOUT NAILS

You don't need a hardware store. Humans built for thousands of years without nails.

### Lashing

Tie sticks together with rope, vine, or strips of cloth. Wrap at phi-spaced intervals for maximum strength.

```
  LASHING PATTERN
  ═══════════════

       ║
  ─────╫─────
       ║
  ─────╫─────
       ║
  ─────╫─────
       ║

  Wrap rope around both sticks
  in a figure-8 pattern.
  Pull tight. Repeat 3x.
```

### Mud Mortar

Mix clay, sand, and water. Use it between adobe bricks or stones. It dries hard and holds everything together.

### Gravity

Heavy things on bottom. Light things on top. That's it. That's the rule.

### Friction

Wedge sticks together at angles. The tighter the fit, the stronger the hold.

## PHI-HARMONIC BUILDING PRINCIPLES

Every shelter you build should follow these rules:

1. **Match phi proportions.** Your rooms, your windows, your doorways — use the golden ratio. Your body knows this shape. It relaxes in it.

2. **Use natural materials first.** Wood, stone, mud, straw. These materials breathe. They regulate humidity. They feel right.

3. **Orient to the sun.** Face your door south (in northern hemisphere). Windows on the south wall catch winter sun. Roof overhang blocks summer sun.

4. **Ventilation is survival.** A sealed box kills you with CO₂. Always have a high vent and a low vent. Hot air out top, fresh air in bottom.

5. **Water off the roof, away from the walls.** Every shelter dies from water. Slope the ground away from your walls. Use gutters if you can.
