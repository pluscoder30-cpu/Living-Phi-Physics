## Foraging (Wild Edible Plants)

Nature provides food if you know what to look for. **Rule #1: If you don't know it, don't eat it.**

### Safe Foraging Checklist

| Plant | Parts Edible | When to Find | How to Prepare |
|-------|--------------|--------------|----------------|
| Dandelion | Flowers, leaves, roots | Spring-Fall | Raw in salad, roast roots for coffee |
| Plantain | Leaves, seeds | Year-round | Cook like spinach, seeds ground into flour |
| Clover | Flowers, leaves | Spring-Summer | Raw, or dry for tea |
| Cattail | Roots, shoots, pollen | Spring-Fall | Cook roots like potatoes, use pollen as flour |
| Wild berries | Varies | Summer | **Only if you're 100% sure** |

### Berry Safety Rules

| Color | Safe? | Example |
|-------|-------|---------|
| Blue/Black | Usually yes | Blueberries, blackberries |
| Red | **Be careful** | Raspberries (safe), some poisonous |
| White | **Avoid** | Most white berries are poisonous |
| Orange | **Avoid** | Most orange berries are poisonous |

**Golden Rule:** Never eat berries you can't positively identify.

## Food Preservation

Preserve food now to eat later. These methods work without electricity:

### Preservation Methods Comparison

| Method | Best For | Shelf Life | Equipment Needed |
|--------|----------|------------|------------------|
| Drying | Fruits, herbs, meat | 6-12 months | Warm dry place, string |
| Smoking | Meat, fish | 3-6 months | Smoke house, wood |
| Salting | Meat, vegetables | 1-2 years | Salt, containers |
| Fermenting | Cabbage, vegetables | 6-12 months | Salt, crock |
| Root cellaring | Root vegetables | 3-6 months | Cool dark place |

### Quick Preservation Recipes

**Jerky (Dried Meat):**
1. Slice meat thin (1/4 inch)
2. Salt heavily
3. Hang in warm dry place for 3-5 days
4. Store in paper bags

**Sauerkraut (Fermented Cabbage):**
1. Shred cabbage
2. Mix with 3% salt by weight
3. Pack tight in jar
4. Wait 2-4 weeks

**Smoked Fish:**
1. Clean and fillet fish
2. Salt for 24 hours
3. Smoke at 80-100°F for 6-12 hours
4. Store in cool place

## The Phi-Garden Layout

Use phi (φ = 1.618) to optimize your garden. This isn't just theory—phi spacing actually works better than standard spacing.

### Why Phi Spacing Works

Plants need:
- Enough space for roots
- Enough space for leaves
- But not so much space that you waste ground

Phi spacing gives plants 1.6× more room than standard spacing. This means:
- Better air circulation (less disease)
- More sunlight per plant
- Easier weeding and harvesting

## Solar Still (Water from Ground)

When water is scarce, build a solar still:

```
    SOLAR STILL DIAGRAM
    
         Clear plastic sheet
         (or glass)
              ↓
    ┌─────────────────────┐
    │~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~│ ← Sun heats ground
    │~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~│
    │~~~~~~~~~~●~~~~~~~~~~│ ← Rock in center
    │~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~│
    └──────────┬──────────┘
               │
               ▼
         Collection cup
         (under center)
    
    How it works:
    1. Dig hole 2-3 feet deep
    2. Place cup in center
    3. Cover with plastic sheet
    4. Put rock on plastic over cup
    5. Sun heats ground, water evaporates
    6. Condenses on plastic, drips into cup
    
    Yield: 1-2 cups per day
```

The phi-garden isn't just a layout—it's a philosophy. Work with nature's patterns, not against them. Plant at phi spacing, rotate crops in phi-ladder, and watch your garden thrive.
