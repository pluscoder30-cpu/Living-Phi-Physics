# PHI-MEDIA: Phi-Physics Foundations for Media and Journalism

## BUILD FROM THE VERY BEGINNING

### Layer 1: Truth as Phi-Coherence
- A news story is true when its coherence > C_crit
- Misinformation = coherence below C_crit
- The phi-truth-test: does this story increase the reader's coherence?
- Hidden zero: classical journalism assumes zero bias (objectivity = zero). But zero doesn't exist. The best journalism is phi-coherent (bias at φ-ground, not zero).
- **Degenerate limit:** As information content → 0, coherence → undefined (no signal to evaluate). As information content → ∞, coherence → 1 (all contexts simultaneously available).
- **Falsification:** If a story with coherence > C_crit is consistently judged as misinformation by experts, or vice versa, the C_crit threshold is miscalibrated.

### Layer 2: The Phi-Journalism-Ethic
- Report at phi-ratios: 61.8% facts, 23.6% context, 14.6% analysis
- The phi-source-verification: verify sources at φ-thresholds
- The phi-correction: corrections at φ-intervals (prompt, not delayed)
- **Degenerate limit:** As reporting → 0% facts, content becomes pure opinion (coherence = 0). As reporting → 100% facts, content becomes raw data dump (coherence ≈ C_crit but no narrative).
- **Falsification:** If phi-ratio reporting (61.8/23.6/14.6) produces lower audience comprehension than equal-ratio reporting (33/33/33) in A/B testing, the ratios are wrong.

### Layer 3: Phi-Media-Production
- Articles at phi-structure (intro:body:conclusion = 1:φ:φ²)
- Videos at phi-pace (φ× natural speaking rate)
- Podcasts at phi-duration (φ × standard episode length)
- Social media at phi-frequency (post at phi-intervals)
- **Degenerate limit:** As article length → 0, structure collapses to a headline (intro dominates). As length → ∞, intro:body:conclusion → 0:1:0 (body dominates).
- **Falsification:** If phi-pace video (φ× speaking rate) causes measurable comprehension loss versus 1× rate in controlled tests, the multiplier is wrong.

### Layer 4: The Phi-Newsroom
- Newsroom layout at phi-proportions
- Story assignment at phi-rankings
- Deadline management at phi-intervals
- The phi-editorial-meeting: duration = φ × optimal
- **Degenerate limit:** As newsroom size → 1, all phi-proportions collapse to a single workspace. As size → ∞, phi-proportions become indistinguishable from uniform allocation.
- **Falsification:** If phi-interval deadlines produce more missed deadlines than equal-interval deadlines, the interval model is wrong.

### Layer 5: The Phi-Media Laws (10)
1. Truth Is Coherence — **Degenerate:** At zero information, truth is undefined. **Falsification:** Coherent stories can be false if C_crit is miscalibrated.
2. Bias Is at Phi-Ground (Not Zero) — **Degenerate:** At infinite information, bias → 0 regardless. **Falsification:** If phi-grounded bias produces worse outcomes than zero-bias assumption, the model fails.
3. Stories Follow Phi-Structure — **Degenerate:** Single-word stories collapse to point (no structure). **Falsification:** Non-phi-structured stories outperform phi-structured ones in audience retention.
4. Sources Are Phi-Verified — **Degenerate:** At zero sources, verification is vacuous. **Falsification:** Phi-verified sources are not more reliable than standard verification.
5. Corrections Are Phi-Prompt — **Degenerate:** Immediate correction (φ→0) is indistinguishable from prevention. **Falsification:** Phi-prompt corrections do not reduce misinformation spread faster than linear corrections.
6. Media Production Is Phi-Timed — **Degenerate:** At φ=1, timing is uniform (no phi-effect). **Falsification:** Phi-timed production does not outperform equal-timed production in quality metrics.
7. The Newsroom Is a Living System — **Degenerate:** At 1 person, the "system" is the individual. **Falsification:** Newsroom coherence metrics do not correlate with output quality.
8. Information Recurses at φ⁻¹ — **Degenerate:** After 0 recursions, no information is generated. **Falsification:** φ⁻¹ recursion does not produce diminishing returns at each level.
9. The Media Ladder Invariant — **Degenerate:** Single-rung ladder is trivially invariant. **Falsification:** The ladder ratio does not hold across different media scales (small blog to global network).
10. Media Is Self-Referential — **Degenerate:** Media about media about media → infinite regress. **Falsification:** Self-referential media does not show phi-recursive structure in content analysis.
