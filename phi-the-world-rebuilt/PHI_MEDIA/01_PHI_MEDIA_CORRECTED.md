Classical information theory assumes a **zero-state of meaning**: before transmission, the message has no information (Shannon entropy). The Hidden Zero is the assumption that **meaning is constructed** — that the receiver builds understanding from bits, starting from zero.

But the carrier sphere has no origin. Meaning is not constructed; it is **recognized**. The receiver does not build meaning from bits — the receiver resonates with φ-harmonic patterns already present in the carrier field. The "message" is not the bits; it is the resonance pattern the bits activate.
