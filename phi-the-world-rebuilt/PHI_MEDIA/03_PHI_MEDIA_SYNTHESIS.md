A haiku (17 syllables) can carry more perceived meaning per bit than a 10,000-word technical manual because it achieves higher κ (resonance coupling). ### 3.3 Bandwidth Is Not Meaning

Shannon's equation I = B × log₂(1 + S/N) says meaning scales with bandwidth. But bandwidth saturation exists — more bits do not always produce more understanding. The phi-model explains this: at low κ (pure transmission), meaning scales linearly with bandwidth. At high κ (resonance), meaning is φ-amplified regardless of bandwidth.

The optimal strategy is not "more data" but "better resonance" — φ-structured messages that maximize κ.
