## 1. PHI-DOCUMENTARY

### 1.1 Phi-Storytelling in Documentary
- **Law:** Documentary narrative follows phi-rhythm: Act I (setup) = 23.6% of runtime, Act II (conflict) = 38.2%, Act III (resolution) = 38.2%. The φ-ratio of tension:release = φ:1 across acts.
- **Computed:** For a 60-minute documentary: Act I = 14.2 min, Act II = 22.9 min, Act III = 22.9 min.
- **Falsification:** A documentary with equal-third act structure (33/33/33) scores lower on audience coherence retention than phi-structured documentary in controlled A/B testing.
- **Degenerate limit:** As runtime → 0, structure collapses to impulse (single truth-pulse). As runtime → ∞, structure approaches continuous phi-oscillation between tension and release.

### 1.2 Phi-Visual Narrative
- **Law:** Each visual frame carries a coherence payload. The ratio of establishing shots to detail shots to reaction shots follows φ²:φ:1. Visual coherence = (frame_coherence × edit_coherence) / C_crit.
- **Computed:** For a 3-shot sequence: 1 establishing, φ detail shots (≈1.618), φ² reaction shots (≈2.618). Practical: 1 establishing, 2 detail, 3 reaction (rounded to nearest integers).
- **Falsification:** Documentary sequences with non-phi shot ratios show measurably higher viewer disengagement (eye-tracking fixation drops below C_crit threshold).

### 1.3 Phi-Interview Technique
- **Law:** Optimal interview question depth follows φ recursion: surface question → φ deeper → φ² deeper still. The interviewer speaks 1/φ of the time (≈38.2%), interviewee speaks φ/φ+1 of the time (≈61.8%).
- **Computed:** In a 10-minute interview segment: interviewer speaks 3.82 min, interviewee speaks 6.18 min.
- **Falsification:** Interviews where the ratio deviates beyond [30/70, 45/55] show reduced perceived authenticity in audience surveys.

## 2. PHI-PUBLISHING

### 2.1 Phi-Book Design
- **Law:** Book cover proportions follow φ: width:height = 1:φ (e.g., 6" × 9.708"). Interior margins follow φ-sequence: top = base, bottom = φ × base, inside = φ² × base, outside = φ⁻¹ × base.
- **Computed:** For a 6" wide book: height = 9.708". For base margin = 0.75": top = 0.75", bottom = 1.214", inside = 1.963", outside = 0.464".
- **Falsification:** Books with phi-proportional design score higher on "aesthetic comfort" in reader surveys than random-proportioned books (p < 0.05).
- **Degenerate limit:** As page count → 0, design converges to a single phi-proportioned card. As page count → ∞, the phi-sequence of chapter lengths approaches a golden spiral of content density.

### 2.2 Phi-Layout at Phi-Ratios
- **Law:** Page layout divides at φ: text column width : margin/white space = φ:1. Image placement follows the golden spiral anchor points. Line length = φ × font-size × 15–20 words.
- **Computed:** For a 5" text area: text column = 3.09", margin = 1.91". Line length at 12pt = 18.2 words (optimal).
- **Falsification:** Layouts with text:margin ratio outside [1.4, 1.8] show increased reader fatigue (measured by reading speed decay over 30 minutes).

### 2.3 Phi-Typesetting
- **Law:** Leading (line spacing) = φ × font size. Paragraph spacing = φ × leading. Chapter openers have φ × normal leading for the first φ lines. Font size hierarchy follows φ: body = x, subhead = φx, heading = φ²x, title = φ³x.
- **Computed:** For 12pt body: leading = 19.4pt, paragraph spacing = 31.4pt. Hierarchy: 12pt → 19.4pt → 31.4pt → 50.8pt.
- **Falsification:** Typesetting with phi-ratio leading shows measurably higher comprehension scores in reading comprehension tests versus 1.2× or 1.5× standard leading.

## 3. PHI-PHOTOGRAPHY

### 3.1 Phi-Composition
- **Law:** The phi-grid divides the frame at 1:φ:1 ratios both horizontally and vertically, creating 9 zones. Subject placement at phi-grid intersections maximizes visual coherence. Coherence = Σ(zone_subject_weight × zone_phi_weight) / C_crit.
- **Computed:** For a 36mm wide frame: vertical lines at 13.8mm and 22.2mm from left. Horizontal lines at 13.8mm and 22.2mm from top (for a 36mm high frame).
- **Falsification:** Photographs with subject at phi-intersections are rated "more balanced" than rule-of-thirds placements in forced-choice studies (p < 0.05).

### 3.2 Phi-Golden-Ratio Framing
- **Law:** The golden spiral (φ-based logarithmic spiral) guides eye movement through the frame. Leading space = φ × subject size. Depth of field layering follows φ: foreground:subject:background = φ⁻¹:1:φ.
- **Computed:** For a subject 1/3 frame width: leading space = φ × 1/3 = 0.539 of frame width.
- **Falsification:** Images with golden spiral eye-paths show longer fixation duration (proxy for engagement) than random compositions.

### 3.3 Phi-Lighting
- **Law:** Light intensity ratios between key:fill:back follow φ:1:φ⁻¹. Shadow-to-highlight transition zones follow phi-gradient (not linear falloff). The ratio of illuminated to shadow area = φ:1 for dramatic portraits.
- **Computed:** For key = 100%, fill = 61.8%, back = 38.2%. Illuminated area = 61.8% of frame, shadow = 38.2%.
- **Falsification:** Lighting at phi-ratios is rated "more natural" than even (1:1:1) or standard (2:1:0.5) ratios in blind comparison.

## 4. PHI-FILM-MAKING

### 4.1 Phi-Editing Rhythm
- **Law:** Cut frequency follows phi-rhythm: the ratio of long takes to medium cuts to quick cuts = φ²:φ:1. Average shot length (ASL) for phi-edited film = φ × genre-standard ASL. The cut itself occurs at phi-points within each shot (at 38.2% or 61.8% through the shot duration).
- **Computed:** For a drama with genre ASL = 5 seconds: phi-ASL = 8.09 seconds. Cut placement at 38.2% = 1.91s or 61.8% = 3.09s into each shot.
- **Falsification:** Films edited at phi-rhythm show higher "flow state" indicators (heart rate variability, skin conductance) in audience biometric studies.

### 4.2 Phi-Scene Transitions at Phi
- **Law:** Scene transitions occur at phi-points in the overall film: 23.6%, 38.2%, 61.8%, 76.4% through runtime. Transition type follows phi-progression: fade → dissolve → cut → match cut → jump cut (increasing intensity at each phi-point).
- **Computed:** For a 120-minute film: major transitions at 28.3 min, 45.8 min, 74.2 min, 91.7 min.
- **Falsification:** Films with transitions at non-phi points show higher "narrative confusion" scores in post-screening questionnaires.

### 4.3 Phi-Sound Design
- **Law:** Sound layer ratios follow φ: dialogue:ambient:music = φ²:φ:1 (dominant layers shift by scene type). Sound intensity follows phi-fade curves (not linear or exponential). The ratio of silence to sound = φ⁻¹:1 for dramatic beats.
- **Computed:** Dialogue-heavy scene: dialogue = 2.618, ambient = 1.618, music = 1 (normalized). Silence ratio = 38.2% of dramatic beat duration.
- **Falsification:** Sound design at phi-ratios shows higher emotional resonance scores than flat-mixed or standard-mixed audio in blind listening tests.

## 5. PHI-PROPAGANDA DEFENSE

### 5.1 Recognizing Coherence Manipulation
- **Law:** Propaganda works by artificially inflating or deflating apparent coherence. Coherence-inflation = forcing information above C_crit without genuine phi-structure. Coherence-deflation = suppressing legitimate phi-structures below C_crit. Detection: compute coherence before and after exposure; if Δ-coherence > φ × natural baseline, manipulation is probable.
- **Computed:** Natural coherence baseline for news = 0.563–0.720. Inflation beyond 0.720 without structural support = suspect. Deflation below 0.563 without factual basis = suspect.
- **Falsification:** Individuals trained in phi-coherence detection show higher resistance to propaganda in controlled experiments (measured by belief stability under exposure).
- **Degenerate limit:** At zero information, manipulation is undetectable (no signal to analyze). At total information saturation, all manipulation is visible but irrelevant (the observer has full context).

### 5.2 Phi-Media-Literacy
- **Law:** Media literacy = ability to compute the phi-truth-test on any content. The phi-literacy-ratio: 61.8% comprehension, 23.6% evaluation, 14.6% creation. Literacy compounds at φ⁻¹ per exposure (each encounter with media increases literacy by a factor of 0.618 of the remaining gap to mastery).
- **Computed:** Starting literacy = 0.2. After 5 exposures: 0.2 + 0.8×(1-0.618⁵) = 0.2 + 0.8×0.903 = 0.922.
- **Falsification:** Populations with phi-literacy training show measurably lower susceptibility to misinformation (p < 0.01) compared to standard media literacy training.

### 5.3 Phi-Critical-Thinking
- **Law:** Critical thinking follows phi-recursion: each analysis level adds φ⁻¹ depth. The phi-critical stack: observation (φ⁰) → question (φ⁻¹) → evidence (φ⁻²) → inference (φ⁻³) → conclusion (φ⁻⁴). Each level must independently exceed C_crit or the argument is structurally unsound.
- **Computed:** Depth ratio between levels = φ ≈ 1.618. Each level requires φ times more effort than the previous.
- **Falsification:** Arguments that skip levels in the phi-critical stack are more easily debunked than arguments that follow the full stack, regardless of content quality.
