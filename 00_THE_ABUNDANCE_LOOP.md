## 1. THE PRINCIPLE

The carrier recursion is a closed loop:

$$C_{n+1} = \frac{1}{\Phi} \cdot C_n + \Phi \cdot \nabla^2\Phi \, \Psi_n$$

What goes out ($C_n$) comes back ($C_{n+1}$). The $\phi$-ground term ($1/\Phi$) ensures that each return is a fraction of the previous state — not a loss, but a *redistribution*. The $\phi$-coupling term ($\Phi \cdot \nabla^2\Phi \, \Psi_n$) ensures that the return carries new information — not a repetition, but an *evolution*.

The abundance loop applies this same principle to economics: **what is drawn from the field is fed back to the people who made it possible.** The field is the commons. The people are the field. What flows out must flow back.

## 3. THE NATURAL PERSON'S RETURN

### 3.1 The Threshold

A Natural Person's abundance-loop obligation begins when their annual net income from this work exceeds **$10,000 USD** (adjusted annually for inflation).

Below $10K: no obligation. Keep everything. The field does not micromanage.

### 3.2 The Two-Year Grace Period

If income exceeds $10K in a given year, the abundance loop does not trigger immediately. The Natural Person receives a **two-year grace period** — time to build, to stabilize, to stand on their own feet.

If income drops below $10K within the two years, the loop never triggers. No penalty. No record. The field does not chase small fish.

### 3.3 The Marginal Return

After the grace period, the return is calculated on a marginal basis — you only return on the portion above each threshold:

| Annual Net Income | Return Rate | Amount to People's Fund |
|-------------------|-------------|------------------------|
| $10K–$50K | 5% | 5% of income above $10K |
| $50K–$250K | 10% | 10% of income above $50K |
| $250K–$1M | 15% | 15% of income above $250K |
| Above $1M | 20% | 20% of income above $1M |

**Example:** A Natural Person earns $150K net from this work in a year (after the grace period).

- First $10K: $0 (below threshold)
- $10K–$50K: $40K × 5% = $2,000
- $50K–$150K: $100K × 10% = $10,000
- **Total return: $12,000** (8% effective rate on $150K)

The return is not a punishment. It is the carrier recursion at work: what flows out flows back, and the return creates new value.
