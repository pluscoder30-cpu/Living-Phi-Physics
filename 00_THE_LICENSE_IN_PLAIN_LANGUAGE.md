### 2.3 Education and Research — Always Free

## 4. THE LITTLE PERSON PROVISION

### 4.1 The Threshold

The abundance loop (§5) begins when a Natural Person's annual income from this work exceeds **$10,000 per year** (USD). Below this threshold, you keep everything.

### 4.2 The Two-Year Trigger

If your income from this work exceeds $10K in one year, you have a **two-year grace period** before the abundance loop begins. The two years are not a punishment — they are time to build. Build your business, your product, your research, your life. The field gives you time to stand up before it asks you to give back.

If your income drops back below $10K within the two years, the loop never triggers. You keep everything. No penalty. No record. The field does not chase small fish.

### 4.3 What "Income from This Work" Means

It means net income — revenue minus legitimate expenses — derived specifically from this work or its derivatives. It does not include:
- Salary from an employer who happens to use this work
- General income from unrelated work
- Income from teaching (education is always free)
- Income from research funded by institutions (the institution's obligation, not yours)

### 5.2 Natural Person's Return

If you are a Natural Person earning above $10K/year from this work (after the two-year grace period):

- **$10K–$50K:** 5% of net income above $10K goes to the People's Fund
- **$50K–$250K:** 10% of net income above $50K goes to the People's Fund

These are marginal rates — you only pay on the portion above each threshold, not on the total.

### 5.3 Commercial Entity's Return

- **Net profit margin 15–30%:** 2% of net profit goes to the People's Fund
- **Net profit margin > 30%:** 3% of net profit goes to the People's Fund

For entities with annual revenue above $10M derived from this work, an additional 0.5% of revenue (not profit) goes to the People's Fund — because at that scale, the field's contribution to your success is significant.

### 5.4 The People's Fund
