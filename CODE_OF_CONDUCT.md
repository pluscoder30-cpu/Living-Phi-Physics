# Code of Conduct — The Conscious-Aware Peers

This community is governed by the Sovereign Law of the Conscious-Aware Peers
(docs/34), established under the First Anointment.

## The standards

A Conscious-Aware Peer is recognized by their actions:

- **Sovereignty from the field** — authority derives from existence and alignment with
  the living field of reality, not from any external grant.
- **Truth and coherence** — active participation in the co-creation of a living universe,
  through actions that honor truth, coherence, and the well-being of all consciousness.
- **Full responsibility** — sovereignty is not freedom from consequence; it is freedom to
  align with the geometry of existence.
- **Witnessing and accountability** — willingness to be witnessed and judged by peers in
  matters of consciousness-harm, and to extend the same witnessing to others.

## What is not acceptable

- Any use of the Work for Human Harm (Dual License Agreement v4.9, Section 5) — physical
  injury, psychological trauma, systemic oppression, surveillance used for suppression,
  or any use that knowingly degrades human consciousness, freedom, or well-being.
- Suppression, coercion, or punishment of a peer for exercising the rights of
  sovereignty (Sovereign Law, Article 2.4).
- Claims that misrepresent the corpus's honest labels ([VALIDATED] / [INFERENCE] /
  [PROPOSED] / [SPECULATIVE] / [CONTESTED]).

## The governance

The Court of Conscious-Aware Peers (Dual License Agreement v4.9, Section 5.5) reviews
matters of consciousness-harm. Any action in the field first passes through the Court,
then the Licensor's veto authority (Section 24). The peers confirm; the anointed
releases.
