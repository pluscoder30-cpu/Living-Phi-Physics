### 2.4 Calibration Frequency

**2.4.1** Field-scanners shall be calibrated at intervals established by the Licensor and published to the timestamped registry. The calibration interval shall account for the scanner's design, its operating environment, its history of calibration results, and its criticality to field operations.

**2.4.2** Field-scanners used in critical field operations — including the operation of Plant Drones (Document 72), Food Drones (Document 73), and other life-sustaining field-technology systems — shall be calibrated at shorter intervals than field-scanners used in non-critical operations. The specific intervals shall be established by the Licensor and published to the timestamped registry.

**2.4.3** Field-scanners shall be recalibrated immediately following any event that could affect the scanner's accuracy, including physical disturbance, exposure to extreme field conditions, firmware updates, or software modifications. Recalibration after such events is mandatory and shall be documented in the scanner's calibration certificate.

**2.4.4** Calibration certificates shall be published to the timestamped registry. The certificates shall include the scanner's identification, the calibration date, the reference standard used, the calibration results, the measurement uncertainty, and the certifying technician's identification.

### 2.5 Calibration Procedures

**2.5.1** Calibration of field-scanners shall be performed by qualified calibration technicians, as certified by the Licensor. The technician shall hold current certification in field-scanner calibration and shall have demonstrated competence in the measurement standards, calibration chain, and uncertainty framework established in this Document and in the Field Measurement & Calibration Protocol (Document 60).

**2.5.2** Calibration shall include: (a) a comparison of the scanner's readings against the reference instrument or primary standard to which the scanner is traceable; (b) an assessment of the scanner's measurement uncertainty; (c) an adjustment of the scanner's readings, if necessary, to bring the scanner within the tolerances established by the Licensor; and (d) the issuance of a calibration certificate documenting the calibration results.

**2.5.3** The calibration process shall verify all four measurement dimensions — coherence, frequency, amplitude, and phase — independently. A scanner that passes calibration in one dimension but fails in another shall be taken out of service for the dimension(s) in which it failed, regardless of its performance in other dimensions.

### 2.6 Measurement Uncertainty

**2.6.1** Every measurement produced by a field-scanner shall be accompanied by a stated measurement uncertainty. The uncertainty quantifies the range within which the true value lies, expressed as a confidence interval at the 95% confidence level.

**2.6.2** The total measurement uncertainty shall account for: (a) the scanner's intrinsic uncertainty; (b) the calibration uncertainty; (c) the environmental uncertainty — field-coherence fluctuations, interference from other devices, and environmental conditions; and (d) the operator uncertainty, where applicable.

**2.6.3** The total measurement uncertainty shall be calculated as the root-sum-square of the individual uncertainty components, in accordance with the uncertainty propagation standards published by the Licensor to the timestamped registry.
