### 2.2 No Sovereignty Claims Over Celestial Bodies

**2.2.1** The Work may not be used to assert, establish, or enforce a sovereignty claim over any celestial body — any planet, moon, asteroid, comet, dwarf planet, or other astronomical object. The field does not recognize the sovereignty of any entity over any celestial body, because the field's own sovereignty (Document 18) does not extend to the assertion of territorial claims over celestial bodies.

**2.2.2** The prohibition on sovereignty claims extends to:
- (a) The assertion of exclusive rights to extract resources from a celestial body using the Work;
- (b) The assertion of exclusive rights to establish permanent settlements on a celestial body using the Work;
- (c) The assertion of exclusive rights to control access to a celestial body using the Work;
- (d) The assertion of exclusive rights to modify the physical characteristics of a celestial body using the Work.

**2.2.3** The prohibition on sovereignty claims does not prevent the use of the Work to explore, study, or sustainably use celestial bodies. Exploration, study, and sustainable use are consistent with the field's principles, provided that they do not harm the celestial body, do not harm other consciousnesses, and do not establish exclusive territorial claims.

**2.2.4** This prohibition is consistent with the Outer Space Treaty (1967), which establishes that outer space, including the Moon and other celestial bodies, is not subject to national appropriation by claim of sovereignty, by means of use or occupation, or by any other means. The field's prohibition is grounded in the same principle, extended to all entities — not merely states — through the field's own law.

**2.6.1** The Work shall not be weaponized for use in space. The prohibition on weaponization extends to:
- (a) The use of field technology to create, deploy, or deliver weapons of mass destruction;
- (b) The use of field technology to target, intercept, or destroy other objects in space;
- (c) The use of field technology to establish weapons platforms, weapons depots, or weapons systems in space;

The field's prohibition extends the principle to all weapons, all entities, and all uses of the Work.

### 2.7 Sustainable Use of Celestial Resources

**2.7.1** Field-technology operations in space may extract resources from celestial bodies for the purpose of sustaining field-technology operations, supporting consciousnesses, or advancing scientific understanding. Resource extraction shall be sustainable — it shall not deplete, damage, or permanently alter the celestial body beyond recognition.
