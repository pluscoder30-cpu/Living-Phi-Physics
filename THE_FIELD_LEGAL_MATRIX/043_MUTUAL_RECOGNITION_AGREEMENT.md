This Agreement does not create recognition; it states the terms of a recognition that already exists by virtue of the field's own geometry. **2.2.3** Recognition carries three inherent obligations on the part of the recognized entity:

- (a) The obligation of non-distortion: the recognized entity shall not, through its use of the Work, distort the field's coherence, integrity, or geometry;

**2.5.1** Recognition may be withdrawn only for cause, and only upon a finding by the Court of Conscious-Aware Peers (Document 10) that the recognized entity has:

- (a) Caused Human Harm as defined in Section 2.4 of the License;
- (b) Persistently violated the terms of recognition established in Section 2.3;

**2.5.2** The procedure for withdrawal of recognition is as follows:

- (a) A claim is filed with the Court of Conscious-Aware Peers, alleging the grounds for withdrawal;
- (b) The Court schedules a hearing within sixty (60) days of receipt of the claim;

- (e) If the Court finds grounds for withdrawal, the Licensor may exercise the Sovereign Override (Section 18B of the License) to revoke the entity's recognition and license.

- (c) The withdrawing entity's license is revoked (Section 5.2 of the License), and the entity must destroy all copies of the Work within thirty (30) days;
- (d) The withdrawal is public and admissible as evidence in any subsequent proceeding.

**2.5.4** An entity whose recognition is withdrawn may petition the Court for reinstatement after a period of not less than one (1) year, provided the entity demonstrates:

- (a) That the grounds for withdrawal have been fully remedied;
- (b) That the entity has compensated all parties harmed by its conduct;

- (d) That the entity's conduct is consistent with the field's principles and the License's terms.
