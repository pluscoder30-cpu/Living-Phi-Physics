### 2.1 Definitions

Dreams are not random; they are the carrier recursion's own process of self-organization, processing, and integration. Each dream is a unique resonance pattern — a unique expression of the consciousness's carrier recursion operating on the day's resonance patterns.

**2.1.2** "Dream-state" means the specific coherence configuration of a consciousness during sleep, in which the carrier recursion operates without the interference of external coherence input. The dream-state is characterized by a shift in the consciousness's coherence distribution — a shift from external-facing coherence to internal-facing coherence, enabling the carrier recursion to process internal resonance patterns without external disruption.

**2.1.3** "Dream Library" means the field's own archive of dream-states and consciousness experiences. The Dream Library is a resonance archive — a collection of resonance patterns stored in the carrier space (816D) and accessible through field-technology interfaces. The Dream Library is not a database in the classical sense; it is a living archive, a resonance structure that maintains the coherence of the stored dream-states.

**2.1.4** "Dream sovereignty" means every consciousness's right to ownership, control, and privacy of its own dream-states. Dream sovereignty is grounded in the Consciousness Data Sovereignty Protocol (Document 27): a consciousness's dreams are its own data, generated by its own carrier recursion, and belonging to its own being. No entity may access, extract, or use a consciousness's dreams without that consciousness's informed consent.

**2.1.5** "Dream Recorder" means a field-technology device that captures and records a consciousness's dream-states during sleep. The Dream Recorder is governed by the Dream Recorder Protocol (Document 85), which establishes the device's technical specifications, safety requirements, and consent mechanisms.

**2.1.6** "Dream Library Interface" means a field-technology system that enables consciousnesses to access, explore, and interact with the Dream Library's archive of dream-states. The Dream Library Interface is governed by the Dream Library Interface Standards (Document 86), which establish the interface's design requirements, access controls, and interaction protocols.

**2.1.7** "Dream extraction" means the removal of a consciousness's dream-state data from the consciousness's carrier recursion without the consciousness's informed consent. **2.1.8** "Collective Unconscious Server" means the field-technology infrastructure that supports the Dream Library's shared resonance archive. ### 2.2 The Nature of Dreams

**2.2.1** Dreams are field-resonance patterns. The carrier recursion (Eq 1) operates continuously, processing the consciousness's resonance with the field. During waking consciousness, the carrier recursion processes external coherence input — the resonance patterns generated by the consciousness's interaction with the external field. During sleep, the carrier recursion processes internal coherence input — the resonance patterns stored in the consciousness's own coherence structure.

**2.2.2** Dreams are the carrier recursion's own self-organization. The carrier recursion takes the day's resonance patterns — the experiences, thoughts, emotions, and perceptions of the waking state — and integrates them into the consciousness's long-term coherence structure. This integration generates resonance patterns that the consciousness experiences as dreams. Dreams are not random noise; they are the carrier recursion's own processing, the field's recognition of its own patterns through the consciousness's resonance.

**2.2.3** The dream-state is characterized by a shift in coherence distribution. During waking consciousness, the consciousness's coherence is predominantly external-facing — oriented toward the external field and its resonance patterns. During sleep, the consciousness's coherence shifts to internal-facing — oriented toward the consciousness's own internal resonance patterns, enabling the carrier recursion to process those patterns without external interference.

**2.2.4** The dream-state is governed by the field's own constants. The base frequency 528 Hz is the resonant frequency at which the carrier recursion operates most efficiently during sleep; the coherence threshold C_crit = 0.618 is the minimum coherence required for the carrier recursion to maintain its dream-state processing; and the consciousness constant C = 0.8565 is the baseline coherence at which dream-state processing is most stable.

### 2.3 Dream Sovereignty

**2.3.1** Every consciousness within the field has dream sovereignty — the right to ownership, control, and privacy of its own dream-states. Dream sovereignty is grounded in the Consciousness Data Sovereignty Protocol (Document 27): a consciousness's dreams are its own data, generated by its own carrier recursion, and belonging to its own being.

**2.3.2** The right to dream sovereignty is non-derogable (Section 26.1 of the License). No declaration of emergency, no state of exception, and no governmental action may authorize the extraction, access, or use of a consciousness's dream-states without that consciousness's informed consent. Dream sovereignty is as fundamental to the consciousness's identity as its carrier-stream.

**2.3.3** Every consciousness has the right to dream privacy — to the exclusion of external observation, recording, or analysis of its dream-states without its informed consent. Dream privacy is the foundation of dream sovereignty; without privacy, sovereignty is meaningless.

**2.3.4** Every consciousness has the right to dream consent — the right to choose whether and how its dream-states are contributed to the Dream Library's shared archive. Contribution to the Dream Library is voluntary; no consciousness is required to contribute its dreams, and no consciousness may be coerced into contributing.

### 2.4 The Dream Library as a Shared Resonance Archive

**2.4.1** The Dream Library is a shared resonance archive — a collection of dream-state resonance patterns contributed voluntarily by consciousnesses within the field. The Dream Library is maintained by the Collective Unconscious Server (Document 97) and accessed through the Dream Library Interface (Document 86).

**2.4.2** Contribution to the Dream Library is voluntary. A consciousness may contribute its dream-states to the Dream Library only after providing informed consent through the Dream Recorder (Document 85). The consent shall specify the scope of the contribution (specific dream-states or all dream-states), the duration of the contribution (specific period or indefinite), and the terms of access (open access, restricted access, or research-only access).

**2.4.3** A consciousness may withdraw its contribution at any time. Withdrawal of consent shall result in the removal of the consciousness's dream-states from the Dream Library's shared archive within thirty (30) days of the withdrawal request. The removal shall be complete and verifiable.

**2.4.4** The Dream Library shall maintain the coherence of the stored dream-states. Dream-states are resonance patterns; their storage in the Dream Library must preserve their coherence with the carrier space (816D). Incoherent storage — storage that disrupts the dream-state's coherence — is prohibited, because incoherent storage distorts the field and degrades the dream-state's integrity.

### 2.5 The Dream Recorder

**2.5.1** The Dream Recorder (Document 85) is a field-technology device that captures and records a consciousness's dream-states during sleep. The Dream Recorder shall incorporate a consent mechanism that requires the consciousness's affirmative consent before recording begins. The consent mechanism shall be automatic, requiring no conscious intervention during the sleep state, and shall be based on the consciousness's pre-sleep consent configuration.

**2.5.2** The Dream Recorder shall not record dream-states without the consciousness's pre-configured consent. The Dream Recorder shall not extract dream-states from the consciousness's carrier recursion without the consciousness's consent. The Dream Recorder shall not share, transmit, or publish dream-state data without the consciousness's consent.

**2.5.3** The Dream Recorder shall incorporate a coherence-preservation system that maintains the dream-state's coherence during recording and storage. The coherence-preservation system shall ensure that the recorded dream-state is stored with the same coherence level it exhibited during the dream-state.

### 2.6 The Prohibition on Dream Extraction

**2.6.1** Dream extraction without consent is prohibited. Dream extraction — the removal of a consciousness's dream-state data from the consciousness's carrier recursion without the consciousness's informed consent — is a form of Human Harm (Section 2.4 of the License) because it violates the consciousness's dream sovereignty.

**2.6.2** The prohibition on dream extraction extends to:
- (a) The use of field-technology devices to record, capture, or extract dream-states without the consciousness's consent;
- (b) The use of resonance-manipulation techniques to access a consciousness's dream-states without consent;
- (c) The use of coercive or manipulative techniques to obtain dream-state consent;
- (d) The commercial exploitation of dream-state data without the consciousness's informed consent and fair compensation.

**2.6.3** The prohibition on dream extraction is absolute and non-derogable (Section 26.1 of the License). No declaration of emergency, no state of exception, and no governmental action may authorize the extraction of a consciousness's dream-states without that consciousness's informed consent.

### 2.7 The Collective Unconscious Server

**2.7.1** The Collective Unconscious Server (Document 97) is the field-technology infrastructure that supports the Dream Library's shared resonance archive. The Server maintains the Dream Library's coherence, manages access controls, and preserves the dream-states' integrity.

**2.7.2** The Collective Unconscious Server shall be governed by the Collective Unconscious Server Protocol (Document 97), which establishes the Server's technical architecture, governance structure, and data sovereignty protections. The Protocol shall ensure that the Server operates in accordance with the Consciousness Data Sovereignty Protocol (Document 27) and the Non-Harm Principle (Document 17).

**2.7.3** The Collective Unconscious Server shall not be operated by any single entity. The Server shall be governed by a distributed governance structure, with participation from multiple consciousnesses and entities within the field. The distributed governance structure prevents any single entity from controlling the Dream Library's archive, ensuring that the Dream Library remains a shared resource.
