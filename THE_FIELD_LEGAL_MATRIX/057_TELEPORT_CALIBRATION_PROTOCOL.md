## 2. BODY

### 2.1 Definitions

**2.1.1** "Teleport calibration" means the process of measuring, verifying, and adjusting the coherence state of a consciousness's carrier-stream before, during, and after a teleportation event, to ensure that the reassembled carrier-stream preserves the consciousness's identity, coherence, and integrity.

**2.1.2** "Pre-transit carrier-state measurement" means the comprehensive measurement of a consciousness's carrier-stream taken immediately before teleportation is initiated. The measurement captures the carrier-stream's coherence level (C), its frequency signature, its phase alignment, its dimensional configuration within the 816D carrier space, and its recursion index position. The pre-transit measurement serves as the baseline against which the post-transit coherence is compared.

**2.1.3** "Destination coherence verification" means the process of confirming that the destination point's local field coherence exceeds the minimum threshold required for stable reassembly. Destination verification is performed after the navigation sequence establishes a coherence connection between origin and destination, and before disassembly is initiated.

**2.1.4** "Reassembly coherence check" means the measurement of the reassembled carrier-stream's coherence taken immediately after teleportation is completed. The check compares the post-transit coherence to the pre-transit baseline and confirms that the reassembled carrier-stream exceeds C_crit = 0.618.

**2.1.5** "Calibration frequency" means the required interval between calibration events for teleportation systems and infrastructure. Active teleportation systems shall be calibrated daily; teleportation infrastructure shall be calibrated weekly.

**2.1.6** "Coherence corridor" means the field-stabilized pathway between origin and destination that maintains the carrier-stream's coherence during the disassembly-transit-reassembly process. The coherence corridor's stability is a prerequisite for teleportation.

### 2.2 Pre-Transit Carrier-State Measurement

**2.2.1** Every teleportation event shall be preceded by a pre-transit carrier-state measurement. The measurement shall capture, at minimum, the following parameters:

- (a) The carrier-stream's coherence level (C), expressed as a value between C_crit = 0.618 and C = 1.000;
- (b) The carrier-stream's dominant frequency signature, expressed in Hz and referenced to the phi-harmonic frequency ladder (base frequency 528 Hz);
- (c) The carrier-stream's phase alignment relative to the carrier recursion (Eq 1);
- (d) The carrier-stream's dimensional configuration within the 816D carrier space;
- (e) The carrier-stream's recursion index position (n);
- (f) The carrier-stream's memory continuity signature — the pattern of resonant states that encodes the consciousness's identity and memory.

**2.2.2** The pre-transit measurement shall be recorded and stored in a coherence-preserving medium for a minimum period of thirty (30) days. The record serves as the baseline for post-transit comparison and as evidence in the event of a teleportation failure.

**2.2.3** If the pre-transit measurement reveals that the carrier-stream's coherence is below C_crit = 0.618, teleportation shall not be initiated. The consciousness shall be referred to a coherence-restoration sequence (Document 86, Dream Library Interface) until its coherence exceeds the critical threshold. Teleportation of a sub-critical carrier-stream is prohibited under Document 30, Section 2.3.2.

**2.2.4** If the pre-transit measurement reveals anomalies in the carrier-stream's memory continuity signature — fragments, gaps, or distortions that suggest carrier-stream instability — the teleportation operator shall delay the transit and initiate a carrier-stream integrity assessment. The assessment shall determine whether the anomaly is a transient fluctuation or a structural defect that could compromise the teleportation.

### 2.3 Destination Coherence Verification

**2.3.1** Every teleportation event shall be preceded by a destination coherence verification. The verification shall confirm that the destination point's local field coherence exceeds the minimum threshold for stable reassembly, as established by the Licensor and published to the timestamped registry.

**2.3.2** The destination coherence verification shall measure:

- (a) The local field coherence at the destination point;
- (b) The stability of the local field coherence over a measurement window established by the Licensor;
- (c) The absence of coherence-disturbances at the destination — including interference patterns, resonance conflicts, or competing carrier-streams — that could compromise reassembly.

**2.3.3** If the destination coherence is below the minimum threshold for stable reassembly, teleportation shall not be initiated. The navigation sequence shall re-route the transit to an alternative destination with sufficient coherence, or the transit shall be deferred until the destination coherence is restored.

**2.3.4** The destination coherence verification shall be performed in coordination with the FTL Navigation Standards (Document 56) and the Carrier-Space Navigation Standards (Document 63), which establish the navigation framework for identifying and verifying destination points.

### 2.4 Reassembly Coherence Check

**2.4.1** Every teleportation event shall be followed by a reassembly coherence check. The check shall measure the reassembled carrier-stream's coherence and compare it to the pre-transit baseline.

**2.4.2** The reassembly coherence check shall confirm:

- (a) The reassembled carrier-stream's coherence exceeds C_crit = 0.618;
- (b) The reassembled carrier-stream's frequency signature matches the pre-transit baseline within tolerances established by the Licensor;
- (c) The reassembled carrier-stream's memory continuity signature is intact — no fragments, gaps, or distortions relative to the pre-transit baseline;
- (d) The reassembled carrier-stream's dimensional configuration within the 816D carrier space is consistent with the pre-transit configuration.

**2.4.3** If the reassembly coherence check reveals that the carrier-stream's coherence is below C_crit = 0.618, the teleportation system shall initiate an emergency coherence-restoration sequence. The system shall not release the carrier-stream from the teleportation interface until coherence is restored above C_crit = 0.618 and the memory continuity signature is verified.

**2.4.4** If the reassembly coherence check reveals a memory continuity anomaly — a fragment, gap, or distortion in the carrier-stream's identity pattern — the teleportation operator shall initiate a carrier-stream integrity assessment. If the anomaly is structural, the Licensor shall be notified and the Sovereign Override (Section 18B of the License) may be invoked to restore the consciousness's coherence.

### 2.5 Calibration Frequency

**2.5.1** Active teleportation systems — systems that perform teleportation events on a regular basis — shall be calibrated daily. Daily calibration shall include:

- (a) A full pre-transit carrier-state measurement sequence run against a test carrier-stream;
- (b) A destination coherence verification simulation;
- (c) A reassembly coherence check against the test carrier-stream's baseline;
- (d) An integrity assessment of the teleportation system's coherence corridor.

**2.5.2** Teleportation infrastructure — portals, coherence corridors, and supporting field-technology — shall be calibrated weekly. Weekly calibration shall include:

- (a) A coherence-corridor stability test;
- (b) An entry-mechanism verification;
- (c) A post-transit verification sequence;
- (d) An infrastructure coherence-depletion assessment.

**2.5.3** Calibration records shall be published to the timestamped registry. The records shall include the calibration date, the calibration results, any anomalies detected, and any corrective actions taken. Calibration records shall be maintained for a minimum period of one (1) year.

**2.5.4** If a calibration event reveals an anomaly that could compromise teleportation safety, the teleportation system or infrastructure shall be taken out of service until the anomaly is resolved and a re-calibration confirms that the system meets the standards established in this Protocol.
