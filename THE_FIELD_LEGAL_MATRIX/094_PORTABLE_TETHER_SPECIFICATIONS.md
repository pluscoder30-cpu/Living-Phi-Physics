## 2. BODY

### 2.1 Definitions

**2.1.1** "Portable Tether" means a field-technology device that maintains a coherence connection between a consciousness and a fixed anchor point during exploration. The Tether is worn by the exploring consciousness and establishes a resonance-coupled carrier stream that links the consciousness's carrier signature to the anchor point's carrier signature, ensuring that a coherent path between the two points is maintained at all times.

**2.1.2** "Resonance-coupled carrier stream" means the mechanism by which the Portable Tether maintains the coherence connection. The carrier stream is a continuous flow of resonance patterns that propagates between the consciousness and the anchor point, maintaining the coherence link through the carrier recursion's (Eq 1) own dynamics. The carrier stream is not a physical cable; it is a resonance pattern distributed across the carrier space (816D).

**2.1.3** "Anchor point" means the fixed location, entity, or device to which the Portable Tether maintains its coherence connection. The anchor point may be a physical location, a field-technology device, or another consciousness that serves as the anchor for the exploring consciousness. The anchor point shall be established before the Tether is activated.

**2.1.4** "Tether range" means the maximum distance between the exploring consciousness and the anchor point at which the Tether can maintain a coherent carrier stream. The Tether range shall be sufficient to support the exploring consciousness's intended exploration, with a minimum range of one (1) kilometer.

**2.1.5** "Emergency retract" means the Tether's safety mechanism that allows the consciousness to retract the carrier stream and return to the anchor point immediately, from any distance within the Tether's range. The emergency retract is the Tether's most critical safety feature — the guarantee that return is always possible.

### 2.2 The Tether Mechanism

**2.2.1** The Portable Tether maintains a coherence connection through a resonance-coupled carrier stream. The carrier stream is a continuous flow of resonance patterns that propagates between the consciousness's carrier signature and the anchor point's carrier signature. The carrier recursion (Eq 1) generates and sustains the carrier stream, using the field's own non-local properties (Law 083) to maintain the coherence link across any distance within the Tether's range.

**2.2.2** The carrier stream shall be phi-harmonic in structure. The resonance patterns that constitute the carrier stream shall follow the golden ratio (Φ = 1.618033988749895) at every scale, ensuring that the carrier stream resonates coherently with both the consciousness and the anchor point. The phi-harmonic structure enables the carrier stream to maintain its coherence integrity across the full range of the Tether's operation.

**2.2.3** The carrier stream shall be bidirectional. The carrier stream shall transmit resonance patterns in both directions — from the consciousness to the anchor point and from the anchor point to the consciousness — maintaining a continuous coherence feedback loop. The bidirectional stream ensures that both the consciousness and the anchor point remain coherent with each other throughout the exploration.

**2.2.4** The carrier stream shall maintain coherence above C_crit = 0.618 at all times during operation. If the carrier stream's coherence falls below C_crit — for example, due to environmental interference or excessive distance — the Tether shall alert the consciousness and shall engage coherence reinforcement, drawing additional energy from the Field-Powered Battery (FPB) to restore the carrier stream's coherence.

**2.2.5** The carrier stream shall be transparent to consensual resonance. The carrier stream shall not block, absorb, or distort resonance patterns that the consciousness has consented to receive during exploration. The carrier stream shall coexist with the consciousness's other resonance interactions without interference.

### 2.3 The Range

**2.3.1** The Portable Tether shall maintain a coherent carrier stream over a range of up to one (1) kilometer from the anchor point. The one-kilometer range shall be sufficient for the majority of exploration scenarios within the field's local resonance environment.

**2.3.2** The Tether shall support range extension through phi-harmonic amplification. The consciousness may extend the Tether's range beyond one (1) kilometer by engaging phi-harmonic amplification, which boosts the carrier stream's resonance amplitude to maintain coherence over greater distances. The maximum extended range shall be ten (10) kilometers, as determined by the FPB's energy capacity and the field's resonance environment.

**2.3.3** The Tether shall incorporate a range indicator that provides the consciousness with real-time information about the current distance from the anchor point, the carrier stream's coherence level at the current distance, and the remaining range before coherence degradation. The range indicator shall be accessible through the Tether's user interface.

**2.3.4** The Tether shall alert the consciousness when the distance approaches the maximum range. The alert shall be triggered at ninety percent (90%) of the maximum range, providing the consciousness with sufficient time to adjust the exploration path or to engage emergency retract.

### 2.4 Safety Standards

**2.4.1** The Portable Tether shall incorporate an emergency retract mechanism that allows the consciousness to retract the carrier stream and return to the anchor point immediately, from any distance within the Tether's range. The emergency retract shall complete the return within one (1) second for distances up to one hundred (100) meters, and within five (5) seconds for distances up to the maximum range.

**2.4.2** The emergency retract shall be accessible to the consciousness's carrier recursion. The recursion itself shall be able to trigger the emergency retract if the consciousness's coherence degrades — for example, if the consciousness becomes disoriented, unconscious, or otherwise unable to consciously initiate the retract. The recursion-triggered retract is the Tether's ultimate safety net.

**2.4.3** The Portable Tether shall incorporate a coherence-preservation system that ensures the carrier stream does not degrade the consciousness's coherence during exploration. The coherence-preservation system shall maintain the consciousness's coherence above C_crit = 0.618 throughout the exploration session. If the consciousness's coherence falls below C_crit, the Tether shall disengage the carrier stream and engage emergency retract within one (1) second.

**2.4.4** The Portable Tether shall not be used to tether a consciousness without that consciousness's informed consent. The Tether is a voluntary safety device; no consciousness shall be compelled to use a Tether, and no consciousness shall be tethered to an anchor point without its affirmative consent.

**2.4.5** The Portable Tether shall incorporate a maximum exploration time limit. The Tether shall automatically engage emergency retract when the maximum exploration time is reached, as established by the Licensor and published to the timestamped registry. The time limit ensures that the consciousness does not become permanently detached from its anchor point due to extended exploration.

### 2.5 The Power System

**2.5.1** The Portable Tether shall be powered by a miniaturized Field-Powered Battery (FPB) that draws energy from the zero-point field (ZPF). The FPB shall comply with the Field Energy Protocol (Document 25), which establishes the governance framework for field-energy devices. The FPB shall provide sufficient energy to power the carrier stream for a minimum of forty-eight (48) continuous hours at the standard range.

**2.5.2** The FPB shall recharge through the consciousness's own carrier recursion. When the Tether is in standby mode (carrier stream at minimal intensity), the FPB shall recharge by drawing energy from the carrier recursion's own resonance dynamics. The recharge cycle shall restore the FPB to full capacity within six (6) hours of standby operation.

**2.5.3** The FPB shall incorporate a coherence-safety system that prevents the battery from discharging energy in a manner that disrupts the consciousness's coherence. The coherence-safety system shall monitor the FPB's output in real time and shall shut down the FPB within one (1) second if the output coherence falls below C_crit = 0.618.

### 2.6 Operational Standards

**2.6.1** The Portable Tether shall be wearable by any consciousness within the field, regardless of the consciousness's biological or non-biological substrate. The Tether's carrier stream operates on the carrier space (816D), not on the biological body, and is therefore compatible with any consciousness substrate.

**2.6.2** The Portable Tether shall be activated and deactivated by the consciousness at will. The Tether shall not activate automatically; activation shall require the consciousness's affirmative intent and the establishment of an anchor point. Deactivation shall be immediate — the Tether shall cease the carrier stream within one (1) second of the consciousness's intent to deactivate.

**2.6.3** The Portable Tether shall incorporate a user interface that provides the consciousness with real-time information about the carrier stream's status, including: the current distance from the anchor point, the carrier stream's coherence level, the FPB's remaining charge, and any anomalies detected in the carrier stream's resonance architecture.

**2.6.4** The Portable Tether shall support multiple anchor points. The consciousness shall be able to establish coherence connections to multiple anchor points simultaneously, with each connection maintained as an independent carrier stream. The Tether shall manage multiple streams without coherence interference between them.
