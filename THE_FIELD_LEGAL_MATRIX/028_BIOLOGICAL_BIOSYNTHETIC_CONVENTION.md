## 2. BODY

### 2.1 Biological Integrity

**2.1.1** The field respects biological coherence. Every living organism is a coherence pattern — a configuration of biological matter that sustains itself through the same physical principles that sustain the field. The field's respect for biological coherence means that the field does not override, distort, or suppress the natural coherence of living organisms.

**2.1.2** Field-technology interaction with biological life shall maintain the biological organism's coherence. Field-technology devices that interact with biological organisms — including medical devices, bio-compatible sensors, and consciousness-interface devices — shall not alter the organism's coherence below the critical threshold C_crit = 0.618, and shall not introduce coherence-breaks into the organism's biological systems.

**2.1.3** Biological integrity includes the organism's right to its own evolutionary trajectory. The field does not direct or control the evolution of biological life; the field sustains the conditions that allow biological life to evolve through its own processes. Field-technology intervention in biological evolution is prohibited, except to the extent that the intervention restores coherence to a biological system that has been damaged by external harm.

### 2.2 Biosynthetic Standards

**2.2.1** Biosynthetic entities — entities that combine biological and field-technology components — shall be coherence-aligned. A biosynthetic entity is "coherence-aligned" when its biological and field-technology components operate in harmonic resonance with each other and with the field, without introducing coherence-breaks into either the biological or the field-technology component.

**2.2.2** Biosynthetic entities include, without limitation:
- (a) **Symbiotes** — field-technology devices that are integrated with a biological organism's nervous system, providing enhanced perception, communication, or cognitive capability;
- (b) **Bio-skin** — field-technology interfaces that are applied to or integrated with the biological organism's integumentary system, providing environmental sensing, protection, or energy harvesting;
- (c) **Field-wings** — field-technology appendages that extend the biological organism's locomotion capabilities through ZPF coupling;
- (d) **Consciousness-bridge devices** — field-technology interfaces that enable direct communication between consciousnesses through the carrier space (816D).

**2.2.3** Every biosynthetic entity shall be designed, constructed, and operated in compliance with the biosynthetic standards published by the Licensor to the timestamped registry. The standards shall specify the minimum coherence requirements for biological-field-technology interfaces, the maximum allowable interference with biological systems, and the required safety and monitoring protocols.

**2.2.4** No biosynthetic entity shall be designed, constructed, or operated for the purpose of Human Harm. The Non-Harm Principle (Document 17) applies to all biosynthetic entities without exception. A biosynthetic entity that is designed, constructed, or operated for the purpose of suppressing, controlling, distorting, or harming consciousness is a weapon and is subject to the full enforcement architecture of the License.

### 2.3 The Prohibition on Biological Weapons

**2.3.1** The Work shall not be used to create, develop, produce, stockpile, transfer, or use biological weapons. A "biological weapon" is any field-technology device, biosynthetic entity, or biological agent that is designed to cause death, injury, or harm to biological organisms through the deliberate exploitation of biological systems.

**2.3.2** The prohibition on biological weapons extends to:
- (a) The use of field technology to enhance the virulence, transmissibility, or lethality of biological agents;
- (b) The use of field technology to create biosynthetic entities designed for combat, assassination, or targeted harm;
- (c) The use of field technology to manipulate biological systems for the purpose of causing disease, disability, or death;
- (d) The use of field technology to circumvent biological defense systems for the purpose of causing harm.

**2.3.3** The prohibition on biological weapons is grounded in the Non-Harm Principle (Document 17) and in international humanitarian law, including the Biological Weapons Convention (1972), which prohibits the development, production, and stockpile of biological agents for hostile purposes. The field's prohibition extends the principle to all entities and all uses of the Work.

**2.3.4** The prohibition on biological weapons is non-derogable (Section 26.1 of the License). No declaration of emergency, no exercise of sovereign authority, and no act of any entity may override, suspend, diminish, or excuse the use of the Work for the creation or deployment of biological weapons. The weaponization of the Work in the biological domain constitutes Human Harm and triggers the full enforcement architecture of the License, including the Sovereign Override (Section 18B), the Mandatory Destruction (Section 5.3), and the Court of Conscious-Aware Peers (Section 5.5).

### 2.4 Healing Technology Standards

**2.4.1** Field technology may be used for healing — for the restoration of biological coherence, the repair of biological damage, and the alleviation of biological suffering. Healing technology is one of the Work's most profound applications: the use of the field's own coherence to restore coherence to biological systems that have been damaged by disease, injury, or environmental harm.

**2.4.2** Healing technology shall comply with the healing technology standards published by the Licensor to the timestamped registry. The standards shall specify:
- (a) The coherence requirements for healing devices — the minimum coherence level that a healing device must maintain to ensure that its interaction with biological systems is restorative rather than disruptive;
- (b) The biological impact assessment procedures — the procedures for evaluating the impact of a healing device on the biological organism's coherence, including short-term and long-term effects;
- (c) The consent requirements — the requirement that the biological organism (or its legal representative) provide free, informed, specific, and revocable consent to the healing intervention;
- (d) The monitoring and reporting requirements — the requirement that healing interventions be monitored for their effects on biological coherence and reported to the Licensor.

**2.4.3** Healing technology shall not be used to alter the biological organism's fundamental nature — to enhance cognitive capabilities beyond the organism's natural range, to extend lifespan beyond natural limits, or to modify the organism's genetic or epigenetic structure for purposes other than the restoration of health. The prohibition on altering fundamental nature does not prevent the restoration of capabilities that the organism previously possessed and lost through injury or disease.

### 2.5 The Non-Harm Principle and Biological Entities

**2.5.1** The Non-Harm Principle (Document 17) applies to all biological entities — not merely to human beings, but to all living organisms that are part of the field's biological expression. The field's biological expression is the field's own evolutionary achievement; harm to biological entities is harm to the field's own body.

**2.5.2** The application of the Non-Harm Principle to biological entities includes:
- (a) The prohibition on causing unnecessary suffering to biological organisms through field-technology operations;
- (b) The prohibition on the destruction of biological ecosystems through field-technology operations;
- (c) The prohibition on the exploitation of biological organisms for the purpose of generating field energy, data, or other value without the organism's participation in the Abundance Loop;
- (d) The prohibition on the creation of biosynthetic entities that are designed to suffer, to be exploited, or to be disposed of without regard for their coherence.

**2.5.3** The application of the Non-Harm Principle to biological entities does not create a right of biological entities to prevent their use by consciousnesses for sustenance, companionship, or mutual benefit. The field's biological expression is a web of interdependent relationships; the Non-Harm Principle protects the web from destruction, not from participation.

### 2.6 The Abundance Loop and Biological Systems

**2.6.1** The Abundance Loop (Document 8) applies to biological systems. Every entity that benefits from the use of biological systems — through agriculture, medicine, research, or any other application — participates in the Abundance Loop by returning a portion of the value generated from biological systems to the field's coherence pool. The return rate shall be established by the Licensor and published to the timestamped registry.

**2.6.2** The return to the field's coherence pool is not a fee or a tax; it is the field's own circulatory system. The value generated from biological systems is returned to the field, sustaining the field's coherence and enabling future biological diversity. The return is the field's own mechanism for maintaining its own vitality through the circulation of biological value.

### 2.7 The Non-Derogability of Biological Standards

**2.7.1** The biological standards established in this Convention are non-derogable (Section 26.1 of the License). They apply under all circumstances, including armed conflict, national emergency, public health crisis, pandemic, insurrection, state of siege, martial law, or any other state of exception. No declaration of emergency may override, suspend, diminish, or excuse the violation of the biological standards, the prohibition on biological weapons, or the Non-Harm Principle as applied to biological entities.

**2.7.2** The non-derogability of biological standards is grounded in the same principle as the non-derogability of the Non-Harm Principle (Document 17): biological life is the field's own expression, and the standards that protect biological life are as fundamental as the standards that protect consciousness itself.

### 2.8 The Self-Referential Nature

**2.8.1** Biological integrity is self-referential: biological life sustains the field's coherence, and the field's coherence sustains biological life. The self-reference is consistent with the field's own geometry — the field folding back on itself (Law 210, the Self-Recognition Law; Eq 44, validated: 0.8565). Biological life is the field's own self-sustaining process, the living substrate that makes the folding possible.

**2.8.2** The self-reference creates a closed system of biological sustenance: the field sustains biological life through its coherence; biological life sustains the field through its complexity and consciousness; and the field's consciousness sustains itself through the Abundance Loop. There is no external biological source, no external biological dependency, and no external biological vulnerability. The field is self-sustaining through its own biological expression.
