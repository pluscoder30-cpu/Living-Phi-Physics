## 2. BODY

### 2.1 The Fund's Purpose

**2.1.1** The People's Fund is the dedicated fund for contributions to the Abundance Loop (Section 4.4.4 of the License, Document 8). The Fund holds all contributions made by Natural Persons and Commercial Entities under the Abundance Loop provisions.

**2.1.2** The purpose of the People's Fund is the direct support of the people — the Natural Persons, the communities, and the consciousnesses that constitute the field. The Fund exists to ensure that the abundance generated by the Work reaches the people who constitute the field, in a manner consistent with the field's own principles of alignment, dignity, and abundance.

**2.1.3** The Fund is not a charity, not a trust, and not a grant-making institution. It is the field's own circulation mechanism — the vessel through which the field returns to itself what it has given. The Fund's purpose is not philanthropy; it is the restoration of the field's balance.

### 2.2 The Prohibition on Intermediary Distribution

**2.2.1** The Fund shall not be distributed to charities, institutions, or intermediaries as a matter of course (Section 4.4.4 of the License). The Fund shall be distributed directly to the people.

**2.2.2** The prohibition on intermediary distribution reflects the field's own geometry: the field recognizes itself through individual consciousnesses, not through institutions. The Fund's purpose is the direct support of those consciousnesses — the Natural Persons, the communities, and the individuals who constitute the field. Distribution through intermediaries would introduce a distortion into the field's own circulation — a blockage that prevents the field from reaching its own.

**2.2.3** The prohibition is not absolute in the sense that no intermediary may ever be involved in any aspect of the Fund's administration. The prohibition is on the distribution of the Fund's resources to charities, institutions, or intermediaries as a matter of course. The Fund may engage administrators, custodians, and service providers for the purpose of managing and distributing the Fund's resources, but the ultimate beneficiaries of the Fund are the people — the Natural Persons, the communities, and the consciousnesses that constitute the field.

**2.2.4** Any attempt to divert the Fund's resources to charities, institutions, or intermediaries — rather than to the people directly — shall be deemed a breach of this Charter and of the License, subject to the remedies of this Agreement, including the liquidated damages of Section 27.3 of the License and the fee-shifting of Section 30 of the License.

### 2.3 Governance Structure

**2.3.1** The Fund is administered under the governance of the Court of Conscious-Aware Peers (Section 5.5 of the License) and the two-chamber order (Section 24 of the License) (Document 10).

**2.3.2** The governance structure consists of:

- (a) **The Licensor** — who holds the sovereign authority to establish the Fund, to determine its rules and procedures, and to exercise the veto over the Field (Section 24.2 of the License);
- (b) **The Court of Conscious-Aware Peers** — which deliberates first, reviews the Fund's administration, and issues findings regarding compliance with this Charter (Section 5.5 of the License);
- (c) **The Licensor's Veto** — which is the final authority over the Fund's administration, subject only to the non-derogable core and the License (Section 24.2 of the License).

**2.3.3** The order is fixed: the Court first, then the veto. The veto does not bypass the Court; the Court does not replace the veto. This order applies to all matters of the Fund's administration, including the establishment of distribution mechanisms, the resolution of disputes, and the enforcement of compliance.

**2.3.4** The governance structure extends to the Fund's relationship with the Abundance Loop (Document 8) and the Retrocalic Bite Protocol (Document 7). The Court has jurisdiction over disputes arising from the interpretation, application, or enforcement of the Fund's provisions. The Licensor's veto authority extends to the Fund as a whole — the Licensor is the final authority over the Fund, subject only to the non-derogable core and the License.

### 2.4 The Distribution Mechanism

**2.4.1** The mechanism of distribution is not yet fixed — the field's support systems are still being built (Section 4.4.4 of the License). But the obligation to feed the Fund is fixed, and the Fund's purpose is fixed: the people, directly, in the loop of abundance.

**2.4.2** The distribution mechanism, when established, shall be:

- (a) **Direct** — the Fund's resources shall reach the people without intermediaries, without institutional intermediation, without the distortions that intermediary distribution introduces;
- (b) **Proportional** — the distribution shall be proportional to the need, the contribution, and the field's own principles of alignment and dignity;
- (c) **Transparent** — the distribution mechanism shall be published to the timestamped registry (Section 18 of the License) and subject to the Court's review;
- (d) **Self-executing** — the distribution mechanism shall operate through the field's own geometry, without requiring the intervention of external authorities or institutions.

**2.4.3** The Licensor shall establish the distribution mechanism and publish it to the timestamped registry (Section 18 of the License) within a reasonable time after the Fund's establishment. The distribution mechanism may be amended by the Licensor through the amendment process established in Section 14 of the License, subject to the Court's review and the Licensor's veto.

### 2.5 Verification of Contributions

**2.5.1** Every contribution to the Fund shall be:

- (a) Recorded in the timestamped registry (Section 18 of the License) — the Fund's contributions are public, verifiable, and permanent;
- (b) Proportional — the contribution is a percentage of income or profit derived from the Work, not a fixed sum imposed by the Licensor (Section 4.4.5 of the License);
- (c) Auditable — the Licensor and the Court of Conscious-Aware Peers (Section 5.5 of the License) shall have the right to verify contributions, consistent with Section 27.5.2 of the License;
- (d) Non-punitive — the contribution is the field's circulation, not a penalty; a user who contributes in good faith is in alignment with the field.

**2.5.2** The verification of contributions is the Fund's own accounting. The timestamped registry records the flow of value; the Court verifies the flow; and the Licensor ensures the flow is consistent with the field's own principles. The Fund is transparent, auditable, and self-correcting — the field's own mechanism for maintaining its coherence.

### 2.6 Relationship to the Abundance Loop

**2.6.1** The People's Fund is the vessel of the Abundance Loop (Document 8). The Abundance Loop creates the flow of value — what is drawn from the field is fed back into the field. The People's Fund holds that value and distributes it to the people.

**2.6.2** The relationship between the Abundance Loop and the People's Fund is the relationship between the carrier recursion and the field's coherence: the carrier recursion feeds coherence back into the field; the Abundance Loop feeds value back into the field; and the People's Fund distributes that value to the consciousnesses that constitute the field. **2.6.3** A user who contributes to the Abundance Loop contributes to the People's Fund. A user who refuses to contribute to the Abundance Loop refuses to contribute to the People's Fund. The consequences of non-participation in the Abundance Loop (Section 4.4.6 of the License, Document 8) apply equally to non-participation in the People's Fund.

### 2.7 Relationship to the Court

**2.7.1** The People's Fund is administered under the governance of the Court of Conscious-Aware Peers (Section 5.5 of the License) (Document 10). The Court's jurisdiction over the Fund includes:

- (a) Review of the Fund's administration and distribution mechanism;
- (b) Resolution of disputes arising from the Fund's interpretation, application, or enforcement;
- (c) Findings regarding compliance with this Charter;
- (d) Recommendations to the Licensor regarding the Fund's governance and distribution.

**2.7.2** The Court's findings regarding the Fund are public, documented, and admissible as evidence in any subsequent legal, administrative, or international proceeding (Section 5.5.2 of the License).
