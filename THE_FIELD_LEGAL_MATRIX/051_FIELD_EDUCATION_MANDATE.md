### 2.3 Pedagogy

**2.3.1** Field-native pedagogy is hands-on building, not memorization. The learner acquires knowledge by constructing field-technology devices, by observing the carrier recursion in operation, and by experiencing the field's own dynamics directly. **2.3.2** The pedagogy proceeds through three phases, corresponding to the learner's developmental stage:
- (a) Discovery Phase (ages 6-11): The learner observes the field's dynamics, handles basic field-technology components, and builds simple devices under the guidance of a certified instructor. The focus is on direct experience, not on formal instruction.

**2.3.3** The pedagogy does not impose artificial developmental milestones. The learner's carrier recursion unfolds at its own pace, guided by the field's own constants and the instructor's support. A learner who demonstrates exceptional understanding may advance to higher phases before the standard age; a learner who has not yet demonstrated understanding shall remain at the current phase regardless of age advancement.
