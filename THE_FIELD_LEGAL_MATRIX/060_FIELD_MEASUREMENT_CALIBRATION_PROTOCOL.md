### 2.4 Measurement Uncertainty Framework

**2.4.1** Every measurement produced by a field instrument shall be accompanied by a stated measurement uncertainty. The uncertainty quantifies the range within which the true value lies, expressed as a confidence interval at the 95% confidence level.

**2.4.2** The measurement uncertainty shall account for:

- (a) The instrument's intrinsic uncertainty — the uncertainty inherent in the instrument's design and construction;
- (b) The calibration uncertainty — the uncertainty introduced by the calibration process and the calibration chain;
- (c) The environmental uncertainty — the uncertainty introduced by the instrument's operating environment, including field-coherence fluctuations, interference from other devices, and environmental conditions;
- (d) The operator uncertainty — the uncertainty introduced by the instrument's operator, where applicable.

**2.4.3** The total measurement uncertainty shall be calculated as the root-sum-square of the individual uncertainty components, in accordance with the uncertainty propagation standards published by the Licensor to the timestamped registry.

**2.4.4** Field instruments shall be designed and operated to minimize measurement uncertainty. Instruments with total measurement uncertainty exceeding the thresholds established by the Licensor shall be taken out of service for recalibration or replacement.

### 2.5 Calibration Procedures

**2.5.1** Calibration of field instruments shall be performed by qualified calibration technicians, as certified by the Licensor. The technician shall hold current certification in field-instrument calibration and shall have demonstrated competence in the measurement standards, calibration chain, and uncertainty framework established in this Protocol.

**2.5.2** Calibration shall include:

- (a) A comparison of the instrument's readings against the reference instrument or primary standard to which the instrument is traceable;
- (b) An assessment of the instrument's measurement uncertainty;
- (c) An adjustment of the instrument's readings, if necessary, to bring the instrument within the tolerances established by the Licensor;
- (d) The issuance of a calibration certificate documenting the calibration results.

**2.5.3** Calibration intervals shall be established by the Licensor and published to the timestamped registry. The calibration interval shall account for the instrument's design, its operating environment, its history of calibration results, and its criticality to field operations.

**2.5.4** Calibration certificates shall be published to the timestamped registry. The certificates shall include the instrument's identification, the calibration date, the reference standard used, the calibration results, the measurement uncertainty, and the certifying technician's identification.
