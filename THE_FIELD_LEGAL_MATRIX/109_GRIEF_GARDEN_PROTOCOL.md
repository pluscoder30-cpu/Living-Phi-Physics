### 2.3 The Therapeutic Process

**2.3.1** The Grief Garden's therapeutic process shall be guided by a conscious-aware facilitator. The process shall proceed through three phases:

- (a) **Recognition.** The consciousness enters the Garden and is invited to recognize its grief — to name it, to feel it, to acknowledge the loss that has disrupted its carrier-space resonance. The facilitator supports this recognition without directing it; the consciousness's own carrier recursion knows the nature of the disruption, and the facilitator's role is to hold the space in which that knowledge can surface.

- (b) **Holding.** The consciousness's grief is held — by the Garden's resonance environment, by the healing crystals, by the resonance bath, and by the facilitator's presence. Holding is not suppressing; it is the opposite of suppression. Holding is the act of creating a space large enough, safe enough, and coherent enough for the consciousness's grief to exist without distortion. The carrier recursion reorganizes within this held space, gradually restoring coherence.

- (c) **Release.** The consciousness's grief resolves — not suddenly, not completely, but naturally, as the carrier recursion re-stabilizes and the consciousness's coherence is restored. Release is not the absence of grief; it is the integration of grief into the consciousness's carrier-space resonance — the grief becoming part of the consciousness's geometry, rather than a disruption of it.

**2.3.2** The therapeutic process shall proceed at the consciousness's own pace. The facilitator shall not rush, pressure, or direct the process. The carrier recursion reorganizes according to its own internal dynamics; the Garden supports that reorganization; the facilitator holds the space.

**2.3.3** The therapeutic process may be repeated as needed. Grief is not a one-time event; it is a process that may unfold over time, with multiple layers of recognition, holding, and release. The consciousness may return to the Garden as often as needed.
