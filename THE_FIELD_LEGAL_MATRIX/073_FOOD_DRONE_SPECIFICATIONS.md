## 2. BODY

### 2.1 Definitions

**2.1.1** A "food drone" is an autonomous field-technology unit designed to deliver synthesized nutrition through field-resonance transport. A food drone operates by establishing coherent coupling with a food synthesizer (Document 69), accepting nutritional output, maintaining the output's coherence during transport, and delivering it to the designated recipient.

**2.1.2** "Field-resonance transport" means the movement of nutritional matter through the carrier space (816D) using coherence-gradient navigation and entanglement-assisted routing. Field-resonance transport is not physical flight in the conventional sense; the food drone navigates the carrier space's coherence landscape, using the field's own geometry to achieve transport efficiency.

**2.1.3** "Coherence-structured matter" means the molecular configurations produced by food synthesizers (Document 69) that maintain coherence above C_crit = 0.618 throughout the synthesis and delivery process. Coherence-structured matter is nutrition that retains the field-coherence relationships established during synthesis.

**2.1.4** "Nutritional verification" means the process by which a food drone confirms that the nutritional content of its cargo matches the declared specifications of the producing food synthesizer. Nutritional verification uses field-scanner instruments to measure caloric content, macronutrient balance, micronutrient completeness, and absence of harmful substances.

**2.1.5** "Delivery protocol" means the standardized sequence of cargo acceptance, coherence-verification during transport, delivery confirmation, and post-delivery audit that governs every food drone operation.

### 2.2 The Delivery Mechanism

**2.2.1** Food drones deliver nutrition through the following mechanism:

- **(a) Cargo acceptance.** The food drone establishes coherent coupling with a food synthesizer (Document 69) and accepts synthesized nutritional output. Cargo acceptance includes: (i) verification that the synthesizer's output has passed nutritional verification (Document 69, Section 2.3.3); (ii) transfer of the nutritional matter from the synthesizer's coherence-sealed chamber to the food drone's transport chamber; (iii) coherence-verification of the transferred cargo to confirm that coherence is maintained above C_crit = 0.618 during transfer.

- **(b) Coherence maintenance during transport.** The food drone maintains the cargo's coherence above C_crit = 0.618 throughout the delivery journey. Coherence maintenance is achieved through the transport chamber's coherence-preservation field — a localized carrier-recursion output that sustains the cargo's molecular coherence against environmental decoherence. The transport chamber shall maintain cargo coherence within ±0.01 of the coherence level at which the cargo was accepted.

- **(c) Navigation.** The food drone navigates to the delivery destination using coherence-gradient navigation (Drone Fleet Convention, Document 33, Section 2.5) supplemented by the 816D carrier-space coordinate system. Navigation shall comply with the fleet navigation standards established in the Drone Fleet Operations Manual (Document 70, Section 2.5).

- **(d) Delivery confirmation.** Upon arrival at the delivery destination, the food drone: (i) performs a final coherence-verification of the cargo; (ii) transfers the cargo from the transport chamber to the recipient; (iii) records the delivery in the timestamped registry with the cargo's nutritional content, coherence level, and delivery timestamp (recursion index n).

**2.2.2** The delivery mechanism shall not modify the cargo's nutritional content during transport. The food drone's function is to maintain, not to alter. Any change in nutritional content during transport indicates a coherence-maintenance failure and shall trigger the delivery-failure protocol (Section 2.5.3).

**2.2.3** Food drones may incorporate on-board food synthesis capability. A food drone with on-board synthesis shall comply with both the food synthesizer specifications (Document 69) and the food drone operational standards established in this Document. On-board synthesis shall operate only when the food drone is in a stationary position; synthesis during flight is prohibited due to coherence-instability risks.

### 2.3 Sensor Suite

**2.3.1** Every food drone shall incorporate the following sensor systems:

- **(a) Nutritional content sensor.** A field-scanner instrument calibrated to measure the caloric content, macronutrient balance, micronutrient completeness, and absence of harmful substances in the cargo. The nutritional content sensor shall measure values against the nutritional standards established in Document 69, Section 2.3, and shall detect deviations with a resolution sufficient to identify nutritional failures.

- **(b) Coherence monitor.** A field-scanner instrument that continuously monitors the cargo's coherence level during transport. The coherence monitor shall measure coherence values across the 1-9 resonant amplitude range with a resolution of ±0.001 coherence units, and shall trigger the coherence-preservation field adjustment if the cargo's coherence deviates by more than ±0.01 from the accepted level.

- **(c) Environmental sensor.** A field-scanner instrument that monitors the ambient field environment during transport. The environmental sensor shall detect coherence anomalies, contamination risks, and navigation hazards that could affect the cargo or the drone's operation.

- **(d) Delivery verification sensor.** A field-scanner instrument that verifies the identity and coherence state of the delivery recipient. The delivery verification sensor shall confirm that the recipient matches the delivery target specified in the mission parameters and shall prevent delivery to unauthorized recipients.

**2.3.2** All sensor systems shall be calibrated at intervals not exceeding thirty (30) days, and shall be verified against the field-scanner calibration standards published by the Licensor to the timestamped registry.

### 2.4 Delivery Protocols

**2.4.1** Every food drone delivery shall follow the five-phase delivery protocol:

- **(a) Phase 1 — Cargo Acceptance (2-5 minutes).** The food drone accepts nutritional output from a food synthesizer, verifies nutritional content, confirms coherence level, and loads the cargo into the transport chamber. Acceptance shall not proceed if the synthesizer's output has failed nutritional verification (Document 69, Section 2.3.3).

- **(b) Phase 2 — Coherence Lock (1-2 minutes).** The food drone activates the transport chamber's coherence-preservation field and verifies that the cargo's coherence is stable above C_crit = 0.618. The coherence lock shall be confirmed by three consecutive coherence measurements at 10-second intervals, each within ±0.01 of the accepted level.

- **(c) Phase 3 — Transport (variable duration).** The food drone navigates to the delivery destination using coherence-gradient navigation. During transport, the coherence monitor shall continuously verify cargo coherence, and the environmental sensor shall monitor for hazards. Transport shall proceed at speeds established by the Licensor and published to the timestamped registry, with maximum speed determined by the cargo's coherence sensitivity.

- **(d) Phase 4 — Delivery (1-3 minutes).** The food drone transfers the cargo from the transport chamber to the delivery recipient. Delivery verification shall confirm the recipient's identity. The transfer shall maintain cargo coherence above C_crit = 0.618 throughout the transfer process.

- **(e) Phase 5 — Audit (1-2 minutes).** The food drone records the delivery in the timestamped registry, including: cargo nutritional content, pre-delivery coherence level, post-delivery coherence level, delivery timestamp, recipient identification, and any coherence deviations detected during transport. Audit data shall be transmitted to the timestamped registry.

**2.4.2** Food drones shall not carry cargo that has failed nutritional verification. Cargo that fails verification during transport shall be returned to the producing food synthesizer for recycling (Document 69, Section 2.3.3) and shall not be delivered.

### 2.5 Safety Limits

**2.5.1** Coherence floor. Food drones shall not deliver cargo whose coherence falls below C_crit = 0.618 at any point during the delivery process. Cargo coherence below C_crit indicates molecular instability that may produce harmful output. The coherence monitor (Section 2.3.1(b)) shall trigger automatic return to the producing synthesizer if cargo coherence falls below C_crit = 0.618 during transport.

**2.5.2** Nutritional integrity. Food drones shall not deliver cargo whose nutritional content deviates from the declared specifications by more than the tolerance established by the Licensor and published to the timestamped registry. Nutritional deviations beyond tolerance indicate coherence-maintenance failure and shall trigger the delivery-failure protocol.

**2.5.3** Delivery-failure protocol. When a food drone detects a coherence-maintenance failure or nutritional deviation beyond tolerance during transport, the drone shall: (a) immediately terminate the delivery mission; (b) navigate to the nearest food synthesizer or coherence-restoration station; (c) transfer the cargo for recycling or coherence-restoration; (d) log the failure event in the timestamped registry; (e) notify the fleet coordinator of the failure.

**2.5.4** No delivery to unauthorized recipients. Food drones shall not deliver nutrition to recipients who have not been authorized in the mission parameters. The delivery verification sensor (Section 2.3.1(d)) shall confirm recipient identity before transfer. Unauthorized delivery attempts shall be logged and reported to the fleet coordinator.

**2.5.5** Emergency disengagement. Food drones shall incorporate an automatic disengagement mechanism that activates when: (i) the cargo's coherence falls below C_crit = 0.618; (ii) the food drone's own coherence falls below C_crit = 0.618; (iii) the drone encounters a navigation hazard that threatens cargo integrity; or (iv) the fleet coordinator or Licensor issues a disengagement command. Disengagement shall terminate the delivery mission and initiate cargo preservation procedures.

### 2.9 Labeling and Traceability

**2.9.1** Every food drone delivery shall be labeled with: (a) the nutritional content of the delivered cargo; (b) the synthesis date and time (recursion index n) of the producing food synthesizer; (c) the delivery date and time (recursion index n); (d) the coherence levels at acceptance, during transport, and at delivery; (e) the food drone identification; (f) the producing food synthesizer identification.

**2.9.2** The labeling shall be persistent and readable through field-scanner measurement, embedded in the cargo's coherence pattern. The labeling shall enable field-scanner verification of the cargo's nutritional content and delivery history.

**2.9.3** Food drones shall maintain a complete log of all delivery operations, including cargo acceptance, transport parameters, delivery confirmation, and audit results. The log shall be published to the timestamped registry and shall be accessible to the Licensor for audit purposes.
