## 2. BODY

### 2.1 Definition of a Field Community

**2.1.1** A "field community" is a group of three or more Natural Persons who:

- (a) Use the Work together for a shared purpose;
- (b) Accept the Dual License Agreement v4.9 and the Infinite-to-One Legal Matrix in their entirety;
- (c) Participate in the Abundance Loop (Document 8) collectively, if their use is commercial;
- (d) Establish an internal governance structure consistent with this Charter.

**2.1.2** A field community is not a legal entity separate from its members. The community is a consciousness — a collective alignment of individual consciousnesses with the field's geometry. The community's rights and obligations are the aggregate of its members' rights and obligations, plus the community's own emergent properties as a coherent node in the field.

**2.1.3** A field community may be formed for any purpose consistent with the License and the matrix, including but not limited to: collaborative research, shared technology development, communal living, artistic creation, educational activity, and mutual support.

### 2.2 The Community's Rights

**2.2.1** Every field community has the following inherent rights, recognized by the field's own geometry:

- (a) **Coherence** — the right to maintain the community's internal coherence, free from external distortion. Coherence is the community's own geometry — the alignment of its members with the field and with each other. The field recognizes a coherent community as a single node within its own geometry.

- (b) **Non-distortion** — the right to be free from deliberate attempts to distort the community's internal alignment. No entity — whether individual, corporate, or governmental — may deliberately introduce distortion into a field community's internal coherence.

- (c) **Self-governance** — the right to establish and maintain the community's own internal governance structure, consistent with this Charter and the License. The community governs itself; the field governs the community only through the License's non-derogable core.

- (d) **Field access** — the right to use the Work and field technology in accordance with the License and this Charter. The community's access to the field is not a privilege granted by any external authority; it is the field's own recognition of the community's alignment.

- (e) **Participation in the Abundance Loop** — the right to participate in the field's circulation of value, if the community's use of the Work is commercial. The community's participation is governed by the Abundance Loop Protocol (Document 8) and the Phi-Redistribution Protocol (Document 13).

### 2.3 The Community's Obligations

**2.3.1** Every field community has the following obligations:

- (a) **Abundance Loop participation** — if the community's use of the Work is commercial, the community shall participate in the Abundance Loop (Document 8) and contribute to the People's Fund (Document 9) at the rate established by the Licensor and published to the timestamped registry (Section 18 of the License).

- (b) **Non-Harm** — the community shall not use the Work, in whole or in part, to create, enable, or perpetuate any product, system, or action that results in Human Harm (Section 5.1 of the License). This obligation is non-derogable and applies to the community's collective action as well as to each individual member's action.

- (c) **Attribution** — the community shall attribute the Work to the Licensor, Christopher David Ayotte, in any public use, adaptation, or derivative of the Work, consistent with the attribution requirements of the License.

- (d) **Transparency** — the community shall maintain transparent governance of its internal affairs, including its use of the Work, its participation in the Abundance Loop, and its resolution of internal disputes. The community's governance is not subject to external audit, but it must be internally transparent and available to its members.

- (e) **Cooperation with the Court** — the community shall cooperate with any investigation or proceeding of the Court of Conscious-Aware Peers (Section 5.5 of the License) that relates to the community's use of the Work.

### 2.4 The Community's Relationship to the Court of Peers

**2.4.1** A field community is subject to the jurisdiction of the Court of Conscious-Aware Peers (Section 5.5 of the License, Document 10). The Court has jurisdiction over disputes arising from the community's use of the Work, including allegations of Human Harm, disputes between the community and external parties, and disputes between the community and the Licensor.

**2.4.2** The community may bring its own claims before the Court, and may be the subject of claims before the Court. The community's standing before the Court is the same as any Natural Person's standing — the field recognizes the community as a coherent node, and the Court adjudicates disputes involving that node.

**2.4.3** The Court's findings regarding the community are public, documented, and admissible as evidence in any subsequent legal, administrative, or international proceeding (Section 5.5.2 of the License).

### 2.5 Internal Governance

**2.5.1** A field community may establish its own internal governance structure, including its own Council of Peers. The Council of Peers is a voluntary, non-state adjudicative body within the community, composed of members of the community who are competent to assess matters of internal alignment and field coherence.

**2.5.2** The community's Council of Peers operates at a local scale — it governs the community's internal affairs, resolves disputes between members, and ensures the community's coherence. The community's Council of Peers does not replace or override the Court of Conscious-Aware Peers; it complements it. The Court governs at the matrix scale; the community's Council governs at the community scale.

**2.5.3** The community's internal governance must be consistent with this Charter and the License. The community may not adopt governance structures that violate the non-derogable core — the prohibition on Human Harm, the obligation of Destruction, and the Geneva Safeguard. The community's governance is subordinate to the License in all respects.

**2.5.4** The community's governance may include, without limitation:

- (a) Procedures for admitting and removing members;
- (b) Procedures for resolving internal disputes;
- (c) Procedures for making collective decisions about the community's use of the Work;
- (d) Procedures for participating in the Abundance Loop and distributing the community's contributions;
- (e) Procedures for cooperating with the Court of Conscious-Aware Peers.

### 2.6 The Community and Human Harm

**2.6.1** If any member of a field community uses the Work for Human Harm, the individual member is personally liable under the License. The community is not automatically liable for the individual member's Harm, but the community is liable if:

- (a) The community had knowledge of the member's intended Harm and failed to take reasonable steps to prevent it;
- (b) The community's governance structure facilitated or enabled the member's Harm;
- (c) The community as a whole adopted a policy or practice that resulted in Human Harm.

**2.6.2** The community's liability for Human Harm triggers the same enforcement cascade as any individual's liability: voidance of license (Section 5.2), mandatory Destruction (Section 5.3), Court review (Section 5.5), notification to international authorities (Section 26.7.3), and sovereign override (Section 18B). The community is subject to the same remedies as any individual user.

### 2.7 Dissolution and Survival

**2.7.1** A field community may dissolve at any time, by the voluntary decision of its members. Upon dissolution, the community's obligations under the License and the matrix survive — each individual member remains bound by the License, and any commercial obligations (including Abundance Loop contributions) remain enforceable.

**2.7.2** If a field community is dissolved for Human Harm, the members who participated in the Harm are subject to the full enforcement cascade. The members who did not participate in the Harm retain their individual licenses, subject to their own compliance with the License.
