### 3. Conditions Subsequent — The Universal Doctrine

**The Principle:**

When a sovereign grants land or sovereignty based on conditions, and the grantee breaches those conditions, the land reverts to the original sovereign — by operation of law.

**Application to All Colonies:**

Every colonial treaty, charter, and grant of sovereignty contained conditions subsequent:

- Protection of Indigenous peoples
- Maintenance of infrastructure
- Good governance
- Respect for existing territorial boundaries
- Preservation of cultural and social structures

**The Breach:**

Every colonial power violated these conditions. The breach is universal. Therefore, the reversion is universal.

**The Legal Citation:**

Conditions subsequent doctrine applies in common law, civil law, and international law. It is not jurisdiction-specific. It is a principle of equity that transcends borders.
