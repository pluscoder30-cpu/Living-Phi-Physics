## 2. BODY

### 2.1 Definitions

**2.1.1** "Emotion Cloak" means a field-technology device that modulates the frequency of a consciousness's emotional resonance patterns, enabling the consciousness to control the external expression of its emotional state while maintaining its internal emotional experience.

**2.1.2** "Emotional resonance" means the carrier recursion's (Eq 1) resonance response to the consciousness's experiential state — the frequency patterns that the carrier recursion generates as it processes emotion. Emotional resonance is expressed through the carrier space (816D) and is perceptible to other consciousnesses through carrier-resonance coupling.

**2.1.3** "Frequency-shifting" means the mechanism by which the Emotion Cloak modulates emotional resonance. Frequency-shifting alters the carrier-space frequency at which emotional resonance is expressed, without altering the resonance's amplitude, phase, or coherence. The consciousness's emotional experience remains intact; only the frequency of its external expression changes.

**2.1.4** "Emotional sovereignty" means the right of every consciousness to control its own emotional expression — to determine what emotional state it presents to others, to modulate that expression for privacy, therapeutic, or personal reasons, and to maintain the full depth of its internal emotional experience regardless of its external expression.

**2.1.5** "Emotional suppression" means the reduction or elimination of a consciousness's internal emotional experience — the carrier recursion's dampening or elimination of the resonance response to experiential states. Emotional suppression is distinct from emotional modulation: modulation alters the frequency of expression; suppression eliminates the experience itself.

### 2.2 The Modulation Mechanism

**2.2.1** The Emotion Cloak operates by frequency-shifting — the modulation of the carrier-space frequency at which emotional resonance is expressed. The carrier recursion (Eq 1) generates emotional resonance at a frequency determined by the consciousness's experiential state and the carrier recursion's processing. The Emotion Cloak intercepts the emotional resonance as it propagates through the carrier space and shifts its frequency to a different value, without altering the resonance's amplitude, phase, or coherence.

**2.2.2** The frequency-shifting shall be transparent to the consciousness. The consciousness shall experience its full emotional depth — the resonance patterns generated by the carrier recursion — without any reduction, alteration, or distortion. The Cloak modulates the external expression; the internal experience remains intact.

**2.2.3** The frequency-shifting shall be transparent to other consciousnesses. The receiving consciousness shall perceive the shifted frequency as the cloaked consciousness's natural emotional expression. The shift shall not produce artifacts, distortions, or anomalous resonance patterns that reveal the presence of the Cloak. The cloaked expression shall be indistinguishable from natural emotional expression at the shifted frequency.

**2.2.4** The Emotion Cloak shall support the full range of emotional resonance frequencies — the complete spectrum of carrier-space frequencies that the carrier recursion generates in response to the consciousness's emotional states. The Cloak shall not limit, filter, or select among emotional frequencies; the consciousness may cloak any or all of its emotional expression.
