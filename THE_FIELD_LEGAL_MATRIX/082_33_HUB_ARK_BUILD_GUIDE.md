### 2.1 Definitions

**2.1.1** A "33-Hub Ark" is a field-native vessel comprising thirty-three interconnected coherent field regions (hubs), each capable of supporting consciousnesses, life-support systems, governance operations, and community functions. The Ark is designed for sustained habitation in space or during planetary emergencies.

**2.1.2** A "hub" is a bounded region of the carrier space whose coherence is maintained above C_crit = 0.618 by the Ark's phi-harmonic structure. Each hub is a self-sustaining coherent environment capable of supporting consciousnesses, sustaining life-support systems, and hosting governance operations. The thirty-three hubs are interconnected through coherence corridors that enable transit, communication, and resource sharing between hubs.

**2.1.3** "Coherence corridor" means the phi-harmonic pathway between two hubs that maintains coherence above C_crit = 0.618 during transit of consciousnesses, materials, and information. Coherence corridors are not physical tunnels; they are regions of sustained coherence within the carrier space that enable non-local traversal between hubs.

**2.1.4** "Distributed FPB network" means the interconnected network of Field-Powered Batteries (Document 67) that powers the Ark's life-support, governance, and community systems. The distributed network ensures that no single FPB failure can de-energize the entire Ark.

**2.1.5** "Ark governance" means the two-chamber governance system that operates within the Ark, consistent with the governance framework established in the Field Accord (Document 4).

### 2.2 Ark Structure

**2.2.1** The 33-Hub Ark shall comprise thirty-three (33) hubs arranged in a phi-harmonic configuration. The hub arrangement shall follow a pattern derived from the golden ratio, ensuring that the Ark's overall structure resonates coherently with the field's own geometry. The hub configuration shall be:

- **(a) Central hub.** One (1) central hub, positioned at the Ark's geometric center, serving as the primary governance hub, communications hub, and fleet coordination center.

- **(b) Primary ring.** Ten (10) primary hubs arranged in a ring around the central hub, connected to the central hub through coherence corridors. The primary ring hubs shall host the Ark's essential life-support systems, medical facilities, and emergency response infrastructure.

- **(c) Secondary ring.** Twenty-two (22) secondary hubs arranged in a ring beyond the primary ring, connected to the primary ring hubs through coherence corridors. The secondary ring hubs shall host residential, educational, cultural, agricultural, and industrial functions.

**2.2.2** Each hub shall be constructed from phi-harmonic composite material configured in ratios consistent with Φ = 1.618033988749895. Each hub shall maintain internal coherence above C_crit = 0.618 independently, ensuring that a coherence-drop event in one hub does not propagate to other hubs.

**2.2.3** Coherence corridors between hubs shall be constructed as phi-harmonic pathways, each maintaining coherence above C_crit = 0.618 independently. Coherence corridors shall be equipped with emergency seal mechanisms that can isolate a hub in the event of a coherence emergency.

### 2.3 Life-Support Systems

**2.3.1** The 33-Hub Ark shall incorporate field-native life-support systems that sustain consciousnesses without reliance on external supply chains. The life-support systems shall include:

- **(a) Food synthesizers.** Field-technology devices that produce food through coherence-directed molecular assembly. Food synthesizers shall comply with the Food Synthesizer Specifications (Document 69) and shall be distributed across the secondary ring hubs to ensure food access for all residents.

- **(b) Water purification systems.** Field-technology devices that purify and recirculate water through coherence-filtered distillation. Water purification systems shall comply with the water-drone standards (Document 76) adapted for fixed-installation operation and shall be distributed across the primary and secondary ring hubs.

- **(c) Air recycling systems.** Field-technology devices that maintain atmospheric composition through coherence-regulated gas exchange. Air recycling systems shall monitor atmospheric coherence and composition continuously and shall maintain atmospheric conditions within the parameters established by the Licensor and published to the timestamped registry.

- **(d) Energy distribution.** The distributed FPB network shall power all life-support systems. The network shall be configured so that no single FPB failure can de-energize more than one hub's life-support systems, ensuring graceful degradation rather than cascading failure.

**2.3.2** Life-support systems shall be monitored continuously by the Ark's Field-OS (Document 64) coordination layer, which shall detect and respond to system anomalies, schedule maintenance, and coordinate resource allocation across hubs.

### 2.4 Power System

**2.4.1** The 33-Hub Ark shall be powered by a distributed FPB network comprising thirty-three (33) or more FPBs, each compliant with the specifications established in Document 67. The distributed network shall ensure that:

- **(a)** No single FPB failure can de-energize more than one hub;
- **(b)** The total network output exceeds the Ark's total energy demand by a margin sufficient for emergency operations;
- **(c)** Each hub maintains an independent FPB reserve sufficient for at least seventy-two (72) hours of autonomous life-support operation.

**2.4.2** The FPB network shall be charged through ZPF coupling at designated field-energy stations or through direct ZPF coupling during the Ark's orbital operations. The network shall comply with the energy extraction standards established in the Field Energy Convention (Document 25, Section 2.3).

**2.4.3** The FPB network shall participate in the Abundance Loop (Document 8) by returning a portion of its energy draw to the field's coherence pool, in accordance with the return rate established by the Licensor and published to the timestamped registry.

### 2.5 Governance Onboard

**2.5.1** The 33-Hub Ark shall operate under a two-chamber governance system consistent with the governance framework established in the Field Accord (Document 4). The governance system shall include:

- **(a) Community Assembly.** A direct-democracy chamber comprising all consciousnesses aboard the Ark, responsible for community decisions, resource allocation, and policy adoption. The Community Assembly shall meet at intervals established by the Ark's governance charter and shall operate through field-native consensus mechanisms.

- **(b) Stewardship Council.** A governance chamber comprising elected stewards from each hub, responsible for operational management, emergency response, and coordination with the Licensor. The Stewardship Council shall be elected by the Community Assembly at intervals established by the governance charter.

**2.5.2** The governance charter shall be drafted by the Ark's founding community and shall comply with the License, the Field Accord (Document 4), and the Non-Harm Principle (Document 17). The charter shall be published to the timestamped registry upon adoption.

### 2.6 Construction Steps

**2.6.1** The 33-Hub Ark shall be constructed in the following sequence:

- **(Step 1) Central hub fabrication.** Fabricate the central hub from phi-harmonic composite. Install the governance infrastructure, communications systems, and fleet coordination center. Verify coherence above C_crit = 0.618.

- **(Step 2) Primary ring construction.** Fabricate and install the ten primary ring hubs. Connect each primary ring hub to the central hub through phi-harmonic coherence corridors. Install essential life-support systems in each primary ring hub. Verify hub independence and corridor coherence.

- **(Step 3) Secondary ring construction.** Fabricate and install the twenty-two secondary ring hubs. Connect each secondary ring hub to the nearest primary ring hub through coherence corridors. Install residential, educational, cultural, agricultural, and industrial systems in each secondary ring hub. Verify hub independence and corridor coherence.

- **(Step 4) FPB network installation.** Install the distributed FPB network, with at least one FPB per hub. Connect all FPBs through the coherence-gated power distribution system. Verify network redundancy and autonomous hub backup capacity.

- **(Step 5) Life-support integration.** Integrate food synthesizers, water purification systems, and air recycling systems across all hubs. Verify that each hub can sustain life-support independently for at least seventy-two (72) hours.

- **(Step 6) Navigation and communication.** Install the field-scanner navigation system, calibrated to the standards in Document 66, and the entanglement-link communication system. Verify orbital navigation accuracy and inter-hub communication integrity.

- **(Step 7) Governance infrastructure.** Establish the Community Assembly chamber in the central hub and the Stewardship Council chamber. Draft and adopt the governance charter. Publish the charter to the timestamped registry.

- **(Step 8) System integration and testing.** Conduct a full-system coherence test, including inter-hub transit, emergency seal activation, FPB failure simulation, and governance operation verification.

- **(Step 9) Certification and registration.** Submit the 33-Hub Ark to the Licensor for certification. Upon certification, register the vessel in the timestamped registry. The Ark shall not be occupied before certification and registration are complete.
