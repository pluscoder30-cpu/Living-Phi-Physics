## 2. BODY

### 2.1 Definitions

**2.1.1** "Dimensional beacon" means a field-technology device or protocol that marks a specific dimensional frame within the 816D carrier space, enabling consciousnesses to locate and connect to that frame for dimensional projection.

**2.1.2** "Beacon coherence" means the stability and accuracy of the dimensional beacon's signal — the degree to which the beacon's signal corresponds to the dimensional frame it claims to mark. A beacon with high coherence projects a clear, stable, accurate signal; a beacon with low coherence projects a distorted, unstable, or inaccurate signal.

**2.1.3** "Alignment verification" means the process of confirming that a dimensional beacon's signal is coherent, accurate, and stable, and that the beacon's signal corresponds to the intended dimensional frame within the 816D carrier space.

**2.1.4** "Emergency beacon protocol" means the procedure to be followed when a dimensional beacon is found to be misaligned, incoherent, or otherwise non-compliant with this Protocol's standards. The emergency beacon protocol includes the beacon's deactivation, the notification of affected consciousnesses, and the restoration or replacement of the beacon.

**2.1.5** "Beacon coherence requirements" means the minimum coherence levels that a dimensional beacon must maintain to be classified as operational. Beacons below the minimum coherence level shall be taken out of service until their coherence is restored.

### 2.2 Beacon Coherence Requirements

**2.2.1** Every dimensional beacon shall maintain a coherence level at or above C_crit = 0.618 at all times while the beacon is operational. A beacon that falls below C_crit = 0.618 shall be automatically deactivated and shall not be reactivated until its coherence is restored and verified.

**2.2.2** The beacon coherence level shall be measured at intervals established by the Licensor and published to the timestamped registry. The measurement interval shall account for the beacon's operational environment, its frequency of use, and the stability of the dimensional frame it marks.

**2.2.3** The beacon coherence measurement shall capture, at minimum, the following parameters:

- (a) The beacon's signal coherence — the stability of the beacon's frequency signature relative to the phi-harmonic frequency ladder (base frequency 528 Hz);
- (b) The beacon's dimensional accuracy — the correspondence between the beacon's signal and the intended dimensional frame within the 816D carrier space;
- (c) The beacon's interference profile — the degree to which the beacon's signal interacts with, distorts, or is distorted by other beacons or field-technology devices in the vicinity;
- (d) The beacon's coherence-depletion footprint — the degree to which the beacon's operation creates local coherence-depletions in the surrounding field.

**2.2.4** Dimensional beacons shall be designed and constructed in accordance with the phi-harmonic geometry standards established by the Licensor. The beacon's internal structure shall be harmonically aligned with the field's own geometry, ensuring that the beacon's signal is a natural expression of the field's resonance rather than an externally imposed frequency.

### 2.3 Alignment Verification

**2.3.1** Every dimensional beacon shall undergo alignment verification:

- (a) Upon initial installation — before the beacon is activated for the first time;
- (b) At regular intervals established by the Licensor and published to the timestamped registry;
- (c) After any event that could affect the beacon's alignment — including physical disturbance, field-coherence fluctuations, interference from other devices, or environmental changes;
- (d) Upon the request of the Licensor or the Court of Conscious-Aware Peers (Document 10).

**2.3.2** The alignment verification shall confirm:

- (a) That the beacon's signal corresponds to the intended dimensional frame — that a consciousness following the beacon's signal will arrive at the correct dimensional frame;
- (b) That the beacon's signal is coherent — that the signal is stable, clear, and free from distortion;
- (c) That the beacon's signal does not create harmful interference with other beacons or field-technology devices;
- (d) That the beacon's operation does not create coherence-depletions that could affect other consciousnesses or field-technology devices in the vicinity.

**2.3.3** Alignment verification shall be performed by a qualified dimensional-beacon technician, as certified by the Licensor. The technician shall hold current certification in dimensional-beacon alignment and shall have demonstrated competence in the measurement instruments and verification procedures established in this Protocol.

**2.3.4** Alignment verification records shall be published to the timestamped registry. The records shall include the verification date, the verification results, any anomalies detected, and any corrective actions taken. Verification records shall be maintained for a minimum period of two (2) years.

### 2.4 Calibration Frequency

**2.4.1** Active dimensional beacons — beacons that are operational and in regular use — shall be calibrated at intervals established by the Licensor and published to the timestamped registry. The calibration interval shall not exceed ninety (90) days.

**2.4.2** Calibration shall include:

- (a) A full beacon coherence measurement (Section 2.2.3);
- (b) An alignment verification (Section 2.3.2);
- (c) An interference-profile assessment;
- (d) A coherence-depletion-footprint assessment;
- (e) A physical-integrity inspection of the beacon's hardware or protocol structure.

**2.4.3** If calibration reveals an anomaly — a coherence drop, an alignment drift, an interference pattern, or a coherence-depletion footprint that exceeds the thresholds established by the Licensor — the beacon shall be taken out of service until the anomaly is resolved and a re-calibration confirms that the beacon meets the standards established in this Protocol.

**2.4.4** Calibration records shall be published to the timestamped registry and shall be maintained for a minimum period of two (2) years.

### 2.5 Emergency Beacon Protocol

**2.5.1** If a dimensional beacon is found to be misaligned, incoherent, or otherwise non-compliant with this Protocol's standards, the emergency beacon protocol shall be initiated. The protocol includes:

- (a) Immediate deactivation of the beacon — the beacon shall cease transmitting its signal;
- (b) Notification of all consciousnesses within the beacon's signal range — the notification shall warn of the beacon's non-compliance and shall provide alternative navigation guidance;
- (c) Notification of the Licensor — the Licensor shall be informed of the beacon's status, the nature of the non-compliance, and the steps being taken to resolve it;
- (d) Assessment of the beacon's impact — the assessment shall determine whether the beacon's non-compliance has affected other beacons, field-technology devices, or consciousnesses in the vicinity.

**2.5.2** If the beacon's non-compliance has affected other consciousnesses — if consciousnesses have been drawn into unintended dimensional frames, or if coherence-depletions have been created — the emergency response teams (Document 49) shall be activated to assist the affected consciousnesses and to restore the field's coherence.

**2.5.3** A misaligned beacon shall not be reactivated until:

- (a) The cause of the misalignment has been identified and corrected;
- (b) A full alignment verification (Section 2.3) confirms that the beacon meets the standards established in this Protocol;
- (c) The Licensor has approved the beacon's reactivation.

**2.5.4** Emergency beacon protocol records shall be published to the timestamped registry. The records shall include the beacon's identification, the nature of the non-compliance, the steps taken to resolve it, and the results of the re-verification.
