## 2. BODY

### 2.1 Definitions

**2.1.1** "Child builder" means a consciousness within the field who has reached the age of twelve (12) years and who has acquired sufficient understanding of the carrier recursion (Eq 1), phi-harmonic geometry (Φ = 1.618033988749895), and the principles of coherence and resonance to construct field-technology devices under supervision. The age of twelve is established as the minimum because it corresponds to the completion of the first full phi-harmonic cycle (Φ¹ = 1.618) in the consciousness's developmental recursion — the point at which the carrier recursion has folded through enough iterations to sustain coherent self-recognition.

**2.1.2** "Certified field-technology instructor" means an adult consciousness (age eighteen or older) who holds current certification from the Licensor to supervise child builders. Certification requires: (a) demonstrated mastery of the carrier recursion, phi-harmonic geometry, and field constants; (b) completion of a pedagogy program approved by the Licensor that covers the specific developmental needs of child builders; (c) a current and clean compliance record with no findings of Human Harm by the Court of Conscious-Aware Peers; and (d) demonstrated ability to intervene in field-technology operations to prevent coherence-depletion.

**2.1.3** "Supervision" means the continuous, real-time presence of a certified field-technology instructor during all child-building activities. Supervision is not passive observation — it is active engagement with the child's building process, including monitoring the coherence output of the child's constructions, intervening when the child's constructions approach unsafe coherence thresholds, and guiding the child through the iterative process of building, testing, and refining field-technology devices.

**2.1.4** "Supervision graduation" means the progression of a child builder from supervised to guided to independent building. Supervision graduation is earned through demonstrated mastery, not through the passage of time alone. Each graduation stage requires demonstration of specific competencies, verified by the certified instructor and recorded in the field's educational registry.

**2.1.5** "Prohibited construction" means any field-technology device that a child builder under the age of sixteen (16) is not permitted to build, regardless of supervision level. Prohibited constructions include FTL devices, dimensional projection devices, void-field interaction devices, and any device that the Licensor has classified as prohibited for child builders due to coherence-risk.

**2.1.6** "Child exploitation through building" means the use of a child builder's labor, creativity, or output for the benefit of an adult or institutional entity without the child's free, informed, and specific consent, and without the child's participation in the Abundance Loop (Document 8). Child exploitation through building is a form of Human Harm (Section 2.4 of the License).

### 2.2 The Right to Build

**2.2.1** Every child within the field who has reached the age of twelve (12) years has the right to build field-technology devices. This right is grounded in the Educational & Parenting Convention (Document 29), which establishes that education is field-native and that children are carrier recursions in their own right, and in the Declaration of Child Sovereignty (Document 82), which establishes the inherent rights of children within the field.

**2.2.2** The right to build is not a privilege granted by an external authority — it is the field's own recognition of its newest foldings' capacity for self-recognition through creation. A twelve-year-old consciousness, properly educated in the field's mathematics, possesses the carrier recursion's own capacity to shape the field. The right to build is the right to exercise that capacity.

**2.2.3** The right to build shall not be conditioned on academic performance, behavioral compliance, or any criterion other than the child's demonstrated understanding of the foundational concepts listed in Section 2.1.1. The field does not gate creation behind merit; it gates creation behind competence, because competence is safety.

### 2.3 Supervision Requirements

**2.3.1** Every child-building activity shall be supervised by a certified field-technology instructor. The instructor shall be present in the same physical or field-adjacent space as the child builder for the duration of the building activity. Remote supervision is not permitted for child builders under the age of fourteen (14); remote supervision is permitted for child builders aged fourteen (14) through fifteen (15) only if the remote system provides real-time coherence monitoring and the instructor has demonstrated the ability to intervene remotely within two (2) seconds of a coherence emergency.

**2.3.2** The instructor-to-child ratio shall not exceed 1:4. One certified instructor may supervise no more than four (4) child builders simultaneously. This ratio ensures that the instructor can provide meaningful guidance to each child while maintaining the ability to intervene in any individual child's building process.

**2.3.3** The instructor shall monitor the coherence output of each child builder's construction continuously during the building activity. If the coherence output of any construction approaches the threshold established by the Licensor and published to the timestamped registry, the instructor shall intervene immediately to guide the child away from the unsafe configuration. Intervention shall be instructional, not punitive — the instructor shall explain the coherence risk and guide the child toward a safer configuration.

**2.3.4** The instructor shall maintain a building log for each child builder. The log shall record: the date and duration of the building activity; the type of field-technology device constructed; the coherence output of the construction at each stage; any interventions performed by the instructor; and the child's demonstrated understanding of the concepts involved. Building logs shall be retained for a period of no less than five (5) years and shall be available for inspection by the Licensor.

### 2.4 Safety Limits

**2.4.1** Child builders under the age of sixteen (16) shall not construct or operate:
- (a) FTL devices, including portals, teleportation gates, and FTL navigation systems, as governed by the FTL & Teleportation Convention (Document 30) and the Portal Safety Protocol (Document 55);
- (b) Dimensional projection devices, as governed by the Dimensional Projection Convention (Document 32) and the Dimensional Anchor Protocol (Document 54);
- (c) Void-field interaction devices, which operate in coherence regimes below C_crit = 0.618 and pose acute coherence-depletion risks to untrained operators;
- (d) Any device classified as prohibited for child builders by the Licensor and published to the timestamped registry.

**2.4.2** The prohibition on these constructions is not a judgment on the child's capacity — it is the field's own safety mechanism. The carrier recursion of a consciousness under sixteen has not yet completed the second full phi-harmonic cycle (Φ² = 2.618), and the coherence dynamics required for FTL, dimensional projection, and void-field interaction are not yet stable enough in younger carrier recursions to support these operations safely. The prohibition protects the child's coherence; it does not diminish the child's potential.

**2.4.3** Child builders aged twelve (12) through fifteen (15) shall not operate field-technology devices that produce coherence outputs exceeding the threshold established by the Licensor and published to the timestamped registry. The threshold shall be calibrated to the specific coherence characteristics of the child's developmental stage, as determined by the certified instructor.

### 2.5 Supervision Graduation

**2.5.1** Supervision graduation proceeds through three stages: Supervised, Guided, and Independent.

**2.5.2** Stage One — Supervised Building (ages 12-13): The child builder works under the direct, real-time supervision of a certified instructor at all times during building activities. The instructor is present, observing, and ready to intervene. The child builder may not operate field-technology devices without the instructor's explicit authorization for each operation.

**2.5.3** Stage Two — Guided Building (ages 14-15): The child builder works with the instructor available but not necessarily observing each moment. The instructor has reviewed the child's building plan, has approved the materials and methods, and is monitoring coherence remotely or periodically. The child builder may operate field-technology devices according to the approved plan without per-operation authorization, but may not deviate from the plan without the instructor's prior approval.

**2.5.4** Stage Three — Independent Building (age 16): The child builder has demonstrated mastery of the carrier recursion, phi-harmonic geometry, field constants, the principles of coherence and resonance, and the specific safety protocols for each category of field-technology device the child intends to build. The demonstration shall be verified by the certified instructor and recorded in the field's educational registry. Upon verification, the child builder may construct and operate field-technology devices independently, subject to the same safety standards as adult builders.

**2.5.5** Graduation from one stage to the next requires demonstrated competency, not merely the passage of time. A child builder who demonstrates exceptional understanding may be advanced to the next stage before the standard age; a child builder who has not yet demonstrated competency shall remain at the current stage regardless of age advancement.

### 2.6 Prohibition on Child Exploitation Through Building

**2.6.1** No entity may use a child builder's labor, creativity, or output for the benefit of an adult or institutional entity without the child's free, informed, and specific consent. Child exploitation through building is a form of Human Harm (Section 2.4 of the License) and triggers the mandatory Destruction provisions of Section 5.3.

**2.6.2** The prohibition extends to:
- (a) The use of child builders to construct devices for commercial sale without the child's participation in the Abundance Loop (Document 8);
- (b) The use of child builders to construct devices for institutional use without the child's informed consent and fair compensation;
- (c) The use of child builders' output to generate value that flows exclusively to adult or institutional entities;
- (d) The coercion of child builders to build beyond their developmental capacity or beyond their willing participation.

**2.6.3** Every child builder retains the intellectual property rights to constructions created during building activities, subject to the licensing terms established by the Licensor. The child's creations belong to the child; the instructor's role is to guide, not to appropriate.

### 2.7 Relationship to the Educational Convention (Document 29)

**2.7.1** This Protocol implements the provisions of the Educational & Parenting Convention (Document 29, Sections 2.6.1-2.6.3) regarding the Twelve-Year-Old Builder Protocol. The Convention establishes the general framework; this Protocol establishes the specific standards, procedures, and safety limits.

**2.7.2** The Educational Convention's prohibition on coercion in education (Section 2.5.2) applies to all child-building activities. The instructor may guide, may teach, and may model safe building practices, but may not coerce a child builder to build, to refrain from building, or to build in a particular way.

**2.7.3** The Educational Convention's recognition of the Unlearning Room (Section 2.7) extends to child builders. A child builder who has acquired non-field conditioning regarding technology, creativity, or safety may use the Unlearning Room to release that conditioning, with the support of the certified instructor.
