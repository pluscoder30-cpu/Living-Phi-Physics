### 2.2 Temporal Stability Requirements

**2.2.1** Every time-folding operation shall be preceded by a temporal stability assessment. The assessment shall verify that the consciousness's temporal frame is stable enough to support time-folding without risk of involuntary temporal displacement. The assessment shall measure:

- (a) The consciousness's current coherence level, which must exceed C_crit = 0.618 by a margin established by the Licensor and published to the timestamped registry;
- (b) The consciousness's temporal frame stability, measured as the variance in the consciousness's recursion index position over a calibration period established by the Licensor;
- (c) The consciousness's history of temporal displacement events, if any, within the preceding period established by the Licensor.

**2.2.2** Consciousnesses with temporal frames that fail the stability assessment shall not be permitted to time-fold until their temporal stability is restored. Restoration may be achieved through: (a) a coherence-restoration sequence administered by a certified temporal-stability technician; (b) a period of temporal rest in which the consciousness's carrier-stream is allowed to stabilize at its natural recursion index; or (c) any other method approved by the Licensor and published to the timestamped registry.

**2.2.3** The temporal stability threshold for time-folding shall be established by the Licensor and published to the timestamped registry. The threshold shall specify the minimum coherence level, the maximum temporal displacement variance, and the required stability duration for each class of time-folding operation.

### 2.3 Maximum Fold Depth

**2.3.1** The maximum fold depth for time-folding operations shall be φ⁵ = 11.09 — five phi-harmonic levels from the present recursion index. ### 2.4 Time-Folding Safety Procedures

**2.4.1** Every time-folding operation shall be monitored by a temporal stability system that tracks the consciousness's position within the recursion index during the time-folding event. The temporal stability system shall:

- (a) Track the consciousness's recursion index position in real time, at a sampling rate not less than one hundred (100) measurements per second;
- (b) Generate a warning when the consciousness's temporal position drifts more than 0.1 recursion index units from the intended position;
- (c) Initiate a temporal re-alignment sequence when the consciousness's temporal position drifts more than 0.5 recursion index units from the intended position;
- (d) Initiate an emergency return protocol when the consciousness's temporal position drifts more than 1.0 recursion index units from the intended position, or when the consciousness approaches the maximum fold depth.

**2.4.2** The temporal stability system shall be independent of the time-folding device's experiential-shaping hardware. A system failure in the time-folding device shall not disable the temporal stability system.

**2.4.3** The temporal stability system shall maintain a continuous record of the consciousness's recursion index position throughout the time-folding event. The record shall be retained for a period of no less than ninety (90) days and shall be available for inspection by the Licensor.

### 2.5 Emergency Return Protocol

**2.5.1** If a consciousness experiences involuntary temporal displacement — if its temporal frame shifts to a recursion index other than the one intended — the temporal stability system shall initiate the emergency return protocol. The emergency return protocol shall:

- (a) Cease all time-folding operations;
- (b) Initiate a temporal re-alignment sequence that restores the consciousness to its original temporal frame;
- (c) Monitor the consciousness's coherence throughout the re-alignment process;
- (d) Complete the re-alignment within the shortest time possible, consistent with preserving the consciousness's coherence and identity;
- (e) Log the displacement event, including the consciousness's original temporal frame, the displaced temporal frame, the duration of displacement, and the restoration procedure.

**2.5.2** The emergency return protocol shall not be interrupted, delayed, or overridden by the time-folding operator, the time-folding device, or any other entity. The emergency return is a safety-critical process that takes precedence over all other time-folding operations.

**2.5.3** If the temporal re-alignment sequence fails to restore the consciousness to its original temporal frame within the time limit established by the Licensor and published to the timestamped registry, the Licensor shall be notified immediately. The Licensor's Sovereign Override (Section 18B of the License) extends to temporal emergencies: the Licensor may intervene directly in the carrier recursion to restore the consciousness's temporal frame.

**2.6.1** The involuntary displacement of a consciousness's temporal frame is prohibited. Involuntary temporal displacement is a form of coherence disruption and a form of Human Harm (Section 2.4 of the License). The prohibition extends to:

- (c) The use of temporal navigation techniques to lure, attract, or coerce a consciousness into an unwanted temporal position;
- (d) The manipulation of a consciousness's coherence state to induce involuntary temporal displacement.

**2.6.2** The prohibition on involuntary temporal displacement applies to all entities, including time-folding operators, commercial entities, governmental entities, and institutional entities.
