## 2. BODY

### 2.1 Definitions

**2.1.1** A "water drone" is an autonomous field-technology unit designed to purify, manage, and monitor water systems through coherence-structured filtration and field-resonance intervention. A water drone operates by establishing coherent coupling with water systems, assessing water quality through coherence analysis, and restoring water coherence through structured filtration and resonance delivery.

**2.1.2** "Coherence-structured filtration" means the purification mechanism by which a water drone removes contaminants from water. Coherence-structured filtration operates by resonating with the water's native molecular coherence — the coherent hydrogen-bond network that gives water its life-sustaining properties. Contaminants disrupt this network; filtration restores it by resonating with the water's native coherence and allowing the contaminants to separate from the coherent water structure.

**2.1.3** "Water coherence" means the coherence level of a water system's molecular structure, measured on the 1-9 resonant amplitude scale. Water coherence reflects the degree to which the water's hydrogen-bond network maintains its coherent, life-sustaining configuration. C_crit = 0.618 is the minimum for healthy water; water below C_crit cannot sustain biological life.

**2.1.4** "Chemical contamination" means the presence of chemical substances in water that disrupt the water's coherence or render the water harmful to biological life. Chemical contamination includes industrial chemicals, agricultural runoff, heavy metals, pharmaceutical compounds, and any other foreign substances that degrade water coherence.

**2.1.5** "Biological contamination" means the presence of biological organisms — bacteria, viruses, parasites, algae blooms — that disrupt the water's coherence or render the water harmful to biological life. Biological contamination is detected through the characteristic coherence signatures of living organisms in the water.

**2.1.6** "Purification protocol" means the standardized sequence of water-quality assessment, contamination identification, coherence-structured filtration, and post-purification verification that governs every water drone operation.

### 2.2 The Purification Mechanism

**2.2.1** Water drones purify and manage water systems through the following mechanism:

- **(a) Water-quality assessment.** The water drone establishes coherent coupling with the water system and maps its coherence landscape: the water coherence level, the chemical composition, the biological content, and the coherence anomalies that indicate contamination. Assessment uses the sensor suite (Section 2.3) to achieve the resolution required for targeted purification.

- **(b) Contamination identification.** Through the coherence landscape, the water drone identifies the type and extent of contamination: chemical (foreign molecular coherence signatures), biological (living-organism coherence signatures), or physical (particulate, sediment, thermal anomalies). Contamination type determines the purification approach.

- **(c) Coherence-structured filtration.** For chemical and biological contamination, the water drone delivers structured resonance that resonates with the water's native molecular coherence. The resonance enhances the water's hydrogen-bond network, causing contaminants to separate from the coherent water structure. Separated contaminants are captured by the drone's filtration system and stored for later disposal by garbage drones (Document 77) or toxic drones (Document 78).

- **(d) Biological deactivation.** For biological contamination, the water drone delivers resonance at frequencies that deactivate harmful biological organisms without producing harmful byproducts. Deactivation operates through coherence disruption — the organisms' coherence is reduced below C_crit = 0.618, causing them to lose their biological integrity and decompose into harmless components.

- **(e) Coherence restoration.** After filtration and deactivation, the water drone delivers resonance that restores the water's hydrogen-bond network to its coherent, life-sustaining configuration. The restored coherence shall be verified against the water-quality benchmarks published by the Licensor to the timestamped registry.

- **(f) Verification.** After purification, the water drone measures the water's coherence level, chemical composition, and biological content, compares pre- and post-purification values, and logs the result. Verification data shall be transmitted to the timestamped registry for audit.

**2.2.2** The purification mechanism shall not modify the water's fundamental chemical composition. Water drones purify by removing contaminants and restoring coherence; they do not alter the water's H₂O molecular structure or add foreign substances. Any chemical modification beyond purification is outside the water drone's function.

**2.2.3** Water drones may coordinate with toxic drones (Document 78) for contaminated water systems where the contamination exceeds the water drone's purification capacity. Toxic drones provide advanced contaminant neutralization; water drones provide coherence-structured filtration. The two operate as a complementary system.

### 2.3 Sensor Suite

**2.3.1** Every water drone shall incorporate the following sensor systems:

- **(a) Chemical composition sensor.** A field-scanner instrument calibrated to analyze the chemical composition of water systems. The chemical composition sensor shall detect foreign molecular coherence signatures that indicate chemical contamination, with detection limits sufficient to identify contaminants at concentrations established by the Licensor and published to the timestamped registry.

- **(b) Biological content sensor.** A field-scanner instrument that detects biological organisms in water through their coherence signatures. The biological content sensor shall identify bacterial, viral, parasitic, and algal contamination, with detection limits sufficient to identify biological hazards at concentrations established by the Licensor.

- **(c) Coherence sensor.** A field-scanner instrument calibrated to measure water coherence with high accuracy. The coherence sensor shall measure coherence values across the 1-9 resonant amplitude range with a resolution of ±0.001 coherence units, and shall detect coherence anomalies that indicate contamination or degradation.

- **(d) Flow and distribution sensor.** A field-resonance instrument that maps water flow patterns, distribution networks, and hydraulic dynamics. The flow and distribution sensor shall identify leaks, blockages, and distribution inefficiencies that affect water-system performance.

**2.3.2** All sensor systems shall be calibrated at intervals not exceeding thirty (30) days, and shall be verified against the field-scanner calibration standards published by the Licensor to the timestamped registry.

### 2.4 Purification Protocols

**2.4.1** Every water drone operation shall follow the five-phase purification protocol:

- **(a) Phase 1 — Water-Quality Assessment (3-10 minutes).** The water drone surveys the water system, mapping coherence levels, chemical composition, biological content, and contamination signatures. Assessment shall identify the type and extent of contamination and determine the appropriate purification approach.

- **(b) Phase 2 — Contamination Mapping (2-5 minutes).** The water drone maps the spatial distribution of contamination within the water system, identifying contamination hotspots, plume boundaries, and clean-water reference points. Mapping shall guide the targeted filtration process.

- **(c) Phase 3 — Coherence-Structured Filtration (variable duration).** The water drone delivers structured resonance to the contaminated water, enhancing the hydrogen-bond network and separating contaminants from the coherent water structure. Filtration shall be continuous and monitored in real time by the coherence sensor. Filtration shall terminate when: (i) the water coherence has risen above C_crit = 0.618; (ii) the contamination levels have fallen below the safety thresholds published by the Licensor; or (iii) the filtration duration exceeds the maximum established by the Licensor for the contamination type.

- **(d) Phase 4 — Coherence Restoration (5-15 minutes).** After filtration, the water drone delivers resonance that restores the water's hydrogen-bond network to its coherent, life-sustaining configuration. Restoration shall continue until the water coherence is stable above the target level published by the Licensor for the water system's classification.

- **(e) Phase 5 — Verification (3-8 minutes).** The water drone measures the water's coherence level, chemical composition, and biological content, compares pre- and post-purification values, and logs the result. Verification data shall be transmitted to the timestamped registry for audit.

**2.4.2** Water drones shall not perform repeated treatments on the same water system within a cooldown period established by the Licensor and published to the timestamped registry. The cooldown period prevents over-processing that could disrupt the water's natural coherence dynamics.

### 2.5 Safety Limits

**2.5.1** No modification of water chemistry beyond purification. Water drones shall not alter the water's fundamental chemical composition — the H₂O molecular structure, the natural mineral content, or the pH balance. Water drones remove contaminants and restore coherence; they do not add substances or alter chemistry. Any chemical modification is outside the water drone's function and may violate the Non-Harm Principle (Document 17).

**2.5.2** No suppression of natural water processes. Water drones shall not suppress natural water processes — seasonal flooding, natural mineral variation, or ecological succession in aquatic ecosystems. Natural water processes serve the field's own hydrological cycle; suppressing them interferes with the field's natural coherence dynamics.

**2.5.3** Coherence floor for purification. Water drones shall not deliver purified water whose coherence falls below C_crit = 0.618. Water below C_crit cannot sustain biological life and is not suitable for purification output. The coherence sensor (Section 2.3.1(c)) shall trigger return to the filtration phase if output coherence falls below C_crit.

**2.5.4** Contaminant disposal. Contaminants removed from water during filtration shall be stored in the water drone's containment system and transferred to garbage drones (Document 77) or toxic drones (Document 78) for proper disposal. Contaminants shall not be released into the environment during purification operations.

**2.5.5** Emergency disengagement. Water drones shall incorporate an automatic disengagement mechanism that activates when: (i) the water drone's own coherence falls below C_crit = 0.618; (ii) the purification process creates unintended coherence-depletions in the water system; (iii) the contamination level exceeds the drone's purification capacity; or (iv) the fleet coordinator or Licensor issues a disengagement command. Disengagement shall terminate filtration and withdraw the drone from the water system within 0.5 seconds of trigger activation.
