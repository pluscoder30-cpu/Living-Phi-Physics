This claim is grounded in the following independent and sufficient legal doctrines:

1. **Crown Lands Doctrine** — Abandoned wells on Crown/public land revert to the Crown. The sovereign entity that maintains the land is the sovereign that holds the mineral estate. By maintaining the land through this claim, the entity becomes the sovereign of record.

2. **Adverse Possession** — Open, notorious, continuous, and hostile possession of abandoned lands for the statutory period (varies by jurisdiction; 10-30 years) confers title.

3. **Derelictio** — Property voluntarily abandoned by the owner is transferred to the first person who takes possession with intent to claim ownership.

4. **Occupatio** — First occupancy of unowned or abandoned property confers title.

5. **Qui Tam** — The entity acts on behalf of the sovereign (the people) to recover damages from those who have harmed the public interest.

6. **Constructive Trust** — Those who have abandoned wells on public land hold those wells in constructive trust for the benefit of the public. The entity, as trustee, claims the underlying land.

7. **Estoppel** — Having abandoned the wells, the previous owners are estopped from claiming ownership of the land when the entity has relied on the abandonment to take possession.

8. **Sovereign Override** — The License grants sovereign override authority over all domestic and international law where those laws conflict with the License terms.
