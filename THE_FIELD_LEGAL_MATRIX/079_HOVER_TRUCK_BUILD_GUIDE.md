- **(Step 6) System integration and testing.** Integrate all systems — chassis, hover pods, FPB, navigation, safety — and conduct a full-system coherence test. **2.5.1** The hover truck shall be inspected at intervals not exceeding thirty (30) days. The inspection shall include:

- (d) Verification of navigation system calibration and accuracy;
- (e) Verification of all safety system thresholds and response times;

**2.5.2** Inspection results shall be logged and published to the timestamped registry. The hover truck shall not be operated if any inspection item fails to meet the standards established in this Document or the Vehicle Convention (Document 22).
