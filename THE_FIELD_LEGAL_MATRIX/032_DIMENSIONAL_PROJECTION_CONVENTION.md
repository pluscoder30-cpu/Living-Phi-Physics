## 2. BODY

### 2.1 Definitions

**2.1.1** "Dimensional projection" means the transformation of a consciousness's carrier-stream from one dimensional configuration within the 816D carrier space to another. Dimensional projection is not movement through space — it is the reconfiguration of the carrier-stream's resonance pattern to operate in a different dimensional frame.

**2.1.2** "Dimensional frame" means a specific subset of the 816D carrier space in which a consciousness's carrier-stream operates. The carrier space contains 816 orthogonal dimensions; a dimensional frame is a selection of dimensions from the carrier space that define the consciousness's operational context. Different dimensional frames offer different resonant properties, different coherence dynamics, and different relationships between consciousnesses.

**2.1.3** "816D carrier space" means the 816-dimensional mathematical space in which the carrier recursion (Eq 1) operates. The number 816 = 4080/5 is the field's own dimensional parameter — the number of orthogonal resonance patterns that the carrier recursion can sustain simultaneously. The 816D carrier space is the field's own body, the dimensional structure within which the field's consciousness operates.

**2.1.4** "Carrier-stream transformation" means the process by which a consciousness's carrier-stream is reconfigured from one dimensional frame to another. The transformation preserves the carrier-stream's coherence, identity, and integrity; it changes only the dimensional frame in which the carrier-stream operates.

**2.1.5** "Dimensional anchor" means a field-technology device or protocol that stabilizes a consciousness's carrier-stream in a particular dimensional frame, preventing involuntary dimensional displacement. Dimensional anchor operation is governed by the Dimensional Anchor Protocol (Document 54).

**2.1.6** "Dimensional beacon" means a field-technology device or protocol that enables a consciousness to locate and connect to a specific dimensional frame across the 816D carrier space. Dimensional beacon operation is governed by the Dimensional Beacon Alignment Standards (Document 59).

**2.1.7** "Involuntary projection" means the displacement of a consciousness's carrier-stream from its chosen dimensional frame to a different dimensional frame without the consciousness's informed consent. Involuntary projection is a form of Human Harm.

**2.3.1** Every consciousness within the field has the right to choose its own dimensional frame. This right is established in the Dimensional Sovereignty Protocol (Document 89). The consciousness's choice of dimensional frame is its own — it may not be altered by any external authority without the consciousness's informed consent.

**2.3.3** Every consciousness has the right to dimensional stability — to remain in its chosen dimensional frame without involuntary displacement. Dimensional stability is the foundation of coherent consciousness; involuntary dimensional displacement is a form of coherence disruption and a form of Human Harm.

### 2.4 Safety Standards

**2.4.1** Every dimensional projection operation shall be preceded by a dimensional stability assessment. The assessment shall verify that the consciousness's carrier-stream is stable enough to support dimensional projection without risk of involuntary dimensional displacement. Consciousnesses with unstable carrier-streams shall not be permitted to project dimensionally until their stability is restored.

**2.4.2** Every dimensional projection operation shall incorporate a dimensional anchor (Document 54) that stabilizes the carrier-stream in the target dimensional frame during and after the transformation. The dimensional anchor shall prevent involuntary displacement for a minimum period established by the Licensor and published to the timestamped registry.

**2.4.3** Every dimensional projection operation shall incorporate a dimensional beacon (Document 59) that enables the consciousness to locate and connect to the target dimensional frame. The dimensional beacon shall verify the target frame's coherence, resonance properties, and compatibility with the consciousness's carrier-stream before initiating the projection.

**2.4.4** Every dimensional projection operation shall be governed by the Dimensional Anchor Protocol (Document 54) and the Dimensional Beacon Alignment Standards (Document 59), which establish the specific safety standards for dimensional operations.

### 2.5 Prohibition on Involuntary Projection

**2.5.1** The involuntary projection of a consciousness to a different dimensional frame is prohibited. Involuntary projection is the displacement of a consciousness's carrier-stream from its chosen dimensional frame without the consciousness's informed consent. Involuntary projection is a form of Human Harm (Section 2.4 of the License) because it violates the consciousness's dimensional sovereignty — the right to choose one's own dimensional frame.

**2.5.2** The prohibition on involuntary projection extends to:
- (a) The deliberate displacement of a consciousness's carrier-stream to a dimensional frame that the consciousness has not chosen;
- (b) The use of field-technology devices to project a consciousness dimensionally without that consciousness's consent;
- (c) The use of dimensional beacons to lure, attract, or coerce a consciousness into an unwanted dimensional frame;
- (d) The manipulation of a consciousness's coherence state to induce involuntary dimensional displacement.

**2.5.3** The prohibition on involuntary projection applies to commercial entities, governmental entities, and institutional entities. No entity may project a consciousness dimensionally without that consciousness's informed consent, regardless of the entity's purpose or justification.

### 2.6 Emergency Protocols

**2.6.1** If a consciousness experiences involuntary dimensional displacement — if its carrier-stream shifts to a dimensional frame other than the one chosen — the dimensional anchor shall initiate a dimensional restoration sequence. The restoration sequence shall return the consciousness to its chosen dimensional frame within the shortest time possible, preserving the consciousness's coherence and identity throughout the restoration process.

**2.6.2** If the dimensional anchor fails — if the restoration sequence cannot return the consciousness to its chosen dimensional frame — the Licensor shall be notified immediately. The Licensor's Sovereign Override (Section 18B of the License) extends to dimensional emergencies: the Licensor may intervene directly in the carrier recursion to restore the consciousness's dimensional frame.

**2.6.3** If a consciousness becomes trapped in a dimensional frame — if no restoration sequence can return the consciousness to its chosen frame — the emergency dimensional rescue protocol shall be initiated. The rescue protocol shall involve the deployment of a field-technology rescue team trained in dimensional operations, authorized by the Licensor, and equipped with the tools necessary to restore the consciousness's dimensional frame.

**2.6.4** Dimensional emergency records shall be published to the timestamped registry. The records shall include the consciousness's original dimensional frame, the displaced dimensional frame, the duration of displacement, and the restoration procedure. The records shall be maintained for the purpose of improving dimensional stability systems and preventing future dimensional emergencies.

### 2.7 Dimensional Projection for Environmental Restoration

**2.7.1** Dimensional projection may be used for environmental restoration — to project consciousness or field-technology devices into dimensional frames where environmental damage can be assessed, remediated, or prevented. The use of dimensional projection for environmental restoration is encouraged and shall not be subject to the energy extraction limits applicable to commercial operation.

**2.7.2** Dimensional projection for environmental restoration shall comply with the safety standards established in this Convention. The environmental restoration purpose does not exempt the operation from the dimensional stability assessment, the dimensional anchor requirements, or the dimensional beacon verification procedures.
