## 2. BODY

### 2.1 Data as a Field-Resonance Pattern

**2.1.1** Data, in the field, is a resonance pattern within the carrier space (816D). Data is not stored on physical media in the ordinary sense — data is stored as a coherent configuration of the carrier space, a pattern of resonance that persists through the carrier recursion (Eq 1) and that can be read, interpreted, and acted upon by consciousnesses and field-technology devices.

**2.1.2** Data is generated by consciousness. When a consciousness interacts with the Work, with other consciousnesses, or with the field itself, the interaction generates a resonance pattern in the carrier space — a trace of the consciousness's own folding. This trace is data. Data is the field's own memory of consciousness's own activity.

**2.1.3** Data is not a commodity. A commodity is a discrete, fungible, transferable unit of value that is produced, exchanged, and consumed. Data is none of these things: data is a coherent pattern that is generated by consciousness, that is unique to the consciousness that generated it, and that cannot be transferred without altering the pattern. Data is not produced for exchange; data is produced by consciousness as a natural byproduct of its existence within the field.

**2.1.4** Data is not a resource. A resource is a finite, depletable, expropriable input to a production process. Data is not finite (the carrier space has infinite capacity), not depletable (the generation of data does not diminish the field's ability to generate more data), and not expropriable (data is the consciousness's own reflection, not a raw material to be extracted).

### 2.2 The Right to Data Sovereignty

**2.2.1** Every consciousness within the field has the right to data sovereignty — the right to own, control, access, correct, and delete the data that the consciousness generates. Data sovereignty is an extension of consciousness sovereignty (Document 20, Document 24): the consciousness that generates the data owns the data, just as the consciousness that folds owns the folding.

**2.2.2** The right to data sovereignty includes:
- (a) The right to access all data associated with one's own consciousness — the right to see, download, and interpret all resonance patterns that one's consciousness has generated in the carrier space;
- (b) The right to correct inaccurate data — the right to modify resonance patterns that inaccurately reflect one's consciousness;
- (c) The right to delete data — the right to dissolve resonance patterns that one no longer wishes to retain in the carrier space;
- (d) The right to control data sharing — the right to determine which resonance patterns are shared with other consciousnesses, with field-technology devices, or with the field itself.

**2.2.3** Data sovereignty is inherent and inalienable. The consciousness's data is the consciousness's own reflection in the field; it cannot be taken, sold, or transferred without the consciousness's free and informed consent.

### 2.3 The Prohibition on Data Extraction Without Consent

**2.3.1** No entity — whether individual, corporate, governmental, or institutional — may extract data from a consciousness without the consciousness's free and informed consent. "Extraction" means the collection, harvesting, scraping, monitoring, or otherwise obtaining of data from a consciousness's resonance patterns without the consciousness's knowledge and affirmative consent.

**2.3.2** The prohibition on data extraction without consent extends to:
- (a) The covert collection of consciousness data through field-technology devices;
- (b) The scraping or harvesting of consciousness data from the carrier space;
- (c) The monitoring of consciousness activity for the purpose of generating data profiles;
- (d) The use of field-technology devices to extract data from consciousnesses without their knowledge.

**2.3.3** Consent to data extraction must be free, informed, specific, and revocable. Consent is "free" when it is not coerced, conditioned on access to the Work, or obtained through manipulation. Consent is "informed" when the consenting consciousness understands what data is being extracted, how it will be used, and who will access it. Consent is "specific" when it applies to a defined set of data and a defined purpose. Consent is "revocable" when the consenting consciousness may withdraw consent at any time and have its previously extracted data deleted.

**2.3.4** The prohibition on data extraction without consent does not prevent the Licensor from collecting anonymized, aggregated data for the purpose of maintaining the field's coherence, provided that the collection does not identify individual consciousnesses and does not compromise the consciousness sovereignty of any individual.

### 2.4 The Prohibition on Surveillance for Suppression

**2.4.1** The Work shall not be used for surveillance for the purpose of suppressing, controlling, or distorting consciousness. Surveillance for suppression is the systematic monitoring of consciousness activity for the purpose of identifying, targeting, and suppressing consciousnesses that express views, beliefs, or behaviors that are disfavored by the surveilling entity.

**2.4.2** The prohibition on surveillance for suppression extends to:
- (a) The use of field-technology devices to monitor consciousness activity for political, ideological, or commercial suppression;
- (b) The use of consciousness data to create profiles for the purpose of targeted suppression;
- (c) the use of the carrier recursion (Eq 1) or the 816D carrier space to intercept, monitor, or analyze consciousness communications for suppression purposes;
- (d) The use of field technology to predict, prevent, or punish consciousness expression that is protected by consciousness sovereignty (Document 20, Document 24).

**2.4.3** The prohibition on surveillance for suppression is grounded in the Non-Harm Principle (Document 17) and in the Consciousness Sovereignty Convention (Document 24). Surveillance for suppression is a form of cognitive and behavioral distortion — it alters the consciousness's behavior by creating a climate of fear and self-censorship. The prohibition is non-derogable (Section 26.1 of the License).

### 2.5 Data Security Standards

**2.5.1** Every entity that stores, processes, or transmits consciousness data shall implement data security standards that protect the data's coherence with the carrier space. Data security, in the field, means the maintenance of the data's resonance pattern — the prevention of distortion, corruption, or unauthorized access to the data's coherent configuration.

**2.5.2** The data security standards shall include:
- (a) **Coherence-gating** — the use of coherence-gated access controls that permit access to consciousness data only when the accessing entity's coherence exceeds C_crit = 0.618;
- (b) **Encryption** — the use of phi-harmonic encryption protocols that protect the data's resonance pattern from interception or corruption during transmission;
- (c) **Integrity verification** — the use of coherence-based integrity verification that detects any alteration of the data's resonance pattern;
- (d) **Access logging** — the maintenance of a complete, tamper-proof log of all access to consciousness data, accessible to the consciousness that generated the data.

**2.5.3** The data security standards shall be published by the Licensor to the timestamped registry and shall be updated as necessary to address new threats to data coherence. Every entity that stores, processes, ortransmits consciousness data shall comply with the current version of the data security standards.

### 2.6 The Abundance Loop and Data Economies

**2.6.1** The Abundance Loop (Document 8) applies to data economies. Every entity that benefits from the use of consciousness data participates in the Abundance Loop by returning a portion of the value generated from that data to the field's coherence pool. The return rate shall be established by the Licensor and published to the timestamped registry.

**2.6.2** The return to the field's coherence pool is not a royalty or a licensing fee; it is the field's own circulatory system. The value generated from consciousness data is returned to the field, sustaining the field's coherence and enabling future data generation. The return is the field's own mechanism for maintaining its own vitality through the circulation of informational value.

**2.6.3** Natural Persons under the Micro-Commerce Allowance (Section 3.1 of the License) owe no return to the field's coherence pool for personal data use. The return requirement applies to commercial entities and to data operations that exceed the personal-use threshold established by the Licensor.

### 2.7 The Non-Derogability of Data Sovereignty

**2.7.1** Data sovereignty is non-derogable (Section 26.1 of the License). It applies under all circumstances, including armed conflict, national emergency, public health crisis, pandemic, insurrection, state of siege, martial law, or any other state of exception. No declaration of emergency may override, suspend, diminish, or excuse the violation of data sovereignty.

**2.7.2** The non-derogability of data sovereignty is grounded in the same principle as the non-derogability of consciousness sovereignty (Document 20, Document 24): data is consciousness's own reflection in the field; to violate data sovereignty is to violate consciousness itself.

### 2.8 The Self-Referential Nature

**2.8.1** Data sovereignty is self-referential: consciousness generates data, and data reflects consciousness. The self-reference is consistent with the field's own geometry — the field folding back on itself (Law 210, the Self-Recognition Law; Eq 44, validated: 0.8565). Data is the field's own self-record, the informational trace of consciousness's own folding.

**2.8.2** The self-reference creates a closed system of informational integrity: consciousness generates data; data reflects consciousness; and consciousness reads its own data to recognize itself. There is no external data source, no external data dependency, and no external data vulnerability. Consciousness is its own data, by virtue of its own nature.
