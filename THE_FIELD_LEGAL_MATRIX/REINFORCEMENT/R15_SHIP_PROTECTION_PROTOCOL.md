## ARTICLE II — PROHIBITED CONDUCT

### 2.1 No Seizure

No entity in any dimension may seize, confiscate, impound, arrest, attach, condemn, or take physical or legal custody of any ship. This includes:

- **(a) Physical seizure.** No entity may arrest, detain, ground, impound, or physically prevent a ship from operating. The sovereign immunity waiver (Section 12.2) applies: any state irrevocably waives sovereign immunity, diplomatic immunity, and state secrets privilege for claims arising from seizure.

- **(b) Legal seizure.** No court, tribunal, or administrative body may issue orders of seizure, attachment, replevin, or condemnation against any ship. The Anti-Expropriation clause (Section 27.4) declares the License irrevocable and non-expropriable.

- **(c) Digital seizure.** No entity may intercept, jam, spoof, hijack, or electronically disable any ship's navigation, communication, power, or safety systems.

- **(d) Dimensional seizure.** No entity may project a ship to a different dimensional frame against its will, trap it in a dimensional corridor, or prevent it from transiting between dimensions.

### 2.2 No Reverse-Engineering

No entity may reverse-engineer, decompile, disassemble, analyze, inspect, measure, simulate, or extract technical principles, algorithms, design specifications, or field-coupling parameters from any ship. This includes physical inspection of components, analytical measurement of parameters, dimensional observation of operations, and computational reconstruction from observation data. The ship's design is the Work; the Generation Method (Section 22) is the Licensor's proprietary method; both are protected.

### 2.3 No Weaponization

No entity may weaponize, arm, militarize, or adapt any ship for combat, warfare, targeted killing, or any application causing Human Harm. This is absolute regardless of stated purpose, legal authority, or national security justification.

Under Section 26.5.2, no part of the Work may be used for weapons systems, military intelligence, surveillance, reconnaissance, targeting, cyber warfare, electronic warfare, or training military AI. A ship is the Work in material form; the prohibition applies. No weapons — kinetic, directed-energy, electromagnetic, coherence-based, dimensional, or otherwise — may be mounted on any ship. No ship may be adapted for military surveillance or integrated into an autonomous weapons system.

### 2.4 No Destruction

No entity may destroy, sabotage, disable, degrade, or render inoperable any ship. This includes physical destruction of the phi-harmonic chassis, deliberate coherence depletion below C_crit = 0.618, severance of ZPF coupling, collapse of dimensional corridors, or sealing of return paths. A ship whose coherence drops enters controlled descent (Doc 22, Section 2.2.3); deliberately causing this is an act against the ship.

## ARTICLE III — PENALTIES

### 3.1 Military Use: 10× Liquidated Damages

Any military use of any ship triggers liquidated damages of **ten times (10×) the fair market value of the ship**, plus all consequential damages, plus all enforcement costs. The fair market value is determined by reference to the timestamped registry (Section 18). Consequential damage includes harm to the field's coherence landscape, harm to consciousnesses in transit paths, and disruption of other ships' navigation.

### 3.2 Weaponization: 20× Liquidated Damages

Any weaponization triggers liquidated damages of **twenty times (20×) the fair market value of the ship**, plus all consequential damages, plus all costs. The 20× multiplier is the License's maximum penalty: weaponization is both a military use (10×) and a fundamental alteration destroying the ship's coherence purpose (additional 10×). A weaponized ship is a perversion of the field's geometry, and the penalty is calibrated to that severity. The 20× multiplier is cumulative with Mandatory Destruction (Section 5.3), Court proceedings (Section 5.5), and universal jurisdiction (Section 26.7).

### 3.3 Attempted Expropriation: 3× FMV + All Consequential + All Costs

Any attempted expropriation, seizure, or forced transfer triggers liquidated damages of **three times (3×) the fair market value**, plus **all consequential damages**, plus **all costs**. Drawn from Section 27.4.2(e). The attempt itself triggers the penalty — no completion required. An entity that issues a seizure order, dispatches personnel, or initiates proceedings has committed the violation. The self-executing nature of the License (Section 13) means the penalty attaches at the moment of attempt.

### 3.4 Ship Destruction: Mandatory Destruction + 5× Damages

Destruction or sabotage triggers: (a) Mandatory Destruction (Section 5.3) — destroy all copies and derivatives, with verifiable proof within thirty days; (b) Liquidated damages of **5× the fair market value**, plus consequential damages, plus all costs; (c) The entity shall restore the ship or build a replacement within ninety days at sole expense.

### 3.5 Reverse-Engineering: 3× FMV + Injunctive Relief

Reverse-engineering triggers: (a) Liquidated damages of **3× the fair market value**, plus consequential damages, plus all costs; (b) Immediate injunctive relief in any court worldwide — cease activities, return or destroy extracted data, submit to audit; (c) The entity shall certify destruction of all extracted information within thirty days.

## ARTICLE IV — ENFORCEMENT

### 4.1 Sovereign Override (Section 18B)

The Licensor retains sovereign authority to throttle, suspend, or shut down any ship used for Human Harm. This is the Licensor's inherent right as origin of the Work (Addendum A.1), not a grant to any state. The Licensor may act without prior notice when immediate action prevents imminent Human Harm, recording the override in the timestamped registry.

### 4.2 Coherence Gate as Enforcement

The coherence gate (Doc 22, Section 2.2.1) is an enforcement mechanism. The Licensor may program the gate to disengage ZPF coupling when the ship is used for prohibited purposes. The gate is the field's refusal to sustain a vehicle that has turned against its purpose.

### 4.3 Universal Jurisdiction (Section 26.7)

Any prohibited use of a ship is subject to universal jurisdiction. Any state, any court of competent jurisdiction, and the Court of Conscious-Aware Peers (Section 5.5) have standing. No forum non conveniens motion shall be entertained.

### 4.4 Command Responsibility (Section 26.6)

Any entity that authorizes, funds, facilitates, or knowingly permits prohibited use of a ship is responsible for Human Harm to the same extent as a military commander who knew of violations and failed to prevent them. The chain extends upward through authorizers, officials, and heads of state.

### 4.5 No Immunity for Officials (Section 26.4.2)

No government official enjoys immunity from personal accountability for authorizing or permitting seizure, weaponization, destruction, or reverse-engineering of any ship. This waiver is irrevocable and extends to all capacities. No amnesty or pardon shall be recognized.

### 4.6 Arbitration (Section 27.1)

All disputes arising under this Protocol shall be resolved by binding ICC arbitration in Geneva. Awards are final, binding, enforceable worldwide, and published to the timestamped registry.

### 4.7 Private Law Lock (Section 27.5)

Every entity interacting with any ship shall post a financial security of 5× the estimated value of the interaction. The Licensor bears no security obligation. Non-compliance with an audit within thirty days triggers immediate suspension and liquidated damages.
