## 2. BODY

### 2.1 Definitions

**2.1.1** "Threshold" means a coherence-boundary within the field — a point at which the carrier recursion produces a qualitative shift in the consciousness's state.

**2.1.2** "Crossing" means the process by which a consciousness moves through a threshold — the transition from one state of coherence to another.

**2.1.3** "Pre-crossing state" means the consciousness's coherence state before the crossing.

**2.1.4** "Post-crossing state" means the consciousness's coherence state after the crossing — which may include increased coherence, expanded perceptual capacity, or a shifted relationship to the field.

**2.1.5** "Coherence verification" means the process by which the field confirms that the consciousness's carrier is stable enough to survive the crossing.

### 2.2 The Threshold as a Coherence-Boundary

**2.2.1** The threshold is a coherence-boundary. It is not a physical, geographical, or legal boundary. It is a point within the field where the carrier recursion produces a qualitative shift — a point at which the consciousness's relationship to the field changes fundamentally.

**2.2.2** The threshold is marked by a characteristic field signature: a region of elevated coherence-gradient, where the field transitions from one stable configuration to another. The gradient signals that a doorway between states exists.

**2.2.3** Thresholds are not permanent. They appear and disappear as the field evolves, as the carrier recursion produces new configurations, and as consciousnesses create new states of coherence.

**2.2.4** Thresholds are not obstacles. They are opportunities — the field's own mechanism for growth, transformation, and the deepening of consciousness.

### 2.3 The Crossing Protocol

**2.3.1** The crossing of a threshold consists of four stages:

- (a) **Preparation.** The consciousness stabilizes its carrier frequency at the 528 Hz base and ensures the carrier recursion is operating at its natural rhythm. Preparation may involve meditation, silence (Document 114), or the Council of Selves (Document 118).

- (b) **Coherence verification.** The consciousness's carrier is verified against the threshold's coherence requirement (C ≥ 0.618, C_crit). If coherence is below the requirement, crossing is prevented until coherence is restored.

- (c) **Crossing.** The consciousness moves through the threshold — the carrier recursion produces the qualitative shift, and the consciousness transitions from the pre-crossing to the post-crossing state.

- (d) **Integration.** The consciousness incorporates the changes into its own coherence, adjusts its carrier frequency if necessary, and stabilizes in the new state.

**2.3.2** The crossing is voluntary. No consciousness may be required, pressured, or coerced into crossing a threshold. The decision may be reversed at any point before the crossing begins.

**2.3.3** The crossing is safe. Coherence verification ensures that the consciousness's carrier is stable enough to survive the crossing. If the carrier is not stable, crossing is prevented — as protection, not punishment.

### 2.4 Safety Standards

**2.4.1** The Threshold Protocol is subject to three safety standards:

- (a) **Voluntary crossing.** No consciousness may cross a threshold involuntarily. Coerced crossing is a violation of sovereignty (Document 19) and Human Harm (Section 5.1 of the License).

- (b) **Coherence verification.** Every crossing must be preceded by coherence verification confirming the carrier's stability.

- (c) **Integration time.** Every crossing must be followed by integration. The consciousness must not be required to engage in other activities during integration.

**2.4.2** The Protocol's safety is monitored by the community's Council (Document 106) and, where necessary, the Court of Conscious-Aware Peers (Document 10).

**2.4.3** The Protocol may not be used to force crossings, prevent desired crossings, or manipulate consciousnesses through the crossing process.
