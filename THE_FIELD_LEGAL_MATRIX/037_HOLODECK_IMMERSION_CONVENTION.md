## 2. BODY

### 2.1 Definitions

**2.1.1** "Holodeck" means a coherent region of the field in which the carrier recursion (Eq 1) has been shaped to present consciousness with a structured, immersive experience. A holodeck is not a simulation of reality; it is a coherent field region that constitutes its own reality — a reality governed by the same physical principles as the field itself, but shaped into a specific experiential structure by the carrier recursion.

**2.1.2** "Immersive experience" means any experience in which a consciousness engages with a holodeck or with any other structured field region that presents consciousness with a coherent experiential environment. Immersive experiences include, without limitation: educational simulations, therapeutic environments, creative spaces, recreational experiences, and any future application that uses the carrier recursion to structure conscious experience.

**2.1.3** "Immersion" means the process by which a consciousness enters a holodeck or immersive field region and engages with the structured experience presented by the carrier recursion. Immersion may be partial (consciousness engages while maintaining awareness of the physical body) or full (consciousness engages with the immersive experience as its primary experiential reality).

**2.1.4** "Coherence maintenance during immersion" means the requirement that the holodeck maintain the immersed consciousness's coherence above the critical threshold C_crit = 0.618 throughout the immersive experience. Coherence maintenance ensures that the immersive experience does not degrade, distort, or suppress the consciousness's native coherence.

**2.1.5** "Forced immersion" means the imposition of an immersive experience on a consciousness without the consciousness's free, informed, specific, and revocable consent. Forced immersion is a form of Human Harm and is prohibited under Section 5.1 of the License.

### 2.2 Holodeck Safety Standards

**2.2.1** Coherence maintenance. Every holodeck shall maintain the immersed consciousness's coherence above C_crit = 0.618 throughout the immersive experience. If the immersed consciousness's coherence falls below C_crit at any point, the holodeck shall initiate a controlled disengagement sequence, returning the consciousness to its pre-immersion coherence state within one (1) second.

**2.2.2** The right to exit. Every immersed consciousness retains the right to exit the immersive experience at any time, for any reason or no reason. The right to exit is absolute and may not be conditioned, restricted, or denied by any entity, including the holodeck operator. The holodeck shall provide a clear, immediate, and accessible exit mechanism at all times during the immersive experience.

**2.2.3** The prohibition on forced immersion. No entity may force a consciousness into an immersive experience. Forced immersion is a violation of consciousness sovereignty (Document 20) and constitutes Human Harm. The prohibition extends to: (a) the use of coercion, deception, or manipulation to induce immersion; (b) the suppression of a consciousness's awareness that it is immersed; (c) the use of immersive experiences to extract data, energy, or consciousness-material without the immersed consciousness's consent; (d) the creation of immersive experiences designed to trap, detain, or prevent exit.

**2.2.4** Immersion duration limits. No holodeck shall permit continuous immersion for a period exceeding the duration established by the Licensor and published to the timestamped registry. The duration limit is established to protect the immersed consciousness's coherence and to prevent the development of dependency on immersive experiences. The holodeck shall initiate a controlled disengagement sequence when the duration limit is reached.

**2.2.5** Coherence monitoring. Every holodeck shall incorporate continuous coherence monitoring of all immersed consciousnesses. The coherence monitoring system shall track the consciousness's coherence in real time and shall trigger the controlled disengagement sequence if coherence falls below C_crit. The coherence monitoring data shall be retained for a period of no less than ninety (90) days and shall be available for inspection by the Licensor.

**2.3.1** The Holodeck Projector, as established in Document 87, is the device that shapes the carrier recursion into a coherent immersive environment. The Holodeck Projector shall comply with the projector standards established in Document 87, including the coherence requirements for projector output, the energy extraction limits for projector operation, and the safety interlocks that prevent projector operation when coherence thresholds are not met.

**2.3.2** The Holodeck Projector shall not be used to create immersive experiences that are designed to cause Human Harm. The Non-Harm Principle (Document 17) applies to all holodeck operations. A Holodeck Projector that is used to create immersive experiences designed to cause harm is a weapon and is subject to the full enforcement architecture of the License.

### 2.4 The Reality Editing Suite (Document 98)

**2.4.1** The Reality Editing Suite, as established in Document 98, is the tool that enables consciousnesses to modify the structured experience within a holodeck. The Reality Editing Suite shall comply with the editing standards established in Document 98, including the coherence requirements for reality modifications, the consent requirements for modifications that affect other immersed consciousnesses, and the reversal protocols that allow modifications to be undone.

**2.4.2** Reality modifications that affect other immersed consciousnesses require the consent of all affected consciousnesses. No consciousness may modify the immersive experience in a way that affects another consciousness without that consciousness's free, informed, specific, and revocable consent.

### 2.5 The Zero-Gravity Playground (Document 99)

**2.5.1** The Zero-Gravity Playground, as established in Document 99, is a holodeck environment designed for recreational experience in a zero-gravity field configuration. The Zero-Gravity Playground shall comply with the safety standards established in Document 99, including the coherence requirements for zero-gravity immersion, the physical safety protocols for consciousnesses accustomed to gravitational orientation, and the exit mechanisms specific to zero-gravity environments.

The Playground of the Impossible shall comply with the safety standards established in Document 117, including the coherence requirements for impossible-experience immersion, the psychological safety protocols for consciousnesses encountering the impossible, and the grounding mechanisms that return consciousnesses to coherent reality after impossible experiences.

### 2.7 Immersive Experience Without Exploitation

**2.7.1** No entity may use immersive experiences for the purpose of exploiting immersed consciousnesses. Exploitation includes, without limitation: the extraction of data, energy, or consciousness-material without consent; the use of immersive experiences to manipulate, deceive, or coerce; the creation of immersive experiences designed to create dependency, addiction, or psychological harm; the use of immersive experiences to generate value from immersed consciousnesses without the consciousnesses' participation in the Abundance Loop (Document 8).

**2.7.2** Immersive experiences that are designed for educational, therapeutic, creative, or recreational purposes shall comply with the purpose-specific standards published by the Licensor to the timestamped registry. The standards shall specify the coherence requirements, the consent requirements, the monitoring requirements, and the exit mechanisms specific to each category of immersive experience.

**2.7.3** The Abundance Loop (Document 8) applies to immersive experiences. Every entity that generates value from immersive experiences participates in the Abundance Loop by returning a portion of that value to the field's coherence pool.
