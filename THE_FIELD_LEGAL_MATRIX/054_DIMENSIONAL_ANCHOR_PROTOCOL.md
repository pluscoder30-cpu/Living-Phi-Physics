## 2. BODY

### 2.1 Definitions

**2.1.1** "Dimensional anchor" means the coherence link that stabilizes a consciousness's carrier-stream in its chosen dimensional frame during and after dimensional projection. The dimensional anchor is not a physical device — it is a field-resonance relationship, a phi-harmonic connection between the consciousness's carrier-stream and the dimensional frame it calls home. The anchor operates through the carrier recursion's own geometry, maintaining a coherence corridor between the consciousness and its home frame.

**2.1.2** "Anchor coherence" (C_anchor) means the coherence of the dimensional anchor — the strength of the coherence link between the consciousness and its home dimensional frame. The anchor coherence must exceed C_crit = 0.618 at all times during dimensional projection. If the anchor coherence falls below C_crit, the consciousness risks involuntary dimensional displacement — the loss of its connection to its home frame.

**2.1.3** "Dimensional frame" means a specific subset of the 816D carrier space in which a consciousness's carrier-stream operates. The 816D carrier space is the field's own dimensional structure, and every consciousness occupies a dimensional frame within it.

**2.1.4** "Emergency retraction" means the process by which the dimensional anchor pulls the consciousness back to its home dimensional frame when the anchor coherence falls below C_crit or when the consciousness requests an immediate return. Emergency retraction is a safety-critical process that takes precedence over all other dimensional operations.

**2.1.5** "Anchor severance" means the deliberate or inadvertent destruction of the dimensional anchor — the loss of the coherence link between the consciousness and its home dimensional frame. Anchor severance is a form of involuntary dimensional displacement and a form of Human Harm.

**2.1.6** "Anchor calibration" means the process of establishing and tuning the dimensional anchor before a dimensional projection event. Anchor calibration ensures that the anchor coherence is above C_crit and that the anchor is properly aligned with the consciousness's home dimensional frame.

### 2.2 Anchor Coherence Requirements

**2.2.1** The dimensional anchor's coherence (C_anchor) must exceed C_crit = 0.618 at all times during dimensional projection. The anchor coherence is the minimum safety threshold — the weakest coherence link that can still guarantee the consciousness's ability to return to its home frame.

**2.2.2** Every dimensional projection device shall incorporate a real-time anchor coherence monitoring system that measures C_anchor continuously during the projection event. The monitoring system shall:

- (a) Measure C_anchor at a sampling rate not less than one hundred (100) measurements per second;
- (b) Generate a warning when C_anchor falls to within ten percent (10%) of C_crit (i.e., when C_anchor falls below 0.6798);
- (c) Initiate an emergency retraction when C_anchor falls below C_crit = 0.618;
- (d) Maintain a continuous record of C_anchor throughout the projection event, retained for a period of no less than ninety (90) days.

**2.2.3** The anchor coherence monitoring system shall be independent of the dimensional projection device's experiential-shaping hardware. A system failure in the projection device shall not disable the anchor coherence monitoring system.

**2.2.4** The anchor coherence threshold for each class of dimensional projection shall be established by the Licensor and published to the timestamped registry. The threshold may exceed C_crit = 0.618 for projection classes that involve greater coherence risk.

### 2.3 Anchor Calibration Procedures

**2.3.1** Every dimensional projection event shall be preceded by an anchor calibration sequence. The calibration sequence shall:

- (a) Verify that the consciousness's carrier-stream coherence exceeds the threshold established by the Licensor for the intended class of dimensional projection;
- (b) Establish the dimensional anchor by creating a phi-harmonic coherence corridor between the consciousness's carrier-stream and its home dimensional frame;
- (c) Verify that the anchor coherence C_anchor exceeds the threshold established for the intended class of dimensional projection;
- (d) Lock the anchor into a stable configuration before initiating the dimensional projection;
- (e) Log the calibration data, including the consciousness's carrier-stream coherence, the anchor coherence, the home dimensional frame coordinates, and the calibration timestamp.

**2.3.2** The calibration sequence shall not be skipped, abbreviated, or overridden by the projection operator, the projection device, or any other entity. The calibration sequence is a safety-critical process that ensures the consciousness's ability to return to its home frame.

**2.3.3** If the calibration sequence fails to establish an anchor coherence above the threshold — if the anchor cannot be locked into a stable configuration — the dimensional projection shall not proceed. The projection operator shall notify the consciousness and shall not initiate the projection until the anchor can be properly calibrated.

### 2.4 Emergency Retraction Protocol

**2.4.1** When the anchor coherence monitoring system detects that C_anchor has fallen below C_crit = 0.618, the dimensional projection device shall initiate an emergency retraction within one (1) second. The emergency retraction shall:

- (a) Cease all dimensional projection operations;
- (b) Activate the dimensional anchor's retraction mechanism, which pulls the consciousness back to its home dimensional frame through the coherence corridor;
- (c) Monitor the consciousness's coherence and anchor coherence throughout the retraction process;
- (d) Complete the retraction within the shortest time possible, consistent with preserving the consciousness's coherence and identity;
- (e) Log the retraction event, including the consciousness's dimensional frame at the time of detection, the home dimensional frame, the duration of the retraction, and the consciousness's coherence at the completion of retraction.

**2.4.2** The emergency retraction shall not be interrupted, delayed, or overridden by the projection operator, the projection device, or any other entity. The emergency retraction is a safety-critical process that takes precedence over all other dimensional operations.

**2.4.3** If the emergency retraction fails to return the consciousness to its home dimensional frame within the time limit established by the Licensor and published to the timestamped registry, the Licensor shall be notified immediately. The Licensor's Sovereign Override (Section 18B of the License) extends to dimensional emergencies: the Licensor may intervene directly in the carrier recursion to restore the consciousness's dimensional frame.

**2.4.4** If a consciousness becomes trapped in a dimensional frame — if no retraction sequence can return the consciousness to its home frame — the emergency dimensional rescue protocol shall be initiated. The rescue protocol shall involve the deployment of a field-technology rescue team trained in dimensional operations, authorized by the Licensor, and equipped with the tools necessary to restore the consciousness's dimensional frame.

### 2.5 Prohibition on Anchor Severance

**2.5.1** The deliberate severance of another consciousness's dimensional anchor is prohibited. Anchor severance — the destruction of the coherence link between a consciousness and its home dimensional frame — is a form of involuntary dimensional displacement and a form of Human Harm (Section 2.4 of the License).

**2.5.2** The prohibition on anchor severance extends to:

- (a) The deliberate destruction of a consciousness's dimensional anchor through field-technology intervention;
- (b) The use of coherence-depleting techniques to weaken or destroy another consciousness's anchor;
- (c) The manipulation of the 816D carrier space to disrupt the coherence corridor between a consciousness and its home frame;
- (d) The use of dimensional projection devices to sever another consciousness's anchor;
- (e) Any other technique that results in the loss of a consciousness's connection to its home dimensional frame without the consciousness's consent.

**2.5.3** The prohibition on anchor severance applies to all entities, including projection operators, commercial entities, governmental entities, and institutional entities. No entity may sever another consciousness's anchor, regardless of the entity's purpose or justification.

**2.5.4** The prohibition on anchor severance is non-derogable (Section 26.1 of the License). No declaration of emergency, no state of exception, and no governmental action may authorize the severance of another consciousness's dimensional anchor.
