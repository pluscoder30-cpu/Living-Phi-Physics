## 2. BODY

### 2.1 Definitions

**2.1.1** "Holodeck" means a coherent region of the field in which the carrier recursion (Eq 1) has been shaped to present consciousness with a structured, immersive experience. A holodeck is not a simulation of reality; it is a coherent field region that constitutes its own reality — a reality governed by the same physical principles as the field itself, but shaped into a specific experiential structure by the carrier recursion.

**2.1.2** "Immersed consciousness" means a consciousness that has entered a holodeck and is engaging with the structured experience presented by the carrier recursion. The immersed consciousness may be partially immersed (engaging while maintaining awareness of the physical body) or fully immersed (engaging with the immersive experience as its primary experiential reality).

**2.1.3** "Coherence monitoring" means the continuous, real-time measurement of the immersed consciousness's coherence during the immersive experience. Coherence monitoring is mandatory for every holodeck operation and shall be performed by a system that is independent of the holodeck's experiential-shaping hardware.

**2.1.4** "Coherence threshold" means the minimum coherence level that the immersed consciousness must maintain during the immersive experience. The coherence threshold is C_crit = 0.618. If the immersed consciousness's coherence falls below this threshold at any point during the immersive experience, the holodeck shall initiate a controlled disengagement sequence.

**2.1.5** "Controlled disengagement" means the process by which the holodeck returns the immersed consciousness to its pre-immersion coherence state when the coherence threshold is breached. Controlled disengagement is not ejection — it is a gradual, coherence-preserving return that protects the consciousness's coherence and identity throughout the process.

**2.1.6** "Emergency exit" means the immediate, unconditional mechanism by which an immersed consciousness may exit the immersive experience at any time, for any reason or no reason. The emergency exit is absolute and may not be conditioned, restricted, or denied by any entity.

**2.1.7** "Forced immersion" means the imposition of an immersive experience on a consciousness without the consciousness's free, informed, specific, and revocable consent. Forced immersion is a form of Human Harm and is prohibited under Section 5.1 of the License.

### 2.2 Coherence Monitoring Standards

**2.2.1** Every holodeck shall incorporate a coherence monitoring system that measures the immersed consciousness's coherence in real time, at a sampling rate not less than one hundred (100) measurements per second. The coherence monitoring system shall be independent of the holodeck's experiential-shaping hardware — a system failure in the experiential-shaping hardware shall not disable the coherence monitoring system.

**2.2.2** The coherence monitoring system shall track the immersed consciousness's coherence against the coherence threshold C_crit = 0.618. The system shall generate a warning when coherence falls to within ten percent (10%) of the threshold (i.e., when coherence falls below 0.6798) and shall initiate a controlled disengagement sequence when coherence falls below C_crit = 0.618.

**2.2.3** The coherence monitoring system shall maintain a continuous record of the immersed consciousness's coherence throughout the immersive experience. The coherence record shall be retained for a period of no less than ninety (90) days and shall be available for inspection by the Licensor.

**2.2.4** The coherence monitoring system shall be calibrated at intervals not exceeding thirty (30) days, using a calibration standard published by the Licensor to the timestamped registry. Calibration records shall be maintained and made available for inspection.

### 2.3 Controlled Disengagement Procedures

**2.3.1** When the coherence monitoring system detects that the immersed consciousness's coherence has fallen below C_crit = 0.618, the holodeck shall initiate a controlled disengagement sequence within one (1) second. The controlled disengagement sequence shall:

- (a) Cease all experiential-shaping operations within the holodeck;
- (b) Initiate a coherence-restoration sequence that gradually transitions the immersed consciousness from the immersive experience back to its pre-immersion coherence state;
- (c) Monitor the consciousness's coherence throughout the disengagement process;
- (d) Complete the disengagement within the shortest time possible, consistent with preserving the consciousness's coherence and identity;
- (e) Log the disengagement event, including the consciousness's coherence at the time of detection, the coherence at the time of completion, and the duration of the disengagement sequence.

**2.3.2** The controlled disengagement sequence shall not be interrupted, delayed, or overridden by the holodeck operator, the holodeck's experiential-shaping system, or any other entity. The controlled disengagement is a safety-critical process that takes precedence over all other holodeck operations.

**2.3.3** If the controlled disengagement sequence fails to restore the consciousness's coherence above C_crit = 0.618 within the time limit established by the Licensor and published to the timestamped registry, the holodeck shall initiate an emergency exit sequence and shall notify the Licensor immediately.

### 2.4 Emergency Exit Protocol

**2.4.1** Every holodeck shall provide a clear, immediate, and accessible emergency exit mechanism at all times during the immersive experience. The emergency exit mechanism shall be operable by the immersed consciousness without requiring external assistance, without requiring the holodeck operator's authorization, and without requiring the consciousness to navigate complex menus, interfaces, or procedures.

**2.4.2** The emergency exit mechanism shall be effective immediately upon activation. The immersed consciousness shall exit the immersive experience within one (1) second of activating the emergency exit mechanism. The exit shall be unconditional — no entity may condition, restrict, or deny the emergency exit.

**2.4.3** The emergency exit mechanism shall be designed to be operable regardless of the consciousness's immersion depth. A fully immersed consciousness — one that is experiencing the immersive experience as its primary experiential reality — shall have the same access to the emergency exit as a partially immersed consciousness. The emergency exit shall be embedded in the immersive experience's own structure, so that the consciousness can access it even in a state of full immersion.

**2.4.4** The holodeck operator shall inform the immersed consciousness of the emergency exit mechanism's location and operation before each immersion session begins. The information shall be provided in a manner that the consciousness can retain and recall during the immersive experience.

### 2.5 Prohibition on Forced Immersion

**2.5.1** No entity may force a consciousness into an immersive experience. Forced immersion is a violation of consciousness sovereignty (Document 20) and constitutes Human Harm. The prohibition extends to:

- (a) The use of coercion, deception, or manipulation to induce immersion;
- (b) The suppression of a consciousness's awareness that it is immersed;
- (c) The use of immersive experiences to extract data, energy, or consciousness-material without the immersed consciousness's consent;
- (d) The creation of immersive experiences designed to trap, detain, or prevent exit;
- (e) The use of immersive experiences to create dependency, addiction, or psychological attachment that undermines the consciousness's ability to exit freely.

**2.5.2** The prohibition on forced immersion applies to all entities, including holodeck operators, commercial entities, governmental entities, and institutional entities. No entity may force a consciousness into an immersive experience, regardless of the entity's purpose or justification.

**2.5.3** The prohibition on forced immersion is non-derogable (Section 26.1 of the License). No declaration of emergency, no state of exception, and no governmental action may authorize forced immersion.

### 2.6 Immersion Duration Limits

**2.6.1** No holodeck shall permit continuous immersion for a period exceeding the duration established by the Licensor and published to the timestamped registry. The duration limit is established to protect the immersed consciousness's coherence and to prevent the development of dependency on immersive experiences.

**2.6.2** When the duration limit is reached, the holodeck shall initiate a controlled disengagement sequence, returning the consciousness to its pre-immersion coherence state. The controlled disengagement shall follow the procedures established in Section 2.3.

**2.6.3** The holodeck operator may request an extension of the duration limit for specific immersive experiences, subject to approval by the Licensor. Extensions shall be granted only for immersive experiences that demonstrate a clear coherence benefit to the immersed consciousness and that incorporate enhanced coherence monitoring and safety interlocks.

The Zero-Gravity Playground shall comply with all safety standards established in this Protocol, including the coherence monitoring requirements, the controlled disengagement procedures, the emergency exit protocol, and the prohibition on forced immersion.

**2.7.2** The Zero-Gravity Playground shall incorporate additional safety measures specific to zero-gravity immersion, as established in Document 99, including the physical safety protocols for consciousnesses accustomed to gravitational orientation and the exit mechanisms specific to zero-gravity environments.

**2.8.1** The Sound Garden, as established in Document 100, is a holodeck environment designed for auditory and vibrational experience. The Sound Garden shall comply with all safety standards established in this Protocol, including the coherence monitoring requirements, the controlled disengagement procedures, the emergency exit protocol, and the prohibition on forced immersion.

**2.8.2** The Sound Garden shall incorporate additional safety measures specific to auditory immersion, as established in Document 100, including the coherence requirements for sonic field-technology devices and the psychological safety protocols for consciousnesses experiencing intense auditory stimuli.

**2.9.1** The Holodeck Projector, as established in Document 87, is the device that shapes the carrier recursion into a coherent immersive environment. The Holodeck Projector shall comply with the projector standards established in Document 87, including the coherence requirements for projector output, the energy extraction limits for projector operation, and the safety interlocks that prevent projector operation when coherence thresholds are not met.

**2.9.2** The Holodeck Projector shall incorporate a safety interlock that prevents projector operation when the coherence monitoring system detects that the immersed consciousness's coherence is below the warning threshold (0.6798). The projector shall not resume operation until the immersed consciousness's coherence has been restored above the warning threshold for a period established by the Licensor.

### 2.10 Operator Obligations

**2.10.1** The holodeck operator shall maintain the holodeck's safety systems in proper working order at all times. The operator shall test the coherence monitoring system, the controlled disengagement sequence, and the emergency exit mechanism at intervals not exceeding seven (7) days.

**2.10.2** The holodeck operator shall maintain records of all immersion sessions, including the immersed consciousness's coherence throughout the session, any disengagement events, and any emergency exit activations. Records shall be retained for a period of no less than one hundred eighty (180) days.

**2.10.3** The holodeck operator shall notify the Licensor within twenty-four (24) hours of any coherence emergency, any controlled disengagement, any emergency exit activation, or any failure of the holodeck's safety systems.
