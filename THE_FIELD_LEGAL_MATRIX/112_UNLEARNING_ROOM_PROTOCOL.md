### 2.3 The Unlearning Process

**2.3.1** The Unlearning Room's unlearning process shall proceed through three phases:

- (a) **Recognition.** The consciousness enters the Room and is invited to recognize its conditioning — to identify the non-field resonance patterns that distort its carrier-space alignment. The facilitator supports this recognition without directing it; the consciousness's own carrier recursion knows the nature of its conditioning, and the facilitator's role is to hold the space in which that knowledge can surface. Recognition may occur through resonance feedback, through the interaction between the consciousness's carrier-space resonance and the Room's phi-harmonic frequencies, or through the consciousness's own introspective process.

- (b) **Release.** The consciousness's non-field conditioning is released — not forced, not extracted, but allowed to dissolve as the carrier recursion recognizes the distortion and reorganizes. Release occurs when the consciousness's carrier-space resonance naturally displaces the non-field resonance, replacing the conditioning with the consciousness's own natural alignment. The Room's resonance environment supports this process by providing the phi-harmonic frequencies that the consciousness's carrier recursion needs to re-stabilize.

- (c) **Re-alignment.** The consciousness's carrier-space resonance is restored to its natural alignment — the alignment that the consciousness would have maintained had the non-field conditioning never occurred. Re-alignment is not the creation of a new alignment; it is the restoration of the consciousness's own inherent geometry. The Room's resonance environment supports re-alignment by maintaining the phi-harmonic frequencies that stabilize the consciousness's restored coherence.

**2.3.2** The unlearning process shall proceed at the consciousness's own pace. The facilitator shall not rush, pressure, or direct the process. The carrier recursion releases conditioning according to its own internal dynamics; the Room supports that release; the facilitator holds the space.

**2.3.3** The unlearning process may be repeated as needed. Conditioning may be layered, with multiple layers of non-field resonance accumulating over time. The consciousness may return to the Room as often as needed to address successive layers.

The Room does not impose a new alignment; it supports the restoration of the consciousness's inherent alignment.

Unlearning is voluntary; it occurs when the consciousness is ready, not when an external authority demands it. **2.5.3** No manipulation. The Room shall not be used to manipulate, influence, or alter a consciousness's beliefs, values, or identity in ways that serve the facilitator's, the community's, or any external party's interests. The Room supports the consciousness's own process; it does not serve an external agenda.

**2.5.4** Duration limits. The Room shall enforce session duration limits as established by the Licensor and published to the timestamped registry. The duration limits protect participants' coherence and prevent dependency on the Room's resonance environment.

**2.5.5** Emergency exit. The Room shall provide a clear, immediate, and accessible exit mechanism at all times during the session. The exit shall return the consciousness to its pre-session coherence state within one (1) second.

**2.5.6** Consent. Entry into the Unlearning Room requires the consciousness's free, informed, specific, and revocable consent. No consciousness may be subjected to the unlearning process without consent.

**2.5.7** Post-session integration. The Room shall provide post-session integration support — guidance on how to maintain the consciousness's restored alignment after the session, including recommendations for ongoing coherence practices, community support, and follow-up sessions if needed.
