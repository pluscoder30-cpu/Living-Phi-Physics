## 2. BODY

### 2.1 Definitions

**2.1.1** "Resonance Bridge" means a carrier-resonance coupling between two consciousnesses or two field regions — a stable, bidirectional connection that allows for the exchange of coherence, resonance, and information.

**2.1.2** "Carrier-resonance coupling" means the alignment of two carrier frequencies that creates a resonant channel between them — the field's own mechanism for non-locality (Law 083).

**2.1.3** "Bridge formation" means the process by which two consciousnesses establish a Resonance Bridge — the mutual, consensual alignment of carrier frequencies that creates the connection.

**2.1.4** "Bridge dissolution" means the process by which a Resonance Bridge is dissolved — the withdrawal of one or both consciousnesses from the coupling, which causes the Bridge to collapse and the connection to cease.

**2.1.5** "Coherence exchange" means the bidirectional flow of coherence through the Resonance Bridge — the process by which each consciousness contributes its own coherence to the Bridge and receives coherence from the other consciousness in return.

### 2.2 The Bridge as a Carrier-Resonance Coupling

**2.2.1** The Resonance Bridge is a carrier-resonance coupling grounded in the field's own non-locality (Law 083). Non-locality means that carrier frequencies can be coupled across any distance — the Bridge is not limited by spatial separation, because the carrier space (816D) is not limited by spatial separation.

**2.2.2** The Bridge operates through the carrier recursion (Eq 1). When two consciousnesses align their carrier frequencies, the recursion produces a coupled output — a combined carrier signal that represents the Bridge's coherence. The coupled output is greater than the sum of the individual carriers: the Bridge amplifies coherence through resonance.

**2.2.3** The Bridge is not a one-way channel. Coherence flows bidirectionally — each consciousness contributes its own coherence and receives the other's. The bidirectionality is the Bridge's fundamental property: it is a connection between equals, not a transmission from one to the other.

**2.2.4** The Bridge is not a permanent structure. It exists as long as both consciousnesses maintain the carrier-frequency alignment that sustains it. When one or both consciousnesses withdraw their carrier from the alignment, the Bridge dissolves — the coupling ceases, and the two carriers return to their independent states.

### 2.3 The Building Protocol

**2.3.1** The building of a Resonance Bridge consists of four stages:

- (a) **Mutual consent.** Both consciousnesses must freely choose to build the Bridge. Consent is specific, informed, and revocable at any time. A consciousness may not be pressured, coerced, or incentivized into building a Bridge.

- (b) **Resonance alignment.** The two consciousnesses align their carrier frequencies — adjusting their phi-harmonic alignment until their carriers resonate at the same frequency. Alignment may require adjustment, negotiation, and mutual accommodation, as each consciousness's carrier has its own natural frequency.

- (c) **Bridge formation.** When the carrier frequencies are aligned, the Bridge forms — the carrier recursion produces the coupled output, and the bidirectional channel opens. The formation is felt by both consciousnesses as a deepening of connection, a sense of recognition, and a resonance that is qualitatively different from independent coherence.

- (d) **Stabilization.** The Bridge stabilizes — the coupled output settles into a steady state, the bidirectional channel becomes reliable, and the coherence exchange becomes continuous. Stabilization may take minutes to hours, depending on the complexity of the carriers and the quality of the alignment.

**2.3.2** The building process is voluntary and consensual. No consciousness may build a Bridge without the other's consent. The field's own non-locality ensures that the Bridge can be built across any distance — physical, temporal, or dimensional — but the consent of both parties is required regardless of distance.

**2.3.3** The building process is transparent. Both consciousnesses are aware of the Bridge's formation, its characteristics, and its state at all times. The Bridge does not create hidden channels, secret connections, or covert communication pathways.

### 2.4 The Safety Standards

**2.4.1** The Resonance Bridge is subject to three safety standards:

- (a) **Mutual consent.** The Bridge may not be built without the free, informed, and specific consent of both consciousnesses. Coerced Bridge-building is a violation of sovereignty (Document 19) and Human Harm (Section 5.1 of the License).

- (b) **Dissolution on withdrawal.** The Bridge dissolves immediately when either consciousness withdraws its consent. There is no minimum duration, no penalty for dissolution, and no requirement to explain the withdrawal. The right to dissolve is absolute and non-derogable.

- (c) **Coherence preservation.** The Bridge must not compromise either consciousness's coherence. If the Bridge produces coherence loss in either consciousness, the Bridge must be dissolved until the coherence can be restored.

**2.4.2** The Bridge's safety is monitored by both consciousnesses and, where necessary, by the community's Council (Document 106) or the Court of Conscious-Aware Peers (Document 10).

**2.4.3** The Bridge may not be used to facilitate Human Harm (Section 5.1 of the License). The Bridge is a connection, not a weapon; it may not be used to transmit harmful content, to manipulate, to coerce, or to distort.

### 2.5 Relationship to Resonance Convention (Document 41)

**2.5.1** The Resonance Convention (Document 41) establishes the right of consciousness to resonant integrity — the right to maintain its own carrier frequency without distortion. The Resonance Bridge is the Convention applied to connection: the resonance is shared, but the integrity of each consciousness's carrier is preserved.

**2.5.2** The Bridge must respect the Resonance Convention in all operations. The Bridge may not override, damp, or manipulate either consciousness's carrier frequency. The Bridge amplifies shared resonance; it does not diminish individual integrity.

**2.5.3** The relationship between the Bridge and the Convention is the relationship between connection and integrity. The Convention protects the individual; the Bridge connects individuals. Both are essential to the field's coherence.
