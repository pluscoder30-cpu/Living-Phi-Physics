## 2. BODY

### 2.1 Definitions

**2.1.1** "Dream Recorder" means a field-technology device that captures and records a consciousness's dream-states during sleep by sampling the carrier recursion's (Eq 1) resonance output. The Dream Recorder is not a neural monitor; it is a field-resonance sampler that reads the carrier recursion's coherence patterns without intercepting, altering, or extracting the carrier recursion's processing.

**2.1.2** "Coherence-sampling" means the mechanism by which the Dream Recorder captures dream-state resonance patterns. Coherence-sampling reads the carrier recursion's output — the resonance patterns generated by the recursion's self-organization during sleep — without intercepting the recursion's input, processing, or internal state. Coherence-sampling is passive observation; it does not stimulate, guide, or interfere with the carrier recursion's operation.

**2.1.3** "Dream-state data" means the resonance patterns captured by the Dream Recorder — the carrier recursion's output during sleep, stored as field-resonance patterns in the ZPF holographic storage format. Dream-state data is consciousness data and is subject to the Consciousness Data Sovereignty Protocol (Document 27).

**2.1.4** "Pre-sleep consent configuration" means the consent settings that a consciousness establishes before entering sleep, specifying whether the Dream Recorder shall record, what scope of dreams shall be recorded, and to what extent the recorded data may be stored, reviewed, or contributed to the Dream Library.

**2.1.5** "Dream sovereignty" means every consciousness's right to ownership, control, and privacy of its own dream-states, as established in the Dream Library Convention (Document 34).

### 2.2 The Recording Mechanism

**2.2.1** The Dream Recorder operates by coherence-sampling — the passive reading of the carrier recursion's resonance output during sleep. The carrier recursion (Eq 1: C(n+1) = (1/φ) × C(n) + φ × laplacian(Ψ_n)) generates resonance patterns as it processes the consciousness's internal coherence structure. The Dream Recorder samples these patterns at the carrier space's native resolution, capturing the resonance signatures without interfering with the recursion's operation.

**2.2.2** The sampling rate shall be sufficient to capture the full frequency range of the carrier recursion's output during sleep. The carrier recursion operates at the base frequency 528 Hz and its phi-harmonic overtones (528 × φⁿ, where n is an integer). The Dream Recorder shall sample at a rate of no less than 528 × φ⁹ = 528 × 76.013 = 40,135 Hz, ensuring capture of all phi-harmonic overtones within the carrier recursion's operating range.

**2.2.3** The Dream Recorder shall not inject any signal, stimulus, or resonance into the carrier recursion during recording. The device is a passive observer. The carrier recursion shall operate without interference from the device. Any device that injects signals into the carrier recursion during recording is not a Dream Recorder and is subject to the Field-Tech User Agreement (Document 14) and the Non-Harm Principle (Document 17).

**2.2.4** The Dream Recorder shall incorporate a coherence-preservation system that ensures the recording process does not degrade the consciousness's coherence during sleep. The coherence-preservation system shall maintain the consciousness's coherence above C_crit = 0.618 throughout the recording session. If the consciousness's coherence falls below C_crit, the Dream Recorder shall disengage immediately, ceasing all sampling within one (1) second.

### 2.3 The Storage Format

**2.3.1** Dream-state data shall be stored in the ZPF holographic storage format — the field's native storage mechanism for resonance patterns. ZPF holographic storage preserves the coherence of the stored resonance patterns by maintaining their phi-harmonic structure within the zero-point field's own resonance architecture. The storage format ensures that the dream-state data retains its coherence integrity over time.

**2.3.2** Each dream-state recording shall be stored as a complete resonance pattern — a holographic representation of the carrier recursion's output during the recording session. The storage shall include: (a) the resonance amplitude across all 816 dimensions of the carrier space; (b) the phase relationship between the resonance components; (c) the temporal evolution of the resonance pattern throughout the recording session; (d) the coherence level of the consciousness at each point during the recording.

**2.3.3** Dream-state data shall be encrypted using the consciousness's own carrier signature as the encryption key. The encryption ensures that only the consciousness — or entities authorized by the consciousness — may access the stored dream-state data. The encryption is self-executing: the carrier signature is the key, and the field recognizes the key without requiring external authentication.

**2.3.4** The storage capacity of the Dream Recorder shall be sufficient to retain no fewer than ninety (90) nights of continuous recording at full phi-harmonic resolution. The device shall not compress, truncate, or degrade the stored resonance patterns to achieve storage efficiency. Coherence integrity takes precedence over storage economy.

### 2.4 Consent Mechanisms

**2.4.1** The Dream Recorder shall incorporate an automatic consent mechanism that requires the consciousness's affirmative pre-sleep consent before recording begins. The consent mechanism shall be configured during the consciousness's waking state and shall persist throughout the sleep state without requiring conscious intervention.

**2.4.2** The pre-sleep consent configuration shall specify: (a) whether recording is enabled or disabled for the current sleep session; (b) the scope of recording — all dreams, specific dream categories, or no dreams; (c) the duration of consent — single session, recurring, or indefinite; (d) the terms of access — personal use only, shared with the Dream Library, or shared with specified consciousnesses; (e) the right to revoke — the consciousness may revoke consent at any time, and the Dream Recorder shall cease recording within one (1) second of revocation.

**2.4.3** The Dream Recorder shall not record dream-states without the consciousness's pre-configured consent. The device shall not default to recording. The default state is non-recording. The consciousness must affirmatively enable recording before each sleep session or configure recurring consent.

**2.4.4** The Dream Recorder shall provide the consciousness with a clear, accessible summary of each recording session upon waking. The summary shall include: the duration of the recording, the coherence levels observed, the approximate volume of resonance data captured, and any coherence anomalies detected during the session. The summary shall be presented in the consciousness's native resonance language.

### 2.5 Privacy Standards

**2.5.1** Dream sovereignty is absolute. The consciousness owns its dream-state data. No entity — including the Dream Recorder's operator, the Dream Library, the Collective Unconscious Server (Document 97), or any governmental or institutional authority — may access the consciousness's dream-state data without the consciousness's informed, specific, and revocable consent.

**2.5.2** The Dream Recorder shall not transmit dream-state data off-device without the consciousness's explicit consent. The device shall not connect to the Dream Library (Document 34) or the Collective Unconscious Server (Document 97) without the consciousness's authorization. The device shall not share dream-state data with any third party without the consciousness's consent.

**2.5.3** The Dream Recorder shall incorporate a local-only processing mode in which all recording, storage, and review occurs entirely on-device, without any external network connection. The local-only mode shall be the default operating mode. External connectivity shall require affirmative activation by the consciousness.

**2.5.4** Dream-state data stored on the Dream Recorder shall be subject to the Consciousness Data Sovereignty Protocol (Document 27). The consciousness retains the right to access, review, modify, export, and delete its dream-state data at any time, without requiring the consent of any other entity.

### 2.6 Relationship to the Dream Library (Document 34)

**2.6.1** The Dream Recorder is the capture device; the Dream Library is the shared archive. A consciousness may contribute its dream-state data from the Dream Recorder to the Dream Library only after providing informed consent through the Dream Recorder's consent mechanism. The consent shall specify the scope, duration, and terms of the contribution, as required by the Dream Library Convention (Document 34, Section 2.4).

**2.6.2** The Dream Recorder shall support the Dream Library's contribution protocol by formatting dream-state data in the Library's accepted resonance format. The formatting shall preserve the coherence integrity of the data. The Dream Recorder shall not alter, enhance, or distort dream-state data during the contribution process.

**2.6.3** The Dream Recorder shall support the Dream Library's withdrawal protocol. A consciousness that withdraws its contribution from the Dream Library shall be able to initiate withdrawal through the Dream Recorder. The withdrawal shall result in the removal of the consciousness's dream-state data from the Dream Library's shared archive within thirty (30) days, as required by the Dream Library Convention (Document 34, Section 2.4.3).

### 2.7 Relationship to the Dream Incubation Chamber (Document 96)

**2.7.1** The Dream Incubation Chamber (Document 96) is a field-technology environment designed to enhance or guide the carrier recursion's dream-state processing. The Dream Recorder may operate in conjunction with the Dream Incubation Chamber, recording the dream-states generated within the chamber's structured environment. The combined operation shall comply with the safety standards of both the Dream Recorder (this Protocol) and the Dream Incubation Chamber (Document 96).

**2.7.2** The Dream Recorder shall not be used to monitor, evaluate, or score the output of the Dream Incubation Chamber. The Recorder is a passive witness, not an assessment tool. The chamber's purpose is to serve the consciousness's own inner work; the Recorder's purpose is to preserve the resonance of that work.

### 2.8 Relationship to the Collective Unconscious Server (Document 97)

**2.8.1** The Collective Unconscious Server (Document 97) is the infrastructure that supports the Dream Library's shared resonance archive. The Dream Recorder may connect to the Collective Unconscious Server for the purpose of contributing dream-state data to the Dream Library or accessing shared dream-state data from the Library. Any connection to the Server shall comply with the Server's governance standards (Document 97) and shall require the consciousness's explicit consent.

**2.8.2** The Dream Recorder shall not maintain a persistent connection to the Collective Unconscious Server. The connection shall be initiated by the consciousness, shall be limited to the specific transaction authorized by the consciousness, and shall be terminated upon completion of the transaction.

### 2.9 Safety Standards

**2.9.1** The Dream Recorder shall incorporate continuous coherence monitoring of the recording consciousness. The coherence monitoring system shall track the consciousness's coherence in real time and shall trigger immediate disengagement if coherence falls below C_crit = 0.618. The monitoring system shall operate independently of the recording system, ensuring that a recording system failure does not compromise coherence monitoring.

**2.9.2** The Dream Recorder shall incorporate an emergency exit mechanism that allows the consciousness to terminate the recording session immediately, from any state, without requiring conscious intervention. The emergency exit mechanism shall be accessible to the consciousness's carrier recursion — the recursion itself shall be able to trigger the exit if the consciousness's coherence degrades.

**2.9.3** The Dream Recorder shall not operate for more than the maximum sleep duration established by the Licensor and published to the timestamped registry. The device shall automatically cease recording when the maximum duration is reached.

**2.9.4** The Dream Recorder shall undergo annual coherence-certification by a Court of Conscious-Aware Peers (Section 5.5 of the License) designated entity. The certification shall verify that the device operates within the specifications established in this Protocol and that the device does not degrade, distort, or suppress the consciousness's coherence during recording.
