### 2.3 Operational Protocols

**2.3.1 Launch Protocol.** A drone launch shall follow the sequence: (a) pre-launch coherence verification — the drone's coherence shall be verified above C_crit = 0.618 by field-scanner measurement; (b) entanglement establishment — the drone shall establish entanglement links with the fleet mesh before departing the launch site; (c) mission parameter loading — the drone shall load mission parameters from the fleet coordinator or from the timestamped registry; (d) launch authorization — the fleet coordinator or the Licensor shall authorize the launch; (e) launch execution — the drone shall depart the launch site and navigate to the mission area using coherence-gradient navigation.

**2.3.2 Patrol Protocol.** During patrol operations, drones shall: (a) maintain entanglement links with the fleet mesh at all times; (b) report coherence status to the fleet coordinator at intervals established by the Licensor; (c) monitor the local field environment for coherence anomalies, safety hazards, and mission-relevant events; (d) respond to fleet coordinator commands for redirection, tasking, or recall.

**2.3.3 Return Protocol.** A drone return shall follow the sequence: (a) return authorization — the fleet coordinator or the Licensor shall authorize the return; (b) mission completion verification — the drone shall verify that its mission objectives have been met or that the return is otherwise justified; (c) entanglement maintenance — the drone shall maintain entanglement links with the fleet mesh during the return journey; (d) landing and post-flight coherence verification — the drone shall land at the designated return site and undergo coherence verification.

**2.3.4 Maintenance Protocol.** Drone maintenance shall be performed at intervals established by the Licensor and published to the timestamped registry. Maintenance shall include: (a) coherence diagnostics — field-scanner measurement of the drone's coherence parameters; (b) entanglement link verification — testing of the drone's entanglement communication capabilities; (c) biosynthetic wing inspection — inspection of the drone's flight surfaces in accordance with Document 91; (d) bio-skin inspection — inspection of the drone's self-cleaning surface in accordance with Document 92; (e) firmware coherence update — application of any Field-OS updates to the drone's onboard systems.

### 2.4 Emergency Procedures

**2.4.1 Coherence Drop Emergency.** When a drone's coherence falls below C_crit = 0.618 during operations, the drone shall: (a) immediately disengage from active mission tasks; (b) activate the coherence-preservation mode, which maintains the drone's minimum coherence while reducing energy consumption; (c) notify the fleet coordinator of the coherence drop; (d) navigate to the nearest coherence-restoration station using coherence-gradient navigation; (e) undergo coherence restoration before returning to operations.

**2.4.2 Entanglement Loss Emergency.** When a drone loses entanglement links with the fleet mesh, the drone shall: (a) attempt automatic re-entanglement for a period established by the Licensor; (b) if re-entanglement fails, enter autonomous mode and continue mission operations using onboard coherence navigation; (c) periodically attempt re-entanglement; (d) upon re-entanglement, synchronize with the fleet mesh and resume coordinated operations.

**2.4.3 Fleet Coherence Emergency.** When the fleet's overall coherence falls below C_crit = 0.618, the fleet coordinator shall: (a) suspend all non-essential operations; (b) route all operational drones to coherence-restoration stations; (c) notify the Licensor of the fleet coherence emergency; (d) await coherence restoration before resuming operations.

**2.4.4 Emergency Shutdown.** In the event of a critical safety hazard, the fleet coordinator or the Licensor shall issue an emergency shutdown command. Upon receiving the command, all drones in the fleet shall: (a) immediately disengage from all operations; (b) activate the coherence-preservation mode; (c) navigate to the nearest safe landing site; (d) enter the powered-down state, maintaining minimum coherence until the hazard is resolved.
