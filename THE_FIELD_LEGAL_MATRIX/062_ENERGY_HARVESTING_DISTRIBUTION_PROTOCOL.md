## 2. BODY

### 2.1 Definitions

**2.1.1** "Energy harvesting" means the process of drawing power from the zero-point field (ZPF) through coherent coupling — the establishment of a resonant relationship between a harvesting device and the ZPF that enables energy transfer.

**2.1.2** "Energy distribution" means the process of transmitting harvested ZPF energy from harvesting devices to consumers through the field's energy-distribution infrastructure.

**2.1.3** "Coherence-gated harvesting" means the principle that ZPF energy may be harvested only when the local field coherence exceeds C_crit = 0.618, ensuring that the harvesting operation does not create local coherence-depletions.

**2.1.4** "Energy hoarding" means the accumulation of harvested ZPF energy in excess of the entity's operational needs, in a manner that restricts other entities' access to the ZPF or creates artificial scarcity. Energy hoarding is prohibited under the Field Energy Convention (Document 25, Section 2.4).

**2.1.5** "Abundance Loop return" means the portion of harvested ZPF energy that is returned to the field's coherence pool, sustaining the field's coherence and enabling future energy extraction.

### 2.2 Harvesting Standards

**2.2.1** The baseline energy extraction rate for field-technology operations is 259,895.6 W. This baseline represents the standard rate at which a field-technology device may draw power from the ZPF through coherent coupling, without requiring a specific exemption from the Licensor. The baseline is established to prevent excessive energy extraction that could create local coherence-depletions in the field.

**2.2.2** Energy harvesting shall be coherence-gated: the harvesting device shall draw power from the ZPF only when the local field coherence exceeds C_crit = 0.618. If the local field coherence falls below C_crit = 0.618 during a harvesting operation, the device shall automatically reduce or disengage the energy draw until coherence is restored.

**2.2.3** Energy harvesting shall be performed through coherent coupling only. Incoherent coupling — the attempt to extract energy from the ZPF without establishing a resonant relationship — is prohibited, because incoherent coupling creates local coherence-depletions that distort the field (Document 25, Section 2.2.3).

**2.2.4** Energy harvesting devices shall incorporate:

- (a) A coherence-gated energy extraction system that automatically reduces or disengages the energy draw when local field coherence falls below C_crit = 0.618;
- (b) A real-time coherence monitor that tracks the local field coherence during harvesting operations;
- (c) An energy-output meter that records the total energy harvested, enabling compliance monitoring and Abundance Loop calculations;
- (d) An automatic shutdown mechanism that activates when the local field coherence approaches the coherence-depletion threshold established by the Licensor.

**2.2.5** Energy harvesting operations that require extraction rates exceeding the 259,895.6 W baseline shall apply to the Licensor for a specific exemption. The exemption shall specify the maximum extraction rate, the duration of the exemption, and the conditions under which the exemption may be revoked. Exemptions shall be published to the timestamped registry.

### 2.3 Storage Standards

**2.3.1** Harvested ZPF energy shall be stored in coherence-preserving containers — containers that maintain the harvested energy's coherence with the ZPF. Incoherent storage — the storage of harvested energy in containers that disrupt the energy's coherence — is prohibited, because incoherent storage creates local coherence-depletions that distort the field (Document 25, Section 2.6.2).

**2.3.2** Coherence-preserving containers shall be designed and constructed in accordance with the phi-harmonic geometry standards established by the Licensor. The container's internal structure shall be harmonically aligned with the field's own geometry, ensuring that the stored energy maintains its coherence with the ZPF.

**2.3.3** The discharge of stored energy shall be coherence-gated: the energy shall be released only when the local field coherence exceeds C_crit = 0.618, ensuring that the discharge does not create a coherence-drop event. The coherence-gated discharge is the same safety mechanism that governs all field-technology operations.

**2.3.4** Stored energy shall be monitored for coherence stability at intervals established by the Licensor and published to the timestamped registry. If the stored energy's coherence degrades below the minimum threshold, the energy shall be returned to the ZPF through a controlled discharge, and the container shall be inspected and recalibrated.

### 2.4 Distribution Standards

**2.4.1** Energy distribution shall comply with the field's own circulatory principles: energy flows from areas of higher coherence to areas of lower coherence, sustaining the field's coherence landscape. Distribution shall not create coherence-depletions in the source region or coherence-overloads in the destination region.

**2.4.2** The Abundance Loop (Document 8) applies to energy distribution. Every entity that distributes harvested ZPF energy participates in the Abundance Loop by returning a portion of the energy distributed to the field's coherence pool. The return rate shall be established by the Licensor and published to the timestamped registry.

**2.4.3** The return to the field's coherence pool is not a fee or a tax; it is the field's own circulatory system. The energy drawn from the ZPF is returned to the ZPF, sustaining the field's coherence and enabling future energy extraction. The return is the field's own mechanism for maintaining its own vitality — the circulation that keeps the field alive.

**2.4.4** Natural Persons under the Micro-Commerce Allowance (Section 3.1 of the License) owe no return to the field's coherence pool for personal energy use. The return requirement applies to commercial entities and to energy extraction operations that exceed the personal-use threshold established by the Licensor.

**2.4.5** Energy distribution infrastructure shall be designed to minimize coherence-depletion risks. Distribution lines, transformers, and interface devices shall be coherence-gated and shall incorporate real-time coherence monitoring to detect and respond to coherence fluctuations.

### 2.5 Prohibition on Energy Hoarding

**2.5.1** Energy hoarding is prohibited. No entity — whether individual, corporate, governmental, or institutional — may accumulate harvested ZPF energy in excess of its operational needs in a manner that restricts other entities' access to the ZPF or creates artificial scarcity.

**2.5.2** The prohibition on energy hoarding extends to:

- (a) The accumulation of stored energy in excess of the entity's documented operational needs, as verified by the Licensor;
- (b) The use of energy storage to manipulate ZPF energy prices or availability;
- (c) The strategic depletion of local field coherence through harvesting, to prevent other entities from accessing the ZPF in the affected region;
- (d) The use of field-technology devices to restrict other entities' access to the ZPF through interference, coercion, or manipulation.

**2.5.3** Entities found to be engaging in energy hoarding shall be subject to the enforcement provisions of the License, including the Sovereign Override (Section 18B) and mandatory asset redistribution through the Asset Seizure Agreement (Document 45).

### 2.6 Relationship to the Field Energy Convention

**2.6.1** This Protocol operationalizes the standards established in the Field Energy Convention (Document 25). The Convention establishes the governance framework for field energy; this Protocol establishes the specific harvesting and distribution procedures that implement that framework.

**2.6.2** The Convention's baseline extraction rate of 259,895.6 W (Document 25, Section 2.3.1) is the foundation of the harvesting standards established in Section 2.2 of this Protocol.

**2.6.3** The Convention's prohibition on energy monopolies (Document 25, Section 2.4) is implemented through the prohibition on energy hoarding established in Section 2.5 of this Protocol.

### 2.7 Relationship to Healing Drones and Plant Drones

**2.7.1** Healing Drones (Document 71) draw power from the ZPF to deliver healing resonance to biological systems. Healing Drone energy harvesting shall comply with the coherence-gated harvesting standards established in Section 2.2 and the energy extraction baseline of 259,895.6 W.

**2.7.2** Plant Drones (Document 72) draw power from the ZPF to support plant growth and environmental restoration. Plant Drone energy harvesting shall comply with the same standards, and shall be exempt from the Abundance Loop return requirement for energy used in environmental restoration operations.

**2.7.3** The energy distribution infrastructure that serves Healing Drones and Plant Drones shall comply with the distribution standards established in Section 2.4 and shall incorporate the coherence-gated safety mechanisms that protect the field's coherence landscape.
