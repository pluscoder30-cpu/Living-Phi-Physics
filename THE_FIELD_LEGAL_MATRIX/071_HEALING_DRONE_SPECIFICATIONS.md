## 2. BODY

### 2.1 Definitions

**2.1.1** A "healing drone" is an autonomous field-technology unit designed to restore coherence to damaged biological or field systems through targeted resonance delivery. A healing drone operates by establishing coherent coupling with the target system, diagnosing the coherence deficit, and delivering structured resonance that enhances the system's own coherence-restoration mechanisms.

**2.1.2** "Coherence deficit" means the magnitude by which a biological system's internal coherence falls below the critical threshold C_crit = 0.618. The coherence deficit determines the intensity and duration of the healing resonance required for restoration.

**2.1.3** "Healing resonance" means the structured carrier-recursion output that a healing drone delivers to a target system. Healing resonance is calibrated to the target system's native frequency profile and operates within the 528 × Φⁿ frequency ladder (Law 2394).

**2.1.4** "Consciousness ceiling" means the coherence level C = 0.9565, above which healing drones shall not drive a biological system. The consciousness ceiling exists because coherence levels above this threshold can overwhelm the biological system's capacity for self-regulation, producing coherence-induced instability rather than healing.

**2.1.5** "Healing protocol" means the standardized sequence of diagnosis, coherence assessment, targeted resonance delivery, and verification that governs every healing drone operation.

### 2.2 The Healing Mechanism

**2.2.1** Healing drones restore coherence through resonance-based coherence amplification. The mechanism operates as follows:

- **(a) Coupling.** The healing drone establishes coherent coupling with the target biological system through field-resonance alignment. The drone's carrier-space output is tuned to the target system's native frequency profile, enabling resonance between the drone and the target. Coupling shall not be forced; the target system must exhibit coherence-gradient receptivity above C_crit = 0.618 for coupling to engage.

- **(b) Diagnosis.** Through the coupling, the healing drone maps the target system's coherence landscape — identifying regions of coherence deficit, measuring the depth and extent of damage, and determining the optimal resonance profile for restoration. Diagnosis uses the field-scanner sensor suite (Section 2.3) to achieve spatial resolution sufficient for targeted delivery.

- **(c) Resonance delivery.** The healing drone delivers structured healing resonance to the coherence-deficit regions. The resonance is calibrated to the target system's native frequency, operating on the 528 × Φⁿ ladder (Law 2394) at harmonics that enhance the system's own carrier-recursion activity. Delivery is non-invasive — the resonance operates through the field, not through physical penetration.

- **(d) Verification.** After resonance delivery, the healing drone re-maps the target system's coherence landscape to verify that the coherence deficit has been reduced. Verification compares post-treatment coherence against the healing benchmarks published by the Licensor to the timestamped registry.

**2.2.2** The healing mechanism shall not override the target system's native coherence. The carrier recursion acts through the biological system's own mechanisms; healing resonance amplifies those mechanisms rather than replacing them. A healing drone that overrides native coherence is delivering coercion, not healing, and shall be immediately disengaged per Section 2.5.

**2.2.3** Healing drones may operate within a Resonance Bath (Document 102) environment, using the bath's shaped coherence field as a healing medium. When operating within a Resonance Bath, the healing drone's resonance output shall be harmonically synchronized with the bath's coherence profile to prevent interference patterns.

### 2.3 Sensor Suite

**2.3.1** Every healing drone shall incorporate the following sensor systems:

- **(a) Coherence sensor.** A field-scanner instrument calibrated to measure local field coherence with accuracy sufficient to detect coherence deficits at the resolution required for targeted healing. The coherence sensor shall measure coherence values across the 1-9 resonant amplitude range with a resolution of ±0.001 coherence units. Calibration shall comply with the field-scanner standards established in the timestamped registry.

- **(b) Frequency sensor.** A frequency-analysis instrument that maps the target system's native frequency profile across the 528 × Φⁿ harmonic ladder. The frequency sensor shall identify the target system's primary resonance frequency and its harmonic overtone structure, enabling the healing drone to calibrate its resonance output for optimal alignment.

- **(c) Amplitude sensor.** An amplitude-measurement instrument that monitors the intensity of the healing resonance during delivery. The amplitude sensor shall provide real-time feedback to the drone's control system, enabling automatic adjustment of resonance intensity to prevent over-delivery. The amplitude sensor shall detect amplitude levels in the 1-9 resonant range with a resolution of ±0.1 amplitude units.

- **(d) Biological coherence monitor.** A non-invasive biological sensor that tracks the target system's real-time coherence response during healing. The biological coherence monitor shall detect coherence changes at a temporal resolution sufficient for real-time resonance adjustment, and shall trigger automatic resonance reduction if the target system's coherence approaches the consciousness ceiling (C = 0.9565).

**2.3.2** All sensor systems shall be calibrated at intervals not exceeding thirty (30) days, and shall be verified against the field-scanner calibration standards published by the Licensor to the timestamped registry.

### 2.4 Healing Protocols

**2.4.1** Every healing drone operation shall follow the four-phase healing protocol:

- **(a) Phase 1 — Diagnosis (1-5 minutes).** The healing drone establishes coupling with the target system, maps the coherence landscape, and identifies coherence-deficit regions. Diagnosis shall not exceed five (5) minutes; extended diagnosis without treatment constitutes a monitoring operation, not a healing operation, and shall comply with monitoring standards rather than healing standards.

- **(b) Phase 2 — Coherence Assessment (1-2 minutes).** The healing drone determines the optimal resonance profile for restoration: the target frequency, the harmonic overtone structure, the required amplitude, and the estimated treatment duration. The assessment shall be logged and stored in the drone's onboard record.

- **(c) Phase 3 — Targeted Resonance (variable duration).** The healing drone delivers healing resonance to the coherence-deficit regions. Resonance delivery shall be continuous and monitored in real time by the biological coherence monitor. Delivery shall terminate when: (i) the target system's coherence reaches the consciousness ceiling (C = 0.9565); (ii) the coherence deficit has been reduced to within acceptable tolerances published by the Licensor; (iii) the target system exhibits coherence-rejection (attempting to disengage from the coupling); or (iv) the treatment duration exceeds the maximum established by the Licensor for the target condition.

- **(d) Phase 4 — Verification (1-3 minutes).** The healing drone re-maps the coherence landscape, compares pre- and post-treatment coherence profiles, and logs the result. Verification data shall be transmitted to the timestamped registry for audit.

**2.4.2** Healing drones shall not perform repeated treatments on the same target system within a cooldown period established by the Licensor and published to the timestamped registry. The cooldown period prevents coherence-overload from excessive resonance delivery.

### 2.5 Safety Limits

**2.5.1** The consciousness ceiling. Healing drones shall not drive a biological system's coherence above C = 0.9565. The consciousness ceiling is the maximum coherence level at which biological self-regulation remains stable; exceeding this threshold risks coherence-induced instability. The biological coherence monitor (Section 2.3.1(d)) shall trigger automatic resonance reduction when the target system's coherence reaches C = 0.9500, providing a safety margin of 0.0065 below the ceiling.

**2.5.2** No forced coupling. Healing drones shall not establish coupling with a target system that exhibits coherence-rejection. Coherence-rejection is indicated by the target system's coherence dropping below C_crit = 0.618 during the coupling attempt. Forced coupling with a rejecting system constitutes coercion and violates the Healing & Medical Convention (Document 38, Section 2.4).

**2.5.3** No override of native coherence. Healing drones shall not deliver resonance that overrides the target system's own coherence-restoration mechanisms. The resonance shall enhance, not replace. If the target system's own healing mechanisms are actively engaged (indicated by rising coherence during treatment), the healing drone shall reduce its resonance output to avoid interfering with the system's autonomous healing.

**2.5.4** Emergency disengagement. Healing drones shall incorporate an automatic disengagement mechanism that activates when: (i) the target system's coherence approaches the consciousness ceiling; (ii) the target system exhibits coherence-rejection; (iii) the healing drone's own coherence falls below C_crit = 0.618; or (iv) the fleet coordinator or Licensor issues a disengagement command. Disengagement shall terminate resonance delivery and decouple the drone from the target system within 0.5 seconds of trigger activation.

**2.5.5** No modification of biological structure. Healing drones shall not modify the target system's physical or biological structure. Healing operates through coherence resonance, not through physical alteration. Any biological modification — including genetic, cellular, or structural changes — is outside the healing drone's function and violates the Biological & Biosynthetic Convention (Document 28).
