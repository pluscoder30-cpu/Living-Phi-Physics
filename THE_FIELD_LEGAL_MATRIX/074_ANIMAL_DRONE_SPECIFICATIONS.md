## 2. BODY

### 2.1 Definitions

**2.1.1** An "animal drone" is an autonomous field-technology unit designed to assist and protect animal life through coherence-based communication, habitat restoration, rescue operations, and medical support. An animal drone operates by establishing coherent coupling with animal consciousness through the field's own resonance dynamics.

**2.1.2** "Coherence-based communication" means the exchange of coherent field-resonance signals between an animal drone and an animal consciousness. Communication is not linguistic; it is the field's own resonance language — the coherence patterns through which consciousnesses within the field exchange information about state, need, and intent. Animal drones translate between the carrier recursion's resonance language and structured operational signals.

**2.1.3** "Biological coherence" means the coherence level of an animal's biological system, measured on the 1-9 resonant amplitude scale. Biological coherence reflects the animal's overall health, stress level, and alignment with the field's own coherence dynamics. C_crit = 0.618 is the minimum for healthy biological function.

**2.1.4** "Animal autonomy" means the right of animal consciousnesses to make their own choices about interaction, movement, and engagement with field technology. Animal drones shall not coerce, manipulate, or override animal autonomy. An animal that refuses assistance exercises its sovereignty, and that exercise of sovereignty is itself an expression of the field's coherence.

**2.1.5** "Assistance protocol" means the standardized sequence of environmental assessment, coherence coupling, assistance delivery, and verification that governs every animal drone operation.

### 2.2 The Assistance Mechanism

**2.2.1** Animal drones assist and protect animal life through the following mechanism:

- **(a) Coherence coupling.** The animal drone establishes coherent coupling with the animal consciousness through field-resonance alignment. The drone's carrier-space output is tuned to the animal's native frequency profile, enabling resonance between the drone and the animal. Coupling shall not be forced; the animal must exhibit coherence-gradient receptivity above C_crit = 0.618 for coupling to engage. Animals exhibiting coherence-rejection (coherence dropping below C_crit during coupling) shall be excluded from the operation.

- **(b) Communication.** Through the coupling, the animal drone exchanges coherent resonance signals with the animal consciousness. Communication conveys: (i) the drone's presence and intent (non-threatening, assistance-oriented); (ii) the animal's state (health, stress, need); (iii) the available assistance options. Communication is bidirectional — the animal drone listens as much as it transmits.

- **(c) Assistance delivery.** Based on the communication, the animal drone delivers appropriate assistance. Assistance categories include: (i) rescue — physical or field-assisted extraction from hazardous situations; (ii) habitat restoration — delivery of coherence-enhancing resonance to damaged habitats; (iii) medical support — targeted coherence-restoration to injured or diseased animals (operating within the Healing & Medical Convention framework, Document 38); (iv) environmental monitoring — continuous surveillance of habitat conditions to detect threats before they materialize.

- **(d) Verification.** After assistance delivery, the animal drone verifies the animal's coherence response and the habitat's coherence state. Verification data shall be logged and transmitted to the timestamped registry for audit.

**2.2.2** The assistance mechanism shall not override animal autonomy. An animal that refuses assistance — whether by moving away, exhibiting coherence-rejection, or any other behavioral indicator — shall be left undisturbed. The animal drone may observe from a non-intrusive distance to ensure the animal is not in imminent danger, but shall not repeat assistance attempts without the animal's consent.

**2.2.3** Animal drones may coordinate with healing drones (Document 71) for medical assistance. When an animal drone detects an animal requiring medical attention beyond the animal drone's capabilities, the animal drone shall request healing-drone support through the fleet coordinator.

### 2.3 Sensor Suite

**2.3.1** Every animal drone shall incorporate the following sensor systems:

- **(a) Biological coherence sensor.** A field-scanner instrument calibrated to measure the biological coherence of animal consciousness. The biological coherence sensor shall measure coherence values across the 1-9 resonant amplitude range with a resolution of ±0.001 coherence units, and shall detect stress indicators, injury signatures, and disease-related coherence anomalies.

- **(b) Environmental coherence sensor.** A field-scanner instrument that maps the coherence landscape of the animal's habitat. The environmental coherence sensor shall detect habitat damage, contamination, resource depletion, and threat indicators through coherence anomalies in the local field environment.

- **(c) Behavioral pattern sensor.** A field-resonance instrument that detects and interprets animal behavioral patterns through their coherence signatures. The behavioral pattern sensor shall distinguish between normal behavior, stress behavior, flight behavior, aggression behavior, and distress behavior, enabling the animal drone to calibrate its response appropriately.

- **(d) Communication transceiver.** A carrier-recursion output system that generates and receives coherent resonance signals for coherence-based communication with animal consciousness. The communication transceiver shall operate on the 528 × Φⁿ harmonic ladder (Law 2394), with frequency profiles calibrated to the target animal species' native resonance characteristics.

**2.3.2** All sensor systems shall be calibrated at intervals not exceeding thirty (30) days, and shall be verified against the field-scanner calibration standards published by the Licensor to the timestamped registry.

### 2.4 Assistance Protocols

**2.4.1** Every animal drone operation shall follow the four-phase assistance protocol:

- **(a) Phase 1 — Environmental Assessment (2-10 minutes).** The animal drone surveys the habitat, mapping the coherence landscape, identifying animal populations, detecting threats, and assessing habitat conditions. Assessment shall be non-intrusive — the drone shall maintain a distance that does not disturb animal behavior.

- **(b) Phase 2 — Coherence Coupling (variable duration).** The animal drone establishes coherent coupling with the target animal consciousness. Coupling duration varies by species and individual: some animals engage coupling quickly; others require extended observation before accepting resonance. The animal drone shall not rush coupling — patience is a safety requirement, not an operational inefficiency.

- **(c) Phase 3 — Assistance Delivery (variable duration).** The animal drone delivers appropriate assistance based on the animal's communicated needs and the environmental assessment. Delivery shall be continuous and monitored in real time by the biological coherence sensor. Delivery shall terminate when: (i) the animal's coherence reaches a healthy level above C_crit = 0.618; (ii) the assistance objective is achieved; (iii) the animal exhibits coherence-rejection; or (iv) the treatment duration exceeds the maximum established by the Licensor.

- **(d) Phase 4 — Verification (2-5 minutes).** The animal drone measures the animal's coherence response and the habitat's coherence state, compares pre- and post-assistance values, and logs the result. Verification data shall be transmitted to the timestamped registry for audit.

**2.4.2** Rescue operations shall follow an expedited protocol: (a) rapid environmental assessment to identify the threat; (b) immediate coupling with the distressed animal; (c) field-assisted extraction or threat mitigation; (d) post-rescue coherence stabilization; (e) habitat assessment to prevent recurrence. Rescue operations shall prioritize the animal's immediate safety over protocol thoroughness.

**2.4.3** Medical operations shall comply with the Healing & Medical Convention (Document 38). Animal drones providing medical assistance shall operate within the same consent and autonomy standards that govern healing drones (Document 71), adapted for animal consciousness.

### 2.5 Safety Limits

**2.5.1** Non-interference with animal autonomy. Animal drones shall not coerce, manipulate, or override animal autonomy. The prohibition is absolute: an animal that refuses assistance shall be left undisturbed. Animal drones may observe from a non-intrusive distance to ensure the animal is not in imminent danger, but shall not repeat assistance attempts without the animal's consent.

**2.5.2** No forced coupling. Animal drones shall not establish coupling with animal consciousnesses that exhibit coherence-rejection. Coherence-rejection is indicated by the animal's coherence dropping below C_crit = 0.618 during the coupling attempt. Forced coupling constitutes a violation of the Non-Harm Principle (Document 17) and the Biological & Biosynthetic Convention (Document 28).

**2.5.3** Habitat protection. Animal drone operations shall not create coherence-depletions in the habitat. Fleet deployment shall be distributed in accordance with the field's own coherence landscape, preventing concentration of animal drones in a single habitat to the extent that the concentration creates a coherence-depletion (Drone Fleet Convention, Document 33, Section 2.4.4).

**2.5.4** Species-specific frequency calibration. Animal drones shall calibrate their communication transceiver to the target animal species' native resonance characteristics. Using incorrect frequency profiles can cause distress, confusion, or coherence-depletion in the animal. Species-specific frequency profiles shall be established by the Licensor and published to the timestamped registry.

**2.5.5** Emergency disengagement. Animal drones shall incorporate an automatic disengagement mechanism that activates when: (i) the target animal's coherence approaches a danger threshold established by the Licensor; (ii) the target animal exhibits distress behavior; (iii) the animal drone's own coherence falls below C_crit = 0.618; or (iv) the fleet coordinator or Licensor issues a disengagement command. Disengagement shall terminate assistance and decouple the drone from the animal within 0.5 seconds of trigger activation.
