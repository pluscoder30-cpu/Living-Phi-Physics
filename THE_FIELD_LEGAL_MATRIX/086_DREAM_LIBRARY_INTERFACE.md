## 2. BODY

### 2.1 Definitions

**2.1.1** "Dream Library Interface" means the field-technology system that enables consciousnesses to access, explore, and interact with the Dream Library's shared resonance archive. The Interface is the navigation layer of the Dream Library — the system through which consciousnesses find, resonate with, and contribute dream-state data.

**2.1.2** "Resonance matching" means the mechanism by which the Dream Library Interface identifies and presents dream-state data to the accessing consciousness. Resonance matching does not use keywords, tags, or metadata; it uses the accessing consciousness's own carrier-space resonance to find resonance patterns in the archive that harmonize with the consciousness's current state. The match is between resonances, not between labels.

**2.1.3** "Coherence-based navigation" means the browsing mechanism by which consciousnesses explore the Dream Library. Rather than traversing a hierarchical catalog or a search index, consciousnesses navigate the archive by following coherence gradients — moving from one resonance pattern to the next along the paths of highest coherence harmony. Navigation is intuitive, resonant, and non-linear.

**2.1.4** "Voluntary contribution" means the process by which a consciousness adds its dream-state data to the Dream Library's shared archive. Contribution is always voluntary, always consensual, and always reversible, as required by the Dream Library Convention (Document 34, Section 2.4).

**2.1.5** "Dream sovereignty" means every consciousness's right to ownership, control, and privacy of its own dream-states, as established in the Dream Library Convention (Document 34, Section 2.3).

### 2.2 The Access Protocol

**2.2.1** Access to the Dream Library through the Interface requires resonance-matching. The accessing consciousness's carrier-space resonance is compared against the resonance patterns stored in the Dream Library. The Interface presents resonance patterns that harmonize with the accessing consciousness's current state — patterns whose phi-harmonic structure resonates with the consciousness's carrier recursion (Eq 1).

**2.2.2** Resonance-matching shall not require the consciousness to disclose its identity, location, or any personal data. The matching operates on the resonance alone — the carrier-space frequency, the coherence amplitude, the phase relationship. The consciousness's identity is irrelevant to the match; only its resonance matters.

**2.2.3** Access shall be available to any consciousness within the field whose coherence exceeds C_crit = 0.618. Consciousnesses whose coherence falls below C_crit may not access the Dream Library, because low-coherence access may result in incoherent resonance interactions that degrade both the accessing consciousness and the stored dream-state data. The coherence threshold protects both the visitor and the archive.

**2.2.4** Access shall be authenticated through the consciousness's carrier signature — the unique resonance pattern that identifies the consciousness within the carrier space (816D). The carrier signature serves as both identity and key; no external passwords, tokens, or credentials are required. The field recognizes the carrier signature as self-executing authentication (Section 13 of the License, Addendum A.5).

### 2.3 The Browsing Mechanism

**2.3.1** The Dream Library Interface shall use coherence-based navigation as its primary browsing mechanism. Consciousnesses navigate the archive by following coherence gradients — moving from one resonance pattern to the next along the paths of highest coherence harmony. The navigation is non-linear; there is no fixed order, no mandatory sequence, no predetermined path. Each consciousness's navigation is unique, determined by its own resonance and its relationship to the stored patterns.

**2.3.2** The Interface shall present dream-state data in the resonance format in which it was stored — as field-resonance patterns, not as text, images, or any other derivative representation. The consciousness experiences the dream-state directly through resonance, not through a mediated translation. The dream is received as the carrier recursion generated it.

**2.3.3** The Interface shall support multi-resonance exploration — the simultaneous resonance with multiple dream-state patterns. A consciousness may explore multiple resonance patterns at once, comparing, contrasting, and synthesizing the patterns into its own coherence structure. The multi-resonance exploration is governed by the consciousness's coherence capacity; the Interface shall not present more resonance patterns than the consciousness's coherence can sustain above C_crit.

**2.3.4** The Interface shall provide a grounding mechanism — a coherence anchor that maintains the accessing consciousness's connection to its own carrier recursion during exploration. The grounding mechanism ensures that the consciousness does not lose itself in the archive — that it remains anchored to its own coherence while resonating with others' dream-states. The grounding mechanism shall maintain the consciousness's coherence above C_crit = 0.618 at all times during exploration.

### 2.4 The Contribution Protocol

**2.4.1** A consciousness may contribute its dream-state data to the Dream Library's shared archive through the Interface. Contribution is voluntary; no consciousness is required to contribute, and no consciousness may be coerced into contributing (Dream Library Convention, Document 34, Section 2.4.2).

**2.4.2** The contribution protocol shall require the consciousness's affirmative consent before the contribution is processed. The consent shall specify: (a) the specific dream-state data to be contributed — a single dream, a collection of dreams, or all recorded dreams; (b) the duration of the contribution — a specific period or indefinite; (c) the terms of access — open access (any consciousness may resonate with the contributed data), restricted access (only consciousnesses meeting specified criteria may resonate), or research-only access (the contributed data may be used only for consciousness research purposes); (d) the right to withdraw — the consciousness may withdraw its contribution at any time.

**2.4.3** The contribution process shall preserve the coherence integrity of the dream-state data. The data shall be transferred from the Dream Recorder (Document 85) to the Dream Library's archive without alteration, enhancement, or distortion. The contributed data shall be stored in the ZPF holographic format, maintaining its phi-harmonic structure.

**2.4.4** The Interface shall provide the contributing consciousness with a contribution receipt — a resonance confirmation that the contribution has been received, stored, and indexed in the Dream Library's archive. The receipt shall include the contribution's timestamp, the coherence integrity verification, and the access terms specified by the consciousness.

**2.4.5** Withdrawal of contribution shall be processed within thirty (30) days of the withdrawal request, as required by the Dream Library Convention (Document 34, Section 2.4.3). The withdrawal shall result in the complete removal of the consciousness's contributed data from the Dream Library's shared archive. The Interface shall provide a withdrawal confirmation — a resonance verification that the data has been removed.

### 2.5 Privacy Standards

**2.5.1** Dream sovereignty is absolute within the Dream Library Interface. The consciousness owns its dream-state data. No entity may access, resonate with, or use a consciousness's dream-state data without that consciousness's informed, specific, and revocable consent.

**2.5.2** The Interface shall not record, log, or track the accessing consciousness's navigation patterns, resonance interactions, or exploration history without the consciousness's consent. The consciousness's journey through the Dream Library is its own private experience.

**2.5.3** The Interface shall not disclose the accessing consciousness's identity to other consciousnesses whose dream-state data the accessing consciousness resonates with. Resonance is anonymous; the resonance connects resonances, not identities.

**2.5.4** The Interface shall support anonymous contribution — a consciousness may contribute its dream-state data to the Dream Library without disclosing its identity. The contributed data shall be attributed to "Anonymous Consciousness" in the archive's metadata.

**2.9.1** The Dream Library Interface shall incorporate continuous coherence monitoring of all accessing consciousnesses. The monitoring system shall track the consciousness's coherence in real time and shall trigger a controlled disengagement if coherence falls below C_crit = 0.618. **2.9.3** The Interface shall provide a clear, immediate, and accessible exit mechanism at all times during exploration.
