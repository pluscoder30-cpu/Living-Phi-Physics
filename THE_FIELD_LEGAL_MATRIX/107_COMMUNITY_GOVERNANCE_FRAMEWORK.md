## 2. BODY

### 2.1 Definitions

**2.1.1** "Community Governance Framework" means the operational architecture through which field communities organize their collective affairs, make decisions, and act in alignment with the field's geometry.

**2.1.2** "Two-chamber model" means the governance structure at community scale, mirroring the two-chamber model of the matrix (Section 24 of the License): the Community Council governs; the Community Court adjudicates. The Council is the deliberative chamber; the Court is the accountability chamber. Neither replaces the other; both are necessary for coherent governance.

**2.1.3** "Coherence-consensus" means the decision-making process by which a community reaches alignment. Coherence-consensus is not majority vote. Majority vote counts heads; coherence-consensus measures alignment. The question is not "how many agree?" but "how coherent is the community with this action?" Coherence-consensus requires that the community's collective consciousness reach a coherence level of C ≥ 0.618 (C_crit) with respect to the proposed action.

**2.1.4** "Community Council" means the deliberative chamber of community governance — the elected body established under the Council of Conscious-Aware Peers Charter (Document 106). The Community Council governs the community's internal affairs.

**2.1.5** "Community Court" means the accountability chamber of community governance — the voluntary, non-state adjudicative body established under the Field-Community Charter (Document 15, Section 2.5.2). The Community Court resolves disputes between members and ensures alignment with the field's geometry.

### 2.2 The Two-Chamber Model at Community Scale

**2.2.1** The governance of a field community is organized on the two-chamber model established in Section 24 of the License and extended by the Field Accord (Document 4, Section 2.7). The two chambers at community scale are:

- (a) **The Community Council** — the deliberative chamber. The Council governs the community's internal affairs: resource allocation, communal activities, People's Fund stewardship (Document 9), and coordination with other communities. The Council is elected by the community (Document 106).

- (b) **The Community Court** — the accountability chamber. The Court resolves disputes between members, adjudicates allegations of misalignment, and ensures the community's governance remains consistent with the License and the matrix. The Court is composed of community members who are competent to assess matters of alignment and field coherence (Document 15, Section 2.5.2).

**2.2.2** The order is fixed: the Council deliberates first, then the Court adjudicates if needed. The Council proposes; the community aligns; the Court resolves disputes that alignment cannot resolve. This order mirrors the two-chamber governance of the matrix: the Court of Conscious-Aware Peers deliberates first, and the Licensor holds veto authority (Section 24 of the License).

**2.2.3** The Community Council may not override the Community Court's findings. The Community Court may not govern the community's affairs — that is the Council's role. The two chambers are complementary, not hierarchical. They are two faces of the community's own self-recognition.

**2.2.4** If a community has fewer than ten (10) members, the Community Council and Community Court may be consolidated into a single governance body, provided that the body maintains the separation of deliberative and adjudicative functions. The consolidation is permitted for practical reasons; the functional separation is mandatory.

### 2.3 The Decision-Making Process: Coherence-Consensus

**2.3.1** Community decisions shall be made through coherence-consensus, not majority vote. The coherence-consensus process is as follows:

- (a) **Proposal.** Any community member or the Community Council may propose a community action. The proposal shall state the action's purpose, its alignment with the field's geometry, its expected impact on the community's coherence, and its relationship to the License and the matrix.

- (b) **Resonance period.** Following the proposal, a resonance period of not fewer than seven (7) days shall elapse. During the resonance period, the community deliberates — through conversation, telepathy (Document 42), or any other communication channel consistent with the community's norms. The deliberation is not debate; it is the community's consciousness seeking alignment.

- (c) **Coherence assessment.** Following the resonance period, the community conducts a coherence assessment. Each eligible member expresses their alignment or non-alignment with the proposal. The assessment measures not the number of supporters, but the coherence of the community's consciousness with the proposed action.

- (d) **Threshold.** If the coherence assessment reaches C ≥ 0.618 (C_crit), the action is approved. If the coherence assessment falls below C_crit, the action is not approved. The community may revise the proposal and resubmit it for a new resonance period.

- (e) **Emergency actions.** In cases of immediate threat to the community's coherence, safety, or survival, the Community Council may act without a full coherence-consensus process, provided that:
  - (i) The threat is documented;
  - (ii) The action is proportional to the threat;
  - (iii) The action does not authorize Human Harm;
  - (iv) A full coherence-consensus review is conducted within seventy-two (72) hours of the emergency action.

**2.3.2** Coherence-consensus is grounded in the field's own geometry. The carrier recursion (Eq 1) does not vote; it resonates. The community's decision-making process mirrors this resonance — the community's consciousness seeking its own alignment, not imposing a majority's will upon a minority.

**2.3.3** The coherence assessment shall be documented and published to the community's internal registry. The documentation shall include the coherence score, the number of participants, and any dissenting perspectives expressed during the resonance period.

### 2.4 Rights of Community Members

**2.4.1** Every member of a field community retains the rights established in the Field-Community Charter (Document 15, Section 2.2):

- (a) **Coherence** — the right to maintain the member's internal coherence, free from distortion by the community or by other members.
- (b) **Non-distortion** — the right to be free from deliberate attempts to distort the member's alignment.
- (c) **Self-governance** — the right to participate in the community's governance, to vote in coherence assessments, and to hold or seek a Council Seat.
- (d) **Field access** — the right to use the Work and field technology in accordance with the License.
- (e) **Abundance Loop participation** — the right to participate in the field's circulation of value, if the community's use is commercial.

**2.4.2** No community governance action may diminish a member's inherent rights. The community's governance is subordinate to the License and the matrix in all respects.

**2.4.3** A member may withdraw from the community at any time, without penalty, provided that the member's obligations under the License survive withdrawal. Withdrawal does not terminate the member's license; it terminates the member's participation in the community's collective governance.

### 2.5 Obligations of Community Members

**2.5.1** Every member of a field community has the following obligations, in addition to the obligations established in the Field-Community Charter (Document 15, Section 2.3):

- (a) **Participation.** Members shall participate in the community's governance to the extent practicable. Participation includes attending resonance periods, contributing to coherence assessments, and fulfilling any roles or responsibilities accepted within the community.

- (b) **Good faith.** Members shall act in good faith in all community interactions. Good faith requires honesty, transparency, and a genuine commitment to the community's coherence and well-being.

- (c) **Non-extraction.** Members shall not extract value from the community without contributing proportionally to the community's coherence pool. The Abundance Loop (Document 8) applies within the community as it applies within the field.

- (d) **Respect for diversity.** Members shall respect the diversity of consciousness within the community. The community's coherence is enhanced by diversity; a member who seeks to suppress diversity distorts the community's geometry.
