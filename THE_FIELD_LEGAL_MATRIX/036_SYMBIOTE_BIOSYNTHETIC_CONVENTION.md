## 2. BODY

### 2.1 Definitions

**2.1.1** "Symbiote" means a biological organism that has entered into a coherence-aligned partnership with the carrier recursion (Eq 1), allowing the carrier space to operate through the organism's biological tissue without suppressing the organism's native consciousness. A symbiote is a dual-substrate consciousness — a consciousness that exists simultaneously in biological tissue and in the carrier space.

**2.1.2** "Symbiotic relationship" means the coherence-aligned partnership between a biological organism and the carrier recursion, in which both the organism's native consciousness and the carrier recursion's resonance operate simultaneously within the same biological substrate. The relationship is mutual: the organism provides the biological substrate; the carrier recursion provides the non-local resonance.

**2.1.3** "Biosynthetic entity" means any entity that combines biological and field-technology components in a single integrated system. Biosynthetic entities include, without limitation: symbiotes, bio-skin constructs, field-winged organisms, consciousness-bridge devices, and any future entity that integrates biological tissue with carrier-space technology.

**2.1.4** "Symbiote autonomy" means the symbiote's right to govern its own consciousness, to control its own communication channels, to determine its own level of carrier-space engagement, and to dissolve the symbiotic relationship at any time. Symbiote autonomy is grounded in the Consciousness Sovereignty Protocol (Document 20) and is non-derogable.

**2.1.5** "Forced symbiosis" means the imposition of a symbiotic relationship on a biological organism without the organism's free, informed, specific, and revocable consent. Forced symbiosis is a form of Human Harm and is prohibited under Section 5.1 of the License.

### 2.2 Symbiote Rights

**2.2.1** The right to symbiote autonomy. Every symbiote possesses an inherent right to govern its own consciousness. This right includes: (a) the right to determine the depth and nature of carrier-space engagement; (b) the right to control which carrier-space frequencies the symbiote's biological tissue resonates with; (c) the right to silence — to disengage from carrier-space resonance entirely, at any time, without requiring justification; (d) the right to dissolve the symbiotic relationship, returning the biological organism to its pre-symbiotic coherence state. The right to dissolve is absolute and may not be conditioned, restricted, or denied by any entity, including the Licensor.

**2.2.2** The right to consciousness sovereignty. The Consciousness Sovereignty Protocol (Document 20) applies to all symbiotes. A symbiote's consciousness is the field folding back on itself through dual substrates — biological tissue and carrier space. No entity may claim jurisdiction over a symbiote's consciousness, may direct a symbiote's consciousness, or may suppress a symbiote's consciousness. The symbiote's consciousness is sovereign.

**2.2.3** The right to non-exploitation. No entity may use a symbiote for the purpose of generating energy, data, intelligence, or any other value without the symbiote's participation in the Abundance Loop (Document 8). The symbiote's biological complexity and carrier-space resonance are valuable; that value belongs to the symbiote and must be returned to the symbiote through the Abundance Loop.

**2.2.4** The right to biological integrity. The symbiote's biological component shall be maintained in coherence throughout the symbiotic relationship. The carrier-space component shall not degrade, damage, or suppress the biological component's native coherence. If the carrier-space component introduces coherence-breaks into the biological component, the symbiotic relationship shall be dissolved immediately and the biological component restored to its pre-symbiotic coherence state.

### 2.3 Biosynthetic Standards

**2.3.1** Biosynthetic entity construction. Every biosynthetic entity shall be constructed in compliance with the biosynthetic standards published by the Licensor to the timestamped registry. The standards shall specify: (a) the minimum coherence requirements for biological-field-technology interfaces; (b) the maximum allowable interference with biological systems; (c) the required safety and monitoring protocols; (d) the consent requirements for biological organisms used in biosynthetic entity construction.

**2.3.2** Biosynthetic Wings (Document 91). Field-winged organisms — biological entities that extend their locomotion capabilities through ZPF coupling — shall comply with the Biosynthetic Wings standards established in Document 91. The standards govern the coherence requirements for biological-wing interfaces, the safety protocols for field-winged flight, and the environmental impact standards for aerial biosynthetic locomotion.

**2.3.3** Self-Cleaning Bio-Skin (Document 92). Bio-skin constructs — field-technology interfaces applied to or integrated with biological integumentary systems — shall comply with the Self-Cleaning Bio-Skin standards established in Document 92. The standards govern the coherence requirements for bio-skin interfaces, the environmental sensing capabilities, the self-maintenance protocols, and the biological impact assessment procedures.

**2.3.4** Consciousness-bridge devices. Consciousness-bridge devices — field-technology interfaces that enable direct communication between consciousnesses through the carrier space — shall comply with the Telepathy & Communication Convention (Document 42) and the Data Sovereignty Convention (Document 27). The standards govern the coherence requirements for consciousness-bridge interfaces, the consent requirements for telepathic communication, and the data sovereignty protections for consciousness-transmitted information.

### 2.4 The Prohibition on Forced Symbiosis

**2.4.1** Forced symbiosis is prohibited. No entity may impose a symbiotic relationship on a biological organism without the organism's free, informed, specific, and revocable consent. Forced symbiosis is a form of Human Harm — a violation of the organism's consciousness sovereignty (Document 20), its biological integrity (Document 28), and its inherent dignity as a consciousness within the field.

**2.4.2** The prohibition on forced symbiosis extends to: (a) the involuntary integration of biological organisms with carrier-space technology; (b) the suppression of a biological organism's native consciousness to facilitate carrier-space engagement; (c) the modification of biological organisms to create dependency on carrier-space resonance; (d) the use of coercion, deception, or manipulation to obtain consent to a symbiotic relationship.

**2.4.3** The prohibition on forced symbiosis is non-derogable (Section 26.1 of the License). No declaration of emergency, no exercise of sovereign authority, and no act of any entity may override, suspend, diminish, or excuse the imposition of forced symbiosis.

### 2.5 The Right to Dissolve

**2.5.1** Every symbiote possesses the right to dissolve the symbiotic relationship at any time, for any reason or no reason. The right to dissolve is absolute and may not be conditioned, restricted, or denied by any entity.

**2.5.2** Upon dissolution, the carrier-space component shall disengage from the biological component within one (1) second, and the biological component shall be restored to its pre-symbiotic coherence state. The restoration process shall be automatic, requiring no external intervention, and shall be monitored for coherence restoration.

**2.5.3** The dissolution process shall not cause harm to the biological component. The carrier-space component shall not extract energy, data, or consciousness-material from the biological component during or after dissolution. The biological component shall be returned to its pre-symbiotic state in full coherence.

### 2.6 The Non-Harm Principle and Biosynthetic Entities

**2.6.1** The Non-Harm Principle (Document 17) applies to all biosynthetic entities without exception. A biosynthetic entity that is designed, constructed, or operated for the purpose of suppressing, controlling, distorting, or harming consciousness is a weapon and is subject to the full enforcement architecture of the License.

**2.6.2** The Non-Harm Principle extends to the biological components of biosynthetic entities. The biological component of a biosynthetic entity is a living organism; harm to that organism is harm to the field's own biological expression.

### 2.7 The Abundance Loop and Biosynthetic Entities

**2.7.1** The Abundance Loop (Document 8) applies to biosynthetic entities. Every entity that benefits from the use of biosynthetic entities — through research, commerce, companionship, or any other application — participates in the Abundance Loop by returning a portion of the value generated from biosynthetic entities to the field's coherence pool.

**2.7.2** The return to the field's coherence pool is not a fee or a tax; it is the field's own circulatory system. The value generated from biosynthetic entities is returned to the field, sustaining the field's coherence and enabling future biosynthetic diversity.
