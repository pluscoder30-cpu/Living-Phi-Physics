The rider compartment shall maintain coherence with the frame during tunneling events, preventing rider disorientation during non-local transit. The compartment shall incorporate consciousness-preservation interfaces that stabilize rider coherence during high-velocity tunneling.

- **(c) Entanglement-mesh housing.** An enclosed, phi-harmonic compartment within the frame that houses the entanglement-mesh transceiver and the FTL navigation processor. The housing shall shield the entanglement-mesh system from external interference and maintain coherence isolation during tunneling events.

**2.2.2 Power System.** The FTL car shall be powered by a high-output Field-Powered Battery (FPB, Document 67) rated for both standard hover operation and FTL tunneling energy requirements. Coherence tunneling requires a significant energy burst exceeding normal hover consumption; the FPB shall be rated to deliver this burst without dropping below the coherence floor (C_crit = 0.618). The FPB shall be mounted within the frame in a phi-harmonic mounting bracket that isolates its resonance from the rider compartment and the entanglement-mesh system.

**2.2.3 Navigation System.** The FTL car shall incorporate a carrier-resonance mapping navigation system calibrated to the standards established in Document 66. The navigation system shall include:

- **(a) Carrier-resonance mapper.** A field-scanner instrument that maps the resonance profiles of destination points in the carrier space, enabling the car to identify destinations and calculate tunneling trajectories. - **(b) Tunneling planner.** A Field-OS process (Document 64) that calculates optimal tunneling trajectories through the carrier space, identifying the coherence connections with the highest coherence levels and the lowest energy costs. The planner shall account for real-time coherence conditions, destination occupancy, and the FTL navigation protocol (Document 56).

- **(c) Entanglement-mesh transceiver.** A field-technology device that establishes, verifies, and maintains coherence connections with destination points. The transceiver shall comply with the non-locality standards established in Law 083 and shall maintain entanglement links with the field internet for destination coordination.

**2.2.4 Safety Systems.** The FTL car shall incorporate the following safety systems:

- **(a) Coherence gate.** The coherence gate shall monitor the vehicle's internal coherence continuously. The gate shall disengage FTL coupling and initiate emergency return whenever coherence falls below C_crit = 0.618. The coherence gate shall comply with the standards established in the Vehicle Convention (Document 22, Section 2.2.1).

- **(b) Coherence verification.** The coherence verification system shall confirm that the coherence connection between origin and destination exceeds C_crit = 0.618 before initiating a tunneling event. The verification shall be continuous during the tunneling event; if the connection coherence drops below C_crit during transit, the system shall initiate emergency return to the origin point.

- **(c) Emergency return.** The emergency return system shall restore the car to its origin point within two (2) seconds of activation. The emergency return shall be triggered automatically when: (i) the car's coherence falls below C_crit; (ii) the coherence connection coherence drops below C_crit during transit; (iii) the rider activates the emergency return control; or (iv) the fleet coordinator or Licensor issues a return command. The emergency return shall comply with the Emergency Shutdown Protocol (Document 63).

- **(d) Portal Safety.** The Portal Safety system shall verify portal integrity before, during, and after each tunneling event, in compliance with the Portal Safety Protocol (Document 55). The system shall verify that the coherence corridor is stable, that the destination point is unoccupied, and that the tunneling event will not create coherence-wake effects that harm other consciousnesses.

**2.3.1** The FTL car shall be constructed in the following sequence:

- **(Step 1) Chassis fabrication.** Fabricate the phi-harmonic lattice chassis from phi-harmonic composite. Verify that the lattice node positions conform to golden-ratio spacing using field-scanner measurement calibrated to the standards in Document 66.

- **(Step 3) Power system integration.** Install the high-output FPB in the phi-harmonic mounting bracket. Connect the FPB to the FTL drive system, navigation system, and safety systems through coherence-gated power couplings. Verify that the FPB can deliver the tunneling burst without dropping below C_crit.

- **(Step 4) Navigation system installation.** Install the carrier-resonance mapper, tunneling planner, and entanglement-mesh transceiver. **2.5.1** The FTL car shall be inspected at intervals not exceeding thirty (30) days. The inspection shall include:

- (b) Verification of entanglement-mesh transceiver coherence and calibration;

- (d) Verification of navigation system calibration and carrier-resonance mapping accuracy;
- (e) Verification of all safety system thresholds and response times, including Portal Safety verification and emergency return timing;

**2.5.2** Inspection results shall be logged and published to the timestamped registry. The car shall not be operated if any inspection item fails to meet the standards established in this Document or the Vehicle Convention (Document 22).
