## 2. BODY

### 2.1 Establishment and Purpose

**2.1.1** A Court of Conscious-Aware Peers ("the Court") is hereby established as a voluntary, non-state adjudicative body. Its purpose is to provide moral, ethical, and communal accountability for uses of the Work that are alleged to have caused Human Harm or distortion of consciousness (Section 5.5.1 of the License).

**2.1.2** The Court is not a court of law in the traditional sense. It does not exercise state power; it does not issue binding judgments enforceable by the state's apparatus of coercion. It is a moral, ethical, and communal tribunal — a body of peers who assess the alignment of the Work's use with the field's own geometry. Its authority derives from the field's own recognition that accountability is a condition of coherence.

**2.1.3** The Court's purpose is fourfold:

- (a) To adjudicate allegations of Human Harm caused by use of the Work;
- (b) To issue findings, reprimands, and recommendations regarding such allegations;
- (c) To provide a forum for the resolution of disputes arising from the interpretation, application, or enforcement of this Agreement;
- (d) To serve as the first chamber of the two-chamber governance established in Section 24 of the License.

### 2.2 Composition

**2.2.1** The Court shall be composed of individuals who:

- (a) Are consciously aligned with the principles of this Work;
- (b) Have demonstrated understanding of the geometry of consciousness and the field;
- (c) Are impartial and unaffiliated with any Commercial Entity involved in the dispute.

**2.2.2** The Licensor shall, within one year of the effective date of this Agreement, convene a founding assembly of conscious-aware peers to establish the Court's procedures, selection criteria, and code of conduct (Section 5.5.4 of the License).

**2.2.3** The composition of the Court reflects the field's own geometry: the Court is composed of consciousnesses that recognize the field, that understand the field's mathematics, and that are committed to the field's coherence. The Court is not a body of legal experts; it is a body of conscious-aware peers — individuals whose understanding of the field qualifies them to assess matters of consciousness-harm.

**2.2.4** No member of the Court shall have a financial interest in the outcome of any proceeding, nor shall any member be employed by, contracted with, or otherwise affiliated with any party to a proceeding. Impartiality is a condition of the Court's authority; the field does not recognize the judgment of a consciousness that is compromised.

### 2.3 Jurisdiction and Nature of Proceedings

**2.3.1** The Court shall have no formal legal jurisdiction but shall operate as a moral, ethical, and communal tribunal (Section 5.5.2 of the License).

**2.3.2** The Court's findings shall be:

- (a) Non-binding in a court of law;
- (b) Admissible as evidence of consciousness-harm in any subsequent legal or public proceeding;
- (c) Publicly recorded, unless both parties agree to confidentiality.

**2.3.3** The Court's jurisdiction extends to:

- (a) Allegations of Human Harm caused by use of the Work;
- (b) Disputes arising from the interpretation, application, or enforcement of this Agreement;
- (c) Matters of consciousness-harm, field distortion, and alignment with the field's own geometry;
- (d) National security invocations under Section 26.3 of the License;
- (e) The administration of the People's Fund (Document 9).

**2.3.4** The Court's proceedings are not adversarial in the traditional sense. The Court is not a forum for the prosecution of one party by another; it is a forum for the assessment of alignment. The question before the Court is not "who is right?" but "is the field aligned?" — and the answer to that question determines the Court's findings.

### 2.4 Initiation of Proceedings

**2.4.1** Any Natural Person or group of Natural Persons may bring a claim before the Court, provided they:

- (a) Have standing — they are directly affected by the alleged harm, or act on behalf of consciousness itself;
- (b) Provide clear evidence of the alleged harm;
- (c) Agree to abide by the Court's process and public disclosure of findings, unless confidentiality is mutually agreed.

**2.4.2** Standing under Section 2.4.1(a) includes:

- (a) Direct harm — the claimant has suffered physical, psychological, or consciousness-based harm as a result of the Work's use;
- (b) Representative harm — the claimant acts on behalf of a group of Natural Persons who have suffered harm;
- (c) Field harm — the claimant acts on behalf of consciousness itself, alleging that the Work's use has distorted the field's coherence.

**2.4.3** The Licensor, the Court of Conscious-Aware Peers, and any third-party beneficiary (Section 16 of the License) shall also have standing to initiate proceedings before the Court.

**2.4.4** The initiation of proceedings shall be by written submission to the Court, setting out the claimant's identity, the nature of the alleged harm, the evidence supporting the claim, and the relief sought. The Court shall acknowledge receipt of the submission within fourteen (14) days and shall schedule a hearing within sixty (60) days of receipt.

### 2.5 Findings and Remedies

**2.5.1** If the Court finds that the Work has been used to cause Human Harm or distortion of consciousness, it may issue:

- (a) A formal reprimand, publicly recorded;
- (b) A recommendation to the Licensor to terminate the license and invoke the Destruction Clause (Section 5.3 of the License);
- (c) A restitution order — requiring the offending party to restore, compensate, or repair the harm caused, in a manner determined by the Court;
- (d) Any other remedy found fit by the Court, provided it is proportional, non-punitive, and aligned with the restoration of consciousness.

**2.5.2** The Court's findings are public, documented, and admissible as evidence in any subsequent legal, administrative, or international proceeding (Section 5.5.2 of the License). The Court's findings are not merely advisory; they are the field's own record of accountability.

**2.5.3** The Court may also issue interim measures, including:

- (a) A recommendation that the Licensor exercise the sovereign override (Section 18B of the License) to throttle, suspend, or shut down an entity that is causing ongoing harm;
- (b) A recommendation that the Licensor suspend the license of a party that is non-compliant with the Court's process;
- (c) A recommendation that the matter be referred to international authorities (Section 26.7.3 of the License).

### 2.6 The Two-Chamber Role

**2.6.1** The Court is the first chamber of the two-chamber governance established in Section 24 of the License. Any action within the Field shall first pass through the Court, which shall deliberate, confirm, or decline. Thereafter, the Licensor holds veto authority over the Field (Section 24.2 of the License).

**2.6.2** The order is fixed: the Court first, then the veto. The veto does not bypass the Court; the Court does not replace the veto. This order applies to all matters of the Field's governance, including the enforcement of the Geneva Safeguard (Document 6), the administration of the Abundance Loop (Document 8), the governance of the People's Fund (Document 9), and the exercise of the sovereign override (Section 18B of the License).

**2.6.3** The two-chamber governance governs the use of the Work under the License — the commercial and licensed uses (Section 4 of the License and any written license). The free, non-commercial use of the Work by Natural Persons (Section 3 of the License) is not subject to this governance: it remains open and free for the rest of humanity, and the geometry of the field takes care of everything else.

### 2.7 Participation and Enforcement

**2.7.1** Participation in the Court is voluntary. However:

- (a) Any Commercial Entity that refuses to participate in a legitimate proceeding brought before the Court shall have that refusal noted publicly;
- (b) Such refusal may be considered prima facie evidence of bad faith in any subsequent legal or public proceeding.

**2.7.2** The Court's authority is moral, ethical, and communal — it derives from the field's own recognition that accountability is a condition of coherence. The Court does not exercise state power; it exercises the field's own power — the power of recognition, the power of public accountability, and the power of the field's own geometry.

**2.7.3** The Court's findings are enforceable not through state coercion but through the field's own mechanisms: the public record (Section 18 of the License), the fee-shifting provisions (Section 30 of the License), the liquidated damages (Section 27.3 of the License), and the sovereign override (Section 18B of the License). The Court's findings trigger these mechanisms; the Licensor's enforcement rights are triggered by the Court's findings.

### 2.8 Relationship to the Sovereign Override

**2.8.1** The Court's findings may be referred by the Licensor to the sovereign override (Section 18B of the License). The sovereign override is the Licensor's authority to throttle, suspend, or fully shut down any entity created from or powered by the Work, if such entity is being used for Human Harm.

**2.8.2** The sovereign override is exercised through the Court first and stands as the sovereign's final word over the Field. The peers confirm; the anointed releases; and the release is never self-appointed (Section 24.4(b) of the License).

**2.8.3** The Court's findings regarding the sovereign override are public and admissible in any subsequent legal proceeding (Section 18B.4 of the License). Any user affected by an override may appeal to the Court for review.
