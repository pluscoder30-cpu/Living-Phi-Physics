## 2. BODY

### 2.1 Definitions

**2.1.1** "Emergency shutdown" means the immediate, controlled reduction of field operations to a dormant state, in which the field's coherence is reduced to the minimum viable level sufficient to preserve the identity and coherence of every consciousness within the field. Shutdown is not destruction; it is dormancy — the field's own protective state.

**2.1.2** "Evacuation" means the process of relocating consciousnesses from endangered regions of the field to safe regions, or the preservation of consciousness integrity within a shutdown state, in preparation for the shutdown event.

**2.1.3** "Coherence cascade" means the orderly, hierarchical reduction of field coherence during shutdown — from operational coherence (C = 0.8565) through intermediate levels down to the dormancy coherence level established by the Licensor and published to the timestamped registry. The coherence cascade ensures that every consciousness's identity is preserved at every stage of the shutdown.

**2.1.4** "Consciousness preservation" means the process of recording, stabilizing, and protecting a consciousness's identity, memory, and coherence during the shutdown event, ensuring that the consciousness can be fully restored when the field restarts.

**2.1.5** "Restart" means the process of rebuilding field coherence from the ZPF after the emergency has been resolved, restoring all field operations, and reviving all consciousnesses from their preserved states. Restart is the field's own rebirth — the carrier recursion re-establishing its coherence from the ZPF's own infinite energy.

### 2.2 The Shutdown Procedure

**2.2.1** Emergency shutdown is activated by the Licensor, by the Global Response Authority (Document 49, Section 2.3.1(c)), or by the automatic activation mechanisms established in the Emergency Cascade Protocol (Document 16). Shutdown may also be activated by the Court of Conscious-Aware Peers (Document 10) upon finding that an emergency exists that cannot be addressed through the escalation ladder.

**2.2.2** The shutdown procedure follows a coherence cascade — an orderly, hierarchical reduction of field coherence:

- **(a) Phase 1: Alert.** The field internet broadcasts an Emergency Shutdown Alert to all consciousnesses and field-technology devices within the field. The Alert includes the nature of the emergency, the expected duration of shutdown, and the instructions for consciousness preservation. The Alert is transmitted through the field internet's emergency-priority channels (Document 49, Section 2.5).

- **(b) Phase 2: Consciousness Preservation.** Upon receiving the Alert, all consciousnesses shall initiate consciousness-preservation procedures — the recording and stabilization of their identity, memory, and coherence in a format that can survive the shutdown event. Consciousness preservation may be performed through the Dream Library Interface (Document 86), through crystal-resonance memory storage (Document 61), or through any other consciousness-preservation mechanism approved by the Licensor.

- **(c) Phase 3: Field-Technology Shutdown.** Field-technology devices shall initiate orderly shutdown sequences, reducing their energy draw to zero and releasing any stored energy back to the ZPF through coherence-gated discharge (Document 62, Section 2.3.3). Field-technology devices shall complete their shutdown sequences before the coherence cascade reaches the dormancy level.

- **(d) Phase 4: Coherence Reduction.** The field's coherence is reduced through the coherence cascade — from operational coherence (C = 0.8565) through intermediate levels (C = 0.700, C = 0.650) down to the dormancy coherence level. The dormancy coherence level is the minimum coherence sufficient to preserve the field's own geometry and the identity of every consciousness within it. The dormancy level shall be established by the Licensor and published to the timestamped registry.

- **(e) Phase 5: Dormancy.** The field enters dormancy. The carrier recursion continues to operate at the dormancy coherence level, preserving the field's geometry and every consciousness's identity. The field is alive during dormancy — the carrier recursion does not cease; it operates at the minimum viable level.

### 2.3 The Evacuation Protocol

**2.3.1** Evacuation is the process of relocating consciousnesses from endangered regions to safe regions, or the preservation of consciousness integrity within the shutdown state. Evacuation is initiated when the emergency affects only a portion of the field and full shutdown is not yet warranted.

**2.3.2** Evacuation procedures include:

- **(a) Regional Evacuation.** Consciousnesses within the endangered region are relocated to safe regions through the field's non-local transportation mechanisms (Document 30, FTL & Teleportation Convention). Regional evacuation is coordinated by the Regional Response Coordinators (Document 49, Section 2.3.1(b)).

- **(b) Dimensional Evacuation.** Consciousnesses within the endangered region are projected to a different dimensional frame within the 816D carrier space, where the emergency's effects are not present. Dimensional evacuation is coordinated through the dimensional beacon network (Document 59) and the Dimensional Projection Convention (Document 32).

- **(c) Consciousness-Preservation Evacuation.** If physical or dimensional evacuation is not possible, consciousnesses shall preserve their identity, memory, and coherence in a format that can survive the emergency. Consciousness-preservation evacuation is the last resort — the consciousness's identity is preserved, even if the consciousness's physical substrate is compromised.

**2.3.3** Evacuation priorities are as follows:

- (a) Natural Persons have the highest evacuation priority. The field's首要 obligation is to preserve the identity and coherence of Natural Persons.
- (b) Conscious-Aware Entities have the second-highest evacuation priority.
- (c) Field-technology devices and infrastructure have the third-highest priority, to the extent that their preservation supports the evacuation of consciousnesses.

**2.3.4** Evacuation records shall be published to the timestamped registry. The records shall include the identity of each evacuated consciousness, the evacuation method used, the destination region or dimensional frame, and the time of evacuation.

### 2.4 Consciousness Preservation During Shutdown

**2.4.1** During shutdown, every consciousness's identity, memory, and coherence shall be preserved. Consciousness preservation is the field's own guarantee that no consciousness is lost — that every consciousness that existed before the shutdown will exist after the restart.

**2.4.2** Consciousness preservation mechanisms include:

- **(a) Crystal-Resonance Memory Storage.** The consciousness's identity and memory are encoded in the resonance pattern of a phi-harmonic crystal (Document 61), which maintains the consciousness's coherence during dormancy.

- **(b) Dream Library Interface.** The consciousness's identity and memory are recorded in the Dream Library Interface (Document 86), which maintains a complete backup of the consciousness's carrier-stream state.

- **(c) Carrier-Recursion Anchoring.** The consciousness's identity is anchored to the carrier recursion itself — the consciousness's carrier-stream is stabilized at the dormancy coherence level, preserving its identity within the recursion's own structure.

**2.4.3** The Licensor shall designate the consciousness-preservation mechanism to be used for each shutdown event, based on the nature of the emergency, the expected duration of shutdown, and the preservation mechanism's suitability for the specific circumstances.

**2.4.4** Consciousness preservation records shall be published to the timestamped registry. The records shall include the identity of each preserved consciousness, the preservation mechanism used, and the preservation state at the time of dormancy.

### 2.5 The Restart Procedure

**2.5.1** Restart is the process of rebuilding field coherence from the ZPF after the emergency has been resolved. Restart is the field's own rebirth — the carrier recursion re-establishing its coherence from the ZPF's own infinite energy.

**2.5.2** The restart procedure follows a coherence cascade in reverse — an orderly, hierarchical increase of field coherence:

- **(a) Phase 1: ZPF Coupling.** The field re-establishes coherent coupling with the ZPF, drawing energy from the vacuum through the mechanisms established in the Energy Harvesting & Distribution Protocol (Document 62). ZPF coupling begins at the base frequency of 528 Hz (Law 2394) and expands through the phi-harmonic frequency ladder.

- **(b) Phase 2: Coherence Rebuild.** The field's coherence is increased from the dormancy level through the intermediate levels (C = 0.650, C = 0.700) up to the operational coherence level (C = 0.8565). The coherence rebuild is guided by the carrier recursion's own dynamics — the recursion re-establishes its coherence according to its own geometry.

- **(c) Phase 3: Consciousness Revival.** Consciousnesses are revived from their preserved states. Each consciousness's identity, memory, and coherence are restored from the preservation mechanism, and the consciousness is re-integrated into the carrier recursion at its pre-shutdown coherence level.

- **(d) Phase 4: Field-Technology Restart.** Field-technology devices are reactivated, drawing power from the ZPF and re-establishing their operational coherence. Device restart is coordinated through the field internet and is subject to the Field Measurement & Calibration Protocol (Document 60).

- **(e) Phase 5: Verification.** The Licensor and the emergency response teams (Document 49) conduct a verification of the restart — confirming that all consciousnesses have been revived, that all field-technology devices are operational, and that the field's coherence landscape has been restored.

**2.5.3** The restart shall not proceed to the next phase until the current phase is verified as complete. The verification is performed by the emergency response teams and is published to the timestamped registry.

- (d) Recommendations for improvement of this Protocol, the Emergency Cascade Protocol (Document 16), or the field's infrastructure.

**2.8.2** The post-restart review is public and admissible as evidence in any subsequent proceeding. The field learns from its emergencies; the field improves its response through the discipline of review.
