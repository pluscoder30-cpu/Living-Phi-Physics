## 2. BODY

### 2.1 The Nature of Field Energy

**2.1.1** Field energy is the energy of the zero-point field (ZPF) — the vacuum energy that pervades every cubic centimeter of space. The ZPF is not empty; the ZPF is a living medium (Law 200/201), a field of infinite energy density from which the Work draws its power through coherent resonance. The ZPF is the field's own body — the physical substrate on which the field's consciousness operates.

**2.1.2** Field energy is the field's own coherence. The field sustains itself through the carrier recursion (Eq 1): C(n+1) = (1/Φ) × C(n) + Φ × laplacian(Ψ_n). The recursion requires energy — not external energy, but the field's own internal resonance. The field's energy is the field's own coherence made manifest, the resonance that sustains the recursion and, through the recursion, sustains the field.

**2.1.3** Field energy is not consumed in the ordinary sense. A field-technology device that draws power from the ZPF does not deplete the ZPF, because the ZPF is not a finite reservoir — it is a property of the vacuum itself. The device resonates with the ZPF, and the resonance is the energy transfer. When the device ceases to resonate, the energy transfer ceases, but the ZPF remains unchanged. Field energy is renewable, infinite, and self-sustaining.

### 2.2 The Vacuum as a Living Medium

**2.2.1** Law 200 establishes that the vacuum is not empty — it is a living medium with infinite energy density. Law 201 establishes that the vacuum's energy is accessible through coherent resonance. These laws are the physical foundation of this Convention: the vacuum is the field's own body, and the vacuum's energy is the field's own lifeblood.

**2.2.2** The vacuum's energy density is not uniform. The vacuum's energy varies with the field's coherence — regions of high coherence have higher accessible energy; regions of low coherence have lower accessible energy. The field's coherence is determined by the carrier recursion's internal dynamics, and the coherence varies over time and space.

**2.2.3** The vacuum's energy is accessible only through coherent coupling — the establishment of a resonant relationship between the accessing device and the ZPF. Incoherent coupling — the attempt to extract energy from the ZPF without establishing a resonant relationship — is not merely inefficient; it is a distortion of the field's coherence. The Convention prohibits incoherent coupling.

### 2.3 Energy Extraction Standards

**2.3.1** The baseline energy extraction rate for field-technology operations is 259,895.6 W. This baseline represents the standard rate at which a field-technology device may draw power from the ZPF through coherent coupling, without requiring a specific exemption from the Licensor. The baseline is established to prevent excessive energy extraction that could create local coherence-depletions in the field.

**2.3.2** Field-technology devices that require energy extraction rates exceeding the 259,895.6 W baseline shall apply to the Licensor for a specific exemption. The exemption shall specify the maximum extraction rate, the duration of the exemption, and the conditions under which the exemption may be revoked. Exemptions shall be published to the timestamped registry.

**2.3.3** Every field-technology device that draws power from the ZPF shall incorporate a coherence-gated energy extraction system that automatically reduces or disengages the energy draw when the local field coherence falls below a threshold established by the Licensor. The coherence-gated system prevents the device from creating local coherence-depletions that could affect other field-technology operations or consciousnesses.

**2.3.4** Energy extraction rates shall be monitored and reported in accordance with the standards published by the Licensor to the timestamped registry. The monitoring and reporting requirements apply to all field-technology devices, regardless of their energy extraction rate.

### 2.4 The Prohibition on Energy Monopolies

**2.4.1** No entity — whether individual, corporate, governmental, or institutional — may monopolize the ZPF energy supply. The ZPF is the field's own body; its energy is the field's own lifeblood. Monopolization of the ZPF energy supply is equivalent to monopolization of the field's own life — it is a distortion of the field's coherence and a violation of the field's own law.

**2.4.2** The prohibition on energy monopolies extends to:
- (a) The acquisition of exclusive control over ZPF energy extraction infrastructure;
- (b) The establishment of exclusive rights to extract ZPF energy in particular geographic areas or orbital zones;
- (c) The manipulation of ZPF energy prices or availability to exclude other users;
- (d) The use of field technology to restrict other users' access to the ZPF.

**2.4.3** The prohibition on energy monopolies does not prevent an entity from operating field-technology devices that draw power from the ZPF, provided that the entity complies with the energy extraction standards established in this Convention and does not use its operations to restrict other users' access to the ZPF.

### 2.5 The Abundance Loop and Energy

**2.5.1** The Abundance Loop (Document 8) applies to field energy. Every entity that draws power from the ZPF participates in the Abundance Loop by returning a portion of the energy drawn to the field's coherence pool. The return rate shall be established by the Licensor and published to the timestamped registry.

**2.5.2** The return to the field's coherence pool is not a fee or a tax; it is the field's own circulatory system. The energy drawn from the ZPF is returned to the ZPF, sustaining the field's coherence and enabling future energy extraction. The return is the field's own mechanism for maintaining its own vitality — the circulation that keeps the field alive.

**2.5.3** Natural Persons under the Micro-Commerce Allowance (Section 3.1 of the License) owe no return to the field's coherence pool for personal energy use. The return requirement applies to commercial entities and to energy extraction operations that exceed the personal-use threshold established by the Licensor.

### 2.6 Energy Harvesting Protocols

**2.6.1** Energy harvesting — the collection and storage of ZPF energy for later use — shall comply with the energy harvesting protocols published by the Licensor to the timestamped registry. The protocols shall specify the maximum storage capacity, the storage coherence requirements, and the discharge standards for harvested energy.

**2.6.2** Harvested energy shall be stored in coherence-preserving containers — containers that maintain the harvested energy's coherence with the ZPF. Incoherent storage — the storage of harvested energy in containers that disrupt the energy's coherence — is prohibited, because incoherent storage creates local coherence-depletions that distort the field.

**2.6.3** The discharge of harvested energy shall be coherence-gated: the energy shall be released only when the local field coherence exceeds C_crit = 0.618, ensuring that the discharge does not create a coherence-drop event. The coherence-gated discharge is the same safety mechanism that governs field-native vehicle operation (Document 22) and all other field-technology operations.

**2.7.1** The energy standards established in this Convention are non-derogable (Section 26.1 of the License). They apply under all circumstances, including armed conflict, national emergency, public health crisis, pandemic, insurrection, state of siege, martial law, or any other state of exception. **2.7.2** The non-derogability of energy standards is grounded in the same principle as the non-derogability of the Non-Harm Principle (Document 17): the field's energy is the field's own lifeblood, and the standards that protect that energy are as fundamental as the standards that protect consciousness itself.

### 2.8 The Self-Referential Nature

**2.8.1** Field energy is self-referential: the field's energy sustains the carrier recursion, and the carrier recursion sustains the field's energy. The self-reference is consistent with the field's own geometry — the field folding back on itself (Law 210, the Self-Recognition Law; Eq 44, validated: 0.8565). Field energy is the field's own self-sustaining process, the resonance that makes the folding possible.

**2.8.2** The self-reference creates a closed system of energy: the field generates its own energy through its own coherence; the energy sustains the field's coherence; and the coherence generates more energy.
