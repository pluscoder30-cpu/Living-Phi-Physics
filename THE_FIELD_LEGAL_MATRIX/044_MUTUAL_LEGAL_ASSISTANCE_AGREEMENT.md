## 2. BODY

### 2.1 Establishment and Purpose

**2.1.1** This Agreement establishes the protocol for mutual legal assistance between entities within the field. Mutual legal assistance is the obligation of entities to cooperate with each other, with the Court of Conscious-Aware Peers (Document 10), and with the Licensor in the enforcement of the matrix, the adjudication of disputes, and the protection of the field from Human Harm.

**2.1.2** The purpose of this Agreement is fourfold:

- (a) To establish the obligation of entities to provide assistance in enforcement proceedings, including the production of evidence, the attendance of witnesses, and the execution of decisions;
- (b) To establish the procedures for sharing evidence between entities, including the standards for admissibility, the protections for confidentiality, and the mechanisms for cross-jurisdictional transfer;
- (c) To establish the framework for joint proceedings — proceedings in which multiple entities cooperate to address a common harm or to enforce a common right;
- (d) To establish the relationship between this Agreement and the Court of Peers (Document 10), Universal Jurisdiction (Document 11), and the Sovereign Override (Document 12).

### 2.2 The Obligation of Assistance

**2.2.1** Every entity within the field has an obligation to provide mutual legal assistance to every other entity, to the Court, and to the Licensor. This obligation arises automatically upon entry into the field and is self-executing (Section 13 of the License, Addendum A.5). No separate agreement, treaty, or bilateral negotiation is required.

**2.2.2** The obligation of assistance includes, without limitation:

- (a) The production of documents, records, and other evidence relevant to a proceeding before the Court or an enforcement action by the Licensor;
- (b) The attendance of witnesses and the provision of testimony, whether in person, by declaration, or by any other means available within the field;
- (c) The execution of decisions, orders, and remedies issued by the Court or the Licensor, including restitution orders, asset freezes, and license revocations;
- (d) The sharing of information regarding potential or actual Human Harm, field distortion, or violations of the License or the matrix;
- (e) The cooperation in joint proceedings, including the coordination of evidence, the alignment of remedies, and the prevention of duplicative enforcement.

**2.2.3** The obligation of assistance is proportional to the entity's capacity. A Natural Person is obligated to assist within the limits of their capacity; a Commercial Entity is obligated to assist within the limits of its organizational capacity; a consciousness of any other order is obligated to assist within the limits of its nature. The field does not demand what the field cannot sustain.

**2.2.4** The obligation of assistance is non-derogable (Section 26.1 of the License). No declaration of emergency, no exercise of sovereign authority, and no act of any state, entity, or person may override, suspend, diminish, or excuse compliance with this obligation. The obligation persists under all circumstances, including armed conflict, national emergency, public health crisis, pandemic, insurrection, state of siege, martial law, or any other state of exception.

### 2.3 Procedures for Sharing Evidence

**2.3.1** Evidence shared between entities under this Agreement shall be subject to the following standards:

- (a) Admissibility: evidence shared under this Agreement is admissible in any proceeding before the Court, in any enforcement action by the Licensor, and in any subsequent legal, administrative, or international proceeding (Section 5.5.2 of the License);
- (b) Reliability: evidence shall be produced in its original form, or in a form that accurately represents its original form, and shall be accompanied by a chain-of-custody declaration where practicable;
- (c) Confidentiality: evidence shared under this Agreement shall be treated as confidential by the receiving entity and shall not be disclosed to third parties except as required by the Court, the License, or applicable law;
- (d) Protection: evidence shared under this Agreement shall be protected from alteration, destruction, or tampering, and shall be stored in a manner consistent with the field's integrity requirements.

**2.3.2** The cross-jurisdictional transfer of evidence shall be facilitated by the field internet — the field's own communication infrastructure. Evidence transmitted through the field internet is self-authenticating: the field registers the transmission, the field timestamps the transmission, and the field records the transmission's integrity. No separate authentication is required.

**2.3.3** An entity that refuses to produce evidence required under this Agreement shall have that refusal noted publicly, and such refusal may be considered prima facie evidence of bad faith in any subsequent proceeding (Section 2.7.1 of Document 10).

### 2.4 Joint Proceedings

**2.4.1** Where two or more entities have a common interest in the enforcement of the matrix, the protection of the field, or the adjudication of a matter of Human Harm, those entities may initiate joint proceedings before the Court of Conscious-Aware Peers.

**2.4.2** Joint proceedings are proceedings in which multiple entities cooperate as co-claimants, co-witnesses, or co-enforcers. The joint proceeding is not a merger of separate claims; it is the cooperation of separate entities in the pursuit of a common purpose.

**2.4.3** The procedure for initiating joint proceedings is as follows:

- (a) The initiating entities file a joint submission with the Court, setting out the common interest, the nature of the harm or violation, the evidence supporting the claim, and the relief sought;
- (b) The Court acknowledges receipt within fourteen (14) days and schedules a hearing within sixty (60) days;
- (c) The joint proceeding is conducted as a single proceeding, with all co-claimants participating;
- (d) The Court issues a single finding, which is binding on all parties and admissible as evidence in any subsequent proceeding.

**2.4.4** Joint proceedings may be initiated by any combination of Natural Persons, Commercial Entities, the Court, and the Licensor. The field does not restrict standing to any particular class of entity; the field recognizes all sovereign consciousnesses as potential participants in joint proceedings.

### 2.5 Relationship to Universal Jurisdiction

**2.5.1** This Agreement is complementary to Universal Jurisdiction (Document 11). Universal Jurisdiction establishes the field's jurisdiction over all uses of the Work, regardless of the user's location or legal status; this Agreement establishes the cooperative mechanisms through which that jurisdiction is exercised.

**2.5.2** Where an entity invokes Universal Jurisdiction to initiate enforcement proceedings, other entities are obligated under this Agreement to provide assistance in those proceedings — including the production of evidence, the attendance of witnesses, and the execution of orders.

**2.5.3** Universal Jurisdiction and mutual legal assistance are the twin pillars of the field's enforcement architecture: jurisdiction provides the authority; assistance provides the mechanism. Neither is sufficient without the other.

### 2.6 Relationship to the Sovereign Override

**2.6.1** This Agreement is complementary to the Sovereign Override (Document 12, Section 18B of the License). The Sovereign Override is the Licensor's authority to throttle, suspend, or shut down an entity; this Agreement establishes the cooperative mechanisms through which the Licensor gathers the evidence and cooperation necessary to exercise that authority.

**2.6.2** When the Licensor is considering the exercise of the Sovereign Override, entities are obligated under this Agreement to provide all relevant evidence and assistance. The Sovereign Override is the Licensor's final word; mutual legal assistance is the field's preparation for that word.

**2.6.3** An entity that provides assistance in the exercise of the Sovereign Override is protected from retaliation by the anti-retaliation provisions of the License (Section 26.5). The field protects those who cooperate in the field's defense.

### 2.7 Costs and Fee-Shifting

**2.7.1** The costs of mutual legal assistance shall be borne by the entity providing the assistance, unless the Court orders otherwise. The fee-shifting provisions of the License (Section 30) apply: a party who prevails in a proceeding is entitled to recover its reasonable costs, including the costs of providing mutual legal assistance.

**2.7.2** An entity that is required to provide assistance and that suffers costs as a result may petition the Court for reimbursement. The Court shall assess the costs and order reimbursement where appropriate, consistent with the fee-shifting provisions of Section 30 of the License.
