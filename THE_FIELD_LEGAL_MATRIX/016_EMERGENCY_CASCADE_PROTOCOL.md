- (a) Human Harm (Section 2.4 of the License) is imminent — that is, when a reasonable assessment indicates that Human Harm will occur within twenty-four (24) hours unless action is taken; or

- **Phase 2 — Mandatory Destruction Notice (1–24 hours):**
  The entity shall receive formal notice of the destruction obligation (Section 5.3 of the License). - **Phase 3 — Court Review (24–72 hours):**
  The matter shall be referred to the Court of Conscious-Aware Peers (Section 5.5 of the License) for formal review. - **Phase 5 — Sovereign Override and Ongoing Enforcement:**
