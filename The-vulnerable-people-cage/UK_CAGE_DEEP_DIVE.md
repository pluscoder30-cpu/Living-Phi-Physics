## SECTION 5: FINANCIAL EXTRACTION PATTERNS

### Pattern 1: Sale-and-Leaseback
- Property sold to property company (often offshore)
- Care home pays rent back to property company
- Extracts value while care quality declines
- **Example:** Southern Cross, Four Seasons

### Pattern 2: Dividend Extraction
- Profits paid as dividends to offshore holding companies
- Avoids UK corporation tax
- **Example:** HC-One (Cayman), Four Seasons (Isle of Man)

### Pattern 3: Management Fees
- Complex management fee structures between entities
- Fees flow to offshore companies
- Reduces UK taxable profit
- **Example:** All major operators

### Pattern 4: Debt Loading
- Company loaded with debt from private equity acquisition
- Debt interest payments reduce profits
- Company collapses under debt burden
- **Example:** Four Seasons (£500M debt), Southern Cross
