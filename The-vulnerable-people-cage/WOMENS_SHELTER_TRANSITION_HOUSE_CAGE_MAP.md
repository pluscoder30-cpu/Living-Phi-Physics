## CAGE CRIME PATTERNS IDENTIFIED

### Pattern 1: Insurance-Death Profit Cycle (SAME AS SUPPORTIVE HOUSING)
- Resident dies → insurance pays → operator retains contract → no death-rate metric → cycle repeats
- **Women's shelter variant:** No death tracking, no accountability metric, 76% of homeless women die from overdose

### Pattern 2: Funding Drop-Off After Emergency
- Pandemic funding ($300M) expired Sep 2023
- No replacement funding committed
- 23% of shelters cut programming
- 620+ women/children turned away daily
- **Geometry:** Emergency spike → temporary funding → funding ends → crisis deepens → next emergency → cycle repeats

### Pattern 3: Indigenous Death Targeting
- 5/5 named Whitehorse victims = First Nations women
- 12/17 Yukon drug deaths = First Nations citizens
- 6x violence death rate for Indigenous women
- 26% of women killed by intimate partner (vs 5% population)
- Many on-reserve shelters lack comparable services
- **Geometry:** Colonial spoke — disproportionate death at center of cage

### Pattern 4: Harm Reduction Exclusion
- Only 26% of women-only shelters offer harm reduction
- Many VAW shelters operate "zero tolerance" / abstinence policies
- Women who use drugs barred from shelters
- Despite human rights laws prohibiting drug-use discrimination
- **Geometry:** Policy exclusion → hidden drug use → overdose → death
- Source: OHCHR submission 2026, HIV Legal Network

### Pattern 5: Structural Capacity Deficit
- 627 shelters operating (2014 data)
- 12,058 beds total
- 338 women + 201 children turned away on single day
- 19,000/month turned away
- 69% increase in turn-aways 2014-2018
- **Geometry:** Demand far exceeds supply → women forced back to abusers

### Pattern 6: Workforce Crisis
- Alberta: zero funding increases 10+ years
- YWCA Sudbury: "lose good workers" to better-paying jobs
- ACWS: 8 surveys since 2002 documenting wage gap
- Shelter workers: predominantly women, IBPOC, gender-diverse
- **Geometry:** Underpaid workforce → high turnover → service gaps → resident harm

### Pattern 7: Confidential Settlements (SAME AS SUPPORTIVE HOUSING)
- Deaths → lawsuits → confidential settlements → pattern invisible
- Families can't compare outcomes across facilities
- **Geometry:** Memory wipe — cage survives because accumulation is hidden

### Pattern 8: Executive Compensation Disconnect
- WSC annual report shows major donors ($100K+): TD Bank, Nestlé, Royal LePage, Shoppers Foundation
- Shelter workers underpaid, executive compensation not disclosed for most operators
- **Geometry:** Top-down extraction — growth rewarded regardless of outcomes
