- Closure history


### Trick 3: CRA T3010 Extraction
Every registered charity must file T3010 annually. This reveals:
- Total revenue and expenses
- Executive compensation (top 10 earners)
- Related party transactions
- Board member names

**How to access:**
```
1. Go to apps.cra-arc.gc.ca/ebci/hacc/srch/pub/dsplyRprtngPrd
2. Enter charity name or registration number
3. Download T3010 filing
4. Extract: Line 4880 (total compensation), Line 4500 (revenue)
```

- Lawsuit filings (statements of claim)
- Settlement agreements (if not sealed)
- Examination transcripts
- Court orders
