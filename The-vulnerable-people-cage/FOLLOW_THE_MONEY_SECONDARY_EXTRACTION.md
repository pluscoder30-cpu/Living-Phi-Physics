1. **Atira Property Management Inc. (APMI)** is a for-profit subsidiary that manages SROs for the non-profit — its financials are opaque
2. **Atira Development Society** handles construction — fees extracted through development

7. **Executive compensation** grows 28-98% regardless of death rates
8. **Zero criminal charges** despite forensic investigation finding rule-breaking

The for-profit extraction layer is the **hidden engine** of the cage. It's where the money goes AFTER it enters the non-profit system. The non-profit provides the moral cover ("helping vulnerable people"). The sole-source contracts provide the funding. The for-profit subsidiaries provide the extraction mechanism.

**The cage survives because death costs nothing and growth rewards failure.**
