## 🚨 CAGE ELEMENTS — HARM & MISMANAGEMENT

### 1. Southern Community Food Security Society — RAYMOND, ALBERTA
**STATUS: PERMANENTLY CLOSED (August 2026)**

**Health Violations Found by Alberta Health Services (AHS):**
- Mouse droppings in food area
- Improperly refrigerated eggs
- Expired toddler formula
- Hand-wash sink inaccessible
- Incompatible storage (food stored with non-food items)
- Overcrowded, disorganized storage
- Overhead door open allowing pest access
- Three cats present in food establishment during inspection

**Shutdown Date:** August 14, 2026
**Organization Statement:** "No food will be distributed through our organization and the thrift store will cease to take donations or selling goods."

**Root Causes Cited:**
- Routinely short-staffed
- Lack of government support
- "Some have put up major roadblocks to our success in helping relieve food insecurity in rural southern Alberta communities"

**Cage Pattern:** Religious-affiliated charity (Latter-day Saints community) → health violations → forced closure → community left without food security services

### 2. SYSTEMIC SERVICE REDUCTION — NATIONAL CRISIS

**The "Unprecedented Demand" Cage:**
- **52%** of food banks reduced food distribution in 2025
- **23%** ran out of food before demand was met
- Only **33%** of Ontario food banks could meet demand
- **150%** increase in visitors at Moose Jaw food bank (4 years)
- **8.7M** food bank visits in Ontario in 2025
- **1M+** Ontarians visited a food bank in the past year

**Service Changes Implemented:**
- Reduced from weekly to bi-weekly visits
- Reduced from 7 days to 5 days of food per visit
- Cut wraparound support services
- Limited how often clients can pick up goods
- Employees purchasing food for programs with personal funds

**CEO Quote (Melissa From, Calgary Food Bank):**
"What we're seeing really across the country and certainly across the province of Alberta has been just unprecedented growth in demand and need for food support and service from food banks."

### 8. THE DEMAND-SUPPLY CAGE

**The Infinite Loop:**
1. Food insecurity rises → food banks expand
2. Food banks expand → government reduces social spending
3. Government reduces spending → food insecurity rises
4. Repeat

**Evidence:**
- Food banks introduced in 1980s as "temporary" solution
- 2026: Now permanent infrastructure with 5,500+ locations
- HungerCount report shows consistent year-over-year increases
- Food bank use has tripled since 2019 in Toronto

**Quote (CBC comment):**
"Just like soup kitchens in the 1930's, food banks were supposed to be 'temporary' when first opened in the 1980's. Well here we are in 2026 and food banks are not only permanent necessities in an ongoing disastrous economy but the demand has increased so much that they can only give needy Canadians less food."

3. **Executives earn $100K-300K** → while staff buy food with personal funds

**The cage is not about feeding people. It's about maintaining a system that perpetuates food insecurity while generating tax benefits, executive compensation, and political cover.**
