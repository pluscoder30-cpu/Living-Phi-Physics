## THE 15 YOUTH JUSTICE CAGE CRIMES

### CRIME 1: Indigenous Overrepresentation (The Colonial Engine)
**Pattern:** Indigenous youth represent ~8% of Canadian youth population but 40-50% of youth in custody. System designed to criminalize Indigenous behaviors.

**Geometry:** This is the **base layer** of the cage. Every other crime sits on top of this one.

**Evidence:**
- Indigenous youth: ~8% of youth population, 43-48% of youth in custody (varies by province)
- Saskatchewan: Indigenous youth = 80%+ of youth in custody
- Manitoba: Indigenous youth = 70%+ of youth in custody
- Youth incarceration rate for Indigenous youth is **44x higher** than non-Indigenous youth
- Federal government acknowledges "overrepresentation crisis" but funding doesn't match
