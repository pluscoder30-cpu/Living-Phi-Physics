## KEY FINDINGS

### 1. The Compensation-Death Disconnect
Executive compensation has grown **28-98%** since 2020 while **330+ residents die annually** in the same system. There is **zero correlation** between death rates and executive pay.

### 2. The Growth-Reward Paradox
- Connective: **900% revenue growth** in 10 years → Won "Housing Provider of the Year" (2025)
- Atira: **252% funding increase** → CEO married to funder's CEO
- BC Housing: **CEO pay doubled** → System produces same death toll

### 3. The Accountability Vacuum
- **0 criminal charges** against ANY operator despite 330+ annual deaths
- **0 criminal charges** despite E&Y forensic investigation finding rule-breaking
- **0 criminal charges** despite evidence destruction (deleted texts, altered minutes)
- **0 criminal charges** despite bodies undiscovered for 3-11 days

### 4. The Insurance Shield
- BC Housing requires $10M CGL insurance from operators
- Insurance premiums may be reimbursed as "non-manageable costs"
- Insurance pays settlements → operator retains contract → death costs nothing
- Taxpayer funds the insurance → taxpayer funds the settlements → cycle repeats

### 5. The Confidential Settlement Eraser
- Multiple settlements with CONFIDENTIAL terms
- Prevents pattern recognition across facilities
- Each family starts from zero — can't compare to previous cases
- Public can't see accumulation of deaths
